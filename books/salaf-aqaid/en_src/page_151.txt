# English source for book page 151

[en]
The noble Prophet, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“A man outwardly does the deeds of the people of Paradise all his life, while he is one of the people of Hell; and a man outwardly does the deeds of the people of Hell, while he is one of the people of Paradise.”

[en]
On another occasion he said:

[en]
“A man does the deeds of the people of Paradise until there is only an arm's length between him and Paradise; then what is written in the decree overtakes him, and he does the deeds of the people of Hell until he enters Hell. And a man does the deeds of the people of Hell, until there is only an arm's length between him and Hell; then what is written in the decree overtakes him, and he does the deeds of the people of Paradise and goes into Paradise.”

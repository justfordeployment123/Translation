# English source for book page 18

Whereas the greatest corruption is that someone should corrupt another's beliefs, spread corruption in his ideas and doctrines, and turn him away from the natural path on which Allah created him. The Prophet said:

[en]
“Every newborn is born upon the natural disposition (fitrah). It is his parents who later make him a Jew, a Christian or a Magian.”

[en]
On another occasion the noble Prophet, peace be upon him, addressed the noble Companions and said:

[en]
“Listen carefully! My Lord has commanded me to teach you what you do not know. Among the things He has taught me this very day is this: ‘All wealth that I have given to a servant is lawful. I created all My servants hanif (upright upon the truth). The devils came to them and led them astray from the religion. The devils declared unlawful to them what I had made lawful for them. It was the devils who told them to associate with Me that for which I have sent down no evidence ...’”

# English source for book page 48

[en]
And do not be like the ones who became divided and differed after the clear proofs had come to them.

[en]
The noble Prophet, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“This community will split into seventy-three groups. Seventy-two groups will go to Hell and one group to Paradise. That group is the ‘Jama'ah’ (the community).”

[en]
On another occasion he said:

[en]
“Hold fast to the Jama'ah. Beware of division. Satan is with the one who is alone, and he is further away from two (than from one). Whoever wants the middle of Paradise, let him hold fast to the Jama'ah.”

[en]
Abdullah ibn Mas'ud, may Allah be pleased with him, said: “The Jama'ah is whatever agrees with the truth, even if you are alone.”

[en]
In conclusion: Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah are those who hold to the way of the noble Prophet, peace be upon him, his Companions and their Successors, and who follow their path in beliefs, in word and in deed.

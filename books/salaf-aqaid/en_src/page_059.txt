# English source for book page 59

[hen]
The Principles of the Creed of the Righteous Predecessors (Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah)

[en]
Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah, who follow the way of the Righteous Predecessors, act on clear and established principles in beliefs and in deeds and conduct. These principles are taken from the Quran and the Sunnah according to the understanding of the Righteous Predecessors.

[en]
The noble Prophet, peace be upon him, explained the principles of the religion very clearly. It is now not permissible for anyone to add a new principle to the principles of the religion and claim that it is part of them. That is why Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah hold to the principles of the religion. They avoid the use of innovated terms and use the terms of the Shariah. In this way the true continuity of the Righteous Predecessors becomes evident.

[en]

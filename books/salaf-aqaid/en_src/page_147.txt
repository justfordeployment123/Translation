# English source for book page 147

[en]
Fifth: The disbelief of doubt: The one who commits the disbelief of doubt is not certain whether the Prophet is truthful or a liar. He is in doubt. He hesitates over whether to accept what the Prophet says or not. What is required of a person is that he be certain about the teachings the Messenger brought, that they are undoubtedly the truth; but whoever is hesitant about following the Prophet's teachings, or thinks that the reality could be contrary to the Prophet's teachings, such a person is caught in the disbelief of doubt. These kinds of disbelief make it necessary that if one who commits any of them dies holding that same belief, he remains in Hell forever and all his deeds are lost. Allah the Exalted says:

The second kind, minor disbelief: Minor disbelief does not take a person out of the fold of Islam. The Lawgiver has applied the word disbelief to some major sins by way of rebuke and reprimand, because such major sins are traits of disbelief.

# English source for book page 27

[fen]
This sermon is called the “Khutbat al-Hajah” (the sermon of need). It is prescribed to recite this sermon before saying anything important and necessary. The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, taught the noble Companions to recite this sermon before an important address, whether it was the address at a marriage or on Friday. This sermon is reported in most books of hadith. The wording of some narrations differs from one another, but its importance is acknowledged. For its detailed referencing, see the book (Khutbat al-Hajah) by the hadith master of his age, the great scholar Muhammad Nasir ad-Din al-Albani, may Allah have mercy on him.

Thus knowledge and action are inseparable; the two are never apart from each other. Action is the face of knowledge and its essence.

Most of the evidence of the Quran and the Sunnah shows that faith has many degrees and branches. Faith both increases and decreases. The faith of different believers is also greater or lesser. Allah the Exalted says:

[en]
And those who have believed will increase in faith.

[en]
And He says:

[en]
It has increased them in faith.

[en]
And He says:

[en]
And when His verses are recited to them, it increases them in faith; and upon their Lord they rely.

[en]
And He says:

[en]
It is He who sent down tranquillity into the hearts of the believers that they would increase in faith along with their (present) faith.

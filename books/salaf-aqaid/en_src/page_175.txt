# English source for book page 175

[en]
Allah the Exalted says:

[en]
So when the magicians came.

[en]
And He says:

[en]
And they presented a great (feat of) magic.

[en]
And He says:

[en]
But the devils disbelieved, teaching people magic.

[en]
However, it is also a fact that a magician can harm anyone by magic only if Allah the Exalted wills it. If Allah the Exalted does not will it, he cannot harm a single hair of anyone. So it was said:

[en]
But they do not harm anyone through it except by permission of Allah. And the people learn what harms them and does not benefit them.

[en]
Accordingly, whoever believes that magic brings benefit or harm without the permission of Allah has committed disbelief.

[fen]
(al-Baqarah 2:102; see al-Mughni 8/150–151)

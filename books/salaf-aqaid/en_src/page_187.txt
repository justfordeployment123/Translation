# English source for book page 187

[en]
So ask the people of the message if you do not know.

[en]
Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah say that understanding of the religion cannot be attained unless a person both acquires knowledge and acts according to it. Whoever has acquired much knowledge but has not acted according to it and has not followed the Sunnah of the Prophet is not a faqih (one who has understanding of the religion).

[en]
Here end the details of the seventh principle, “The Sources for Deriving Rulings and Issues, and the Method of Reasoning from Evidence”, among the principles of religion that are established according to Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah. Now the details of the eighth principle in this series, that “obedience to Muslim rulers in what is permissible” is obligatory, are set out.

# English source for book page 68

[en]
The Oneness of worship requires that worship be done for Allah the Exalted alone, and for no one else.

[en]
Worship is of two kinds: the speech of the heart and the tongue, and the action of the heart and of the other working limbs of the body. The Noble Quran says:

[en]
{{QEN 6:162 6:163}}

[en]
In another place Allah the Exalted says:

[en]
Unquestionably, for Allah is the pure religion.

[en]
Second, worship should be in accordance with the teachings of Allah and His Messenger, peace be upon him.

[en]
Thus, when the Oneness of Allah is upheld in worship, obedience and humble submission, the requirements of (there is no god but Allah) are fulfilled. And by following the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, completely in this, the requirements of (Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah) are fulfilled.

# English source for book page 56

[en]
First: The only way to be rid of sectarianism, and to set the Muslims in general, and the scholars and callers in particular, on the path of unity and agreement, is to adopt the beliefs of the Righteous Predecessors, because these beliefs are taken from divine revelation and the life of the Prophet, and the noble Companions also held these same beliefs.

[en]

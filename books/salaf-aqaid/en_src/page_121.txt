# English source for book page 121

[en]
He says:

[en]
If you disbelieve - indeed, Allah is Free from need of you. And He does not approve for His servants disbelief. And if you are grateful, He approves it for you; and no bearer of burdens will bear the burden of another.

[en]
Whoever He leads astray has no argument and no excuse, because He sent the Messengers so that no one could have an argument. He attributed a person's deeds to that person himself and made them his own earning. He has charged a human being only with what he is able to bear. Allah the Exalted says:

[en]
This Day every soul will be recompensed for what it earned. No injustice today!

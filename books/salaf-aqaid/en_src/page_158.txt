# English source for book page 158

[en]
{{QEN 4:122}}

[en]
But in the end Allah the Exalted, by His favour and grace, will forgive the disobedient among the people of Tawhid, because this is the promise of Allah the Exalted to the people of Tawhid and not to others. Allah the Exalted says:

[en]
Indeed, Allah does not forgive association with Him, but He forgives what is less than that for whom He wills.

[en]
This brings to an end the explanations of the fourth principle, “Faith in the Texts of the Promises and the Warnings”, among the principles of the religion that are established according to Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah. Now the details of the fifth principle, “Loyalty and Disavowal”, are presented.

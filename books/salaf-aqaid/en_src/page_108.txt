# English source for book page 108

[en]
The major signs of the Hour (al-alamat al-kubra): The major signs of the Hour will show that the Hour is near. Soon after these signs appear, the Hour will come. Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah believe in the major signs of the Hour that are mentioned in the narrations. The details are as follows:

[en]
The appearance of the Mahdi: The Mahdi's name is Muhammad ibn Abdullah. By family he belongs to the Prophet's Household. He will come out from the direction of the east and will rule for seven years. He will fill the earth, which will be full of injustice and oppression, with justice and fairness. In his time the Ummah will enjoy such prosperity as it will never have known before. The earth will bring forth its vegetation. The sky will send down rain. The Mahdi will bestow wealth and goods on countless people.

[en]
The appearance of the Dajjal and the descent of Isa, peace be upon him: Isa, peace be upon him, will descend near the white minaret on the eastern side of Damascus. He will issue rulings according to the Shariah of Muhammad and will act upon it himself. He will kill the Dajjal and establish the rule of Islam in the world. He will descend at the very moment when the army of the people of truth is fully ready to fight the Dajjal. It will be the time of prayer. Having descended, he will pray behind the commander of the army.

[fen]
The tribulation of the appearance of the Dajjal is one of the great tribulations, because the Dajjal will be the source of disbelief, misguidance and tribulations. That is why all the prophets and Messengers warned their nations of the tribulation of the Dajjal. The noble Prophet, peace be upon him, used to seek refuge with Allah from the tribulation of the Dajjal at the end of every prayer. He, peace be upon him, also warned his Ummah of this tribulation.

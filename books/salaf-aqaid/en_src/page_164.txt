Second: Those who deserve love and loyalty in one respect, and enmity and hatred in another: these are the Muslims who do not carry out the obligations of the Shariah and who commit forbidden acts and deeds that do not reach the level of disbelief. Such people must be given sincere advice, and their forbidden acts must be denounced. It is not permissible to stay silent about their offences; rather, they should be denounced, and such people should be enjoined to do good and forbidden from evil. The prescribed punishments (hudud) should be carried out on them and penalties imposed, so that they give up their offences and repent of their sins. Abdullah, may Allah be pleased with him, had the nickname Himar (donkey). When he drank wine, he was brought before the Prophet, and the noble Prophet, peace be upon him, dealt with him in this same way too.

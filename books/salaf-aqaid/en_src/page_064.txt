[en]
The reason for this is that people's hearts by nature accept the truth that the Lord of this universe is Allah the Exalted. However, a person who believes in this kind of Tawhid alone will not be called a believer in Tawhid until he believes in the second kind of Tawhid, the Oneness of worship.

[en]
Second, the Oneness of worship: The Oneness of worship means holding Allah the Exalted as one in the actions of His servants. It is also called Tawhid al-Ibadah (the Oneness of worship). Its meaning is this: a person firmly believes that Allah the Exalted alone is the true deity, and that there is no deity besides Him.

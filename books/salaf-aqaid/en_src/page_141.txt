# English source for book page 141

[en]
The noble Prophet, peace be upon him, urged great caution lest anyone declare another a disbeliever without clear evidence. He said:

[en]
“Whoever says to his brother, ‘O disbeliever!’, then one of the two deserves this title. If he is as he said (then he deserves it); otherwise the word comes back upon the one (who said it).”

[en]
On another occasion he said:

[en]
“And whoever calls a man a disbeliever, or calls him an enemy of Allah, when he is not so, this title will come back upon the one (who said it).”

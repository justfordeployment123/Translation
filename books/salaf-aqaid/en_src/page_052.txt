# English source for book page 52

[en]
They love for the sake of Allah and hate for the sake of Allah. They are gentle towards the Muslims and stern against the disbelievers. They do not change with changes of time and place. In this way they are an example worth following for the righteous, who guide others to the truth and show them the Straight Path.

[en]
Ninth: They take no name for themselves other than Islam and the Sunnah and the Jama'ah.

[en]
Tenth: They pay great attention to spreading correct beliefs and the teachings of the moderate religion. They teach people the matters of the religion; they guide them, teach them goodness, and give their affairs great importance.

[en]
Eleventh: In their sayings, their beliefs and their call, they are the firmest and most steadfast of people.

[en]
Twelfth: They strive with all their might for unity and harmony to remain among the Muslims, and for the Muslims not to fall prey to sectarianism. They urge people to stay united and to avoid division.

[en]
Thirteenth: Allah the Exalted has kept Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah from pronouncing verdicts of disbelief against Muslims. If they pass any judgement on someone, they pass a judgement of complete justice after full investigation.

[en]
Fourteenth: Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah love one another. They pray for mercy for one another.

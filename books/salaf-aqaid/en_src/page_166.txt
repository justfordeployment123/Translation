# English source for book page 166

[en]
And in another place He says:

[en]
You will not find a people who believe in Allah and the Last Day having affection for those who oppose Allah and His Messenger, even if they were their fathers or their sons or their brothers or their kindred.

[en]
Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah hold that love and loyalty for the sake of Allah have certain rights that must be fulfilled. These rights are:

[en]
First: Emigration from the lands of disbelief to the lands of Islam. Excepted from this are the weak, who cannot emigrate for reasons that the Shariah accepts.

[en]
Second: Helping the Muslims, supporting them with one's life, wealth and tongue, and sharing in their sorrow and their joy.

[en]
Third: Liking for the Muslims the same good that one likes for oneself, and disliking for them the same evil that one dislikes for oneself. Not mocking them. Loving them, sitting in their gatherings and taking part in their consultations.

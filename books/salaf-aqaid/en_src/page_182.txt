# English source for book page 182

[en]
Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah do not put anyone's words before the words of Allah and the words of the Messenger. Allah the Exalted says:

[en]
{{QEN 49:1}}

[en]
They know that putting oneself before Allah and His Messenger, peace be upon him, is to speak lies against Allah without knowledge, which is a deception of Satan.

[en]
According to Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah, clear reason always agrees with authentic transmission. That is, sound reason is never contrary to authentic and established reports. When any difficulty arises, they give precedence to authentic transmission. There is no difficulty in this, because nothing has reached us through authentic transmission that reason finds hard to accept. Things have indeed come through authentic transmission that leave reason amazed; however, reason affirms everything that has come through authentic transmission, but it is not necessary that authentic transmission should also affirm everything that occurs to reason.

[en]
Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah do not belittle the importance of reason, because reason is the very ground on which a person is made legally accountable. However, they say: reason does not take precedence over the Shariah, because if it did, the servants of Allah would have no need of the Prophets and Messengers.

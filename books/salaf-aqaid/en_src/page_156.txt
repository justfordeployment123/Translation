# English source for book page 156

[en]
“There is no one whose deeds will take him into Paradise.” It was said: Not even you, O Messenger of Allah? He said: “Not even I, unless Allah covers me with His mercy.”

[en]
Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah do not consider punishment inevitable for anyone whose sin does not reach the level of disbelief. If such a person takes the path of goodness and does good deeds, or repents, or is afflicted with hardships and sufferings and with illnesses that wipe out his sins, then Allah the Exalted sometimes also forgives his sins.

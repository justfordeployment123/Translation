# English source for book page 176

[en]
Whoever considers magic permissible must be put to death, because the Muslims have consensus (ijma') that magic is forbidden. The magician will be asked to repent. If he repents, well and good; otherwise his head is to be struck off.

[en]
One of the principles of the creed of Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah is affirming righteous dreams. A righteous dream is a part of prophethood. The true insight (firasah) of the righteous is also a reality. The Noble Quran says:

[en]
“Indeed I have seen in a dream that I (must) sacrifice you, so see what you think.” He said, “O my father, do as you are commanded. You will find me, if Allah wills, of the steadfast.”

[en]
The noble Prophet, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Nothing of prophethood remains except glad tidings.” The Companions, may Allah be pleased with them, asked: What are the glad tidings? He said:

[en]
“A righteous dream.”

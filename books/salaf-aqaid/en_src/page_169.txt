# English source for book page 169

[en]
Tenth: Not being the first to greet the disbelievers with as-salamu alaykum (peace be upon you).

[en]
Here end the details of the fifth principle, “Loyalty and Disavowal”, among the principles of the religion that Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah hold. Now the details of the sixth principle, “Affirming the Karamat of the Friends of Allah”, are presented.

# English source for book page 161

[en]
“Whoever has three things in him has found the sweetness of faith: the one to whom Allah and His Messenger, peace be upon him, are dearer than all else; the one who loves a servant of Allah only for the sake of Allah; and the one who hates to return to disbelief after Allah has saved him from it, just as he would hate to be thrown into the Fire.”

[en]
Fourth: By adopting this belief, faith becomes complete. The noble Messenger, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Whoever loved for the sake of Allah, hated for the sake of Allah, gave for the sake of Allah and withheld for the sake of Allah has completed his faith.”

[en]
Fifth: Adopting this belief is also necessary because whoever loves others besides Allah and their religion, and hates Allah, His religion and the people of Allah, is a disbeliever and denies Allah utterly.

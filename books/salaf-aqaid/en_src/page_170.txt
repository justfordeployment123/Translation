# English source for book page 170

[hen]
The Sixth Principle: Affirming the Karamat of the Friends of Allah

[en]
One of the important principles of the creed of the Righteous Predecessors, Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah, is affirming the karamat (wonders granted to the friends of Allah). A karamah is something that breaks the normal course of things (something out of the ordinary), which Allah the Exalted brings about at the hands of some of His righteous servants, to honour them.

[fen]
A karamah is something that breaks the normal course of things (something out of the ordinary) which is not linked to a claim of prophethood, nor is it a prelude to prophethood. Allah the Exalted brings it about at the hands of some of His righteous servants, to honour them. These righteous servants of Allah keep to the rulings of the Shariah. If a karamah is not tied to sound faith and righteous deeds, then it is istidraj (something by which Allah gives respite and does not seize at once).

[fen]
Karamat also occurred among the earlier nations, as Surat al-Kahf and other surahs tell. In the first generations of this Ummah, karamat appeared at the hands of the noble Companions and their Successors. Most books of authentic hadiths contain many reports of a great many such karamat, which thousands of trustworthy scholars witnessed and narrated. This chain of karamat still continues, and it will continue for as long as Allah the Exalted wills.

[fen]
The appearance of karamat at the hands of the friends of Allah is in fact a miracle of the noble Prophets, because the karamah of a friend of Allah comes about through the blessing of following the Prophet. The occurrence of karamat is sound according to reason, and it is not rationally impossible.

[fen]
Sometimes Allah the Exalted opens ever new horizons of knowledge for a believing servant, and for him this is a karamah greater than other, material karamat.

# English source for book page 67

[en]
Allah the Exalted argued against them with this very evidence: since you acknowledge the truth that Allah the Exalted alone is the Creator and Provider, the Disposer and Governor of the universe, who controls it, why then do you not believe in the truth that Allah the Exalted alone is the true deity? How strange it is that you accept what necessitates it and yet deny what it necessitates! This is a plain contradiction, which sound reason and an upright natural disposition can never accept. If someone accepts the Oneness of Lordship and does not accept the Oneness of worship, he is not a believer. The polytheists of Makkah accepted the Oneness of Lordship, yet Allah the Exalted did not count them among the people of faith; rather, He called them disbelievers, because they associated partners with Allah the Exalted in worship.

[en]
So this is the point at which the belief of the Righteous Predecessors about the Oneness of worship stands apart from, and differs from, that of others. Unlike others, they do not take Tawhid to mean only that there is no creator but Allah. In their view, the requirements of the Oneness of worship are not fulfilled without two basic things:

[en]
First, every kind of worship is done for Allah the Exalted alone, and for no one else; no right of the Creator is given to the creation, and no quality belonging to the Creator is ascribed to the creation. That is, no one but Allah is worshipped, no one but Him is prayed to, no one but Him is prostrated to, no vow is made to anyone but Him, and no one but Him is relied on.

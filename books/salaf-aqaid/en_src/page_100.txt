# English source for book page 100

[en]
{{QEN 4:65}}

[en]
Every prophet was sent specifically to his own people, whereas he was sent to all the people of the whole world. Allah says:

[en]
And We have not sent you except comprehensively to mankind as a bringer of good tidings and a warner.

[en]
Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah also believe that Allah the Exalted supported the noble Prophet, peace be upon him, with outstanding miracles and clear signs.

[en]
The greatest of these miracles is the Noble Quran itself, with which Allah the Exalted challenged the most eloquent and articulate people on the face of the earth, the people most in command of speech. After the Noble Quran, the greatest miracle of the noble Messenger, peace be upon him, is the Night Journey and the Ascension (al-Isra' wal-Mi'raj).

[en]
Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah believe with certainty that the noble Messenger, peace be upon him, was taken up to the heavens while awake, in body and soul together. This took place on the night of the Night Journey and the Ascension, when he was taken by night from al-Masjid al-Haram to al-Masjid al-Aqsa, as Allah the Exalted says in the Noble Quran:

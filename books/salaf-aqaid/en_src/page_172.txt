# English source for book page 172

[fen]
A karamah neither establishes any ruling of the Shariah nor negates any ruling of the Shariah. The reason is that the sources of the rulings of the Shariah are known: the Book of Allah and the Sunnah of His Messenger, peace be upon him. When a karamah appears at the hands of a Muslim, he should thank Allah for this gift of His. He should ask Allah for steadfastness, and pray that if the karamah is a trial and a test for him, Allah keep him safe from its tribulation. He should keep the matter of the karamah secret, and not make it a means of pride and arrogance by showing and announcing it before people. If he does so, he will fall into the pit of destruction. Allah alone knows how many people there are whom Satan led astray in this way, so that their life in this world and in the Hereafter was ruined, and this became a calamity for them.

Bear in mind that Allah the Exalted has described the attributes of His friends in many verses of the Noble Quran, and they are brought together in verses 63 to 74 of Surat al-Furqan. These attributes are also described in many hadiths. Some of them, by way of example, are these: The friends of Allah have complete faith in the six pillars of faith. They have taqwa, which means fearing Allah, acting on the Sunnah of the Prophet, preparing for the Hereafter, loving for the sake of Allah and hating for the sake of Allah. Seeing the friends of Allah reminds one of Allah. They walk upon the earth gently and humbly. When the ignorant address them, they still say what is right (and walk on). They worship Allah at night. They often pray to Allah the Exalted to protect them from the punishment of Hell. When they spend, they are neither extravagant nor stingy. They do not associate partners with Allah. They do not kill unjustly. They do not commit adultery. They do not bear false witness. When they pass by something vain, they pass by with dignity, and when the verses of their Lord are recited to them (as a reminder), they do not fall upon them deaf and blind. Their prayer is: “Our Lord, grant us from among our wives and offspring comfort to our eyes and make us an example for the righteous.”

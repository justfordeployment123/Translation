[hen]
Other Miracles of the Bearer of the Message, Muhammad, Peace Be upon Him

[en]
The splitting of the moon: that is, the moon splitting into two pieces at his gesture. This was a very great sign of his prophethood that Allah the Exalted granted him.

[en]
This took place in Makkah when the polytheists demanded that he, peace be upon him, show them a sign.

[en]
The increase of food: This miracle appeared more than once at his blessed hands, peace be upon him.

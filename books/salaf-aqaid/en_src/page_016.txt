# English source for book page 16

[en]
It was deviation from the correct creed, turning away from the methodology of the Righteous Predecessors, and being deceived by the false doctrines and embellished words of the followers of falsehood that divided the Ummah into sects, weakened its strength and brought its glory to dust. The Ummah's present wretched state is clear evidence of this.

[en]
There is only one way out of this painful situation: that the Ummah should once again follow the methodology that the Prophet of mercy, peace be upon him, instructed it to adopt, and on which the noble Companions, their Successors and the imams of guidance walked, because the last part of this Ummah will be set right only by what set right its first part. Straying from the path of Tawhid and turning away from the methodology of the Righteous Predecessors is contrary to justice and fairness, and it is also an affront and hostility to reason. Allah the Exalted says:

[en]
We have already sent Our messengers with clear evidences and sent down with them the Scripture and the balance that the people may maintain (their affairs) in justice.

[en]
And the greatest justice is Tawhid. It is the foundation of justice. The greatest wrong is shirk.

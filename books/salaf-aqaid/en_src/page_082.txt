# English source for book page 82

[en]
“He descends, without how.”

[en]
Imam Nu'aym ibn Hammad al-Khuza'i, may Allah have mercy on him, said: “Whoever likens Allah to His creation has disbelieved. Whoever denies the attributes of Allah has disbelieved. And the attributes of Allah that Allah and His Messenger, peace be upon him, have described are not tashbih (likening).”

[en]
One of the elders of the Righteous Predecessors said: “The foot of Islam stands firm only on the bridge of submission.”

[en]
That is why whoever takes the path of the Righteous Predecessors concerning the Essence and the attributes of Allah the Exalted follows the methodology of the Quran concerning the Names and Attributes, whether he lived in the time of the Salaf or in later times. And whoever does not walk the path of the Righteous Predecessors in this matter does not follow the methodology of the Quran, even if he lived in the time of the Righteous Predecessors, alongside the noble Companions and the Successors.

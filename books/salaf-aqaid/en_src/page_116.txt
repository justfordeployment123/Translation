# English source for book page 116

[en]
Faith in the divine decree (believing in the divine decree): Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah firmly believe that both good and evil come about by the judgement of Allah the Exalted and by His decree, and that Allah the Exalted does whatever He wills. Everything is by His intention. Nothing is outside His will and His disposal of affairs. Allah knows all things: those that were, those that are and those that will be. It is He who fixed the decree of everything in the universe, as it already was in His knowledge and as His wisdom required.

[en]
Allah the Exalted is fully aware of the conditions of His servants. It is He who knows their provision, their appointed times, their deeds and all their other affairs. Everything newly brought into being comes into existence in accordance with His knowledge, by His power and by His intention. In short, all that is to happen for all time is the decree, which Allah the Exalted knows beforehand and which the Pen has written down. Allah the Exalted says:

[en]
(This is) the established way of Allah with those (prophets) who have passed on before. And ever is the command of Allah a destiny decreed.

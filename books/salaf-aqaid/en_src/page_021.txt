“I counsel you to fear Allah, and to listen attentively to the ruler and obey him, even if that ruler is an Abyssinian slave. Whoever of you lives after me will see a great deal of disagreement. Beware of newly invented matters, for every newly invented matter is an innovation and every innovation is misguidance.”

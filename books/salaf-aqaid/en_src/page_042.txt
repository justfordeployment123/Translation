# English source for book page 42

Allah the Exalted said about them:

[en]
{{QEN 33:23}}

[en]
After the noble Companions, the most virtuous people among the Righteous Predecessors are those who came after them in the first and second generations. About them the Prophet said:

[en]
“The best of people are the people of my generation, then those who come after them, then those who come after them.”

[en]
Thus the noble Companions and their Successors are more deserving of being followed than others, because their faith was true and they were utterly sincere in the worship of Allah. They were the guardians of the creed and the protectors of the Shariah, who acted upon it in word and deed.

# English source for book page 131

[en]
The noble Messenger, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Whoever loves for the sake of Allah and hates for the sake of Allah, gives for the sake of Allah and withholds for the sake of Allah, has attained complete faith.”

[en]
On another occasion he, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Whoever among you sees something evil, let him change it with his hand. If he cannot do that, then with his tongue. If he cannot do even that, then with his heart, and that is the weakest faith.”

[en]
This is what the noble Companions learned and understood from the noble Messenger, peace be upon him: that faith is belief, speech and action. Faith increases by doing acts of obedience and decreases by doing acts of disobedience.

[en]
Ali ibn Abi Talib, may Allah be pleased with him, said: “Patience is to faith what the head is to the body. Whoever has no patience has no faith.”

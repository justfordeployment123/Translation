[hen]
The Features of the Creed of Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah

[en]
Why is the creed of the Righteous Predecessors alone worth following? A correct creed is the basis and foundation of the religion. Whatever does not stand on this foundation is bound to be wiped out in the end. That is why the noble Prophet, peace be upon him, paid special attention to firmly rooting this creed in the hearts of his Companions, so that men fit for the work would be prepared on firm foundations. The thirteen years of the Quran's revelation in Makkah were spent on making the creed of Tawhid firm. Because of its importance, the noble Messenger, peace be upon him, called to it alone in Makkah and trained his Companions in it.

[en]
To make clear the correct and pure beliefs, it is necessary to study the beliefs of the Righteous Predecessors, and to make a serious effort to bring people to them and to save them from factions and differences. The first thing the callers to Islam should call to is the adoption of correct beliefs.

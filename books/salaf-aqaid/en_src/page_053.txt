# English source for book page 53

[en]
They promote cooperation among themselves. They make up for one another's shortcomings. When they love, they love only for the sake of Allah, and when they bear enmity, that too is solely for the sake of Allah.

[en]
In short, they are of the finest character, very enlightened, broad-minded, highly discerning and far-sighted. In matters of difference they are very open-hearted. They listen to another's point of view with great generosity of spirit. They fully observe the etiquette of disagreement and know its principles and rules well. They pay particular attention to purifying the soul through obedience to Allah.

[en]
In conclusion: Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah are the very sect to whom the noble Prophet, peace be upon him, gave the glad tidings of salvation. This distinction rests entirely on following the Sunnah; and it depends on acting on the teachings that the Sunnah gives about beliefs, acts of worship, conduct, speech and character, and about holding to the Jama'ah of the Muslims.

[en]
Thus the definition of Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah does not go beyond the definition of the Salaf. We have already learned that the Salaf acted on the Quran and the Sunnah. So the Salaf are Ahl as-Sunnah mentioned in the hadith, and Ahl as-Sunnah are the Righteous Predecessors and those who follow their way.

[en]
These specific meanings of Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah define and clarify the sense of this term well. Thus the Kharijites, the Jahmiyyah, the Qadariyyah, the Mu'tazilah, the Murji'ah, the Rafidah and other innovating and misguided sects like them are outside the meaning of Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah.

# English source for book page 47

[en]
This word is derived from ijtima', which is the opposite of tafarruq (iftiraq).

[fen]
Ijtima' means to come together. Tafarruq means to separate.

[en]
Thus jama'ah means a large number of people. A number of people who share one aim and objective is also called a jama'ah.

[en]
Jama'ah is also applied to people who come together in any matter.

[en]
The technical definition of Jama'ah: In technical usage, the Jama'ah means the community of the Muslims. And this is the community of the Salaf of this Ummah, which includes the noble Companions, their Successors and all those who follow them in excellence and goodness until the Day of Resurrection, who have come together on the Quran and the Sunnah, and who have kept to the path shown by the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, outwardly and inwardly, in every respect. Allah the Exalted has urged His servants to hold to the Jama'ah, has commanded them to live in unity and harmony and to promote mutual cooperation, and has forbidden disagreement and sectarianism.

# English source for book page 195

[en]
“Listening and obeying (that is, listening to the ruler and doing as he says) is obligatory on a Muslim person, in what he likes and in what he dislikes, as long as he is not commanded to commit a sin; but when he is commanded to commit a sin, there is no listening and no obeying.”

[en]
And he also said:

[en]
“There is no obedience in disobedience to Allah; obedience is only in what is ma'ruf (good deeds).”

[en]
It is binding on the ruler to fear Allah regarding his subjects. He should know that he is merely an employee whom Allah the Exalted has given this employment: to look after the Ummah, to serve the religion and Shariah of Allah, and to carry out the prescribed punishments of Allah on criminals, whether the criminal is a common person or one of the elite.

[fen]
In their view such a government was not worthy of being called a government. “Government is only that which establishes the religion, whether, after that, the government is good or bad.” Ali, may Allah be pleased with him, said: “People must have government, whether that government is righteous or wicked.” Someone asked: Government, yes, but why one that is not righteous? He said: “Through it too the roads are made safe, the prescribed punishments are established, jihad is waged against the enemy, and the spoils of war are distributed.” (Minhaj as-Sunnah by Ibn Taymiyyah 1/146)

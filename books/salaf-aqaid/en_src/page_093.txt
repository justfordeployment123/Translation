# English source for book page 93

[en]
Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah take great care over teaching the Noble Quran, reciting and interpreting it, memorising it and acting upon it, and give these things special attention. Allah says:

[en]
{{QEN 38:29}}

[en]
They regard the recitation of the Noble Quran as an act of worship and consider it a means of drawing near to Allah, because reciting each of its letters is a good deed. The noble Prophet, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Whoever reads a letter of the Book of Allah will have a good deed for it, and a good deed (is rewarded) tenfold. I do not say that Alif Lam Mim is one letter; rather, Alif is a letter, Lam is a letter and Mim is a letter.”

[en]
Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah do not regard it as permissible to interpret the Noble Quran merely by one's own opinion, because interpreting it merely by one's own opinion in this way amounts to saying something about Allah without knowledge, which is not right.

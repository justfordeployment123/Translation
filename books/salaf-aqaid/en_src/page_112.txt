# English source for book page 112

[en]
This same Ummah will be the first to enter Paradise. Half of the people of Paradise will be from the Ummah of Muhammad. Seventy thousand people of this Ummah will enter Paradise without being brought to account.

[en]
Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah also believe that the sinners among the people of Tawhid will not remain in Hell forever. Only those who associated partners with Allah will remain in Hell for ever and ever. They will never come out of Hell (we seek refuge in Allah!)

[en]
Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah also believe that on the plain of gathering our Prophet, peace be upon him, will have the Pond, whose water will be whiter than milk, sweeter than honey and more fragrant than musk. Its drinking cups will be as many as the stars of the sky. Its length will be a month's journey, and its breadth also a month's journey.

[en]
Whoever drinks from the water of this Pond once will never feel thirst again. Whoever invents an innovation in the religion will be forbidden the water of the Prophet's Pond.

[en]
The noble Messenger, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“My Pond is as large as a month's journey. Its water is whiter than milk and its scent is better than musk. Its jugs will be as many as the stars of the sky. Whoever drinks from this Pond will never feel thirst again.”

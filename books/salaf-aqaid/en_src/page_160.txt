# English source for book page 160

[en]
Let not believers take disbelievers as allies rather than believers. And whoever (of you) does that has nothing with Allah.

[en]
Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah believe that loyalty and disavowal is an important principle of the Shariah. The following reasons bring out the importance of this principle:

[en]
First: This principle is a part of the testimony that there is no god but Allah. “There is no god but Allah” means disowning everything that is worshipped besides Allah. Allah the Exalted says:

[en]
Worship Allah and avoid Taghut.

[en]
Second: It is the strongest link in the chain of faith. The noble Prophet, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“The strongest link of faith is loyalty for the sake of Allah and enmity for the sake of Allah, love for the sake of Allah alone and hatred for the sake of Allah alone.”

[en]

# English source for book page 117

And He says:

[en]
{{QEN 54:49}}

[en]
The noble Prophet, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“No servant is a believer until he believes in the divine decree, its good and its bad, and until he knows that what befell him could not have missed him, and what did not befall him could not have befallen him.”

[en]
Ahl as-Sunnah say that faith in the divine decree is not complete without four things. These four things are called the levels of the divine decree, or the pillars of the divine decree. These four matters are the introduction to understanding the question of the divine decree. Faith in the divine decree is not complete without believing in these pillars, because the four are so bound up with one another that affirming all four is necessary for faith in the divine decree to be complete. If even one of these pillars is missing, faith in the divine decree will be impaired.

[en]
The first level, knowledge: It is necessary to believe that Allah the Exalted has knowledge, both general and detailed, of all that has happened, all that is happening and all that will happen, and of all that has not happened and how it would have happened if it had happened.

# English source for book page 31

[hen]
Essential Definitions

[en]
The linguistic definition of creed: The word aqidah (creed) is derived from aqd, which means: to establish a connection; to settle a matter; to make firm; to gain assurance; to tie firmly; to be firm (for a firm bond to be formed); to cling; to be knotted together; to reinforce a wall or the like with molten lead; to establish firmly.

[en]
Certainty and firm conviction come from this. Its opposite is the Arabic word hall, which means to untie.

[en]
Aqadahu ya'qiduhu aqdan: to tie a knot, to fasten a knot. Uqdat al-yamin (the knot of an oath) and uqdat an-nikah (the knot of marriage) are derived from it. Allah the Exalted says:

[en]
Allah will not impose blame upon you for what is meaningless in your oaths, but He will impose blame upon you for (breaking) what you intended of oaths.

[en]
In this sense, a creed is the judgement and decision of whose truth and firmness the one who holds it has no doubt or suspicion at all.

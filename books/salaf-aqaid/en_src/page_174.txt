# English source for book page 174

[en]
Trickery comes from Satan, and its cause is acts of disbelief and sins. It is particular to those who have gone astray. Allah says:

[en]
And indeed do the devils inspire their allies (among men) to dispute with you. And if you were to obey them, indeed, you would be associators (of others with Him).

[en]
Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah accept as true that magic and magicians exist in the world.

[fen]
Imam Ibn Qudamah, may Allah have mercy on him, wrote: “Magic is knots, incantations and words. The words are spoken or written, or an act is done that affects the body, the heart or the mind of the bewitched person (the one who falls victim to magic) from a distance. Magic exists. There is magic that kills the bewitched person. There is magic that makes him ill. There is magic that keeps a man from approaching his wife. There is magic that separates a man from his wife, so that husband and wife come to hate each other. It also happens that, through the effect of magic, two people come to love each other greatly.” This was said by Imam ash-Shafi'i, may Allah have mercy on him. Imam Ibn Qudamah, may Allah have mercy on him, further wrote: Learning and teaching magic is forbidden. We do not know of any disagreement about this among the people of knowledge. Our companions (the scholars of our school) say that the magician is a disbeliever, whether he considers magic permissible or forbidden; and if magic had no reality, Allah the Exalted would not have commanded us to seek refuge from it. Allah the Exalted says: It was not Solomon who disbelieved, but the devils disbelieved, teaching people magic and that which was revealed to the two angels at Babylon, Harut and Marut. But the two angels do not teach anyone unless they say, “We are a trial, so do not disbelieve (by practicing magic).” And (yet) they learn from them that by which they cause separation between a man and his wife.

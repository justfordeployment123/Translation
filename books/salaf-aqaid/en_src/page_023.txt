# English source for book page 23

[en]
“The Children of Israel split into seventy-two sects, while my Ummah will split into seventy-three sects. All of those sects will go to Hell except one sect.”

[en]
The narrator of the hadith, Abdullah ibn Amr, may Allah be pleased with them both, asked: Messenger of Allah, which sect is that? He said: “The one that I and my Companions are upon.”

[en]
This shows how tremendously important this matter is, and how necessary it is to raise the new generation in accordance with it. It is essential to set the new generation's ideas about belief on the straight path, so that it does not fall into tribulation and does not become a victim of misguidance.

[en]
In this regard Allah the Exalted granted some of our sincere scholars and students of knowledge the ability to take up teaching, research and writing on this subject. Our brother, Shaykh Abdullah ibn Abd al-Hamid al-Athari, is one of them; he wrote this beneficial book to explain the viewpoint of the Salaf in matters of belief. He expressed the wish that I read this book of his and write a foreword to it. I looked at the book, read it and came to the conclusion that the learned author has, with great effort, gathered in it complete and very beneficial information, for which he deserves thanks. In an attractive style and simple language, with good arrangement and division into chapters, he has set out a summary of the beliefs of the Righteous Predecessors.

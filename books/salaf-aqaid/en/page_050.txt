The Distinctive Features of Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah

[en]
Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah have distinctive features that set them apart from other groups. Some of them are as follows:

[en]
First: In beliefs and rulings, and in character and conduct, Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah follow the middle way. They are not extreme. Avoiding both excess and neglect, they take the path of moderation. In this way they are moderate among the other groups of the Ummah, just as this Ummah is moderate among the other nations.

[en]
Second: They take their knowledge of the Shariah directly from the Quran and the Sunnah. They give the Quran and the Sunnah great importance. They explain the rulings of the Quran and the Sunnah according to the understanding of the Righteous Predecessors, and they act on them.

[en]
Third: Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah have only one imam of exalted rank, whose every word they accept, and they reject every word that goes against his. That imam of exalted rank is the bearer of the Message, Muhammad, peace be upon him. It is Ahl as-Sunnah who know his blessed life, sayings and deeds best. That is why they love the Sunnah more than all other people, have a deeply rooted desire to follow the Sunnah, and hold those who act on the Sunnah as their friends.

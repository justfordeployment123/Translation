[ar]

[ar]

[en]
By time, Indeed, mankind is in loss, Except for those who have believed and done righteous deeds and advised each other to truth and advised each other to patience.

[ref]
العصر 3-1:103.

[en]
The noble Prophet, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
قُلْ آمَنْتُ بِاللَّهْ، ثُمَّ اسْتَقِمْ

[en]
“Say: I believe in Allah, then stand firm.”

[ref]
مسند أحمد: 413/3.

[en]
On another occasion he said:

[ar]
الإِيمَانُ بِضْعٌ وَسَبْعُونَ أَوْ بِضْعٌ وَسِتُّونَ شُعْبَةْ، فَأَفْضَلُهَا قَوْلُ لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهْ، وَأَدْنَاهَا إِمَاطَةُ الأَذَى عَنِ الطَّرِيقْ، وَالْحَيَاءُ شُعْبَةٌ مِنَ الإِيمَانْ

[en]
“Faith has seventy-odd or sixty-odd branches, the best of which is saying ‘there is no god but Allah’, and the least of which is removing something harmful from the road. And modesty is a great branch of faith.”

[ref]
صحيح مسلم، الإيمان، حديث: 35.

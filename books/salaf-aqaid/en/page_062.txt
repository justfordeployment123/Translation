Faith in Allah includes believing in the three kinds of Tawhid, holding them as belief, affirming them and acting according to them. The three kinds of Tawhid are these: the Oneness of Lordship (Tawhid ar-Rububiyyah), the Oneness of worship (Tawhid al-Uluhiyyah) and the Oneness of the Names and Attributes (Tawhid al-Asma' was-Sifat). Each of them is explained in detail below.

[en]
First, the Oneness of Lordship: The Oneness of Lordship means this: a person firmly believes that Allah the Exalted is the only Lord and the sole Owner of everything. In this He has absolutely no partner. He alone, by Himself, is the only Creator of everything. He alone runs the order of the universe, and disposes of it and alters it as He wills. He alone provides for all. He alone gives life. He alone causes death.

[en]
The Oneness of Lordship includes believing in the divine decree of Allah the Exalted and in His judgements, and acknowledging that He is utterly one and alone in His Essence. In short, the Oneness of Lordship is to hold Allah the Exalted as one in His actions.

[en]
Here is some of the evidence for the Oneness of Lordship from the Noble Quran:

[en]

Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah believe that the people of faith will see the Lord, the Exalted, with their own eyes in the Hereafter, and will behold Him, and that Allah the Exalted will speak to them and they will speak to Him. Allah the Exalted says:

[ref]
القيٰمة 22:75، 23.

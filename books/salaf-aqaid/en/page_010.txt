Publisher's Note

[en]
The success or failure of any nation, society or individual depends on its beliefs and doctrines, regardless of whether those beliefs and doctrines are right or wrong; it is the nation that is firm in its doctrines and ideas that succeeds. If its beliefs and doctrines are firm and right, success in both religion and the world is assured. A nation that wavers in its beliefs and doctrines fails in this world and is a loser in the Hereafter too. That is why all the noble Prophets, peace be upon them, first of all reformed people's beliefs and doctrines, and Islam too has laid great stress on beliefs and has placed Islam without firm beliefs at the level of hypocrisy. During the thirteen years of his life in Makkah, the noble Messenger, peace be upon him, stressed people's beliefs and doctrines alone. Pillars and rulings such as prayer, fasting, Hajj and zakah were begun after he went to blessed Madinah.

[en]
The fundamental thing among beliefs and ideas is Tawhid, the Oneness of Allah the Exalted. That is, that a correct and true conception of his Creator and Master, the One he worships and prostrates to, should settle in the servant's mind. When the reality of the Creator's greatness and majesty and of the helplessness of the creation becomes clear to a person, he considers it an honour to leap into the fire.

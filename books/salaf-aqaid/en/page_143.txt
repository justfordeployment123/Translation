This principle concerns hidden matters, not matters that are clearly disbelief. Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah do not call any Muslim a disbeliever until the conditions for declaring someone a disbeliever (takfir) are fulfilled and there is no impediment.

[fen]
(A person whose Islam is established with certainty does not cease to be a Muslim on the basis of doubt.) This is a Salafi rule that our Salaf acted upon. In this way they kept far away from takfir. That is why, when Ali, may Allah be pleased with him, was asked about the people of Nahrawan, whether they were disbelievers, he replied: “It is from disbelief that they fled.” He was asked: Then are they hypocrites? He said: “The hypocrites remember Allah very little, while these people remember Allah morning and evening. It is only that they are our brothers who have rebelled against us.” (as-Sunan al-Kubra of al-Bayhaqi: 8/173) In the question of takfir, it is necessary to look both at the type (the root) and at the individual (the branch), because not every disbelief is such that a specific person is declared a disbeliever because of it. It is necessary to distinguish between two things: ruling about a statement that it is disbelief, and singling out the one who says it and saying that he is a disbeliever. Imam Ibn Taymiyyah, may Allah have mercy on him, wrote: “The ruling on one who makes a mistaken interpretation and on an ignorant person who has an excuse is not the same as the ruling on one who harbours hatred and stubbornness, or on the wicked and the evildoer. The ruling on each of these two kinds of people is separate.” (Majmu'at ar-Rasa'il wal-Masa'il: 3/382) He also wrote: “Once this is clear, it is not permissible to rule specifically about any one of these ignorant people that he is with the disbelievers, unless proof is established against him through the message, making it clear that he opposes the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, even though what these ignorant people say is undoubtedly disbelief. This is the rule for declaring a specific person a disbeliever.” (Majmu'at ar-Rasa'il wal-Masa'il: 3/348)

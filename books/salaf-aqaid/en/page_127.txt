[en]
The speech of the heart means that the heart believes, affirms, acknowledges and is certain.

[en]
The speech of the tongue means that the tongue declares its commitment to action, that is, it bears the two testimonies and says that they will be acted upon.

[en]
The action of the heart means that the heart intends, submits, is sincere, is obedient, loves and wills to do righteous deeds.

[en]
The action of the tongue and the working limbs of the body is that they carry out the commands and keep away from what is forbidden.

[en]
There is no faith without action, no speech and action without intention, and no speech, action and intention without conformity to the Sunnah.

[fen]
This is the well-known saying of Imam al-Awza'i, Imam Sufyan ath-Thawri and Imam al-Humaydi, may Allah have mercy on them, narrated by the great scholar al-Lalaka'i, may Allah have mercy on him, (1/74) and by Ibn Battah.

[en]
Allah the Exalted has declared to be true people of faith only those who act in accordance with faith, in whose beliefs and in whose outward and inward deeds and conduct the effects of faith can be seen. Allah the Exalted says:

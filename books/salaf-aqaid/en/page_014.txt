Islam came with the message of Tawhid. Tawhid is the right of Allah the Exalted that is due from His servants. The greatest purpose of creating the creation is precisely that it should bow its head before the Creator:

[en]
Allah the Exalted says:

[ar]
وَمَا خَلَقْتُ الْجِنَّ وَالْإِنْسَ إِلَّا لِيَعْبُدُونْ

[en]
And I did not create the jinn and mankind except to worship Me.

[ref]
الذاريات 56:51.

[en]
The noble Prophets came with this same message. The same guidance was given in the revealed Books. The banner of jihad was raised to spread this very message.

[en]
The noble Prophet, peace be upon him, called to the creed of Tawhid in Makkah for thirteen years. He set its roots firm in the depths of hearts. He made its foundations strong in the sanctuary of the heart. He raised its pillars on the plane of the inner self. In this way the seekers of guidance began to see the bright path of Tawhid. The signs of Allah's way became clearly visible to them. Then Allah the Exalted made the truth prevail and wiped out falsehood. Hearts began to shine with the light of pure Tawhid, and the filth of shirk and disbelief was washed away from them.

[en]
When the Messenger of mercy, peace be upon him, came, the soil of hearts was barren. With the utmost sincerity he watered it with the pure water of the spring of Tawhid. That barren soil turned green and began to flourish. Humanity had fallen into the depths of humiliation; it gained honour and elevation.

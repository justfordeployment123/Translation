And they say, “The Most Merciful has taken a son.” Exalted is He! Rather, they are (but) honored servants. They cannot precede Him in word, and they act by His command. He knows what is (presently) before them and what will be after them, and they cannot intercede except on behalf of one whom He approves. And they, from fear of Him, are apprehensive.

The various kinds of angels: There are many kinds of angels. Some angels have the duty of carrying the Throne. Some were charged with bringing revelation. One angel is appointed over the mountains. Among them are also the keepers of Paradise and Hell. Some angels have the duty of recording people's deeds. Some angels are responsible for taking the souls of the people of faith. Some angels are charged with taking the souls of the disbelievers. A number of angels carry out the duty of questioning in the grave. And there are some angels who pray for mercy and forgiveness for the people of faith, and who love them.

For this edition of the book, for which we are writing this foreword, full care has been taken over corrections, and the essential points that were left out of the first edition have also been added. One distinctive feature of this book is that it quotes the words of the Salaf from the original sources of creed, and makes full use of the evidence of the Quran and the Sunnah, as well as the sayings of the noble Companions, the Successors and the imams of the Salaf related to the subject. This book is without doubt among those books at the sight of which the eyes of the people of Tawhid are cooled and their hearts are filled with joy, while the throats of the opponents go dry on seeing such books and they feel a tightness in their chests.

[en]
“This religion of Islam will surely reach every place that night and day reach. Allah the Exalted will not stop until He has brought this religion to every single house, whether that house is of mud or of hair (a tent), with the honour of the honoured and the humiliation of the humiliated: an honour with which Allah the Exalted will honour Islam (and the people of Islam), and a humiliation with which He will humiliate disbelief (and the people of disbelief).”

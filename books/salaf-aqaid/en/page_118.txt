He knew, even before He created the creatures, what they would do. He was aware of their provision, their appointed times, their deeds and all their movements and moments of stillness. He knew which of them is good and which is bad. This is His eternal knowledge, which has been His from all eternity. He says:

[ar]
إِنَّ اللَّهَ بِكُلِّ شَيْءٍ عَلِيمْ

[en]
Indeed, Allah is Knowing of all things.

[ref]
التوبة 115:9.

[en]
The second level, writing: It is necessary to believe that Allah the Exalted, on the basis of His prior knowledge, has written the decrees of all creatures in the Preserved Tablet. The Preserved Tablet is the Book in which nothing has been left out. All that has happened and all that will happen until the Day of Resurrection is written with Allah in this Book, which, besides Umm al-Kitab (the Mother of the Book), is also called adh-Dhikr (the Reminder), al-Imam (the Register) and al-Kitab al-Mubin (the Clear Book). He says:

[ar]
وَكُلَّ شَيْءٍ أَحْصَيْنَاهُ فِي إِمَامٍ مُبِينْ

[en]
And all things We have enumerated in a clear register.

[ref]
يٰسٓ 12:36.

[en]
The noble Prophet, peace be upon him, said:

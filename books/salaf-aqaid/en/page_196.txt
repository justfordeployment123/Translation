The ruler must be strong, and in carrying out the commands of Allah he must not care for the blame of any blamer. He should be the trustee of the Ummah and its well-wisher. He should protect their religion and ensure the protection of their lives, wealth and honour. He should work for their welfare and well-being. He should maintain peace and security among them. He should strive to improve their deeds and conduct. The ruler should not take revenge on anyone for his own sake. If he becomes angry, he should become angry for the sake of Allah. The noble Prophet, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
مَا مِنْ عَبْدٍ يَسْتَرْعِيهِ اللَّهُ رَعِيَّةْ، يَمُوتُ يَوْمَ يَمُوتُ وَهُوَ غَاشٌّ لِرَعِيَّتِهْ، إِلاَّ حَرَّمَ اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ الْجَنَّةْ

[en]
“Whoever Allah the Exalted entrusts with responsibility for some subjects, and he dies, on the day he dies, in a state of deceiving his subjects, Allah the Exalted makes Paradise forbidden for such a person.”

[en]
And whoever disbelieves in Allah, His angels, His books, His messengers, and the Last Day has certainly gone far astray.

[ref]
النسآء 136:4.

[en]
Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah believe in general terms that the angels exist. As for detailed faith, it is necessary to believe in the existence of those angels for whom there is sound evidence and whom Allah and His Messenger, peace be upon him, have named. Among them are, in particular, these angels:

[en]
Jibril, peace be upon him: bringing revelation to the prophets was his duty.

[en]
Mika'il, peace be upon him: he is charged with sending down the rain.

[en]
Israfil, peace be upon him: blowing the Trumpet is his task.

[en]
The Angel of Death: this angel is charged with taking the souls.

[en]
Malik, the keeper of Hell. Ridwan, the keeper of Paradise. Munkar and Nakir, the angels of the grave.

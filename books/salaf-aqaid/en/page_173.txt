However, according to Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah there are certain rules of the Shariah for affirming karamat, and without these being fulfilled no karamah can be accepted. Not everything that breaks the normal course of things (that is out of the ordinary) is a karamah. Sometimes it is istidraj (being given respite by Allah and not being seized at once), or trickery, magic and the workings of devils play a part in it, and these have nothing to do with karamah. There is a very clear difference between karamah and trickery. A karamah comes from Allah the Exalted, and its cause is obedience to the Lord and obedience to the Messenger, peace be upon him. It is particular to the people of steadfastness. Allah says:

[ar]
وَمَا لَهُمْ أَلَّا يُعَذِّبَهُمُ اللَّهُ وَهُمْ يَصُدُّونَ عَنِ الْمَسْجِدِ الْحَرَامِ وَمَا كَانُوا أَوْلِيَاءَهُ ۚ إِنْ أَوْلِيَاؤُهُ إِلَّا الْمُتَّقُونْ

[en]

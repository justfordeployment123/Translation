Second: The disbelief of arrogance with affirmation: The one who commits this disbelief acknowledges that what the Messenger brought from the Exalted Lord is the truth, but out of arrogance, looking down on the truth and on the people of truth, he refuses to follow the Messenger. An example of it is the disbelief of the accursed Iblis. He neither belied the command of Allah nor denied it, but he fell prey to self-admiration and arrogance. So he did not carry out the command of Allah, and he was rejected and driven away forever.

[en]
Third: The disbelief of turning away: The disbelief of turning away means that a person neither lets the Prophet's teachings reach his ears nor lets them find a way into his heart, but turns away from them. He neither affirms the Messenger nor belies him. He shows neither friendship nor enmity. He does not lend an ear to the Messenger's commands at all. He abandons the truth, neither learning it nor acting on it. He flees from the places where the truth is mentioned. Such a disbeliever has committed the disbelief of turning away.

[en]
Fourth: The disbelief of hypocrisy: The disbelief of hypocrisy is that a person outwardly follows the Prophet's teachings but in his heart belies them and denies them. In this way he displays faith while keeping disbelief in his heart.

[fen]
Hypocrisy is of two kinds: hypocrisy of belief and hypocrisy of action. Hypocrisy of belief, or major hypocrisy, is that there is disbelief in the heart while faith is displayed by the tongue and the working limbs of the body. The one who commits this hypocrisy will be in the lowest pit of Hell; for example, one who belies all or some of the divine teachings, or belies the Messenger, or belies the Prophet's teachings, or dislikes the support and defence of the Prophet's religion, or commits some other act of disbelief like these. The deeds of one who commits hypocrisy of action, or minor hypocrisy, are contrary to the teachings of the Shariah. Such a person does not leave the fold of Islam; for example: a person tells lies. He breaks his promises. He betrays a trust.

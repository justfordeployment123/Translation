Third: Those who deserve enmity and hatred without qualification: these include the polytheists and the disbelievers, whether they are Jews, Christians, Magians, atheists or idol-worshippers. The same ruling also applies to those Muslims who commit acts that make a person a disbeliever, such as calling upon other than Allah, seeking help from other than Allah, relying on other than Allah, insulting Allah and His Messenger, peace be upon him, or the religion of Allah, and separating religion from worldly life in the belief that religion does not meet the demands of the present age and is not suited to modern times. But this ruling will be applied to such people only once the proof has been established against them. So the Muslims should wage jihad against people of this third kind, make life hard for them, and not allow them to go about spreading corruption on the earth. Allah the Exalted says:

Faith in the Last Day (believing in the Day of the Hereafter): Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah believe in the Last Day, which means firmly believing in the Day of Resurrection and fully believing in what the Quran and the Sunnah state explicitly about all that will happen after death. In many places in the Noble Quran, Allah the Exalted has mentioned the Last Day with great emphasis, and has mentioned faith in the Last Day together with faith in Allah. Allah the Exalted says:

[ar]
وَالَّذِينَ يُؤْمِنُونَ بِمَا أُنْزِلَ إِلَيْكَ وَمَا أُنْزِلَ مِنْ قَبْلِكَ وَبِالْآخِرَةِ هُمْ يُوقِنُونْ

[en]
And who believe in what has been revealed to you, (O Muhammad), and what was revealed before you, and of the Hereafter they are certain (in faith).

[ref]
البقرة 4:2.

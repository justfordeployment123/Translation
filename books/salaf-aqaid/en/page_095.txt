The prophets and Messengers brought some truly astonishing and matchless miracles that bore witness to their truthfulness. Whoever denies a single Messenger denies Allah the Exalted and all the Messengers. Allah says:

[fen]
A miracle is an extraordinary event that changes the normal course of the universe in such a way that a human being is unable to do the like of it. Allah the Exalted makes a miracle appear at the hands of a prophet, in keeping with his call, and it makes known and proclaims the truthfulness of the prophet. The occurrence of a miracle is entirely possible, because Allah the Exalted, who set up this world of causes and effects and arranged this order of life, is also fully able to change this order. When He makes a miracle appear, things no longer follow the causes they followed before. In view of the limitless power of Allah the Exalted, this is nothing impossible or strange. Whatever He wills, He does far faster even than the blink of an eye. Allah the Exalted says: His command is only when He intends a thing that He says to it, “Be,” and it is. (Ya-Sin 36:82)

Despite these conditions and events, in every age a group of scholars has kept calling people to pure Tawhid through speech and writing. The nineteenth century proved to be the age of the rise of the pen. Hundreds of thousands of books were written, and this continues to this day. Keeping in view the demands of the present age, godly scholars have also written countless books on the basic beliefs and doctrines of Islam. The book before you is the Urdu translation of “al-Wajiz fi Aqidat as-Salaf as-Salih”, written by the eminent Shaykh Abdullah ibn Abd al-Hamid al-Athari, may Allah preserve him, a well-known writer of the Arab world. Besides defining and explaining creed, this book sets out eleven basic principles which it is essential for the people of Islam to know. The issues of the creed of Tawhid and of the matters of faith have been made clear in the light of the understanding of the noble Companions, the Successors and the scholars of hadith.

“I have left among you two things. As long as you hold on to them both, you will never go astray: the Book of Allah and the Sunnah of His Messenger, peace be upon him.”

[ref]
الموطأ للإمام مالك، حديث: 1395.

[en]
Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah do not say: the Book of Allah, then the Sunnah of the Messenger, peace be upon him. Rather, they say: the Book of Allah and the Sunnah of the Messenger, peace be upon him. They mention the two together because the Sunnah of the Messenger, peace be upon him, is bound up with the Book of Allah, and also because Allah the Exalted has made obedience to Allah and His Messenger, peace be upon him, obligatory. The Sunnah shows how this obligation is to be carried out. It sets out the details of this obligation.

[en]
After the Book of Allah and the Sunnah of the Messenger, peace be upon him, Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah follow the methodology of the Emigrants and the Helpers in general, and the methodology of the Rightly Guided Caliphs, may Allah be pleased with them, in particular. The noble Prophet, peace be upon him, specifically enjoined following the Rightly Guided Caliphs, may Allah be pleased with them.

[en]
After the noble Companions, may Allah be pleased with them, Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah follow the people of the first, virtuous generations. The noble Prophet, peace be upon him, said:

عَلَيْكُمْ بِسُنَّتِي، وَسُنَّةِ الْخُلَفَاءِ الرَّاشِدِينَ الْمَهْدِيِّينْ، تَمَسَّكُوا بِهَا، وَعَضُّوا عَلَيْهَا بِالنَّوَاجِذْ، وَإِيَّاكُمْ وَمُحْدَثَاتِ الأُمُورْ، فَإِنَّ كُلَّ مُحْدَثَةٍ بِدْعَةْ، وَكُلَّ بِدْعَةٍ ضَلاَلَةْ

“Keep to my Sunnah and the Sunnah of the Rightly Guided Caliphs, the well-guided ones. Hold on to it and grip it firmly with your molar teeth. Beware of new things in the religion, for every new thing is an innovation and every innovation is misguidance.”

The kinds of disbelief: Disbelief is the opposite of faith. In the view of the Shariah, disbelief is of two kinds: one is the disbelief that takes a person out of the fold of Islam, and the other is the disbelief that does not take him out of the fold of Islam. Like faith, disbelief too has many branches, some of which entail disbelief, while some are counted among the traits of the disbelievers.

[en]
The first kind, major disbelief: Major disbelief is also called creedal disbelief. This disbelief takes a person out of the fold of Islam. It is a nullifier of faith, that is, through it faith comes to an end and Islam is lost. This disbelief makes it necessary that the one who commits it remain in Hell forever. This disbelief occurs in belief, in speech and in action. It is of five kinds:

[en]
First: The disbelief of denial: The disbelief of denial means believing that the Messenger was not truthful, or claiming that the message the Messenger brought was not the truth, or a person claiming that Allah has forbidden such-and-such a thing and made such-and-such a thing lawful, while he knows that this claim of his is false and contrary to the divine teachings.

Resembling the disbelievers in such things produces love for them in the heart, and hidden love in the heart leads one to resemble them. This is a very deadly tendency. One should always be on guard against it and avoid it.

[en]
Fifth: Not helping the disbelievers, not praising them, not giving them help against the Muslims, and not seeking their help, unless there is a need and the aim is to seek help against disbelievers like them.

[en]
Not inclining towards the disbelievers, avoiding sitting in their company, not making them one's confidants, and not entrusting them with responsibility for important affairs.

[en]
Sixth: Not taking part in the disbelievers' celebrations and festivals, and not congratulating them on such occasions. Not honouring them, and not addressing them with respectful forms of address such as “sir”.

[en]
Seventh: Not asking forgiveness for them, and not praying for mercy for them.

[en]
Eighth: Not taking an apologetic attitude before the disbelievers in matters of religion, avoiding flattering them in this regard, and not professing closeness to them. Not treating them with leniency.

[en]
Ninth: Not making the disbelievers arbiters in any matter, and not following them in anything, because following them amounts to abandoning the command of Allah and His Messenger, peace be upon him.

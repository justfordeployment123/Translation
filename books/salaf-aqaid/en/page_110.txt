[en]
The second blowing, which is called the Nafkhat as-Sa'q (the blast of falling dead), will bring change upon the whole world. The order of the universe will be thrown into disarray. Things will perish, and those whose destruction Allah has decreed will be destroyed.

[en]
The third blowing is the Nafkhat al-Ba'th wan-Nushur (the blast of resurrection and raising up). As a result of it, all human beings will come back to life, rise from their graves and be brought before the Lord of the worlds.

[en]
Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah believe in the resurrection and the raising up. They believe with certainty that Allah the Exalted will bring the dead back to life and raise them from their graves, and they will be brought before Allah barefoot, naked and uncircumcised. The sun will come near them. People will sweat so heavily that some of them will be drenched, sunk in sweat up to their mouths. The first grave to split open will be that of our Messenger Muhammad, peace be upon him, and he will come out of his grave.

[en]
On that great day people will come pouring out of their graves like swarms of locusts and hurry towards the plain of gathering. Their every movement will be utterly silent. A hush will fall everywhere.

[en]
On the plain of gathering the records of deeds will be opened. Every hidden thing will come to light. What used to lie hidden in the breasts will also be laid bare. On that day Allah the Exalted will speak to His servants in such a way that there will be no interpreter between Him and His servants. People will be called by their own names and the names of their fathers.

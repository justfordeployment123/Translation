Prayers will also be made for them, for their righteousness and steadfastness, and if their outward conduct is sound, sincere goodwill will be shown to them. If they commit some violation of the rulings of the religion that is not clear and certain disbelief, it is not permissible even to take up arms against them; rather, patience is necessary, because the noble Prophet, peace be upon him, commanded that the rulers be obeyed in permissible matters unless they descend into open disbelief about which you have conclusive proof, and that one keep away from fighting in times of tribulation. Moreover, he, peace be upon him, commanded that whoever wishes to spread division and discord within the Ummah after unity and agreement have been established, and tries to bring about instability after political stability, is to be fought.

[fen]
Praying for the rulers' righteousness, guidance and steadfastness is the way of the Righteous Predecessors. Imam al-Fudayl ibn Iyad, may Allah have mercy on him, said: “If I had a prayer that would be answered, I would make that prayer for the rulers. We have been commanded to pray for the rulers' righteousness. We have not been commanded to pray against them, even if they commit wrong and oppression, because if they commit wrong and oppression, its burden will fall on them alone, but if they follow the right path, the benefit will be theirs and the Muslims' as well.” Imam al-Hasan al-Basri, may Allah have mercy on him, said: “The oppression of the rulers is a punishment from Allah, and the punishment of Allah is not confronted with swords. It is warded off through supplication, repentance and seeking forgiveness. Indeed, whenever the punishment of Allah has been confronted with the sword, the punishment has only cut deeper.” It is reported that Imam al-Hasan al-Basri, may Allah have mercy on him, saw a man praying against al-Hajjaj ibn Yusuf. He said to him: “May Allah have mercy on you, do not do that. Your rulers are brought forth from among yourselves. We fear that if al-Hajjaj is removed or dies, monkeys and swine will become your rulers.” (Adab al-Hasan al-Basri by Ibn al-Jawzi, p. 119)

[fen]
Imam an-Nawawi, may Allah have mercy on him, said: “Sincere goodwill towards the Muslim rulers means cooperating with them in what is right, obeying them in it and commanding them to do it, advising them very gently, and making them aware of what they are neglecting.” (Sharh Sahih Muslim 2/241)

The Seventh Principle: The Sources for Deriving Rulings and Issues, and the Method of Reasoning from Evidence

[en]
According to the Righteous Predecessors, Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah, the method of deriving rulings and issues and of reasoning from evidence is complete adherence to the Quran and the Sunnah. Allah the Exalted says:

[ar]
وَمَا كَانَ لِمُؤْمِنٍ وَلَا مُؤْمِنَةٍ إِذَا قَضَى اللَّهُ وَرَسُولُهُ أَمْرًا أَنْ يَكُونَ لَهُمُ الْخِيَرَةُ مِنْ أَمْرِهِمْ ۗ وَمَنْ يَعْصِ اللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُ فَقَدْ ضَلَّ ضَلَالًا مُبِينَا

[en]
It is not for a believing man or a believing woman, when Allah and His Messenger have decided a matter, that they should (thereafter) have any choice about their affair. And whoever disobeys Allah and His Messenger has certainly strayed into clear error.

[ref]
الأحزاب 36:33.

[en]
And the Prophet said:

[ar]
تَرَكْتُ فِيكُمْ أَمْرَيْنْ، لَنْ تَضِلُّوا مَا تَمَسَّكْتُمْ بِهِمَا: كِتَابَ اللَّهِ وَسُنَّةَ نَبِيِّهْ

In another place He says:

[ar]
لَا يُكَلِّفُ اللَّهُ نَفْسًا إِلَّا وُسْعَهَا

[en]
Allah does not charge a soul except (with that within) its capacity.

[ref]
البقرة 286:2.

[en]
However, because of the perfect mercy of Allah the Exalted, evil is not to be attributed to Him, since He has commanded good and forbidden evil. Evil is simply one of the things His wisdom requires. Allah the Exalted says:

[ar]
مَا أَصَابَكَ مِنْ حَسَنَةٍ فَمِنَ اللَّهِ ۖ وَمَا أَصَابَكَ مِنْ سَيِّئَةٍ فَمِنْ نَفْسِكْ

[en]
What comes to you of good is from Allah, but what comes to you of evil, (O man), is from yourself.

[ref]
النسآء 79:4.

[en]
Allah the Exalted is free from injustice. Justice is His attribute. He does not wrong anyone by even an atom's weight. All His actions are wholly justice and mercy. Allah the Exalted says:

[ar]
وَمَا أَنَا بِظَلَّامٍ لِلْعَبِيدْ

[en]
And never will I be unjust to the servants.

[ref]
قٓ 29:50.

[en]
And He says:

[ar]
وَلَا يَظْلِمُ رَبُّكَ أَحَدَا

[en]

[ref]
الكهف 49:18.

[en]

[ar]
إِنَّ اللَّهَ لَا يَظْلِمُ مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةْ

[en]
Indeed, Allah does not do injustice, (even) as much as an atom's weight.

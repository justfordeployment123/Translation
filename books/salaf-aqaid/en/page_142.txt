The Difference Between the General Ruling and the Specific Ruling

[en]
In the view of Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah there is a clear difference between two things: passing a ruling of sin or of disbelief on the people of innovation in general terms, and specifically declaring a person whose Islam is established with certainty, and who has then committed some innovation, to be a sinner, a transgressor or a disbeliever.

[en]
They do not specifically call a Muslim a sinner, a transgressor or a disbeliever until the “truth” has been made clear to him by establishing proof against him and removing his doubts.

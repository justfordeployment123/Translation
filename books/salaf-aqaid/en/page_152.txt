But whoever among the believers and the God-fearing dies upon Islam, Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah, looking at his outward Islam, bear a general witness that such people are, in sha Allah, among the people of Paradise. Allah the Exalted says:

[ar]
وَبَشِّرِ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَعَمِلُوا الصَّالِحَاتِ أَنَّ لَهُمْ جَنَّاتٍ تَجْرِي مِنْ تَحْتِهَا الْأَنْهَارْ

[en]
And give good tidings to those who believe and do righteous deeds that they will have gardens (in Paradise) beneath which rivers flow.

[ref]
البقرة 25:2.

[en]
And He says:

[ar]
إِنَّ الْمُتَّقِينَ فِي جَنَّاتٍ وَنَهَرْ

[ar]
فِي مَقْعَدِ صِدْقٍ عِنْدَ مَلِيكٍ مُقْتَدِرْ

[en]
Indeed, the righteous will be among gardens and rivers, In a seat of honor near a Sovereign, Perfect in Ability.

[ref]
القمر 55,54:54.

There is no doubt that shirk is a most grievous crime and a very great wrong, and so its punishment in this world and the Hereafter is nothing but loss. In today's age, when tribulations are spread everywhere and the glitter of the world dazzles people's eyes, those who hold wrong doctrines in matters of belief are openly engaged in harmful activities. The innovations they have produced are spreading. They have revived the wrong doctrines of their leading figures, which had died. They have dug their long-forgotten books out of the pits. Because of these, new ideas are constantly being born. Sects and groups are appearing whose aims differ from one another and are beyond understanding. Every new sect considers the other sect wrong and astray from the right path. Every new group curses and reviles the other group.

[en]
At such a time many people have begun to speak insolently against the creed of Tawhid and the Sunnah. They have plundered people's minds. They have corrupted their beliefs. They have made the matter of shirk seem unimportant to them.

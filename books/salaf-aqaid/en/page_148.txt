[en]
The one who commits them falls under the warning, but he will not be punished by remaining in Hell forever. Examples of them include fighting a Muslim, swearing by other than Allah, taunting someone about his lineage, wailing over the dead, a believer calling his believing brother a disbeliever, and other major sins. Allah says:

[ar]
وَإِنْ طَائِفَتَانِ مِنَ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ اقْتَتَلُوا فَأَصْلِحُوا بَيْنَهُمَا

[en]
And if two factions among the believers should fight, then make settlement between the two.

[ref]
الحجرات 9:49.

[en]

[en]
“Abusing a Muslim is transgression (deviation from the path of truth), and fighting him is disbelief.”

[ref]
صحيح البخاري، الإيمان، حديث: 48.

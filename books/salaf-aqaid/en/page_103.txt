The increase of water, and water gushing forth from his blessed fingers. Food glorifying Allah in his presence: these miracles too appeared many times.

[en]
The healing of the sick: At his hands, peace be upon him, some of his Companions who were sick were cured without medicine.

[en]
Animals showing him reverence and respect. Trees showing humility and submission to him. Stones greeting him, peace be upon him, with salam.

[en]
Swift retribution against some of his enemies.

[en]
His telling of certain matters of the unseen. His giving news of events taking place at very great distances as soon as they happened.

[en]
His telling of matters of the unseen that had not yet happened, which later happened exactly as he had said.

[en]
His being one whose supplications are answered.

[en]
Allah the Exalted protecting him and preventing his enemies from reaching him.

[en]
In this connection, Abu Hurairah, may Allah be pleased with him, narrates that Abu Jahl once said to his companions, “Does Muhammad put his face on the ground in your presence?” They replied, “Yes.” Abu Jahl said, “By al-Lat and al-Uzza, if I see him doing that, I will put my foot on his neck or rub his face in the dust.” Then he came towards the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, while he was praying. He wanted to put his foot on his neck, but his companions saw him running back on his heels and shielding himself with his hands.

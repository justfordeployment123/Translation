It is obligatory for the Ummah to follow the Noble Quran and to accept the judgements of the Quran and the Sunnah in all the affairs of life. Following the Sunnah is necessary because Allah the Exalted sent Muhammad, peace be upon him, as a Messenger to jinn and mankind so that he would explain and expound the Book of Allah to them. Allah the Exalted says:

[ar]
وَأَنْزَلْنَا إِلَيْكَ الذِّكْرَ لِتُبَيِّنَ لِلنَّاسِ مَا نُزِّلَ إِلَيْهِمْ وَلَعَلَّهُمْ يَتَفَكَّرُونْ

[en]
And We revealed to you the message that you may make clear to the people what was sent down to them and that they might give thought.

[ref]
النحل 44:16.

[en]
Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah believe that the Noble Quran is the Speech of Allah, both its letters and its meanings. It came forth from Allah's exalted Self and will go back to Him. This Speech was sent down by Him. It is not created. Allah truly spoke this Speech and revealed it to Jibril, peace be upon him, and Jibril, peace be upon him, brought it to Muhammad, peace be upon him. Allah the Exalted, who is Most Wise and All-Aware, sent down this Quran in clear Arabic. It has reached us through such tawatur that there is no room in it for any doubt or suspicion. (Tawatur means narration by a very large number of people from a very large number of people, at every level of the chain of narration.) Allah the Exalted says:

[ar]
وَإِنَّهُ لَتَنْزِيلُ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينْ

[ar]

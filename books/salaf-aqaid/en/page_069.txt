The methodology and way of Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah in this is as follows:

[en]
Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah worship Allah the Exalted and associate no one with Him. They ask of Allah alone. They seek help from Allah alone; they seek aid from no one besides Him. They rely on Allah alone. They fear no one besides Him. They draw near to Allah by worship, by good deeds and by obedience. They carry out this command of Allah the Exalted:

[ar]
وَاعْبُدُوا اللَّهَ وَلَا تُشْرِكُوا بِهِ شَيْئَا

[en]
Worship Allah and associate nothing with Him.

[ref]
النسآء 36:4.

[en]
Third, the Oneness of the Names and Attributes: The Oneness of the Names and Attributes means believing that Allah the Exalted has the most beautiful Names (al-Asma' al-Husna) and the loftiest attributes. He possesses all the attributes of perfection and is completely free of all attributes of deficiency. This distinction belongs to no one but Him.

[en]
Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah know the Lord, the Exalted, by His attributes that are mentioned in the Quran and the Sunnah. They describe the Lord, the Exalted, only with the attributes that He Himself and the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, have stated. They do not change the meanings of words by moving them away from their original sense. They do not take the path of ilhad regarding the Names and Attributes. They affirm for Allah the Exalted only the Names and Attributes that He has affirmed for Himself.

[fen]
Ilhad means turning away from the path of truth and deviating from the path of guidance. Ta'til (denial) of the attributes of Allah, tahrif (changing their meanings), takyif (describing how they are) and tamthil (likening) are all included in this ilhad.

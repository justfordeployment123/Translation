Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal, may Allah have mercy on him, was asked about faith, and he said: “Speech, action and intention.” He was asked about someone asking, “Are you a believer?” He replied: “Saying this is an innovation.” He was asked again how this question should be answered. He said: “In sha Allah, I am a believer.”

[ref]
شرح أصول اعتقاد أهل السنة والجماعة للالكائي: 361/4، رقم: 1406.

[en]
Whoever commits something the Shariah has forbidden does not become a disbeliever: if a believing servant commits any of these things, then according to Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah the quality of faith will not be taken away from him. Likewise, whoever abandons something the Shariah has made obligatory does not become a disbeliever by abandoning it: if a believing servant abandons any of these things, then according to Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah the quality of faith will not be taken away from him. He does not leave faith unless he commits one of the nullifiers of faith.

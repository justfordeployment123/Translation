The technical definition of Sunnah: In technical usage, the Sunnah means the manner and way on which the noble Prophet, peace be upon him, and his Companions stood in knowledge, belief, word, deed and tacit approval. Tacit approval means the words and actions in which he, peace be upon him, let people continue. The word Sunnah is also used for the sunnahs of acts of worship and of beliefs. The word innovation stands as the opposite of Sunnah.

The linguistic definition of Jama'ah: The word jama'ah is derived from jam', which means to gather something together, to join together, to unite. In Arabic one says jama'tuhu fajtama'a, that is, I brought it together and it gathered.

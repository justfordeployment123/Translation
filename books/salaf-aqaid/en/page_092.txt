[en]
Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah declare anyone who denies any letter of the Noble Quran, or adds anything to it or takes anything away from it, to be a disbeliever. Thus we believe with certainty that every verse of the Noble Quran was sent down by Allah and that it has reached us by way of definitive tawatur. The Noble Quran was not sent down all at once. It was sent down in instalments over twenty-three years, in step with the course of circumstances and events and in answer to questions.

[en]
The Noble Quran consists of one hundred and fourteen surahs, of which eighty-six were revealed in Makkah and twenty-eight in Madinah. The surahs revealed in Makkah are called the Makki surahs, and those revealed in Madinah are called the Madani surahs. Twenty-nine surahs begin with the disjointed letters.

[en]
In the time of the Prophet, the Noble Quran was written down under the supervision of the Prophet, peace be upon him. A few chosen Companions were scribes of the revelation, who at his command used to write down all the verses of the Quran as they were revealed. In the time of the first Caliph, all the verses of the Quran were gathered together in the form of a book. In the time of Uthman, the Noble Quran was gathered upon one harf (one script).

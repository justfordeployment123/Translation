[en]
The Oneness of worship is the very Tawhid to which all the Messengers called. It was the denial of this Tawhid that brought destruction on the earlier nations.

[en]
The Oneness of worship is the beginning and the end of the religion, its outward and its inward. It was the first and the last call of the Messengers. For it the Messengers were sent, the Books were revealed and the swords of jihad were drawn. It is this Tawhid that separated the Muslims from the disbelievers, and the people of Paradise from the people of Hell. This is the Tawhid meant by (there is no god but Allah). Allah the Exalted says:

[ar]
وَمَا أَرْسَلْنَا مِنْ قَبْلِكَ مِنْ رَسُولٍ إِلَّا نُوحِي إِلَيْهِ أَنَّهُ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا أَنَا فَاعْبُدُونْ

[en]
And We sent not before you any messenger except that We revealed to him that, “There is no deity except Me, so worship Me.”

[ref]
الأنبيآء 25:21.

[en]
Allah, who is the Lord, the Creator, the Provider, the Owner, the One who disposes of all things, who gives life and causes death, who possesses all the attributes of perfection and is free of every flaw, in whose hand is everything: it is necessary that He alone be the one deity, who has no partner. It is necessary that He alone be worshipped. Allah says:

[ar]
وَمَا خَلَقْتُ الْجِنَّ وَالْإِنْسَ إِلَّا لِيَعْبُدُونْ

[en]
And I did not create the jinn and mankind except to worship Me.

[ref]
الذاريات 56:51.

[en]
The Oneness of Lordship requires the Oneness of worship, and believing in both is an essential condition. But the case of the polytheists was different: they acknowledged the Oneness of Lordship but denied the Oneness of worship.

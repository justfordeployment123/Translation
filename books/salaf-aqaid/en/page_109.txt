The coming out of Gog and Magog. Three events of sinking into the earth: one in the east, one in the west and one in the Arabian Peninsula. The appearance of the smoke. The rising of the sun from the west. The Beast of the Earth, which will speak to people. The appearance of a fire that will gather people to the plain of gathering.

[en]
Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah believe in the matters of the unseen that, as the Quran and the Sunnah tell us, a person will meet after death.

[en]
These matters of the unseen are: The agonies of death. The coming of the angels of death. The believer's joy that he is going to meet his Lord the Exalted. The presence of Satan at the time of death. The non-acceptance of a disbeliever's faith at the time of death. The Barzakh (the barrier between this world and the Hereafter). The blessings received in the grave. The punishment of the grave. The trial of the grave. The questions of the two angels.

[en]
The martyr being alive with his Lord the Exalted and receiving provision. The reward and honour of the fortunate souls, and the punishment of the wretched souls.

[en]
Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah believe in the Day of Resurrection, the day on which Allah the Exalted will bring the dead back to life, raise people from their graves and call them to account.

[en]

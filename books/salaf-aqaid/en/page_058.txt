Fifth: By believing in this creed, a person gains nearness to Allah and succeeds in attaining His pleasure.

[en]
These distinctive features of the creed belong to Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah, and these qualities do not change with changes of time and place. And praise be to Allah.

[fen]
This explanation makes clear that the claim that “Salafiyyah is the name of a period (a stage in time) and not an Islamic school (madhhab, a school of thought)” is not at all proven true. This claim is wrong because the school of the Righteous Predecessors is built on two great foundations: the best example, and a sound methodology worthy of being followed. By the best example is meant the noble Companions, the Successors and the followers of the Successors, who lived in the first three generations of Islam. By the sound methodology worthy of being followed is meant the way that was practised during those three generations in understanding beliefs, in reasoning from evidence for rulings, in knowledge and faith, and in all the other aspects of the Shariah. Thus adopting Salafiyyah is highly praiseworthy for everyone who adopts it as an example and a methodology for himself, because it is the legacy of his Righteous Predecessors, who, by the testimony of the noble Prophet, peace be upon him, are the best people of this Ummah. However, adopting Salafiyyah in name only, without forming one's creed and acting according to it, is not praiseworthy at all, because what counts is meanings and concepts, not words and terms.

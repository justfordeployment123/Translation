Complete submission before Allah the Exalted with regard to Allah Himself, His command and ruling, obedience, and the following of His Messenger, peace be upon him, is also part of the Islamic creed.

[en]
When the expression “Islamic creed” is used, what is meant is the creed of Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah alone, because that is the Islam which Allah the Exalted chose as the religion for His servants. This was the creed of the people of the virtuous generations of the noble Companions, the Successors and those who followed them. Among Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah, the “Islamic creed” also has other names, which are used as its synonyms and point to it. Of these, the following names are especially well known:

[en]
First: Tawhid; second: the Sunnah; third: the fundamentals of the religion (Usul ad-Din); fourth: al-Fiqh al-Akbar (the greatest understanding); fifth: the Shariah; sixth: the matters of faith. These names serve as designations of the science of creed.

The First Principle: Faith and the Pillars of Faith

[en]
The summary of the beliefs of the Righteous Predecessors (Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah) concerning the fundamentals of faith is that one firmly believes in, and accepts as true, the six pillars of faith mentioned in the hadith of Jibril, peace be upon him. When Jibril, peace be upon him, came to the Prophet, peace be upon him, and asked him about faith, he answered his question and said:

[ar]
أَنْ تُؤْمِنَ بِاللَّهْ، وَمَلاَئِكَتِهْ، وَكُتُبِهْ، وَرُسُلِهْ، وَالْيَوْمِ الآخِرْ، وَتُؤْمِنَ بِالْقَدَرِ خَيْرِهِ وَشَرِّهْ

[en]
“That you believe in Allah, His angels, His Books, His Messengers and the Last Day, and that you believe in the divine decree, its good and its bad.”

[ref]
صحيح مسلم، الإيمان، حديث: 8.

[en]
Faith stands on these six pillars. If even one of them falls, a person is no longer a believer, because a pillar of faith is missing. Faith can only stand on all its pillars, just as a building can only stand on all its columns.

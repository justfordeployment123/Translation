Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah also believe in the Scale (al-Mizan) on which people's deeds will be weighed. This Scale will have two pans.

[en]
They also believe in the opening of the records of deeds. Some will receive their record in their right hand, some in their left hand and some behind their backs.

[en]
The Bridge (as-Sirat) will be set up over Hell. Those who do good deeds will pass over this bridge, and the wicked will slip and fall.

[fen]
This is the bridge over which people will pass into Paradise. People will cross this bridge in accordance with their deeds: some will cross in an instant, in the blink of an eye; some will cross like lightning, some like a strong wind, some like a galloping horse, some like riders on camels; some will cross running, some walking and some crawling. Some will be snatched and thrown into Hell. In short, every person will cross the Bridge according to his deeds, until he is purified of his sins. After crossing the Bridge, people will halt on a large bridge set up between Paradise and Hell. There they will be made to settle the wrongs between them, and retribution (qisas) will be exacted among them. Then, when they have been completely cleansed in every way, they will be given permission to enter Paradise.

[en]
Paradise and Hell are both created. Both of them exist now. They will never perish. Allah the Exalted created them both before the other creations. Paradise is the home of the believers, the people of Tawhid and the God-fearing, while Hell is the abode of the disbelievers, the polytheists, the hypocrites, the irreligious, the criminals and the idol worshippers.

[en]
Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah believe that on the Day of Resurrection the Ummah of Muhammad will be brought to account before all the other nations.

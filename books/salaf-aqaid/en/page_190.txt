[en]
“Whoever dislikes something in his ruler, let him be patient with it, because whoever goes even a hand's span outside (his) authority and obedience and dies in that state, dies the death of Jahiliyyah (ignorance).”

[ref]
صحيح مسلم، الإمارة، حديث: 1849.

[en]
Thus, according to Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah, obedience to the ruler in permissible matters is a great principle of the creed. This is why the imams of the Salaf included it among the beliefs. There is hardly any book of creed in which ample light has not been shed on this principle. It is an obligation of the Shariah on every Muslim, and an essential condition for the political stability, order and discipline of the Islamic state.

[en]
Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah hold that, besides the five daily prayers, the Friday prayer and the two Eid prayers too are to be performed behind the rulers. With them the obligations of enjoining good and forbidding evil, jihad and Hajj will also be carried out, whether they are righteous or wicked and sinful.

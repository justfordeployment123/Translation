Allah the Exalted has mentioned the counsel of Luqman, peace be upon him, to his son:

[ar]
يَا بُنَيَّ لَا تُشْرِكْ بِاللَّهِ ۖ إِنَّ الشِّرْكَ لَظُلْمٌ عَظِيمْ

[en]
O my son, do not associate (anything) with Allah. Indeed, association (with him) is great injustice.

[ref]
لقمان 13:31.

[en]
Abu ad-Darda', may Allah be pleased with him, reports that the Prophet, peace be upon him, said: Allah, the Mighty and Majestic, said:

[ar]
إِنِّي وَالْإِنْسُ وَالْجِنُّ فِي نَبَأٍ عَظِيمْ، أَخْلُقُ وَيُعْبَدُ غَيْرِي، وَأَرْزُقُ وَيُشْكَرُ غَيْرِي

[en]
“Strange indeed is the story of Me and of mankind and the jinn. It is I who create, but someone else is worshipped. It is I who provide, while someone else is thanked.”

[fen]
Although this hadith is sound in meaning, it is weak in its chain of narration; Shaykh al-Albani, may Allah have mercy on him, has declared it weak in as-Silsilah ad-Da'ifah (2371).

[en]
It is the greatest lie that a man should associate anyone with Allah, when it is Allah who created him.

[en]
Allah the Exalted has commanded reform and forbidden spreading corruption. Allah says:

[ar]
وَلَا تُفْسِدُوا فِي الْأَرْضِ بَعْدَ إِصْلَاحِهَا وَادْعُوهُ خَوْفًا وَطَمَعًا ۚ إِنَّ رَحْمَتَ اللَّهِ قَرِيبٌ مِنَ الْمُحْسِنِينْ

[en]
And cause not corruption upon the earth after its reformation. And invoke Him in fear and aspiration. Indeed, the mercy of Allah is near to the doers of good.

[ref]
الأعراف 56:7.

But if someone makes the exception in faith out of doubt about faith, regarding doubt and uncertainty about faith as the exception in faith, Ahl as-Sunnah wal-Jama'ah reject such an exception completely. They do not regard it as an exception but as a corruption of faith.

[en]
Evidence for the exception in faith is found in the Quran and the Sunnah, the reports of the Salaf and the sayings of the imams. The Noble Quran says:

[ar]
وَلَا تَقُولَنَّ لِشَيْءٍ إِنِّي فَاعِلٌ ذَٰلِكَ غَدَا

[ar]
إِلَّا أَنْ يَشَاءَ اللَّهْ

[en]
And never say of anything, “Indeed, I will do that tomorrow,” except (when adding), “If Allah wills.”

[ref]
الكهف 23:18، 24.

[en]
And He says:

[ar]

[en]
So do not claim yourselves to be pure; He is most knowing of who fears Him.

[ref]
النجم 32:53.

[en]

Definition of the Salaf

[en]
The linguistic definition of Salaf: Linguistically, salaf is a thing that has passed, that has gone ahead. In Arabic one says: salafa ash-shay'u salafan, that is, it passed (to pass, to go ahead).

[en]
As-salaf are those who have passed, who have gone ahead. Those who go ahead while travelling in a caravan and reach a place first are also called as-salaf. In the Noble Quran it is said:

[ar]
فَلَمَّا آسَفُونَا انْتَقَمْنَا مِنْهُمْ فَأَغْرَقْنَاهُمْ أَجْمَعِينْ

[ar]
فَجَعَلْنَاهُمْ سَلَفًا وَمَثَلًا لِلْآخِرِينْ

[en]
And when they angered Us, We took retribution from them and drowned them all. And We made them a precedent and an example for the later peoples.

[ref]
الزخرف 56,55:43.

[en]
That is, We made them salaf (a precedent) for those who follow in their footsteps and adopt conduct like theirs, so that later people would take a lesson from their end and remember that if they too did the same, their end would be no different from theirs.

# Review list: *Salaf-e-Saliheen ke Aqaid o Nazariyat*

Every point on which the text needs a human reader's decision. The Urdu was transcribed from the photos, proofread a second time against them by a different reader, and checked automatically: every Quran verse (235 checks) and hadith (72 checks) is identical to its official source, nothing is doubled or lost at any page break, and every reference with several numbers was rendered the way the app shows it and compared with the photo. The English edition was translated from the final Urdu by five translators and then reviewed block by block against it by five other readers, with one glossary of creed terms for the whole book (`ENGLISH.md`). Each item names its page; for sections 1–5 the full note is also at the top of `page_NNN.txt`.

**The PDF is incomplete.** It holds book pages 5–196 (its cover says «صفحات ۵ تا ۱۹۶»); the book's contents run to page 258. Page 196 ends the eighth principle with a full sentence that announces the ninth, so the text stops cleanly, but principles nine onward are missing.

**Footnotes.** This book has many long explanatory footnotes (on miracles, karamat, intercession, the kinds of nifaq, taqlid, obedience to rulers). They are typed in full as `[fn]` blocks: shown on the page and translated in the English edition, but **not read aloud**. Reading them aloud is a one-line change at recording time, and would add roughly 20,000–30,000 ElevenLabs credits (their numbers would first be written as words).

## 6. Points about the book's own Urdu, raised while translating it into English (8 items)

The English follows the Urdu in each case; these are for your judgement.

- **p12:** «شعبہ ڈیزائننگ کے انچارج ہارون الرشید، محمد رمضان شاد اور خرم شہزاد» can mean that only Haroon ur Rasheed heads the design department, or all three; the English gives the title to the first name only.
- **p24:** the Urdu translation says «عزت دار کی عزت اور ذلیل کی ذلت» ("and") where the Arabic has «أَوْ» ("or"); the English follows the Urdu.
- **p88:** «توریت و انجیل و زبور اور قرآن کے سوا دیگر الہامی کتابیں ... حفاظت کا ذمہ نہیں لیا» can be read two ways; the English takes it, from the next sentence, as "the Torah, the Gospel, the Psalms and the other Books, apart from the Quran".
- **p113:** inside the hadith the angels address the Prophet with an honorific («آپ ﷺ نہیں جانتے»); kept, written out, in the English.
- **p144:** «دوسرے سے کہا» ("said to the other") is translated "about the other He said", which is what the Arabic «لِلآخَرِ» means here.
- **p168:** «آپ، جناب اور حضور» (Urdu forms of respectful address) is rendered "respectful forms of address such as “sir”".
- **p185:** «ہر مسلمان کے لیے ضروری ہے کہ وہ دلیل کی بنا پر ایک مسلک کو چھوڑ کر دوسرا مسلک اختیار کرے» says flatly that every Muslim *must* change school on the basis of evidence; translated as it stands.
- **p193:** the Urdu translation of the Umm Salamah hadith opens a quotation and does not close it before «صحابۂ کرام نے عرض کیا»; the English closes it.

The Saying of Imam Muhammad ibn Sirin, may Allah have mercy on him (died 110 AH)

[en]
When the time of death came to Imam Muhammad ibn Sirin, may Allah have mercy on him, he began to weep. He was asked: What has made you weep? He said: I weep for my neglect in the days when I was free, for how few deeds I have for the lofty Paradise, and for how few deeds I have to save me from the blazing Fire.

The Saying of Abu Hazim Salamah ibn Dinar, may Allah have mercy on him (died 140 AH)

[en]
Every deed because of which you dislike death, leave it, and then it will not harm you whenever you die.

[hen]
A Reflection

[en]
If we leave every useless task, useless gathering, useless friendship and useless affair in our lives, and keep our lives busy preparing for the Hereafter, we will have no fear of death. Rather, we will love to meet our Creator and Master.

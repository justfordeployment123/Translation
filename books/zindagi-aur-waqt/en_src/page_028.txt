Today a very large part of our society is living in exactly this state. The sense of life has died, and consciences have died. Days and nights pass in watching films, in amusement and play, in needless trips, in decorating idle gatherings, and in jokes and laughter late into the night. Such a person suffers a double loss, because he has achieved no goal of this world and none of the Hereafter. And if the message of death comes to him in this state, it is ruin. Look at the advice of Zuhayr ibn Nu'aym, may Allah have mercy on him:

[en]
A man said to Zuhayr ibn Nu'aym: O Abu Abd ar-Rahman, will you give me some advice? He said: Yes. Beware that Allah seizes you while you are in a state of heedlessness.

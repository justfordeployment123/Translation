[en]
The second hour in which a person will regret and grieve over his life is in the Hereafter. That is, when people have been given the reward of their deeds, and the people of Paradise have gone into Paradise and the people of Hell into Hell, then the people of Hell will wish to be sent back to the world. As Allah the Exalted says:

[en]
{{QEN 35:36}} And they will cry out therein, Our Lord, remove us; we will do righteousness, other than what we were doing!

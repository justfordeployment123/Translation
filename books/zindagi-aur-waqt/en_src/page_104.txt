[hen]
The Saying of Ibrahim ibn Shayban az-Zahid, may Allah have mercy on him

[en]
Whoever always guards his time and does not waste it in what angers Allah, Allah will guard his religion and his worldly life for him.

[hen]
The Saying of Yazid ar-Raqashi, may Allah have mercy on him

[en]
Speaking to himself, he would say:

[en]
Woe to you, O Yazid! Who will pray for you after death? Who will fast for you after death? Who will please your Lord for you after death? O people, do you not weep over what remains of your lives, and do you not lament over yourselves? O you whose promised appointment is death, whose house is the grave, whose bed is the dust and whose companions will be the worms, and who besides all this awaits the greatest terror, how will you fare?

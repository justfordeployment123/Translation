The Saying of Ahmad ibn Masruq at-Tusi, may Allah have mercy on him

[en]
Ever since you came out of your mother's womb, you have been tearing down your life.

[hen]
The Saying of Ibn al-Faraji, may Allah have mercy on him

[en]
Whoever does not seize the opportunity while it is possible will inherit regret when it is no longer possible.

[en]
That is, when he had youth, free time and favourable circumstances, he did not make use of them or recognise his purpose. Then when old age came and his limbs grew weak, and he could no longer worship properly, all that is left is regret: if only I had worshipped in my youth, while I was healthy and strong.

[hen]
The Saying of Abdullah ibn Munazil, may Allah have mercy on him

[en]
Whoever busies himself with past and future times, his time is lost without benefit.

[hen]
The Saying of Abul-Qasim an-Nasrabadi, may Allah have mercy on him

[en]
Taking care of one's time is one of the signs of an awakened mind.

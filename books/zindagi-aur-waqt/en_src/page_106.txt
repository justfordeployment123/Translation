The Saying of Ahmad ibn Asim al-Antaki, may Allah have mercy on him

[en]
This is an easy gain: set right what remains of your life, and what has passed will be forgiven.

[hen]
The Saying of One of the Wise

[en]
He said:

[en]
One of the wise said: I am amazed at the one who grieves over the loss of his wealth but does not grieve over the passing of his life. And I am amazed that the world is turning away from him and the Hereafter is coming towards him, yet he is busy with the one that is turning away and turns away from the one that is coming towards him.

[hen]
The Saying of One of the Wise

[en]
One of the wise said: Time is breaths that never return.

[hen]
The Saying of One of the Predecessors

[en]
One of the righteous predecessors said: A sign of Allah's displeasure is the wasting of time.

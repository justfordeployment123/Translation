The Saying of Sa'id ibn Jubayr, may Allah have mercy on him (died 95 AH)

[en]
Every day that the believer lives is a gain.

[hen]
The Saying of Muwarriq al-Ijli, may Allah have mercy on him (died 101 AH)

[en]
Son of Adam, your provision is brought to you every day, and still you grieve, while your life is growing shorter and you do not grieve. You seek what will make you rebellious, while you already have what is enough for you.

[hen]
The Saying of Awn ibn Abdullah, may Allah have mercy on him (died 110 AH)

[en]
Woe to me! How can I be heedless when I am not being neglected, that is, every movement of mine is being written? How can my life be easy when a heavy day lies ahead of me? How can I not hasten to good deeds when I do not know when my death will come? How can I be happy in this world when my state in it will not last? How can I prefer this world when it harmed those who preferred it before me? How can my greed for this world increase when my settling place and my eternal home are elsewhere, in the Hereafter? How can I love this world when it is passing and coming to an end? And how can my grief not increase, when I do not know how my Lord will deal with me because of my sins?

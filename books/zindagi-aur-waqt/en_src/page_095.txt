The Sayings of al-Hasan al-Basri, may Allah have mercy on him (died 110 AH)

[hen]
The First Saying

[en]
Son of Adam, you are between two mounts that are driving you on: the night towards the day, and the day towards the night, until they deliver you to the Hereafter. Son of Adam, who could be in greater danger than you?

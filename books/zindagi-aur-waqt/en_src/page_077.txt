Zayd ibn Khalid al-Juhani, may Allah be pleased with him, narrated: The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, led us in the morning prayer at al-Hudaybiyah, after rain that had fallen during the night. When he finished the prayer, he turned to the people and said: Do you know what your Lord has said? They said: Allah and His Messenger know best. He said: Among My servants, some have woken up believing in Me and some disbelieving. Whoever says: We were given rain by the grace of Allah and His mercy, he is a believer in Me and a disbeliever in the stars. And whoever says: We were given rain by such and such a star, he is a disbeliever in Me and a believer in the stars.

[en]
These narrations make it clear that the movements of the stars and planets have no effect on human life. Rather, every change in the world happens only by the command of Allah.

One of the signs that Allah has turned away from a servant is that He lets him be busy with what does not concern him.

[en]
So doing good brings blessing into life, and because of sins a servant's life becomes useless and purposeless. As the Prophet, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
Salman al-Farisi, may Allah be pleased with him, narrated that the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said: Nothing turns back the decree except supplication, and nothing increases life except righteousness.

[en]
Life has already been written, so how does righteousness increase it? The scholars explain that the increase in life here means gaining blessing in life: Allah puts blessing into a person's life so that in a short span he gains much good and earns many good deeds. And the opposite meaning of this hadith makes it clear that sins shorten a person's life, meaning that even if he lives long, he is deprived of gathering much good.

[en]
Here we describe some of the harms of sins, so that readers may benefit.

[en]
The first harm: being deprived of knowledge, and especially being left without understanding of the religion.

[en]
The second harm: being deprived of provision; blessings are taken away and provision becomes tight.

[en]
The third harm: a sense of loneliness and fear is found in the sinner's heart, because of which he dislikes meeting his Lord, and he also feels estranged from people.

[en]
The fourth harm: sin shortens life, whether in the form of illnesses or in the form of being deprived of good.

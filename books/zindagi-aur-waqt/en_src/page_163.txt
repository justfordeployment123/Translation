These were the degrees of love. Now we describe the kinds of love, which are as follows.

[hen]
Love of Allah

[en]
In this love everyone shares, Muslims, Jews, Christians and idolaters, for they all love Allah in the end. But this is not enough to be saved from punishment or to succeed.

[hen]
Love of Everything That Allah Loves

[en]
This kind is necessary for entering Islam, for leaving disbelief, and for success in the Hereafter. The most beloved to Allah will be the one who is strongest in this kind.

[hen]
Love for Allah's Sake, Seeking His Pleasure

[en]
This is one of the requirements of the love of Allah: that a person loves others because of his love for Allah.

[hen]
Love Alongside Allah

[en]
To love someone else alongside Allah is also called the love of association. It is the love of the idolaters, who love others along with Allah, not for Allah's sake nor because of Allah, but as partners in the love of Allah.

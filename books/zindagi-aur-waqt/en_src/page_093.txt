The Saying of Masruq, may Allah have mercy on him (died 63 AH)

[en]
Masruq ibn al-Ajda, may Allah have mercy on him, said:

[en]
A person ought to have some gatherings in which he sits alone, remembers his sins and seeks forgiveness for them.

[hen]
The Saying of al-Ahnaf ibn Qays, may Allah have mercy on him (died 67 AH)

[en]
The fear of having to answer for it before Allah has kept me from saying much.

[en]
It is also narrated about him:

[en]
Al-Ahnaf ibn Qays, may Allah have mercy on him, was asked: Why do you not touch the pebbles? He said: There is no reward in touching them and no sin in leaving them. But I have two habits: I do not backbite a companion who has just left me, and I do not interfere in the affairs of a people who do not bring me into them themselves.

[en]
Note: This report shows that the predecessors avoided sitting idle, picking up or tossing pebbles, and similar useless acts.

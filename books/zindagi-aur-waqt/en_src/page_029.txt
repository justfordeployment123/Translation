The Second Form: Heedless of This World, but Alert for the Hereafter

[en]
This form of heedlessness is good and desired, as long as it is not taken to extremes. To become so completely heedless of the world that a person begins to fall short in the rights he owes others, and does nothing but worship all the time, is blameworthy and disliked. Rather, everyone who has a right must be given his right, as the Prophet, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
Abu Juhayfah, may Allah be pleased with him, narrated that the Prophet, peace be upon him, made Salman al-Farisi and Abu ad-Darda brothers. One day Salman came to visit Abu ad-Darda, and saw Umm ad-Darda, may Allah be pleased with her, in a very neglected state. He asked her: What is the matter with you? She said: Your brother Abu ad-Darda has no need of me.

[en]
Then Abu ad-Darda came, and prepared food for Salman. He said to Salman: You eat, for I am fasting. Salman said: I will not eat until you eat. So at last Abu ad-Darda ate.

[en]
When night came, Abu ad-Darda got up to pray, and Salman said to him: Sleep. So he slept. After a while he got up again, and Salman said: Keep sleeping. When it was the last part of the night, Salman said: Now get up. And the two of them prayed.

[en]
Then Salman said to Abu ad-Darda: Your Lord has a right over you, your own self has a right over you, and your family has a right over you, so give everyone who has a right his right. Then Abu ad-Darda came to the Prophet, peace be upon him, and told him all of this, and he, peace be upon him, said: Salman has spoken the truth.

Chapter One: The Importance of Time in the Light of the Quran

[en]
Whoever disobeyed Allah and spent his life in sin, there will surely come two times when he will express regret and sorrow over the life he lived. He will ask for more time, and wish that he could be given respite. But at that time, regret and sorrow will be of no use, and no one will be given more time.

[en]
Those two times are as follows.

Chapter Three: Important Matters Concerning Time

[en]
Allah the Exalted says:

[en]
{{QEN 9:36}}

[en]
Abu Bakrah, may Allah be pleased with him, narrated that the Prophet, peace be upon him, said: Time has turned around to the state it was in on the day Allah created the heavens and the earth. The year is twelve months, of which four are sacred: three in succession, Dhul-Qa'dah, Dhul-Hijjah and Muharram, and the Rajab of Mudar, which comes between Jumada and Sha'ban.

Abdullah ibn Umar, may Allah be pleased with them both (died 73 AH)

[en]
Nafi, may Allah have mercy on him, the freed slave of Ibn Umar, narrated that Abdullah ibn Umar, may Allah be pleased with them both, would pray all night, then say: Nafi, has the time of sahur come? I would say: No, and he would start praying again, until I said: Yes, it has come. Then he would sit, seek forgiveness and make supplication until morning came.

[en]
Nafi, may Allah have mercy on him, was asked: What did Abdullah ibn Umar do at home? He said: Ablution for every prayer, and between two prayers he would recite the Quran.

[en]
A reflection: that is, his time was spent preparing for prayer or reciting the Quran, and he was kept safe from useless things.

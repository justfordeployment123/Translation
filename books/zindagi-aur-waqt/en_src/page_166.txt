Love of Land and Gold

[en]
Greed for land and gold also plays a large part in ruining life. Such a person also falls within the first kind, for he has taken gathering land and gold as the purpose of life, and this greed has taken him away from life's true purpose. It has left him no regard for brotherhood, no care for relatives, no concern for the poor and the orphan, but only one aim: that I acquire as much land as possible and gather as much wealth as possible. Allah the Exalted says:

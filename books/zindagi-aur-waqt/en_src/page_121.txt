[hen]
Muhammad ibn Nasr al-Marwazi, may Allah have mercy on him (died 294 AH)

[en]
Abu Muhammad ath-Thaqafi, may Allah have mercy on him, said: I heard my grandfather say: I sat with Abu Abdullah Muhammad ibn Nasr al-Marwazi, may Allah have mercy on him, for four years, and in all that long time I never heard him speak about anything other than knowledge.

[en]
This truly makes our hair stand on end: how carefully our elders guarded their tongues, how they turned away from pointless talk, and how they guarded every moment of their lives.

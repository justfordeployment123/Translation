Then the Prophet, peace be upon him, turned to the delegation of Iyad and said: Was any will of Quss ibn Sa'idah found? The delegation said: Yes, after his death a letter was found under his pillow, in which was written:

[en]
O you who announce death, while the dead lie in their graves, with tattered rags left of their clothes upon them.

[en]
Leave them, for they have a day on which they will be called, as one who has fainted is roused from his swoon.

[en]
Some of them will be naked, and some of the dead in their clothes, some of them new and some worn and old.

[en]
When the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, heard this, he said: By the One who sent me with the truth, Quss had believed in the Hereafter.

The Sixth Hadith

[en]
Abdullah ibn Busr, may Allah be pleased with him, narrated that a Bedouin said: O Messenger of Allah, who is the best of people? He, peace be upon him, said: The one whose life is long and whose deeds are good.

[en]
After this hadith, Imam at-Tirmidhi, may Allah have mercy on him, quotes the narration of Abu Bakrah, may Allah be pleased with him, which has these further words:

[en]
He, peace be upon him, was asked: Who is the worst of people? He, peace be upon him, said: The one whose life is long and whose deeds are bad.

[en]
The meaning of this narration clearly shows that whoever valued his life, and spent the time he was given in the obedience of Allah and in good deeds, is, according to Islamic law, the best of people. And whoever did not value the precious time of his life is, according to Islamic law, the worst.

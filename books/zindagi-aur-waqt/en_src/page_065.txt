The Ninth Hadith

[en]
Abdullah ibn Amr, may Allah be pleased with them both, narrated: The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, passed by us while we were repairing our hut. He said: What is this you are doing? We said: It has become worn out, and we are repairing it. He said: I see the matter coming sooner than that.

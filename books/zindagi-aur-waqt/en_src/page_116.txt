Part Three: Deeds of the Followers of the Successors and Those After Them, may Allah have mercy on them

[hen]
Sulayman ibn Tarkhan at-Taymi, may Allah have mercy on him (died 143 AH)

[en]
Hammad ibn Salamah, may Allah have mercy on him, said: Whenever we came to Sulayman at-Taymi, may Allah have mercy on him, at an hour in which Allah could be obeyed, we found him obeying. If it was a time for prayer, we found him praying. If it was not a time for prayer, we found him making ablution, or visiting the sick, or following a funeral, or sitting in the mosque glorifying Allah. Hammad said: We felt that he did not even know how to disobey Allah.

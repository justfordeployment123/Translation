What this blessed verse requires is that you were not created for nothing, without purpose. Rather, your creation has a purpose, and that purpose is the worship of Allah: that every part of life should be lived according to the way the prophets, peace be upon them, taught, so that our joys, our sorrows, our trade and our dealings all become worship.

The Lack of Lawful Provision

[en]
Lawful provision also plays a basic part in making life useful and successful. Lawful provision brings blessing into life, good is found in a person's affairs, he is protected from many evils and wrongs, and he inclines more towards good than towards evil. Eating what is lawful makes the insight of the heart work better, along with the sight of the eyes, so that a person quickly succeeds in seeing and recognising the truth. On the other hand, by eating what is unlawful, even if the sight of the eyes is fine, the sight of the heart is lost; the heart becomes blind, and it is unable to recognise and accept the truth, except for one on whom Allah the Exalted has mercy.

[en]
Eating what is unlawful destroys the good qualities and praiseworthy traits found in a person. Indeed, understand it this way: every good can be expected from one who eats what is lawful, and every evil can be expected from one who eats what is unlawful. So to improve one's life and fill it with good, it is necessary for every Muslim to earn lawful provision and to take special care over it. In His holy Book, the Noble Quran, Allah the Exalted has commanded eating lawful provision in three places: in one place to His close and chosen servants, the prophets, in another to His believing servants, and in the third to all mankind. These three places are as follows.

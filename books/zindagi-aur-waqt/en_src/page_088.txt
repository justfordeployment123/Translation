Chapter Four: The Importance of Time and the Sayings of the Predecessors

[hen]
Part One: Sayings of the Companions, may Allah be pleased with them

[hen]
The Saying of Mu'adh ibn Jabal, may Allah be pleased with him (died 18 AH)

[en]
When the last moments of Mu'adh ibn Jabal, may Allah be pleased with him, came, it was night. He asked his son: Has dawn come? He replied: No, father. A little while passed, and he asked the same question and received the same answer. A little later he was told that morning had come, and he said:

[en]
O Allah, I used to fear You, and today I hope in Your mercy. O Allah, You know that I did not love this world, or staying long in it to dig rivers or plant trees. Rather, I loved the thirst of the midday heat while fasting, worship in the hours of the night, and sitting on my knees beside the people of knowledge in the circles of remembrance.

The people of the world, the children of this world, celebrate their birthdays and rejoice as soon as another year of their life goes by. But the believer thinks the opposite: as many years as have passed, that much of his life is gone, and every day whose sun has set is one day less of his life. As a poet said:

[en]
We rejoice at the days that we pass, while every day that passes brings us closer to death.

[hen]
The Fifth Hadith

[en]
Abu Hurayrah, may Allah be pleased with him, narrated that the Prophet, peace be upon him, said: Allah has left no excuse for a man whose death He delayed until He let him reach sixty years.

[en]
That is, for the person who was given sixty years of life, there is no room left for any excuse. For this is the stage of life when the veils of heedlessness are lifted from a person's mind, he has had his fill of sins, the power of desire is gone, and thoughts of moving from this world to the Hereafter grow stronger. But if, even after reaching this age, he insists on his sins, keeps committing one sin after another, and does not improve his Hereafter, he will fail and be a loser.

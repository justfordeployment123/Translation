Everything Depends on Time

[en]
In one sense, every good a person gains in his life depends on valuing time. If he does not take care of his time, he will be deprived of much good. Of all the nations in this world that rose high and left their finest marks on the pages of history, what they all had in common, and what stands out clearly, is that they made the best use of their time and spent it in pursuit of worthy goals.

[en]
So, O Muslim! O traveller to the Hereafter! Your destination is far, and the road is hard. Along the way are robbers and bandits of every kind, who want to snatch away your religion and your provisions for the journey, your piety and righteousness. So ask Allah for help, keep your heart alive with fear of Him, and keep moving towards your destination. Do not let the pleasures of this world make you forget your goal, for every good thing in this world must perish and will not last, while everything in the Hereafter, every blessing, is eternal and everlasting. So, to gain the good of the Hereafter, value your time, for gaining any good depends on time. Wasting time is not good, and no good can ever be gained by wasting time.

The Kinds of Time

[en]
When a person reflects on his life, he concludes that his life is divided into two kinds of time.

[en]
The first is the time he spent in obedience and good deeds.

[en]

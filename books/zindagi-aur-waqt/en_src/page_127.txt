[en]
It is narrated about al-Hasan al-Basri, may Allah have mercy on him, that in the morning he would recite this verse:

[en]
A young man is gladdened by the piety he has sent ahead, especially when he recognises the illness that will be the death of him.

[en]
And when evening came, he would recite this verse:

[en]
This world does not remain for anyone alive, and no one alive remains in this world.

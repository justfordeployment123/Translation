Chapter Five: The Importance of Time and the Deeds of the Predecessors

[hen]
Part One: Deeds of the Companions, may Allah be pleased with them

[en]
In this chapter we will mention some of the deeds of the righteous predecessors, the Companions, the Successors, their followers and those after them, to show how precious they considered their lives, and especially how much time they spent in worship, so that a love of worship may grow in us too, and we may try to live our lives in their way.

[en]
It is a special feature of this Ummah that not only the Book revealed to it, but also the pure life of its Prophet, peace be upon him, and the lives of the people of its first generation have been preserved. This is a very great blessing and good fortune for us.

[en]
A nation that keeps its predecessors before it remains in great good, and always benefits from their experience. As the well-known saying goes, rather than becoming a lesson oneself, one should learn from the experience of others. And our predecessors were matchless in understanding the reality of this world, in concern for the Hereafter, in seeking good, and in hating and avoiding evil.

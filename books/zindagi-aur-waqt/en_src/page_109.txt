As Abdullah ibn Mas'ud, may Allah be pleased with him, said:

[en]
Whoever among you wishes to follow an example, let him follow the Companions of Muhammad, peace be upon him. For they were the most righteous of heart in this Ummah, the deepest in knowledge, the least affected, the most upright in conduct and the best in state. They were a people whom Allah chose for the company of His Prophet, peace be upon him, and for establishing His religion. So recognise their virtue and follow in their footsteps, for they were on the straight path.

[hen]
Abdullah ibn Umar, may Allah be pleased with them both (died 73 AH), said

[en]
Whoever among you wishes to follow a way, let him follow those who have died: they are the Companions of Muhammad, peace be upon him, who were the best of this Ummah, the most righteous of heart, the deepest in knowledge and the least affected. They were a people whom Allah chose for the company of His Prophet, peace be upon him, and for passing on His religion. So imitate their character and their ways, for they were the Companions of Muhammad, peace be upon him, and they were on the straight path.

Abdullah ibn Mas'ud, may Allah be pleased with him (died 32 AH)

[en]
When people had gone to sleep, Abdullah ibn Mas'ud, may Allah be pleased with him, would stand in prayer, and a humming could be heard from him as he wept, like the humming of bees, until morning came.

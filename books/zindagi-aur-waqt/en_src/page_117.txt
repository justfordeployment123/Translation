Sufyan ibn Sa'id ath-Thawri, may Allah have mercy on him (died 161 AH)

[en]
Abu Muhammad al-Baghlani, may Allah have mercy on him, said: Abdullah, may Allah have mercy on him, told us that a man used to follow Sufyan ath-Thawri, may Allah have mercy on him, to see what he did. Sufyan would always take a brick out of a wall and look at a slip of paper. The man wanted to see what was on it. At last the paper came into his hands, and when he looked, this was written on it: O Sufyan, remember standing before Allah, the Mighty and Majestic.

[hen]
A Reflection

[en]
If we reflect on this, we see how busy our elders kept themselves in admonishing and reminding themselves, and how they used every possible means to remind their souls. For there were trials in their time too, and the snares of desire existed then as well. But they lived as travellers to the Hereafter, and always kept their Hereafter before their eyes.

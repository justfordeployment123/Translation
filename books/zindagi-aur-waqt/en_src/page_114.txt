Sa'id ibn al-Musayyib, may Allah have mercy on him (died 105 AH)

[en]
Sa'id ibn al-Musayyib, may Allah have mercy on him, said: For fifty years I have not missed the opening takbir of the prayer, and for fifty years I have not seen the back of a man's neck in prayer.

[hen]
A Benefit

[en]
That is, for fifty years he did not pray without the opening takbir or outside the front row. Today such things seem almost impossible to us, but looking at the early generations, they are not far-fetched. These people had kept their lives very simple. They had not stretched out the means of living, there was no abundance of resources, and they did not weave long webs of hope. Rather, each of them saw the Hereafter as nearer and more certain than this world. May Allah grant us this too. Ameen.

Al-Hasan ibn Yasar al-Basri, may Allah have mercy on him (died 110 AH)

[en]
Al-Hasan al-Basri, may Allah have mercy on him, was asked: O Abu Sa'id, will you not wash your shirt? Hisham said that he replied: I think the matter of death is even sooner than that.

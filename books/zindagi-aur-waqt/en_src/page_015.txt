Preface

[en]
Surely the most precious thing a person has is his time. Time is like a breath that can never be taken again. A person's breaths and days are counted, and they can be neither more nor less. So whoever spends these breaths in good deeds will find good, and only good, in this world and the Hereafter. And whoever spends them in evil will find evil, and only evil, in this world and the Hereafter. In other words, as you sow, so shall you reap.

[en]
A person's life in this world is the time for sowing, and the life of the Hereafter is the time for reaping.

[en]
So keep sowing the seeds of good in this world, like the farmer who goes to his fields each day, stays busy farming all day long, and when evening comes, returns empty-handed. Those who watch him see that the poor man goes out in the morning and comes back in the evening with nothing in his hands. At last someone asks him: Sir, every morning you go out and work hard all day, yet every evening you come back empty-handed? And the farmer replies: I have sown a crop. I work on it and pour my sweat into it, because I hope that one day my field will ripen and I will be rewarded for my labour. Such is the example of a believer who worships his Lord.

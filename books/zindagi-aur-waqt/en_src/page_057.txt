This span of sixty years was enough for him to succeed. But if love of worldly life and the grip of his desires remain the same, then there is nothing but regret. Especially in the times we live in, with evil everywhere, and the trial of immodesty and the lowering of standards in what people look at, which has caught not only the unmarried but even the old and the married. While the Prophet, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
Abu Hurayrah, may Allah be pleased with him, narrated that the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said: There are three people to whom Allah will not speak on the Day of Resurrection, nor purify them, nor look at them with mercy, and for them is a painful punishment: an old man who commits adultery, a lying king, and a poor man who is arrogant.

[en]
In this narration, each of these three had reasons and barriers that could have kept him from his sin. In old age the power of desire was gone, yet he did evil. The king had every power, yet he lied. And the poor man's poverty and hunger could have kept him from arrogance.

[en]
Abu Bakr ibn al-Mu'ammal, may Allah have mercy on him, said:

[en]
We have only three states: youth, then old age, then death.

[en]
Abu Mus'hir, may Allah have mercy on him, said:

[en]
The souls of the living are only pledges, and soon these pledges, the souls, will be taken back from the living.

[en]
He also said:

[en]
Suppose you were given as long a life as Nuh, peace be upon him, lived, and then you obtained all of this with ease.

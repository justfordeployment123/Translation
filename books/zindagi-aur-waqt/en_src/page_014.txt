All the narrations in this book are authentic except one, and its ruling has also been stated.

[en]
If a narration is from Sahih al-Bukhari or Sahih Muslim, no further sources are given; these two authentic books are considered enough.

[en]
Sometimes, along with the reference to Sahih al-Bukhari or Sahih Muslim, other sources of the same hadith are also given.

[en]
If a hadith is not in the two Sahihs, I have tried to give its sources in full.

[en]
References are given for all the sayings, so that scholars and students may benefit.

[en]
The years in which the righteous predecessors passed away are also mentioned, so that scholars and students may benefit.

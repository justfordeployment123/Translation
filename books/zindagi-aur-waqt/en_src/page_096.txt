The Second Saying

[en]
Son of Adam, you are only a number of days. Whenever a day passes, a part of you passes.

[hen]
The Third Saying

[en]
This world is three days. Yesterday has gone with all that was in it. Tomorrow, you may never reach it. So today is yours; do good deeds in it.

[hen]
The Fourth Saying

[en]
One day al-Hasan al-Basri, may Allah have mercy on him, said to those sitting with him: O company of old men, when the crop is ripe, what is waited for? They said: The harvest. Then he said: O company of young men, sometimes a blight strikes the crop even before it ripens.

[en]
So every old person should take this saying as a reminder that his life is about to end, since the crop has ripened, and should value good deeds. And no young person should be deceived into thinking that a long life still lies ahead, for a crop can be struck by blight even before it ripens.

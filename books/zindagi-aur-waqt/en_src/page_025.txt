The First Kind

[en]
This kind includes every hour and every moment of life that a person spends in the obedience of Allah, or in pursuing worthy goals, for example:

[en]
One, in acts of worship. Two, in meeting lawful needs. Three, in seeking knowledge. Four, in earning a lawful living. Five, in fulfilling duties and obligations. Six, in serving humanity, and so on.

[en]
Whoever spends his life in this first kind of time is successful, and will be rewarded for the time he spent.

[hen]
The Second Kind

[en]

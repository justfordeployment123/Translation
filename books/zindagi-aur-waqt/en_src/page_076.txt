Al-Mughirah ibn Shu'bah, may Allah be pleased with him, narrated that on the day Ibrahim, the son of the Prophet, peace be upon him, died, the sun was eclipsed, and the people said: The sun has been eclipsed because of the death of Ibrahim. The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said: The sun and the moon are two of the signs of Allah. They are not eclipsed for the death or the life of anyone. When you see them eclipsed, call upon Allah and pray until it becomes clear.

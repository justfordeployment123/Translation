This saying of Allah makes it clear that the one who seeks the Hereafter will be given a great deal, while the one who seeks only this world will not be given the whole world, but will be satisfied with a part of it, and in the Hereafter there will be nothing for such a person. So even the one who asks for this world will still not get all of it. To explain this matter further, see a third saying of Allah:

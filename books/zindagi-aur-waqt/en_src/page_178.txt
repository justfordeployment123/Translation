So keep yourself busy at all times in the remembrance of Allah the Exalted, for no one knows which hour will prove to be his last. How many people there are who, at the time of death, spontaneously uttered the same words they used to say most often while healthy and well. As Imam Ibn al-Qayyim, may Allah have mercy on him, said: I heard a man say at the time of his death:

[en]
This cloth for two dirhams, this cloth for two dirhams. For that is what he used to call out in his everyday life.

[en]
So we should recite the declaration La ilaha illallah often even in ordinary times, so that at the time of death Allah grants us the ability to say it. Ameen.

[en]
In the advice that Ibrahim, peace be upon him, and Ya'qub, peace be upon him, gave to their sons, there is also this sentence:

[en]
So do not die except while you are Muslims.

[en]
That is, always remain obedient to Allah, so that whenever death comes to you, you will be Allah's obedient and submissive servants.

[en]
So every Muslim man and woman should consider this hour extremely important, and keep striving and praying to Allah the Exalted for a good end in it. For this is the last card, or think of it as the last ball and the last score, and then it is now or never.

[en]
May Allah grant all of us an end upon faith. Ameen.

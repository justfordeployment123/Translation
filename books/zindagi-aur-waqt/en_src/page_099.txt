The Saying of Wuhayb ibn al-Ward al-Makki, may Allah have mercy on him (died 153 AH)

[en]
If you are able to let nothing keep you busy away from Allah, then do so.

[hen]
The Saying of Dawud at-Ta'i, may Allah have mercy on him (died 165 AH)

[en]
Dawud at-Ta'i's servant woman, his nurse, said to him: O Abu Sulayman, would you not like some bread? He said:

[en]
O nurse, in the time between chewing bread and drinking broth, fifty verses can be recited.

[hen]
The Saying of Shaqiq ibn Ibrahim al-Azdi, may Allah have mercy on him (died 194 AH)

[en]
Be prepared, so that when death comes to you, you do not ask to be sent back.

[en]
This is only possible if we have spent our time in obedience to Allah, and made the fullest use of the great blessing of life, for it will not be given again.

[hen]
The Saying of Imam Muhammad ibn Idris ash-Shafi'i, may Allah have mercy on him (died 204 AH)

[en]
I kept the company of the Sufis, and I gained nothing from them except two sayings. One was their saying: Time is a sword; if you do not cut with it, it will cut you. And the other: If you keep your soul busy with the truth, it is good for you; otherwise, it will keep you busy with falsehood.

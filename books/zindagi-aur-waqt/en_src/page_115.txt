Ata ibn Abi Rabah, may Allah have mercy on him (died 114 AH)

[en]
Ibn Jurayj, may Allah have mercy on him, said: I stayed with Ata, may Allah have mercy on him, for eighteen years. Even though he was old and weak, he would recite two hundred verses of Surah al-Baqarah while standing in prayer, and no part of his body would bend or move.

[hen]
Amr ibn Dinar, may Allah have mercy on him (died 126 AH)

[en]
Sufyan ibn Uyaynah, may Allah have mercy on him, said: Amr ibn Dinar, may Allah have mercy on him, divided the night into three parts: a third for sleep, a third for going over the hadith he had memorised, and a third for prayer.

[hen]
Malik ibn Dinar, may Allah have mercy on him (died 131 AH)

[en]
Sallam ibn Abi Muti, may Allah have mercy on him, said: One night I came to Malik ibn Dinar, may Allah have mercy on him. He was sitting at home without a lamp lit, with a piece of dry bread in his hand that he was chewing. We said to him: O Abu Yahya, is there no lamp? Is there nothing on which to put your bread? He said: Leave me as I am, for by Allah, I regret the life that has passed.

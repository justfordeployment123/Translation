Second, After the Obligatory Prayers

[en]
Abu Umamah al-Bahili, may Allah be pleased with him, narrated that it was asked: O Messenger of Allah, which supplication is most heard? He, peace be upon him, said: The supplication in the last part of the night, and after the obligatory prayers.

[hen]
Third, Between the Adhan and the Iqamah

[en]
Anas ibn Malik, may Allah be pleased with him, narrated that the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said: The supplication between the adhan and the iqamah is not rejected.

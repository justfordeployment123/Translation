Chapter Seven: The Causes of Wasting Time

[en]
In this chapter we will mention some of the things because of which a person's life is wasted, and he is deprived of success in this world and the Hereafter. But before mentioning them, we must know what success is, and what can be called success. Because people belong to different classes, each class has its own standard and measure of success.

[en]
For some people the standard is higher education, for some it is beauty, for some it is getting a good job, for some it is a good business, for some it is finding a good life partner, and for some it is gaining fame and honour.

[en]
But true success is what Allah, Lord of Might, has declared to be success, and that is success in the Hereafter: being saved from Hell and entering Paradise. For all the things we have mentioned are temporary, because they belong to this world, and everything in this world is temporary and passing, while everything in the Hereafter is everlasting and eternal. So to achieve this success, there are some things that it is extremely important to avoid; otherwise, because of their ill effects, the blessing and good of life are taken away.

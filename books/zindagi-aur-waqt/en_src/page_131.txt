[en]
As-Saltan al-Abdi, may Allah have mercy on him, said:

[en]
The passing of the day and the return of the evening have turned the young grey and worn out the old.

[en]
Whenever a night has worn out its day, after it comes a young new day.

[en]
Morning and evening we come and go for our needs, yet the needs of the living are never finished.

[en]
A man's needs die with him, and he still has a need as long as he lives.

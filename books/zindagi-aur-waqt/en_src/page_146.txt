[hen]
Amir ibn Abbas al-Hamdani, may Allah have mercy on him

[en]
He said:

[en]
This world is only a road to Paradise or to the Fire. The nights are a person's merchandise, and the days are the market.

[en]
A poet said:

[en]
A person has a day on which his life will come to an end, then death, and then he will be put into a narrow grave.

[en]
There he will meet Munkar and Nakir in questioning, who deal out punishment to whoever stammers in his answers.

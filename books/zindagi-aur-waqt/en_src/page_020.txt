Yahya ibn Mu'adh ar-Razi, may Allah have mercy on him, said:

[en]
Loss, that is, wasting time, is worse than death, because wasting time cuts a person off from the Truth, while death only cuts him off from the creation.

[en]
So we learn that life is a most precious asset, and it should be spent on purposeful and better things. A sensible person, when he receives his monthly allowance or salary, puts his important and necessary needs first, so that his money goes to purposeful things. In the same way, understanding the purpose of our life, we should first fulfil the most important duties, the obligations, on which our success and salvation in this world and the Hereafter depend.

[hen]
The Greatest Fool

[en]
There is no doubt that the greatest fool is the one who ruins his own capital. For a wise person, the moments of his life are his capital. Whoever squanders his capital and spends it on useless things is the greatest fool and the greatest loser.

[en]
Abu Yazid al-Bistami, may Allah have mercy on him, said:

[en]
Surely the night and the day, that is, time, are the capital of the believer. Their profit is Paradise, and their loss is the Fire.

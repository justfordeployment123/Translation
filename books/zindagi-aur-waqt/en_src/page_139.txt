[hen]
Ahmad ibn Asim al-Antaki, may Allah have mercy on him

[en]
He said: I met a devout, ascetic man and asked him: May Allah have mercy on you, tell me, what is the sign of the fear of Allah? He answered: Being careful. I said: What is the sign of longing? He answered: Striving to obtain what one longs for. I said: What is the sign of hope? He said: Continuing to do righteous deeds. I said to him: Then how has falling short in deeds come into us? He said: Because you have relied on Allah's forbearance and on His covering of your sins. Then the worshipper recited these verses:

[en]
If you understand what I say and take it to heart, then set out yourself towards a life of good before you are carried off to the graveyard.

[en]
Leave being busy with sins and abandon them. How long, and how far, will you keep making excuses?

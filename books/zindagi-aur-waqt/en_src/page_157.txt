So we learn that the remembrance of death is extremely beneficial for a person, and a means of gaining great good. If he forgets death, he will be nearer to evil than to good, and every kind of wrong can be expected from him. That is why, when we read the lives of our predecessors, we find this quality common to all of them: they remembered their death at all times, and this kept them from gathering much of the world and from building up worldly show.

[en]
Sulayman at-Taymi, may Allah have mercy on him, said:

[en]
Two things have cut me off from the pleasure of this world: the remembrance of death, and the remembrance of standing before Allah the Exalted.

[en]

O people, Allah is pure and accepts only what is pure.

[en]
So the worship of a whole life and the work done for the Hereafter will only be accepted if we have taken care over lawful provision in our lives. Otherwise, the unlawful will destroy the work of our lives, and no one will be given a second chance.

Bad Company

[en]
An important means of making life successful is good company. If you sit in the company of good people, those who see you will think you are good, even if you are not so good. And if you are very good but keep the company of bad people, you will be thought bad. And if you sit in the company of people who waste their time and spend their lives in useless things, you will surely waste your time too. Indeed, company even affects a person's religion, as the Messenger, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
A man is upon the religion of his close friend, so let each of you look at whom he befriends.

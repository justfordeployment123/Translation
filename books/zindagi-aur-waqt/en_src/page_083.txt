Fourth, at the Time of the Adhan and in Battle

[en]
Sahl ibn Sa'd, may Allah be pleased with him, narrated that the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said: Two supplications are not rejected, or are seldom rejected: the supplication at the time of the adhan, and in battle, when the fighters are locked together.

[hen]
Fifth, an Hour in Every Night

[en]
Jabir, may Allah be pleased with him, narrated: I heard the Prophet, peace be upon him, say: In the night there is an hour in which no Muslim servant asks Allah for any good of this world or the Hereafter but that He gives it to him, and that is in every night.

The point of evidence: what these two verses make clear is that whoever wants this world will get it, but only as much as Allah wills, and only for whom He wills. The verse contains two phrases: what We will, and for whom We intend. Whoever asks for this world and wants it, Allah will give it to him according to His own will, not his. Secondly, not everyone who asks for this world is given it; rather, Allah gives to whom He wills. And in the second verse, whoever wants the Hereafter and keeps striving for it will be given the Hereafter, provided that he is a believer. So the efforts of the idolater, the disbeliever and the hypocrite in belief are wasted and useless. We seek refuge in Allah.

Life and the Candle

[en]
In one sense, life and a burning candle are closely alike. Just as every passing moment makes the candle shorter, and it keeps getting smaller without pause, so too a person's life is like a burning candle. Every passing moment makes it shorter, taking him further from this world and closer to the Hereafter. As I understand it, the likeness between life and the candle is as follows. Please consider it.

[en]
One: a life spent in sin and disobedience is like a candle burning in the daytime.

[en]
Two: a life spent in heedlessness and amusement is like a candle burning at night while the electric lights are on.

[en]
Three: a life spent in good deeds and obedience is like a candle lit at night in the darkness, when there is no electricity.

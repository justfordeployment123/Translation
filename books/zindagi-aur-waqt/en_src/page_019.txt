So the person whom Allah keeps busy with good deeds, and who spends the precious hours of his life in His obedience, there is surely no greater good for a servant than this. But for a life to pass by in useless things is a sign of Allah's displeasure and the ill fortune of sins.

[en]
This is why Hasan al-Basri, may Allah have mercy on him, said:

[en]
Among the signs that Allah has turned away from His servant is that, to disgrace him, He keeps him busy with useless things.

[hen]
Wasting Time Is More Harmful Than Death

[en]
In one sense, wasting time is more dangerous than death. For death only takes a person away from this world and its people, while wasting time and living a useless life make a person heedless of Allah and the Hereafter, and lasting failure and loss are written for him. May Allah protect us from it.

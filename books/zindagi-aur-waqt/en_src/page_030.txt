This narration makes it clear that if a person is balanced in worship and in his dealings, fulfils all the duties and rights that are due from him, and then pays no attention to the twists and turns and fine details of worldly matters, this is praiseworthy and good. As he, peace be upon him, said to his Companions:

[en]
You know the affairs of your world better than I do.

[en]
That is, each person should keep only as much connection with the world as he cannot do without, for otherwise the world is nothing but an enjoyment of deception.

The Third Form: Alert for This World, but Heedless of the Hereafter

[en]
This form, like the first, is blameworthy and disliked. Our Lord has condemned those who become children of this world alone, whose worries, thoughts, reflection, wisdom and cleverness are all only for this world. Their minds work very well for this world, but for the Hereafter they are zero. As Allah the Exalted says:

[en]

First, in Youth

[en]
As the well-known proverb says:

[en]
Memorising in youth is like engraving on stone, and memorising in old age is like drawing a line on water.

[hen]
Second, the Time of Sahur

[en]
The time before dawn is also excellent and blessed for memorising a text. This is supported by the saying of the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him:

[en]
Indeed, in the pre-dawn meal there is blessing.

[hen]
Third, Between Maghrib and Isha

[en]
The time between Maghrib and Isha is also very good for memorising, and lessons are learned quickly at this time. In the schools of memorisation, teachers usually have students memorise their lessons at this time.

[hen]
A Benefit

[en]
Imam Burhan al-Islam az-Zarnuji, may Allah have mercy on him, says that the student should spend all of his time in seeking knowledge. Indeed, when he feels tired of studying one subject, he should busy himself with another. When Abdullah ibn Abbas, may Allah be pleased with them both, felt tired of theology, he would say to his students: Bring the collections of the poets.

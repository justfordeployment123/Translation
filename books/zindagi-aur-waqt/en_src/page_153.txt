An example of this is found in a hadith of the Prophet, peace be upon him:

[en]
Abu Hurayrah, may Allah be pleased with him, narrated that the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said: When one of you sleeps, Satan ties three knots at the back of his head, and over each knot he breathes: You have a long night, so sleep on. If he wakes up and remembers Allah, one knot is undone. If he makes ablution, the second knot is undone. And if he prays, the third knot is undone too, and he begins the morning lively and in good spirits. Otherwise, he begins the morning in a bad state, sluggish and lazy.

[en]
So we learn that because of sin, misfortune settles over a servant. Indeed, it is narrated from some of the predecessors:

[en]
Part of the punishment for an evil deed is another evil deed after it, and part of the reward for a good deed is being granted another good deed after it.

[en]

Abu Bakr ibn Abi Darim, may Allah have mercy on him, said:

[ar]

[en]
O my eyes, why do you not weep over my life? My life has scattered from my hands, and I did not even notice.

[en]
Now that I have passed sixty years and have made no preparation for the return, what excuse will I have?

Muhammad ibn A'yan, may Allah have mercy on him, relates: I saw a young man who slept very little at night. He would spend the night praying or reciting the Quran, and when the last part of the night began, he would weep and recite these verses:

[ar]
تَفَكَّرْتُ طُولَ اللَّيْلِ فِيمَا جَنَيْتُهُ، وَذَكَّرَتْ نَفْسِي كُلَّ ذَنْبٍ أَتَيْتُهُ

[en]
All night long I reflected on the wrongs I had done, and my soul remembered every sin I had committed.

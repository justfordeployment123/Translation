And in one place He said:

[ar]

[ref]
(الاعراف ۷: آیت ۲۰۵)

[en]
And do not be among the heedless.

[en]
These verses make it clear that when a person becomes heedless of the remembrance of Allah and of the Hereafter, Satan surely drags him towards evil, and he will be held to account for the time he spent in heedlessness. But heedlessness has three forms.

[en]
One, heedlessness of both this world and the Hereafter. Two, heedlessness of this world, but alertness for the Hereafter. Three, alertness for this world, but heedlessness of the Hereafter.

[hen]
The First Form: Heedless of This World and the Hereafter

[en]
This form of heedlessness is the most dangerous and the most terrible: that a person becomes heedless of both his world and his Hereafter. He has no concern for his life in this world and no awareness of the Hereafter. This includes those who spend their lives in useless things that bring no benefit in this world or the next. This is why the predecessors strongly disliked anyone who was heedless of both his world and his Hereafter. As our master Abdullah ibn Mas'ud, may Allah be pleased with him, said:

[ar]
إِنِّي لَأُبْغِضُ الرَّجُلَ أَنْ أَرَاهُ فَارِغًا لَيْسَ فِي شَيْءٍ مِنْ عَمَلِ الدُّنْيَا وَلَا عَمَلِ الْآخِرَةْ

[ref]
الزہد للامام احمد، ص: ۱۹۹

[en]
I truly dislike seeing a man idle, busy neither with any work of this world nor with any work of the Hereafter.

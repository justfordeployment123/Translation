[hen]
Dihqan the Poet, may Allah have mercy on him

[en]
Dihqan passed by a graveyard in the city of Samarra and recited these verses:

[ar]
أَلَا يَا عَسْكَرَ الْأَحْيَاءِ هَذَا عَسْكَرُ الْمَوْتَى

[en]
Listen, O army of the living, this is the army of the dead.

[ar]
أَجَابُوا الدَّعْوَةَ الصُّغْرَى وَهُمْ مُنْتَظِرُو الْكُبْرَى

[en]
They have answered the lesser call, which is death, and now they await the greater call, the Day of Resurrection.

[ar]
يَحُثُّونَ عَلَى الزَّادِ وَمَا زَادٌ سِوَى التَّقْوَى

[en]
They urge you to gather provision, and there is no provision but piety.

[ar]
يَقُولُونَ لَكُمْ جِدُّوا فَهَذَا غَايَةُ الدُّنْيَا

[en]
They are saying to you: Strive, for this is the end of the world.

This is why Imam Ibn al-Qayyim, may Allah have mercy on him, said: Wasting time is even more dangerous than death, because wasting time takes a person away from Allah and the Hereafter, while death only takes him away from this world and its people.

[en]
A person's true life is the part that was spent in the service of Allah. The moments spent in sin and disobedience are a burden upon him. Indeed, death is better than a life of disobedience, for in death there is no burden for the obedient.

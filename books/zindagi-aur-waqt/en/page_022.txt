[hen]
The One Who Wastes Time Is Ungrateful

[en]
If a person reflects, he will see that Allah's favours upon him are countless, and he can never repay them. He cannot even give thanks for a single breath. Time, the span of life, the night and the day, are all a very precious blessing, which Allah praised when He said:

[ar]
وَهُوَ الَّذِي جَعَلَ اللَّيْلَ وَالنَّهَارَ خِلْفَةً لِمَنْ أَرَادَ أَنْ يَذَّكَّرَ أَوْ أَرَادَ شُكُورَا

[ref]
(الفرقان ۲۵: آیت ۶۲)

[en]
And it is He who has made the night and the day in succession for whoever desires to remember or desires gratitude.

[en]
Since we accept that the time of our life is a very precious blessing, on which our success and failure depend, then how do we respond to this blessing, and how grateful are we to our Lord for it? Let us take a look.

[en]
Imam Ibn al-Qayyim, may Allah have mercy on him, who died in the year seven hundred and fifty-one after the Hijrah, writes in his book Madarij as-Salikin that gratitude has five basic principles. They are its foundation, and if even one of them is missing, gratitude is incomplete. These five principles are as follows.

[en]
One: the one who gives thanks should humble himself before the one who did him the favour.

[en]
Two: the one who gives thanks should love the one who did him the favour.

[en]
Three: the one who gives thanks should acknowledge the favour he received.

[en]
Four: the one who gives thanks should praise his benefactor because of that favour.

[en]
Five: the one who gives thanks should not use that favour in things his benefactor dislikes.

[ref]
مدارج السالکین، ۲/ ۶

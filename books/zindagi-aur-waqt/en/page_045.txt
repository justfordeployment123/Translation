The beginning of the surah tells us that mankind is in loss. Imam al-Baghawi, may Allah have mercy on him, explains this loss in his commentary as follows:

[ar]
أَيْ ذَهَابُ رَأْسِ مَالِ الْإِنْسَانِ فِي هَلَاكِ نَفْسِهِ وَعُمُرِهِ بِالْمَعَاصِي

[ref]
تفسیر بغوی، ۴/ ۶۷۹

[en]
That is, a person's capital is lost when his self and his lifetime are destroyed in sins.

[en]
So the one who did not value his life, and spent it in disobedience and in fulfilling his desires, is in truth at a loss.

[en]
It is narrated from one of the predecessors that he said: I understood the meaning of Surah al-Asr from a man selling ice, who was calling out:

[ar]
اِرْحَمُوا عَلَى مَنْ رَأْسُ مَالِهِ يَذُوبْ

[ref]
جامع البیان، تفسیر سورۃ العصر

[en]
People, have mercy on the one whose capital is melting away!

[en]
So life is melting away like ice. Every moment that comes takes us further from this world and closer to the Hereafter, so everyone who stays away from good deeds is moving towards loss. Explaining this further, Imam Hasan al-Basri, may Allah have mercy on him, said:

[ar]
يَا ابْنَ آدَمْ، إِنَّمَا أَنْتَ أَيَّامٌ مَجْمُوعَةْ، كُلَّمَا ذَهَبَ يَوْمٌ ذَهَبَ بَعْضُكْ

[en]
O son of Adam, you are nothing but a collection of days. Whenever a day passes, understand that a part of you has gone.

[en]
It is as if life is melting away, and with every passing day a person comes closer to death.

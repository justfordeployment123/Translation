The Seventh Hadith

[ar]
عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ: مَنْ خَافَ أَدْلَجَ وَمَنْ أَدْلَجَ بَلَغَ الْمَنْزِلْ، أَلاَ إِنَّ سِلْعَةَ اللَّهِ غَالِيَةْ، أَلاَ إِنَّ سِلْعَةَ اللَّهِ الْجَنَّةْ

[ref]
الترمذی، کتاب صفۃ القیامۃ والرقائق، باب ۱۸، رقم الحدیث: ۲۴۵۰، والسلسلۃ الصحیحۃ: ۲۳۳۵، والحاکم فی المستدرک، رقم الحدیث: ۷۸۵۱

[en]
Abu Hurayrah, may Allah be pleased with him, narrated that the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said: Whoever fears sets out early in the night, and whoever sets out early in the night reaches the destination. Beware, the merchandise of Allah is precious. Beware, the merchandise of Allah is Paradise.

[en]
This blessed hadith gives the example of a traveller: a traveller who is concerned about reaching his destination starts his journey in the first part of the night, so that he arrives on time. And the second part of the hadith gives concern for the Hereafter. So a Muslim, while living in this world, should live like a traveller who is always concerned about reaching his destination. He does not get busy with the beautiful sights and other things along the way, nor does he become attached to them; rather, he keeps his destination before his eyes.

[en]
So the person who cared about his life and valued his time is like the traveller who set out on time at night, so that he would be at his destination by the first light of dawn. And the one who did not value his time, and lived a life of amusement and purposelessness, is like the traveller who gave no thought to his destination and did not set out on time. Instead, he fell into accidents before reaching his destination, and became so tangled in the ups and downs of the world that he lost his destination altogether.

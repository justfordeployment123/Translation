Al-Ahnaf ibn Qays, may Allah have mercy on him (died 67 AH)

[en]
The servant of al-Ahnaf ibn Qays, may Allah have mercy on him, related:

[ar]
كَانَ الْأَحْنَفُ قَلَّمَا خَلَا إِلَّا دَعَا بِالْمُصْحَفْ

[ref]
الزہد للامام احمد، ص: ۲۸۶

[en]
Al-Ahnaf was hardly ever alone without calling for the Mushaf, the Quran, to read from it.

[hen]
Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz, may Allah have mercy on him (died 101 AH)

[en]
Al-Mughirah ibn Hakim said: Fatimah bint Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan, the wife of Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz, may Allah have mercy on him, told me:

[ar]
يَكُونُ فِي النَّاسِ مَنْ هُوَ أَكْثَرُ صَوْمًا وَصَلَاةً مِنْ عُمَرْ، وَمَا رَأَيْتُ أَحَدًا أَشَدَّ فَرَقًا مِنْ رَبِّهِ مِنْ عُمَرْ، كَانَ إِذَا صَلَّى الْعِشَاءَ قَعَدَ فِي الْمَسْجِدْ، ثُمَّ يَرْفَعُ يَدَيْهِ فَلَمْ يَزَلْ يَبْكِي حَتَّى يَغْلِبَهُ النَّوْمْ، ثُمَّ يَنْتَبِهُ فَلَا يَزَالُ يَدْعُو رَافِعًا يَدَيْهِ يَبْكِي حَتَّى تَغْلِبَهُ عَيْنَاهْ، يَفْعَلُ ذَلِكَ لَيْلَهُ أَجْمَعْ

[ref]
تذکرۃ الحفاظ، ۱/ ۱۲۰

[en]
There may be people who fasted and prayed more than Umar, but I never saw anyone who feared his Lord more than Umar. When he had prayed Isha, he would sit in the mosque, raise his hands and keep weeping until sleep overcame him. Then he would wake with a start, raise his hands again in supplication and keep weeping, until his eyes overcame him again. He would do this all night.

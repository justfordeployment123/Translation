Abul-Fath al-Busti, may Allah have mercy on him, said:

[ar]
يَا عَامِرًا لِخَرَابِ الدَّهْرِ مُجْتَهِدًا، تَاللَّهِ مَا لِخَرَابِ الْعُمْرِ عُمْرَانُ

[en]
O you who strive to build up what time will ruin, by Allah, there is no rebuilding a life that has been ruined.

[ar]

[en]
And O you who are greedy for the wealth you keep gathering, have you forgotten that the joy of wealth is sorrow?

[ref]

[en]
Ibn Abi Maryam, may Allah have mercy on him, said: I passed by a town that had fallen into ruin, and on the wall of one of its houses I read this verse written:

[ar]
هَذِي مَنَازِلُ أَقْوَامٍ عَهِدْتُهُمْ، فِي رَغْدِ عَيْشٍ رَغِيبٍ مَا لَهُ خَطَرٌ

[en]
These are the homes of a people I knew, living a pleasant and desirable life in which there was no danger.

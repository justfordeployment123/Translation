[hen]
The Fourth Hadith

[ar]
عَنْ أَبِي بَرْزَةَ الْأَسْلَمِيِّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ: لاَ تَزُولُ قَدَمَا عَبْدٍ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ حَتَّى يُسْأَلَ عَنْ عُمْرِهِ فِيمَا أَفْنَاهْ، وَعَنْ عِلْمِهِ فِيمَا فَعَلْ، وَعَنْ مَالِهِ مِنْ أَيْنَ اكْتَسَبَهُ وَفِيمَا أَنْفَقَهْ، وَعَنْ جِسْمِهِ فِيمَا أَبْلاَهْ

[ref]
جامع الترمذی، کتاب صفۃ القیامۃ، باب القیامۃ، رقم الحدیث: ۲۴۱۷، وصحیح الجامع الصغیر للالبانی: ۷۳۰۰، والسلسلۃ الصحیحۃ: ۹۴۶

[en]
Abu Barzah al-Aslami, may Allah be pleased with him, narrated that the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said: The feet of a servant will not move on the Day of Resurrection until he is asked about his life, how he spent it; about his knowledge, what he did with it; about his wealth, where he earned it and how he spent it; and about his body, how he wore it out.

[en]
This hadith tells us about great blessings. Let each of us now reflect for a few moments, and think calmly: what answer have I prepared for these questions? Our state is that not one of us could answer to his teacher or parents for a single day of his life, even though we can hide many words and deeds from them. Just think: how will we answer for our whole life, and before the One who said about Himself:

[ar]
إِنَّ اللَّهَ لَا يَخْفَىٰ عَلَيْهِ شَيْءٌ فِي الْأَرْضِ وَلَا فِي السَّمَاءْ

[ref]
(آل عمران ۳: آیت ۵)

[en]
Indeed, from Allah nothing is hidden in the earth nor in the heaven.

And remember that Satan quickly attacks a heedless heart. As Allah the Exalted says:

[ar]
وَمَنْ يَعْشُ عَنْ ذِكْرِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ نُقَيِّضْ لَهُ شَيْطَانًا فَهُوَ لَهُ قَرِينْ

[ref]
(الزخرف ۴۳: آیت ۳۶)

[en]
And whoever is blinded from remembrance of the Most Merciful - We appoint for him a devil, and he is to him a companion.

[en]
This blessed verse teaches us that a person should stay busy with the remembrance of Allah at all times, should not waste the moments of his life, and should avoid heedlessness.

[hen]
The Fourth Place

[en]
Allah the Exalted says:

[ar]
يَوَدُّ أَحَدُهُمْ لَوْ يُعَمَّرُ أَلْفَ سَنَةٍ وَمَا هُوَ بِمُزَحْزِحِهِ مِنَ الْعَذَابِ أَنْ يُعَمَّرَ ۗ وَاللَّهُ بَصِيرٌ بِمَا يَعْمَلُونْ

[ref]
(البقرۃ ۲: آیت ۹۶)

[en]
Every one of them wishes that he could be granted life a thousand years, but it would not remove him in the least from the punishment that he should be granted life. And Allah is Seeing of what they do.

[en]
Outwardly, this verse was revealed about the Jews, but in its general meaning, in this part of the blessed verse Allah has told us about rebellious and disobedient people, that they are very greedy to be given a longer life. But a life spent in disobedience to Allah and in sins cannot be a means of salvation. Rather, it is a burden.

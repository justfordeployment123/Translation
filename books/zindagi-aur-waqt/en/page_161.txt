[en]
If the love of other than Allah follows the love of Allah and stays within its bounds, it is praiseworthy. And if that love leaves its bounds and overpowers the love of Allah, it is blameworthy.

[en]
Other than Allah includes every creature apart from the Creator. However, love for the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, is a result of the love of Allah.

[en]
Before we go into the details of this, let us first mention the degrees of love that Imam Ibn al-Qayyim, may Allah have mercy on him, described in his book ad-Da' wad-Dawa', which are as follows.

[ref]
الداء والدواء لابن القیم، ص: ۴۲۶ تا ۴۳۸

[en]
Attachment: this is the first degree of love, in which the lover's heart becomes attached to the beloved, and the heart hangs upon his love.

[en]
Inclination: this is the second degree of love. The Arabic word for it comes from a root meaning to pour out; that is, the lover's heart leans and pours itself out completely towards the beloved.

[en]
Constant love: in this degree, the love of the beloved settles in the heart so firmly that it never leaves it at any time, and the two become inseparable.

[en]
Passion: this degree is excessive love, and in the Arabic language it is used in a blameworthy sense. That is why the word for passion has not been used for the love of Allah, nor is it right to use it for His Messenger, peace be upon him.

Sin and Disobedience

[en]
Disobedience plays a major part in wasting life. So first, an introduction to sin: what is sin? Some scholars have explained its meaning as follows:

[ar]
كُلُّ مَا عُصِيَ اللَّهُ بِهِ

[en]
Everything by which Allah the Exalted is disobeyed.

[en]
And in Sahih Muslim, an-Nawwas ibn Sam'an, may Allah be pleased with him, narrated that he asked the Prophet, peace be upon him, about righteousness and sin, and he, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
الْبِرُّ حُسْنُ الْخُلُقِ وَالإِثْمُ مَا حَاكَ فِي صَدْرِكَ وَكَرِهْتَ أَنْ يَطَّلِعَ عَلَيْهِ النَّاسْ

[ref]
صحیح مسلم، کتاب البر والصلۃ، باب تفسیر البر والاثم، رقم الحدیث: ۲۵۵۳

[en]
Righteousness is good character, and sin is what troubles your heart and what you would dislike people to find out about.

[en]
All the evil and corruption that has arisen since the beginning was caused by this disobedience, and the hardships that have befallen humanity were in truth caused by this disobedience.

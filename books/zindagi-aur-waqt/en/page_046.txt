The Tenth Place

[en]
The importance of time can also be judged from the fact that Allah has mentioned time and its various parts in many places in the Noble Quran, though in each place it is mentioned in a different way, as follows.

[hen]
As a Great Blessing

[en]
Allah the Exalted says:

[ar]
وَسَخَّرَ لَكُمُ الشَّمْسَ وَالْقَمَرَ دَائِبَيْنِ ۖ وَسَخَّرَ لَكُمُ اللَّيْلَ وَالنَّهَارْ

[ar]
وَآتَاكُمْ مِنْ كُلِّ مَا سَأَلْتُمُوهُ ۚ وَإِنْ تَعُدُّوا نِعْمَتَ اللَّهِ لَا تُحْصُوهَا

[ref]
(ابراہیم ۱۴: آیت ۳۳ تا ۳۴)

[en]
And He subjected for you the sun and the moon, continuous in orbit, and subjected for you the night and the day. And He gave you from all you asked of Him. And if you should count the favour of Allah, you could not enumerate it.

[hen]
As a Blessing that Calls for Gratitude

[en]
Allah the Exalted says:

[ar]
وَهُوَ الَّذِي جَعَلَ اللَّيْلَ وَالنَّهَارَ خِلْفَةً لِمَنْ أَرَادَ أَنْ يَذَّكَّرَ أَوْ أَرَادَ شُكُورَا

[ref]
(الفرقان ۲۵: آیت ۶۲)

[en]
And it is He who has made the night and the day in succession for whoever desires to remember or desires gratitude.

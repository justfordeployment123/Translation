Junayd al-Baghdadi, may Allah have mercy on him

[en]
He said: I heard as-Sari as-Saqati say: One day I went towards the graveyard, and I met Buhlul, who was sitting in a grave with his legs stretched out, playing with the earth. I said: You, here? He said: Yes, I am with a people who do not hurt me, and if I leave them, they do not backbite me. I said to Buhlul: Bread has become expensive. He said: I do not worry, even if a single grain costs a mithqal of gold. For our duty is to worship Allah as He has commanded us, and it is upon Allah to provide for us, as He has promised us. Then Buhlul walked away from there reciting this verse:

[ar]
يَا مَنْ تَمَتَّعَ بِالدُّنْيَا وَبَهْجَتِهَا، وَلَا تَنَامُ عَنِ اللَّذَّاتِ عَيْنَاهُ

[en]
O you who enjoy this world and its splendour, whose eyes never sleep from its pleasures.

[ar]
أَفْنَيْتَ عُمْرَكَ فِيمَا لَسْتَ تُدْرِكُهُ، تَقُولُ لِلَّهِ مَاذَا حِينَ تَلْقَاهُ

[en]
You have spent your life chasing what you will never attain. What will you say to Allah when you meet Him?

[en]
When the time of death drew near for Umayyah ibn Abi as-Salt, he fainted. When he came to, he recited these verses:

[ar]
كُلُّ عَيْشٍ وَإِنْ تَطَاوَلَ دَهْرًا، صَائِرٌ مَرَّةً إِلَى أَنْ يَزُولَا

[en]
Every life, even if it lasts for ages, must one day come to an end.

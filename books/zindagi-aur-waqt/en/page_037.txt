So Allah answered their demand with the last words of the verse. Allah the Exalted says:

[ar]
أَوَلَمْ نُعَمِّرْكُمْ مَا يَتَذَكَّرُ فِيهِ مَنْ تَذَكَّرَ وَجَاءَكُمُ النَّذِيرُ ۖ فَذُوقُوا فَمَا لِلظَّالِمِينَ مِنْ نَصِيرْ

[en]
Did We not grant you life enough for whoever would remember therein to remember, and the warner had come to you? So taste the punishment, for there is not for the wrongdoers any helper.

[en]
So no excuse will remain for them, because they were given a lifetime, they were given time, but they gained no benefit from it.

The Third Place

[en]
Allah the Exalted says:

[ar]
وَاذْكُرْ رَبَّكَ فِي نَفْسِكَ تَضَرُّعًا وَخِيفَةً وَدُونَ الْجَهْرِ مِنَ الْقَوْلِ بِالْغُدُوِّ وَالْآصَالِ وَلَا تَكُنْ مِنَ الْغَافِلِينْ

[ref]
(الاعراف ۷: آیت ۲۰۵)

[en]
And remember your Lord within yourself in humility and in fear without being apparent in speech - in the mornings and the evenings. And do not be among the heedless.

[en]
The point of evidence in this saying of Allah is the last part of the verse: and do not be among the heedless. For heedlessness is the first crime, from which other crimes are born. The one who becomes heedless in his affairs is the one who has lost the value of time from his heart and mind. If he were aware of his time, he would be careful in his worship and in his dealings.

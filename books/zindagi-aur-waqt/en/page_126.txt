اغْتَنِمْ رَكْعَتَيْنِ زُلْفَى إِلَى اللَّهِ، إِذَا كُنْتَ فَارِغًا مُسْتَرِيحَا

[en]
When you are free and at ease, take the chance to pray two units to draw near to Allah.

[ar]
وَإِذَا هَمَمْتَ بِالْقَوْلِ فِي الْبَاطِلِ، فَاجْعَلْ مَكَانَهُ تَسْبِيحَا

[en]
And when you intend to say something false, put the glorification of Allah in its place.

[en]
Mis'ar ibn Kidam, may Allah have mercy on him, said:

[ar]
نَهَارُكَ يَا مَغْرُورُ سَهْوٌ وَغَفْلَةٌ، وَلَيْلُكَ نَوْمٌ وَالرَّدَى لَكَ لَازِمُ

[en]
O deceived one, your day is forgetfulness and heedlessness, and your night is sleep, and so ruin is bound to be yours.

[ar]
وَتَشْغَلُ فِيمَا سَوْفَ تَكْرَهُ غِبَّهُ، كَذَلِكَ فِي الدُّنْيَا تَعِيشُ الْبَهَائِمُ

[en]
You busy yourself with things whose outcome you will soon hate. This is how the cattle live in this world.

[ref]
الزہد الکبیر للبیہقی، ص: ۲۲۹

[en]
That is, if you spend the whole day in heedlessness, forgetting the Hereafter, and the whole night in sleep, then ruin is certain for you. Do not busy yourself with idle play and useless things that you will later be unable to give up. Such a life is the life of animals: they eat, drink, wander about and sleep all night. But your life has a purpose.

[en]
Ibrahim ibn Adham, may Allah have mercy on him, said: Once I passed through the mountains of Syria, and on one mountain I saw a huge stone on which these Arabic words were engraved:

[ar]
كُلُّ حَيٍّ وَإِنْ بَقِيَ، فَمِنَ الْعُمْرِ يَسْتَقِي

[en]
Every living being, even if it lives on, is drinking from its own life.

[ar]
فَاعْمَلِ الْيَوْمَ وَاجْتَهِدْ، وَاحْذَرِ الْمَوْتَ يَا شَقِي

[en]
So work today and strive, and beware of death, O wretched one!

[ref]
الزہد الکبیر للبیہقی، ص: ۲۳۲

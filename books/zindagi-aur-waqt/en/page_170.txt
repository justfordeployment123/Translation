[en]
Today our surroundings are becoming something like this. Everyone's running about is only for earning money: money must come in, by whatever means it is obtained. They have forgotten that we have been commanded to earn what is lawful and to earn by lawful means, because Allah has forbidden earning by unlawful means. Allah the Exalted says:

[ar]
يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا لَا تَأْكُلُوا أَمْوَالَكُمْ بَيْنَكُمْ بِالْبَاطِلِ إِلَّا أَنْ تَكُونَ تِجَارَةً عَنْ تَرَاضٍ مِنْكُمْ ۚ وَلَا تَقْتُلُوا أَنْفُسَكُمْ ۚ إِنَّ اللَّهَ كَانَ بِكُمْ رَحِيمَا

[ref]
(النساء ۴: آیت ۲۹)

[en]
O you who have believed, do not consume one another's wealth unjustly but only in lawful business by mutual consent. And do not kill yourselves or one another. Indeed, Allah is to you ever Merciful.

[en]
In this blessed verse the word used is al-batil, which means unlawful ways; that is, consuming wealth in unlawful ways has been forbidden. So every unlawful and wrongful way is included in it, such as stealing, seizing someone's property, cheating and deceiving in buying and selling, robbery, betraying a trust, dealing in interest, gambling, and trading in all kinds of forbidden things.

[en]
So, O traveller to the Hereafter, if you want to make your life successful, and make the work you do in this world of deeds useful and fruitful in the Hereafter, then earn what is lawful and eat what is lawful.

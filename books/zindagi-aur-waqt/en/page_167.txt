[en]
Woe to every scorner and mocker Who collects wealth and continuously counts it. He thinks that his wealth will make him immortal.

[hen]
Love of Fame

[en]
To forget the true purpose of one's life for the sake of false fame and temporary position in this world, and to spend all one's abilities and energies on that fame, with no care for the prayers, no concern for fasting, no distinction between lawful and unlawful, no regard for truth and lies, but to take making a name for oneself as the only purpose of life, this too is wrong and unlawful. As Allah the Exalted says:

[ar]
تِلْكَ الدَّارُ الْآخِرَةُ نَجْعَلُهَا لِلَّذِينَ لَا يُرِيدُونَ عُلُوًّا فِي الْأَرْضِ وَلَا فَسَادًا ۚ وَالْعَاقِبَةُ لِلْمُتَّقِينْ

[ref]
(القصص ۲۸: آیت ۸۳)

[en]
That home of the Hereafter We assign to those who do not desire exaltedness upon the earth or corruption. And the best outcome is for the righteous.

[en]
So, apart from the love of Allah and His Messenger, whatever love takes you away from Allah and makes you heedless of the Hereafter, sacrifice it for the love of Allah. In this lies good.

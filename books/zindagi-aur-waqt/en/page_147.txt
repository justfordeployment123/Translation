A poet said:

[ar]
أَشَابَ الصَّغِيرَ وَأَفْنَى الْكَبِيرَ، كَرُّ الْغَدَاةِ وَمَرُّ الْعَشِيِّ

[en]
The return of the mornings and the passing of the evenings have turned the young grey and worn out the old.

[en]
Whenever a night has worn out its day, after it comes a young new day.

[hen]
The First Form

[en]
This form includes every part and period of life spent in disobedience, sin and evil. Such a person is at a loss. He is ruining not only his life in this world, but also his life in the Hereafter, which is eternal and everlasting, and he will deserve sin and punishment for the time he spent this way.

[hen]
The Second Form

[en]
This form of time includes those who are living a heedless and useless life. Heedlessness is the first step by which a person climbs towards sin and evil. The first thing Satan does is make a person heedless. As soon as the heart becomes heedless of Allah's remembrance, of remembering and reflecting on Him, Satan at once spreads his net of whispers.

[en]

Part Three: Deeds of the Followers of the Successors and Those After Them, may Allah have mercy on them

[hen]
Sulayman ibn Tarkhan at-Taymi, may Allah have mercy on him (died 143 AH)

[ar]
قَالَ حَمَّادُ بْنُ سَلَمَةْ: مَا أَتَيْنَا سُلَيْمَانَ فِي سَاعَةٍ يُطَاعُ اللَّهُ عَزَّ وَجَلَّ فِيهَا إِلَّا وَجَدْنَاهُ مُطِيعَا، فَإِنْ كَانَ فِي سَاعَةِ صَلَاةٍ وَجَدْنَاهُ مُصَلِّيَا، فَإِنْ لَمْ تَكُنْ سَاعَةُ صَلَاةٍ وَجَدْنَاهُ إِمَّا مُتَوَضِّئًا، أَوْ عَائِدًا مَرِيضَا، أَوْ مُتَّبِعًا لِجَنَازَةْ، أَوْ قَاعِدًا يُسَبِّحُ فِي الْمَسْجِدْ، قَالَ: فَكُنَّا نَرَى أَنَّهُ لَا يُحْسِنُ أَنْ يَعْصِيَ اللَّهَ عَزَّ وَجَلّ

[ref]
صفۃ الصفوۃ، ۳/ ۲۹۷

[en]
Hammad ibn Salamah, may Allah have mercy on him, said: Whenever we came to Sulayman at-Taymi, may Allah have mercy on him, at an hour in which Allah could be obeyed, we found him obeying. If it was a time for prayer, we found him praying. If it was not a time for prayer, we found him making ablution, or visiting the sick, or following a funeral, or sitting in the mosque glorifying Allah. Hammad said: We saw that he could not bear that Allah be disobeyed.

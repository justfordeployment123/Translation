Chapter Six: The Importance of Time and Arabic Poetry

[en]
Yahya ibn Hubayrah al-Wazir said:

[ar]
وَالْوَقْتُ أَنْفَسُ مَا عُنِيتَ بِحِفْظِهِ، وَأَرَاهُ أَسْهَلَ مَا عَلَيْكَ يَضِيعُ

[en]
Time is the most precious thing you are charged with guarding, yet I see it is the easiest thing for you to waste.

[en]
Yazid ar-Raqashi used to recite this verse as a proverb:

[ar]
إِنَّا لَنَفْرَحُ بِالْأَيَّامِ نَقْطَعُهَا، وَكُلُّ يَوْمٍ مَضَى يُدْنِي مِنَ الْأَجَلِ

[en]
We rejoice at the days as we pass through them, while every day that passes brings us closer to death.

[ar]
فَاعْمَلْ لِنَفْسِكَ قَبْلَ الْمَوْتِ مُجْتَهِدًا، فَإِنَّمَا الرِّبْحُ وَالْخُسْرَانُ فِي الْعَمَلِ

[en]
So work hard for your soul before death, for profit and loss depend only on deeds.

[ref]
صفۃ الصفوۃ، ۳/ ۲۹۰

[en]
Ali ibn Abi Talib, may Allah be pleased with him, said:

[ar]
إِنَّمَا الدُّنْيَا فَنَاءٌ، لَيْسَ لِلدُّنْيَا ثُبُوتُ

[en]
This world is only passing away; this world has no permanence.

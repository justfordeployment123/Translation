Longing: the fifth degree of love is longing, which is really the journey of the heart that sets out towards the beloved at full speed.

[en]
As in the supplication of the Prophet, peace be upon him, in the hadith:

[ar]
وَأَسْأَلُكَ لَذَّةَ النَّظَرِ إِلَى وَجْهِكَ وَالشَّوْقَ إِلَى لِقَائِكْ

[ref]
سنن النسائی، رقم الحدیث: ۱۳۰۶، مسند احمد، صحیح ابن حبان، رقم الحدیث: ۱۹۷۱

[en]
I ask You for the delight of looking at Your Face, and for the longing to meet You.

[en]
Enslavement: this is the last degree of love, in which the lover sees himself as lowly and insignificant before the beloved and sees his own self as nothing. That is why this kind is used in the sense of worship. When the Arabs saw a person who had reached the point of worship in his love for someone, they would say: Love has enslaved him, that is, made him a worshipper of love.

[en]
The reality of devotion and worship is also this: that the lover shows the utmost humility and lowliness before his beloved. That is why the highest and most honoured of all the stations and states of a servant is the station of servitude. And that is why Allah, Glorified and Exalted is He, described His beloved, peace be upon him, in many places in the Noble Quran with the highest quality, that of servant. As Allah the Exalted says:

[ar]
وَإِنْ كُنْتُمْ فِي رَيْبٍ مِمَّا نَزَّلْنَا عَلَىٰ عَبْدِنَا فَأْتُوا بِسُورَةٍ مِنْ مِثْلِهْ

[ref]

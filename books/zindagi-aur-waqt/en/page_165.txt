When the love of the things mentioned in this verse becomes a desire of the self, and in pursuing them a person begins to waste the precious moments of his life and forgets the commands of Allah, then this is what Allah the Exalted says about it:

[ar]
أَفَرَأَيْتَ مَنِ اتَّخَذَ إِلَٰهَهُ هَوَاهُ وَأَضَلَّهُ اللَّهُ عَلَىٰ عِلْمٍ وَخَتَمَ عَلَىٰ سَمْعِهِ وَقَلْبِهِ وَجَعَلَ عَلَىٰ بَصَرِهِ غِشَاوَةً فَمَنْ يَهْدِيهِ مِنْ بَعْدِ اللَّهِ ۚ أَفَلَا تَذَكَّرُونْ

[ref]
(الجاثیۃ ۴۵: آیت ۲۳)

[en]
Have you seen he who has taken as his god his own desire, and Allah has sent him astray due to knowledge and has set a seal upon his hearing and his heart and put over his vision a veil? So who will guide him after Allah? Then will you not be reminded?

How Is Love Ruin?

[en]
A trial that is spreading very fast in our society, and is a great sore that destroys society and indeed whole nations, is relationships with unrelated women who are not lawful to a man, and wasting one's days and nights in their love. Such people ruin not only their lives but also their Hereafter. Indeed, it may be said that this is a form of punishment for them in this world: they keep burning in the fire of their desires and unlawful love, and because of their sins Allah the Exalted has taken His love and remembrance out of their hearts and put the love of another in its place. Whether they are at a happy gathering or a gathering of mourning, travelling or at home, in the house or in the shop, in short, at all times they are thinking of and remembering someone other than Allah. Even at the time of worship, their attention is on that other person, which is a continuous sin. Through the ill effect of this sin, the peace of life is destroyed. Indeed, gaining that other person becomes the very purpose of life, and if that purpose does not seem to be fulfilled, how many unfortunate people there are who end the life that Allah gave them as a blessing, and destroy their Hereafter along with their world.

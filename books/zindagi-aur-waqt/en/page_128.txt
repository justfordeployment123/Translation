Abul-Hasan al-Mada'ini, may Allah have mercy on him, said that one day Sulayman ibn Abd al-Malik put on very fine clothes, then looked at himself in the mirror and, admiring himself, said: By Allah, I am a young king. One of his slave girls heard him. A little later the same girl came to help him make ablution, and as she poured water over his hands she recited these verses:

[ar]
أَنْتَ نِعْمَ الْمَتَاعُ لَوْ كُنْتَ تَبْقَى، غَيْرَ أَنْ لَا بَقَاءَ لِلْإِنْسَانِ

[en]
You would be the finest of possessions if you were to last, but there is no lasting for any human being in this world.

[ar]
أَنْتَ خِلْوٌ مِنَ الْعُيُوبِ وَمِمَّا، يَكْرَهُ النَّاسُ غَيْرَ أَنَّكَ فَانِ

[en]
You are free of faults and of all that people dislike, except that you too will pass away.

[ref]
الزہد الکبیر للبیہقی، ص: ۲۳۳

[en]
That is, however good and handsome a person may become, this one fault is enough for him: he will pass away, he will decline, and he will fall prey to death. Whereas our Lord is a King who is free of all these faults.

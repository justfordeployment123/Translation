As Shaqiq ibn Ibrahim al-Azdi, may Allah have mercy on him, said:

[ar]
اسْتَعِدَّ إِذَا جَاءَكَ الْمَوْتُ لَا تَسْأَلِ الرَّجْعَةْ

[ref]
الزہد الکبیر للبیہقی، ص: ۲۱۱

[en]
Be prepared, so that when death comes to you, you do not ask to be sent back.

[en]
So a person should always be so prepared for death that when the message of death comes, he does not say these words:

[ar]
رَبِّ لَوْلَا أَخَّرْتَنِي إِلَىٰ أَجَلٍ قَرِيبٍ فَأَصَّدَّقَ وَأَكُنْ مِنَ الصَّالِحِينْ

[ref]
(المنافقون ۶۳: آیت ۱۰)

[en]
My Lord, if only You would delay me for a brief term so I would give charity and be among the righteous.

[en]
Rather, let him have a longing to meet his Lord: the Lord whom I worshipped all my life, the time has come today to meet Him, just as a lover longs to meet his beloved.

[en]
Imam Abu Hazim Salamah ibn Dinar, may Allah have mercy on him, was asked: How will the arrival before Allah be? He said:

[ar]
أَمَّا الْمُطِيعُ فَكَقُدُومِ الْغَائِبِ عَلَى أَهْلِهِ الْمُشْتَاقِينَ إِلَيْهْ، وَأَمَّا الْعَاصِي فَكَقُدُومِ الْعَبْدِ الْآبِقِ عَلَى سَيِّدِهِ الْغَضْبَانْ

[ref]
این نحن من ہؤلاء، ۳/ ۲۲۰

[en]
The one who obeys Allah will be like a traveller returning to his family, who long to meet him. And the disobedient sinner will return like a runaway slave brought back to his angry master. And Allah is the one whose help is sought.

Abu Sa'd, may Allah have mercy on him, said:

[ar]

[en]
Indeed, hope for Allah's pardon for your slips, and hasten to good deeds before the chance escapes you.

[en]

[hen]
A Note

[en]
Procrastination is a spiritual illness in which a person neglects his good deeds and keeps telling himself that he will do them soon, for example saying: soon I will pray, or soon I will start this deed. This is a deception of Satan for the son of Adam.

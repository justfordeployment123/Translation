And especially our young generation, through the misuse of these new inventions, is losing Islamic manners and culture. Shame and modesty are disappearing, and outsiders are rapidly bringing their culture into our society, spreading in our Islamic environment a way of life in which even sacred relationships have no regard: no sanctity for mother, sister, daughter or daughter-in-law. A woman's helplessness is made the target of lust, and unfair advantage is taken of the poverty of the poor. The wish to do good to a Muslim brother is disappearing, compassion has gone, and respect for elders and kindness to the young have ended. Everywhere it is every man for himself, each one looking only after his own stomach. If this is progress, then we have no need of such progress. Through such progress nations are not made successful but destroyed.

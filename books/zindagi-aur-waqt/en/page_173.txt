[en]
And consider the advice of the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, on this. He said:

[ar]
لاَ تُصَاحِبْ إِلاَّ مُؤْمِنًا وَلاَ يَأْكُلْ طَعَامَكَ إِلاَّ تَقِيّ

[en]
Keep company only with a believer, and let only a God-fearing person eat your food.

[ref]
سنن ابی داؤد، رقم الحدیث: ۴۸۳۲، جامع الترمذی، رقم الحدیث: ۲۳۹۵، مسند احمد، رقم الحدیث: ۱۱۳۳۷

[en]
And in a narration of Ibn Abbas, may Allah be pleased with them both, he, peace be upon him, was asked: Who are the best of our friends? He described three great signs of the best friend:

[ar]
مَنْ ذَكَّرَكُمْ بِاللَّهِ رُؤْيَتُهُ، وَزَادَ فِي عِلْمِكُمْ مَنْطِقُهُ، وَذَكَّرَكُمْ بِالْآخِرَةِ عَمَلُهْ

[ref]
مسند ابی یعلیٰ، رقم الحدیث: ۲۴۷۳، اسنادہ لین

[en]
The one whose sight reminds you of Allah, whose speech increases your knowledge, and whose deeds remind you of the Hereafter.

[en]
Today, one great reason for our lives being useless and our Hereafter being wasted is that the standard of friendship has changed. For today our standard is money, or our standard is high position, or our standard is good looks, or our standard is coming from a big family, or our standard is a smooth tongue and bad character.

[en]
Whereas we should sit in the company of a person through whom both our world and our Hereafter are set right. Indeed, the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, described the benefit of good company to the extent that whoever sits with the righteous and those who remember Allah, even because of some need of his own, will not be deprived of the mercy of Allah the Exalted.

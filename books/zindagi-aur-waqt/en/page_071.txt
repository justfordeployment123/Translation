Just Think!

[en]
This is a human necessity which cannot be avoided, and which every person faces. If even for the time spent in this, one should ask Allah for forgiveness, then the time that passed in sin, in idle play or in heedlessness is far more deserving that we seek pardon and forgiveness from our Lord for it. So this narration is a clear proof that time should never be considered worthless at any cost, and that we must try as hard as we can to spend it in obedience.

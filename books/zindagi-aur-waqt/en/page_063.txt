[en]
A man came to Abu Dharr, may Allah be pleased with him, while he was at home. During the conversation he asked him: Sir, where are your belongings? He replied: We have another house to which we are going. The man said: As long as you are here, you should have some belongings. He said: The owner of this house will not let us stay here for long.

[ref]
جامع العلوم والحکم، ص: ۴۶۰

[en]
Such was the state of those who were the best of this Ummah. May Allah grant us the same concern. Ameen.

[en]
Abdullah ibn Umar, may Allah be pleased with them both, narrated that the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, took hold of my shoulder and said: Be in this world as if you were a stranger, or one passing along a road.

[en]
In this saying of the Prophet, peace be upon him, two qualities are described: the first is that of a stranger far from home, and the second is that of one passing along a road.

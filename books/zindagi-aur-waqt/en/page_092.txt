The Saying of Abdullah ibn Qays, Abu Musa al-Ash'ari, may Allah be pleased with him (died 44 or 52 AH)

[en]
In the last days of his life, Abu Musa al-Ash'ari, may Allah be pleased with him, began to worship even more. He was told: Do not strain yourself now, but be gentle with yourself. He said:

[ar]
إِنَّ الْخَيْلَ إِذَا أُرْسِلَتْ فَقَارَبَتْ رَأْسَ مَجْرَاهَا أَخْرَجَتْ جَمِيعَ مَا عِنْدَهَا، وَالَّذِي بَقِيَ مِنْ أَجَلِي أَقَلُّ مِنْ ذَلِكْ

[ref]
این نحن من ہؤلاء، ۲/ ۴۰

[en]
When horses are released for a race and draw near the end of their course, they pour out all their strength. And what remains of my life is even less than that.

[hen]
The Saying of Abd ar-Rahman ibn Sakhr, Abu Hurayrah ad-Dawsi, may Allah be pleased with him (died 57 AH)

[en]
Maymun ibn Maysarah, may Allah have mercy on him, narrated that twice a day, at its beginning and its end, Abu Hurayrah, may Allah be pleased with him, would call out loudly:

[ar]
ذَهَبَ اللَّيْلُ وَجَاءَ النَّهَارْ، وَعُرِضَ آلُ فِرْعَوْنَ عَلَى النَّارْ، فَلَا يَسْمَعُهُ أَحَدٌ إِلَّا اسْتَعَاذَ بِاللَّهِ مِنَ النَّارْ

[ref]
تاریخ ابن عساکر، ۱۹/ ۱۲۲، سیر اعلام النبلاء

[en]
The night has gone and the day has come, and the people of Pharaoh have been exposed to the Fire. Whoever heard this call of Abu Hurayrah, may Allah be pleased with him, would seek refuge with Allah from Hell.

[hen]
The Saying of Abu Muslim al-Khawlani, may Allah have mercy on him (died 62 AH)

[en]
Abdullah ibn Thuwab, Abu Muslim al-Khawlani, may Allah have mercy on him, said of himself:

[ar]
لَوْ رَأَيْتُ الْجَنَّةَ عِيَانًا مَا كَانَ عِنْدِي مُسْتَزَادْ، وَلَوْ رَأَيْتُ النَّارَ عِيَانًا مَا كَانَ عِنْدِي مُسْتَزَادْ

[ref]
صفۃ الصفوۃ، ۴/ ۲۱۳

[en]
If I were to see Paradise with my own eyes, I would have no strength for more deeds than I do now. And if I were to see the Fire with my own eyes, I would still have no room for more deeds.

[en]
That is, he was so occupied with worship and acts of piety that there was no room left for more deeds, and he worshipped to the utmost degree.

In his book az-Zuhd, Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal, may Allah have mercy on him, quotes the words of Imam Mansur, may Allah have mercy on him, about this phrase:

[ar]
وَلَا تَنْسَ نَصِيبَكَ مِنَ الدُّنْيَا

[en]
He said:

[ar]
لَيْسَ هُوَ عَرَضٌ مِنْ عَرَضِ الدُّنْيَا، وَلَٰكِنْ نَصِيبُكَ عُمُرُكَ أَنْ تُقَدِّمَ فِيهِ لِآخِرَتِكْ

[ref]
الزہد للامام احمد، ص: ۲۱۴

[en]
It does not mean any of the goods of this world. Rather, your share is your lifetime, in which you send ahead deeds, provisions, for your Hereafter.

The fifth harm: sins bring humiliation upon a person.

[en]
The sixth harm: because of sins, even a person's admirers and supporters begin to blame him.

[en]
The seventh harm: because of sins, the love of that person is taken out of the hearts of the creation.

[en]
The eighth harm: sins make hearts rusty, until Allah the Exalted seals the hearts.

[en]
The ninth harm: small sins lead to great sins, and great sins push a person towards disbelief.

[en]
The tenth harm: because of sins, a person is deprived of the blessing of a good end.

[en]
The eleventh harm: because of sins, a person feels suffocated in his life, his affairs are scattered, and he remains entangled in fulfilling his worldly aims until the message of death arrives.

Forgetting Death

[en]
Forgetting death plays a large part in making life useless and in wasting time. The person who remembers that he must die, that death will come to him, will be concerned about his time and will do his utmost to make his life useful. For the remembrance of death urges a person towards good and makes him ready to avoid useless things.

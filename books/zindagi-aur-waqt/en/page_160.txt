[en]
O traveller to the Hereafter, just think and take account of yourself: will your return be like that of the traveller returning home, with a joy that cannot be measured, or like that of the runaway slave being brought to his master, with clouds of fear hanging over him, breathless, his heart pounding? May Allah help us. Ameen.

[en]
So remember your death, be concerned about your life, and try to spend your moments in obedience to Allah.

[en]
Another great benefit of remembering death is that a person's heart does not fall prey to spiritual illnesses or become attached to evil desires, as the hadith of Abu Hurayrah, may Allah be pleased with him, mentioned earlier shows. In further support of this, consider the saying of Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz, may Allah have mercy on him:

[ar]
لَوْ فَارَقَ ذِكْرُ الْمَوْتِ قَلْبِي سَاعَةً لَفَسَدْ

[en]
If the remembrance of death left my heart for a single hour, my heart would be corrupted.

[en]
So forgetting death plays a major part in wasting life.

Love of Other Than Allah

[en]
One of the things that ruins life is when the love of other than Allah takes over: loving someone else more than Allah and His Messenger, peace be upon him, with a love so excessive that it blinds the person.

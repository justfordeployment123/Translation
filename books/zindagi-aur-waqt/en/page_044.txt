So whoever knows that he is a servant of Allah and will return to Him should also know that he will have to stand before Allah. And whoever knows that he will stand before his Lord should also know that he will be questioned. And whoever knows that he will be questioned should prepare an answer.

[en]
The man said: Sir, then what is the way out? Fudayl ibn Iyad, may Allah have mercy on him, said: The answer is simple. The man asked: How so?

[en]
He, may Allah have mercy on him, said: Earn good deeds in the life that remains, and Allah will forgive the life that has passed. But if you keep sinning in the life that remains, you will be taken to account for both the life that has passed and the life that remains.

[hen]
Summary of Some Forbidden Practices Found among Farmers

[en]
On the way, the teacher turned to Ammar.

[en]
Teacher: Ammar! Thanks be to Allah, today we have given advice and admonition to quite a few people. In particular, we have tried to bring our farmer brothers back to the right path from the failings they had fallen into. Can you tell me which things we made people aware of today?

[en]
Ammar: With the help of Allah the Exalted, today we made clear the ruling on the following matters:

[en]
First: Taking part of the road into one's own land.

[en]
Second: Not giving daughters their share.

[en]
Third: Growing cannabis, opium, hashish and the like.

[en]
Fourth: Selling grapes to a factory that makes alcohol.

[en]
Fifth: Hiring out a bull for mating.

[en]
Sixth: Seizing the rights of workers.

[en]
Seventh: An account of tafadul, an important kind of interest found among farmers.

[en]
Teacher! Today you have explained that all these things are forbidden.

[en]
Atiyyah stopped the car in front of the teacher's house. The teacher got out of the car, thanking Atiyyah for his many kindnesses and his hospitality, and made Atiyyah promise to drop Ammar at his house before going back. Ammar fixed a time with the teacher for ten o'clock the next day, and the car set off towards Ammar's house.

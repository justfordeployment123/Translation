This taxi of yours is a blessing from Allah the Exalted. It is not permissible for you to put this blessing to use in deeds of disobedience and opposition to Allah the Exalted. If this blessing is taken away from you, you will go on blaming yourself all your life, but at that time blaming yourself will be of no benefit to you.

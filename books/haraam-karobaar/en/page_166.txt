Chapter Twenty: Putting Bad Fruit at the Bottom of the Basket and Good Fruit on Top

[en]
Ammar: Teacher, I noticed one more thing, but you did not warn them about it.

[en]
Teacher: What is that?

[en]
Ammar: I saw that when these people pack the fruit, they put the poor-quality fruit at the bottom of the basket and the good fruit on top.

[en]
Teacher: That is, putting bad, small and poor-quality fruit at the bottom of the basket and good, large and ripe fruit on top, so that the buyer, seeing only the top, will assume that all the fruit is like that. This too is a form of adulteration and deception, whereas the better way is to pack the bad and the good, the small and the large fruit separately, so that the buyer is spared loss and does not fall victim to any deception.

[en]
Some traders think that in this way all the fruit sells at once at a single price and that it is a profitable deal, but this thinking is quite wrong, in that Muslims are deceived by it. In this connection a friend of mine told me about something that happened. He says: On our land there were plenty of vegetables, fruit and so on.

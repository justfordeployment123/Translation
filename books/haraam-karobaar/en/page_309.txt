[en]
Servants of Allah! Know that pulling out, or having pulled out, the hair of the face, the eyebrows, the cheeks, the forehead and so on is impermissible and forbidden. The payment for this work counts as forbidden earnings, because the noble Prophet, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
وَإِنَّ اللَّهَ إِذَا حَرَّمَ شَيْئًا حَرَّمَ ثَمَنَهْ

[ref]
مسند أحمد [293/1] صحيح الجامع، رقم الحديث [5107]

[en]
“When Allah the Exalted makes something forbidden, He also makes its price forbidden.”

[en]
Many scholars have given fatwas that this act is forbidden.

[en]
The great scholar Ibn Baz, may Allah have mercy on him, says:

[en]
“It is not permissible to remove the hair of the eyebrows or to have it shortened, because the noble Prophet, peace be upon him, cursed the women who pluck out facial hair and those who have it plucked out.”

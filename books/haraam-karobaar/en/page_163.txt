The blessed Prophet, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
لِكُلِّ غَادِرٍ لِوَاءٌ عِنْدَ اسْتِهِ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةْ، يُرْفَعُ لَهُ بِقَدْرِ غَدْرِهْ

[ref]
صحيح مسلم، رقم الحديث [1738]

[en]
“At the rear of every deceiver a banner will be planted, in proportion to his deceit.”

[en]
What humiliation on the Day of Resurrection could be greater than this? Just think: a man trusts you and buys goods from you, and you are deceiving him?

[en]
The same is true of those who sell watermelons or melons. They too put grass at the bottom and the sides of a truck or trailer and place the watermelons on it, and when selling they say that this truck is full of watermelons. Remember! Using tricks like this is impermissible and forbidden, because it takes the blessing out of one's wealth.

[en]
Brother Mustafa! Fear Allah and remember that this is forbidden, because it takes the blessing out of one's wealth. Brother Mustafa! Fear Allah and give up this deception and this ugly practice. Besides being adulteration, it also makes the goods defective, because when you sell the fruit without saying that there is grass in it, it will be a sale of defective goods. The noble Prophet, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
الْبَيِّعَانِ بِالْخِيَارِ مَا لَمْ يَتَفَرَّقَا، فَإِنْ صَدَقَا وَبَيَّنَا بُورِكَ لَهُمَا فِي بَيْعِهِمَا، وَإِنْ كَتَمَا وَكَذَبَا مُحِقَتْ بَرَكَةُ بَيْعِهِمَا

[ref]
صحيح البخاري، رقم الحديث [2082] صحيح مسلم، رقم الحديث [1532]

[en]
“The two parties to a sale have the option (to cancel or change it) as long as they have not parted. If they both tell the truth and make (the defects and so on) clear, blessing is put into their sale; and if they hide (the defects and so on) and lie, the blessing is taken out of their sale.”

[en]
“The Holy Spirit (Jibril) revealed to me that no soul will die until it has completed the days of its life and its provision. So fear Allah and seek provision in a good (lawful) way, and let nothing (no whispering) drive you to earn provision by disobeying Allah, for whatever is with Allah is attained only through obeying Him.”

[en]
Ammar: Teacher! Please give a most comprehensive piece of advice on how blameworthy and forbidden smoking is. Perhaps Allah the Exalted will guide some shopkeeper through it, and he will repent of this filthy trade from now on.

[en]
Teacher: Yes, certainly. My shopkeeper brother, I will give you some advice. Listen to it carefully; the best people are those who hear the truth and follow it.

[en]
You already know that smoking has become very common. It has become a habit of our friends and acquaintances and a kind of food for them, and day by day its dangers are growing too.

Fourth: Breastfeeding: After giving birth, a woman has to breastfeed her newborn continuously for two years; that is, for two years the newborn's only source of food is the mother. During this time there are many other needs of the child, and duties towards it, that only a mother can fulfil.

[en]
Besides this, looking after the household affairs, and the work of the husband and the other members of the household, are the woman's responsibility; that is, this time too is very busy for a woman.

[en]
Fifth: Physical make-up: Because of menstruation, postnatal bleeding, pregnancy, breastfeeding and so on, a woman's body does not have the strength that a man has. The summary of the whole discussion is that the spheres of work of women and men are as different as earth and sky. There is a difference between the aims in life of the two. A woman's responsibilities are completely different from a man's responsibilities. In such a situation, if a woman is forced to do men's work, it will in no way be justice.

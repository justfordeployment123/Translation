Teacher: Imam Malik ibn Dinar, may Allah have mercy on him, said: I went to my neighbour, and at that time he was dying. He was saying: Two mountains of fire... Oh! Two mountains of fire! I said: What is this rambling talk of yours? He said: Abu Yahya! I had two measures. With one I would measure what I took, and with the other I would measure out what I gave to others. Malik said: I struck one measure against the other and broke them both. He said: Abu Yahya! When you broke them, the pain in my bones grew even worse. Then the man died of that same illness.

That is, measure in full without giving less, and do not be among those who give short measure and weight, and weigh with an accurate balance.

All these texts show that giving short measure and weight is forbidden, and they tell us that it is a very evil deed.

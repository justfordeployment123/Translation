Some time later, the boy came to the school again for some purpose and met that teacher. The boy said: Teacher, say Amin. The teacher said Amin. The boy kept making the teacher say Amin again and again. After that the boy said: O Allah! Just as this teacher's greed for money grieved my mother and deprived me of education, O Allah, deprive him of the fragrance of Paradise. Having made this supplication against him, the boy went back in tears. Just think! What must that poor boy and his poor mother have gone through? How much grief, sorrow and pain both of them must have felt?!

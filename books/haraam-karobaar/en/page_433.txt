Chapter Sixty-Seven: The Question of Abortion Without an Excuse Recognised by the Shariah

[en]
Sa'd: Teacher! This friend of mine, Salim, has come here with his wife. His wife is five months pregnant, and now she wants to end the pregnancy, because she does not want any more children. But having heard what you said, Salim has begun to doubt whether abortion is right or not. He wants some guidance from you about it.

[en]
Teacher: (Turning to Salim) Salim, how are you?

[en]
Salim: Praise be to Allah! All is well.

[en]
Teacher: May Allah the Exalted keep you safe from every evil. Abortion is permissible only in two cases:

[en]
First: After the soul has entered the child, abortion is forbidden by unanimous agreement, because it is killing a soul without right, and killing a soul without right is forbidden according to the Book of Allah, the Sunnah of the Messenger, peace be upon him, and the consensus of the Muslims. However, if the woman's life is in danger, and it becomes clear that if the pregnancy is not ended the woman may die, then in such a case ending the pregnancy is permitted.

[en]
Second: If the soul has not yet entered the child, then in such a case the scholars differ about abortion. Some people of knowledge hold that in a case of necessity such a pregnancy may be ended; for example, if the woman is ill and her illness may grow worse because of the pregnancy, then there is a concession allowing the pregnancy to be ended.

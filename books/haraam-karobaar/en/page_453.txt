“Would you not like them to be equal in goodness?”

[en]
My brother! You should be fair and just among your children in gifts, donations and the like. You should not deprive anyone of his right in the inheritance, and you should divide among your children accordingly. You should fear that leaning towards one side may take you to Hell. It also often happens that because of this injustice the heirs become each other's enemies and go to court for their rights, and as a result both sides keep wasting their money. So it does not befit a father to make any kind of distinction or discrimination among his children except in two cases.

[en]
Husain: What are those two cases?

[en]
Teacher: First: Illness. Second: With the permission of the other heirs.

[en]
Husain: So what is the solution now?

[en]
Teacher: Repent to Allah at once and give up this act.

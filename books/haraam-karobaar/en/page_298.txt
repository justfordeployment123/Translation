Abu Jum'a: Teacher, in this matter there is also another way, similar to the two ways above.

[en]
Teacher: What way is that?

[en]
Abu Jum'a: It is that I give Bahnasi one hundred kilos of Super rice and take exactly one hundred kilos of Kernel rice in return, but along with it I give him two thousand rupees in cash, because Kernel rice is more expensive?

[en]
Teacher: This form is also impermissible, because it involves tafadul; that is, the goods of the one kind being traded are equal for equal, but two thousand rupees are being given in addition, and that is interest.

[en]
Abu Jum'a: Bahnasi! We should follow the Book and the Sunnah and accept the truth, so let us act on the way of two separate deals, so that we do not go against the Shariah.

[en]
Teacher: May Allah the Exalted bless you.

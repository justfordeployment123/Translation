In today's circumstances it is not a praiseworthy act for a woman to go out of the house to take a job; it is better for her to stay at home. Besides this, all men and women should reflect on this saying of Allah the Exalted:

[ar]
وَقَرْنَ فِي بُيُوتِكُنّ

[ref]
[الأحزاب: 33]

[en]
And abide in your houses.

[en]
This command is for all women. The woman who is closest to her Lord is the one who stays at home, especially when, staying at home, she worships Allah and obeys her husband.

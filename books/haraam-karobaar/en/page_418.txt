Chapter Sixty-Four: Misusing a Government or Company Car

[en]
Ammar: Teacher! A close friend of mine is a driver; he drives someone's car for a wage. He often puts that car to his own personal use, or uses it for his friends, acquaintances, relatives and so on, but he does not add the fare for it to the owner's money. That is, when he settles accounts with the owner, he does not let the owner know about his personal use and so on, nor does he pay the fare for it. He is not the only one; countless drivers do this.

[en]
Driver: What harm is there in that? All drivers do this. There is an old saying:

[en]
“The one who cooks the poison is bound to taste it.”

[en]
When I am one man's employee, collect money for him all day long, look after his car as if it were my own and keep it clean, then if someone in my household falls ill in the middle of the night, am I to go searching for someone else's car and not use the car standing at my house, so that the sick person dies in the meantime? Well, glory be to Allah! My mind is not prepared to accept this.

[en]
Ammar: Wait, brother! Let us ask the teacher for its ruling in the Shariah.

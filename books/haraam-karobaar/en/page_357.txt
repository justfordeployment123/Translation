[en]
If either party is left unclear about the deal, or if something other than what both agreed on is put in, that is deception, and it is forbidden. When the customer finds out that you did not put in the part you charged for, he will naturally quarrel with you and say that you have broken your word to him.

[en]
Second: Putting in some other part, cheaper than the goods or the part that both agreed on, is adulteration, and it is reported from the noble Prophet, peace be upon him, that he forbade it. For example, he, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
الْمُسْلِمُ أَخُو الْمُسْلِمْ، وَلاَ يَحِلُّ لِمُسْلِمٍ بَاعَ مِنْ أَخِيهِ بَيْعًا فِيهِ عَيْبٌ إِلاَّ بَيَّنَهُ لَهْ

[ref]
سنن ابن ماجه، رقم الحديث [2246]

[en]
“A Muslim is the brother of a Muslim. It is not permissible for a Muslim to sell his brother something that has a defect; yes, but when he makes that defect clear, (it is permissible).”

[en]
In another narration it is reported that the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, passed by a man in the market who was selling goods; he was selling grain. He, peace be upon him, put his hand into the (heap of) grain, and he, peace be upon him, felt wetness on his hand. He, peace be upon him, said: Owner of the grain! What is this? He replied: O Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him! It got wet because of the rain. He, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
أَفَلاَ جَعَلْتَهُ فَوْقَ الطَّعَامِ حَتَّى يَرَاهُ النَّاسْ! مَنْ غَشَّنَا فَلَيْسَ مِنَّا

[ref]
صحيح مسلم، رقم الحديث [102]

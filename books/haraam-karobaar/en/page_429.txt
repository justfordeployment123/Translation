Third: A woman should not lift the clothing off her body in front of a male doctor. For example, if she has pain in the knee, the woman should not uncover her thighs, because in our society some immoral doctors take an interest in such things; that is why they have written outside their rooms, “Only the patient may come in.” In the same way, some immoral women also like to put their bodies on display. So in such a case the woman should have with her a sane, adult and sensible man, who stays standing beside such an ignorant doctor so that the doctor does not tell her to do anything that is against the Shariah. However, if it is a matter of an operation or the like, the clothing may be lifted and the doctor may look at the affected part of the woman's body, because this is a necessity for which there is no alternative; but even then, every effort should be made, as far as possible, to avoid uncovering. These are a few conditions; keeping them in mind, a woman may be treated by a male doctor.

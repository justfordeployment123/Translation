Third: Some magazines, periodicals and newspapers publish news of the most hideous crimes, reading which a person himself feels ashamed and his head hangs in shame, and pictures of the criminals are printed prominently on the cover of the newspaper or magazine. For example:

[en]
A man and a woman had been in an illicit relationship for some time; when they were caught red-handed, they were killed in the name of honour. Or a boy and a girl were having a love affair, and when they failed to marry, both of them committed suicide, and so on. Such news draws even more attention when an investigative report on it begins to be put together; that is, in this way the evil spreads further, and the constant publicising of such news also becomes one of the causes of the spread of obscenity.

[en]
Fourth: Looking at half-naked pictures like these is forbidden, because believing men and women have been commanded to lower their gaze, even if the non-mahram is before them only in the form of a picture.

[en]
Fifth: How many young people, after reading novels, stories and love tales of this kind, fall prey to their lusts and set off on the wrong path, which ruins their health!

[en]
Sixth: Such vile journals and magazines are killing off religious and Shariah morals; in fact, the writers, publishers and distributors of these magazines are openly declaring war on Allah and His Messenger, peace be upon him. So buying and selling these magazines, periodicals and newspapers, and the earnings that come from them, are forbidden and impermissible.

Teacher: In goods of any one kind, there are two reasons for interest:

[en]
First: A deal in goods of one kind is valid if it is like for like; that is, one kilo of rice may be taken only in exchange for one kilo of rice, because the goods in this deal are of one kind. Any increase or decrease in it is impermissible and comes under interest.

[en]
Second: Possession must be taken in the same sitting in which the deal is settled.

[en]
If even one of the two conditions above is missing in a deal, interest will enter that deal and it will be an invalid sale. So taking a hundred kilos in exchange for a hundred and thirty kilos is not permissible.

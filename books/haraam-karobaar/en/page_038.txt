Ammar said this dua, and then the two of them went into the market. As they passed through the market, Ammar noticed a man who was measuring out rice. Ammar watched him closely: he filled the measure, then poured more rice on top and held it in place with both hands so that it would not fall off. There was almost as much rice heaped on top of the measure as there was inside it. Ammar stood and watched him. Then the same man began to measure out wheat, but this time he was not measuring it the way he had measured the rice. He kept the measure short; in fact, he had changed the measure itself.

[en]
Ammar: Shaykh! What is your view of what this man is doing, and what is its ruling in the Shariah?

[en]
Teacher: This is completely forbidden.

[en]
Ammar: Shaykh! If you allow me, may I speak to him about it?

[en]
Teacher: All right, go.

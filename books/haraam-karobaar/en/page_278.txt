In the Noble Quran, Allah the Exalted says:

[ar]
إِنَّمَا جَزَاءُ الَّذِينَ يُحَارِبُونَ اللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُ وَيَسْعَوْنَ فِي الْأَرْضِ فَسَادًا أَنْ يُقَتَّلُوا أَوْ يُصَلَّبُوا أَوْ تُقَطَّعَ أَيْدِيهِمْ وَأَرْجُلُهُمْ مِنْ خِلَافٍ أَوْ يُنْفَوْا مِنَ الْأَرْضْ

[ref]
[المائدة: 33]

[en]
Indeed, the penalty for those who wage war against Allah and His Messenger and strive upon earth (to cause) corruption is none but that they be killed or crucified or that their hands and feet be cut off from opposite sides or that they be exiled from the land.

[en]
This is the punishment in this world, and the punishment in the Hereafter cannot even be imagined.

[en]
All such intoxicants are harmful to a person's health, religion and morals, and to society. The ruling of fasad fil-ard (spreading corruption in the land) applies to whoever promotes these things, and Allah the Exalted has stated the severest warning for such a man:

[ar]
ذَٰلِكَ لَهُمْ خِزْيٌ فِي الدُّنْيَا ۖ وَلَهُمْ فِي الْآخِرَةِ عَذَابٌ عَظِيمْ

[ref]
[المائدة: 33]

[en]
That is for them a disgrace in this world; and for them in the Hereafter is a great punishment.

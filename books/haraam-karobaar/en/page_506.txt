“There was a short woman among the Children of Israel who used to walk with two tall women. Then she had two high-heeled shoes made of wood, and had a hollow gold ring made and filled it with perfume. Then she went up to those two women, and they did not recognise her. Then, (gesturing) with her hands, she went like this and like this.”

[en]
Imam an-Nawawi, may Allah have mercy on him, says:

[en]
“If such shoes are worn to imitate men, to deceive, or out of arrogance and pride, this is forbidden.”

[en]
By selling these clothes and shoes you are promoting a culture that is against the religion, and it is the culture of people who are enemies of the religion, so you should keep away from having any share in it.

[en]
Zaid: What is the solution to this?

[en]
Teacher: Sell men's clothes, and of women's clothes sell only those that do not have a great deal of decoration and obscene pictures on them; that is, sell a plain variety of clothes and shoes. This will make your provision pure and lawful. Otherwise your earnings will be forbidden earnings, because when Allah the Exalted makes a thing forbidden, He also makes the earnings from it forbidden.

[en]
Zaid: In sha Allah, we will give up buying and selling clothes that are against the Shariah, and we will keep clothes in our shop that are plain.

[en]
Teacher: May Allah reward you with good.

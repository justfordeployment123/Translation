Abbas: Teacher! Expenses are very high and income is small; the needs of life are not met. We cannot steal, so what are we to do? This bribe is something we are forced into.

[en]
Teacher: It does not become you to say this.

[en]
Abbas: Why?

[en]
Teacher: You have two options:

[en]
First: Leave this job and find one whose salary covers your expenses.

[en]
Second: Be content with this and live your life with patience and gratitude.

[en]
Abbas: (bursting out) Shaykh! I have seven children. With my expenses, my salary barely lasts ten days. How am I to get through the rest of the month?

[en]
Teacher: I have told you the solution to the problem, so all I will say is this: fear Allah, and do not raise your children on forbidden wealth, otherwise it will be a torment for you.

[en]
Abbas: Teacher! There is also a rule, after all, that necessities make impermissible things permissible. Besides, a poet has said:

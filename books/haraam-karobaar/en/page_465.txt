Chapter Seventy-Four: Harming Another Heir by Means of a Will

[en]
Husain: Teacher! Apart from the two earlier conditions, is there no other way by which a will can be made in favour of one of one's children?

[en]
Teacher: In the Shariah there is no room for tricks and pretexts. If you adopt any means for a will other than the two earlier conditions, it will be sheer wrongdoing and injustice; that is, harming someone else under cover of a will is not right at all, because the principle of the Islamic Shariah is:

[ar]
لاَ ضَرَرَ وَلاَ ضِرَارْ

[en]
It is permissible neither to harm anyone first nor to harm anyone in return. The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
مَنْ ضَارَّ أَضَرَّ اللَّهُ بِهْ، وَمَنْ شَاقَّ شَقَّ اللَّهُ عَلَيْهْ

[ref]
سنن ابن ماجه، صحيح الجامع، رقم الحديث [7517]

[en]
“Whoever harms anyone, Allah the Exalted will harm him, and whoever is harsh with anyone, Allah the Exalted will be harsh with him.”

[en]
Harming another heir by means of a will can take the following forms:

[en]
First: Depriving an heir of his right by means of a will.

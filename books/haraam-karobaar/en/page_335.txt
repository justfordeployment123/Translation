Chapter Forty-Eight: Having Designs and Floral Patterns Made on the Body

[en]
Young man: Teacher! We make designs and floral patterns on women's faces and hands. This too is a form of adornment. Is it permissible?

[en]
Teacher: The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
لَعَنَ اللَّهُ الْوَاصِلَةَ وَالْمُسْتَوْصِلَةَ وَالْوَاشِمَةَ وَالْمُسْتَوْشِمَةْ

[ref]
صحيح البخاري، رقم الحديث [5937] صحيح مسلم، رقم الحديث [2124]

[en]
“Allah the Exalted has cursed the women who attach artificial hair, those who have it attached, those who tattoo, and those who have themselves tattooed.”

[en]
Al-washimah means the woman who makes the designs, whether on her own body or on the body of another woman.

[en]
Al-mustawshimah means the woman on whose body the designs are made.

[en]
Both such women are cursed. And remember, this ruling is not limited to women only; rather, it applies to men and women alike. Women alone are mentioned in this hadith only because they take more care over these things than men do.

[en]
Washm means pricking the hand, the forehead or any part of the body with a needle, and then filling the spot where the needle went in with kohl, lime or any kind of ink, so that its marks remain under the skin.

Chapter Sixty-Eight: Buying and Selling Contraceptive Medicines

[en]
Salim: Teacher! We have learned Islam's point of view on abortion. Now we also want to know: what is the ruling on using contraceptive medicines?

[en]
Teacher: Experience has proved that all the contraceptive products available in the market at present are harmful. For example, there are some medicines that cause cancer of the breast and the womb, and there are some medicines whose use makes a woman's body very heavy.

[en]
Although these things do not appear harmful on the surface, doctors say that sooner or later they become harmful, and in this matter it is the doctors' word that will be taken as final. Whatever they declare harmful, buying, selling and using it will be forbidden, because the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
لاَ ضَرَرَ وَلاَ ضِرَارْ

[ref]
سنن ابن ماجه، رقم الحديث [2340] صحيح الجامع، رقم الحديث [7517]

[en]
“Do not cause harm to anyone, and do not suffer harm.”

[en]
But if using contraceptive medicines is mostly harmful, and we want to leave a gap of two years, what should we do?

“Whoever drinks alcohol and becomes drunk, his prayer will not be accepted for forty days, and if he dies (without repenting), he will enter Hell. If he repents, Allah will accept his repentance. If he drinks alcohol again and becomes drunk, then (once more) his prayer will not be accepted for forty days; if (during that time) he dies without repenting, he will enter Hell, and if he repents, Allah will accept his repentance. If he drinks alcohol again (a third time) and becomes drunk, his prayer will not be accepted for forty days; if he dies, he will enter Hell, and if he repents, Allah will accept his repentance. If he drinks alcohol again (a fourth time), then Allah the Exalted has taken it upon Himself to give him radghat al-khabal to drink on the Day of Resurrection. The noble Companions asked: O Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, what is this radghat al-khabal? He, peace be upon him, said: The pus and filth of the people of Hell.”

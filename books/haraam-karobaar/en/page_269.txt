Chapter Thirty-Eight: Robbing Daughters of Their Rights

[en]
After praying Zuhr, the teacher, his student, Atiyyah and Abu Jum'a came back to the same place and sat down, and the talk turned to various subjects: how far people in the present age are drifting from the religion, how morals are dying, and how weak Muslims are proving to be when it comes to the rights owed to other people. Atiyyah was very happy to be hosting the teacher and was treating him to fresh fruit. Meanwhile Atiyyah's sister Khadijah came running up in anger. After saying As-salamu alaikum, she turned to Atiyyah.

[en]
Khadijah: Today I will have the teacher settle this.

[en]
Atiyyah: Atiyyah spoke, embarrassed and uneasy: Umm Muhammad, do not say anything about this matter.

[en]
Khadijah: Why? Why should I not talk about it? Am I not even to take my own right?

[en]
Atiyyah: Everything will be done as you wish, but do not talk about it here.

[en]
Khadijah: These are all just false claims of yours, an excuse to put things off.

[en]
Teacher: May Allah protect you both. What is the matter?

[en]
Khadijah: Teacher! Atiyyah has taken possession of my share of our parents' property.

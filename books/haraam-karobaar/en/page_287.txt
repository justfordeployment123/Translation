Second: There must be no nepotism in it.

[en]
Bahnasi: What does nepotism mean?

[en]
Teacher: That is, if someone comes who is your friend or close to you, you gladly let him and take whatever gift he gives; but if someone else comes who gives no gift and is not your friend or close to you either, you think: what is he going to give, and he is not close to me anyway; and so you put him off: come tomorrow, come on such-and-such a day, the bull is tired now, or it is asleep, and so on.

[en]
Bahnasi: But Teacher! The hadith does not mention any condition?

[en]
Teacher: The evidence for these two conditions is in the following hadith. Umar, may Allah be pleased with him, said: The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, gave me some wealth. I said: O Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, I have no need of it. So he, peace be upon him, said: (O Umar!)

“When Allah the Exalted gives you some wealth without your asking, and your heart is not greedy for it, accept it, for it is provision that Allah the Exalted has sent your way.”

[en]
In this hadith the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, made taking wealth permissible under the following two conditions:

[en]

[en]
If someone has wrongfully taken another person's right, he should return it as soon as possible, and he should settle his worldly dealings before that day comes when a person will have nothing but his good deeds and his bad deeds.

[en]
It is narrated from Abu Hurairah, may Allah be pleased with him, that the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Allah the Exalted is pure and loves only what is pure. Allah the Exalted has given the believers the same command that He gave His messengers. Allah the Exalted said: ‘O messengers, eat from the good foods and work righteousness. Indeed, I, of what you do, am Knowing.’ And He said: ‘O you who have believed, eat from the good things which We have provided for you.’ Then he, peace be upon him, mentioned a man who has made a long journey, his hair dishevelled and his body covered in dust. He raises his hands and supplicates: O my Lord! O my Lord! Yet his food is forbidden, his drink is forbidden, his clothing is forbidden, and his nourishment is forbidden. So how will his supplication be answered?”

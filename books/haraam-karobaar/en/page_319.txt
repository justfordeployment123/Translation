“Woe to you both! Who commanded you to do this? They replied: Our king Kisra commanded us to do this. Then the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said: But my Lord has commanded me to grow my beard and to trim my moustache.”

[en]
In all these texts the imperative form is used, which indicates that the matter is obligatory. When something is commanded with the imperative form, there is reward for doing it and punishment for leaving it.

[en]
All the narrations above prove that shaving the beard is against the Sunnah and a sign of weak faith.

Second: Sometimes cases are of the same kind, yet their rulings in the Shariah are different. Let me explain this to you with an example.

[en]
Abu Faris: What is the example?

[en]
Teacher: A man goes to another man and says to him: Give me your daughter so that I may live with her (without marriage), and take these one thousand pounds from me, and I will be witness to it. The man accepts this offer. What is your view about this?

[en]
Abu Faris: That is fornication. No one's consent can make the forbidden lawful.

[en]
Teacher: You are right.

[en]
In the same way, a man goes to another man and says to him: Marry your daughter to me, and take these one thousand pounds from me, and I am witness to it. The man (the father) accepts this offer. What is your view about this?

[en]
Abu Faris: That is a valid marriage; there is nothing wrong with it.

[en]
Teacher: Now just think: the ruling changed only because of one word, zawwijni (marry her to me). Can we say merely on the basis of reason that these two cases are the same?

Second: Whoever trades in tobacco consumes what is forbidden and gathers only what is forbidden, though a person will be questioned about this on the Day of Resurrection. So the cigarette trade harms both a person's religion and his body.

[en]
My shopkeeper brother! Accepting the truth is better than clinging to falsehood. Instead of following people who have strayed from the straight path, you should follow the Shariah, because you will have to answer for what you have done. So set yourself right, take up what is good and become an example for others. I also give you this advice: living continually on forbidden earnings leads to dangerous consequences in this world and in the Hereafter.

My brother! I invite you to give up this forbidden work, join us, and try to bring back to the straight path those young people who are caught in the grip of smoking and drugs. Bear in mind that there are countless ways of earning a living lawfully, and a little lawful earning is better than a great deal of forbidden earning. Always remember this saying: whoever gives up something for the sake of Allah, Allah the Exalted gives him something better in its place.

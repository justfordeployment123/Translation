Fourth: The children of working women are deprived of their full love, affection and tenderness, which weakens the foundations of the family system, and in the end the children's hearts too lose the tenderness towards their mother that Islam requires.

Fifth: By nature a woman loves adornment, fine clothes, jewellery and the like. When she starts earning money herself, she begins to waste most of her earnings on these needless things, and in this way she falls into extravagance.

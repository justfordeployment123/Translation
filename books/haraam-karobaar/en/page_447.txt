Husain: So is magic forbidden in every case?

[en]
Teacher: Yes, magic is disbelief. It is counted among the seven destructive things, and no one can ever be benefited or harmed by it.

[en]

“Avoid the seven destructive things: associating partners with Allah and practising magic.”

[en]
In the Shariah the command is that the magician be killed, and his earnings are forbidden. Foolish, ignorant people of weak faith turn to magicians so that through magic they can take revenge on others and give vent to their enmity and hostility. Those who go to magicians to break the effect of magic, or to remove the effects of an evil shadow and the like, should turn to Allah and make a point of frequent dhikr and of reciting the Mu'awwidhat (the surahs of seeking refuge with Allah).

[en]
The payment that magicians take for this work is impermissible and forbidden; in reality, they are eating the fire of Hell.

[en]
Teacher: (Looking at Ammar, he said) Ammar, this is bay' al-musarrat.

[en]
Ammar: Teacher! What does bay' al-musarrat mean?

[en]
Teacher: It means not milking a she-camel, a goat, a cow and the like, and keeping the milk collected in the udders, so that the buyer thinks the animal gives a great deal of milk. In the Shariah this is forbidden, because it is a deceptive sale and amounts to consuming people's wealth by false means.

[en]
The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
لاَ تُصَرُّوا الإِبِلَ وَالْغَنَمْ

[ref]
صحيح البخاري، رقم الحديث [2150] صحيح مسلم، رقم الحديث [1515]

[en]
“Do not hold back the milk in the udders of she-camels and goats.”

[en]
That is, when you mean to sell an animal, do not collect milk in its udders so as to deceive the buyer. Listen also to the view of Imam an-Nawawi, may Allah have mercy on him, on this.

[en]
Man: What does he say?

[en]
Teacher: He says:

[en]
“Know that collecting milk in the udders of a she-camel, a cow, a goat, a mare, a she-donkey and the like is forbidden, because it is cheating and deception.”

[ref]
شرح صحيح مسلم [161/10]

[en]
Bay' al-musarrat is prohibited for the following two reasons:

[en]
First: It is deception, and through it something is made to appear that has no reality. The noble Prophet, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
الْمُتَشَبِّعُ بِمَا لَمْ يُعْطَ كَلاَبِسِ ثَوْبَيْ زُورْ

[ref]

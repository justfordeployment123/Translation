Ammar: Shaykh! Let us now bring our gathering to a close. And Abu Shawqi, may Allah forgive your mistake and bless you.

[en]
Teacher: Ammar! Through this round I intended to make you aware of some of the practical dealings of Muslims, because questions like these often arise in a student's mind during his studies, or sometimes such a situation comes up in practical life. So I have done all this as practice for you; remember it well.

[en]
Ammar: May Allah reward you! May Allah grant you honour.

[en]
Meanwhile, the Shaykh, Ammar and Abu Shawqi stood up. The Shaykh asked Abu Shawqi's leave, intending to go back, but suddenly Abu Shawqi called out to a drinks seller, ordered drinks and said: You must have this drink.

[en]
Teacher: May Allah bless you! What need was there for this trouble?

[en]
Abu Shawqi: It is nothing.

[en]
Teacher: May Allah reward you well.

[en]
Abu Shawqi ordered the drinks, and they drank. Abu Shawqi embraced the Shaykh and showed him great affection. The Shaykh prayed for good for him and said: We give thanks that you understood the truth so quickly, and I ask Allah to put blessing in your work. As-salamu alaikum wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh.

[en]
Abu Shawqi: Wa alaikum as-salam wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh.

فَإِنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ نَهَى عَنِ الْخَذْفْ، أَوْ كَانَ يَكْرَهُ الْخَذْفْ، إِنَّهُ لاَ يُصَادُ بِهِ صَيْدٌ وَلاَ يُنْكَى بِهِ عَدُوٌّ وَلَكِنَّهَا قَدْ تَكْسِرُ السِّنَّ وَتَفْقَأُ الْعَيْنْ

[ref]
صحيح البخاري، رقم الحديث [5479] صحيح مسلم، رقم الحديث [1954]

[en]
“The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, forbade throwing pebbles, or he said that the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, disliked it, because with it no game can be hunted and no harm can be done to an enemy; rather, it sometimes breaks someone's tooth and puts out an eye.”

[en]
After that, Abdullah ibn Mughaffal, may Allah be pleased with him, saw the same man throwing pebbles again and said: I am telling you a hadith of the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, that he, peace be upon him, forbade it, and still you go on throwing them? I will not speak to you for such and such a number of days.

[en]
Hafiz Ibn Hajar, may Allah have mercy on him, writes in his explanation of it:

[en]
That is, the man was throwing a stone or a date stone. And a “mikhdhafah” (slingshot) is the name for a device in which a stone is placed and shot at birds; what is meant is a slingshot, a sling or the like.

[ref]
فتح الباري [607/9]

[en]
This thing exists in our own time too, in the form of the slingshot, and it is forbidden for the following reasons:

[en]
First: The noble Prophet, peace be upon him, forbade throwing pebbles, stones, date stones and the like.

Teacher: Any one of the following methods may be adopted:

[en]
First: Practise 'azl, as the noble Companions, may Allah be pleased with them, used to do. In a narration in Sahih al-Bukhari, the Companions say:

[ar]
كُنَّا نَعْزِلُ وَالْقُرْآنُ يَنْزِلْ

[ref]
صحيح البخاري، رقم الحديث [5208]

[en]
“We used to practise 'azl, and at that time the Noble Quran was being revealed.”

[en]
By 'azl is meant ejaculating outside the private parts during intercourse.

[en]
Second: Divide a month into four weeks, that is, one week of the days of menstruation and three weeks of purity. Intercourse during menstruation is forbidden anyway, which leaves three weeks. Now have intercourse only in the week after the days of menstruation and in the week before the days of menstruation; in these two weeks pregnancy does not occur. But do not have intercourse in the third week, the one in the middle, because in it pregnancy is bound to occur. By adopting this method you can leave a gap.

[en]
Third: The method of breastfeeding a great deal; that is, if the woman breastfeeds the child at least twenty-four times in a day and a night, then intercourse during that time does not result in pregnancy.

[en]
Fourth: There is a particular syrup which, if it is drunk on the fifth day after the days of menstruation, also prevents pregnancy.

[en]
These four methods are all permissible, and they will never prove harmful to the woman. When Allah the Exalted has not put you in difficulty, why are you needlessly putting yourselves in difficulty?

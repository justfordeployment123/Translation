He replied:

[ar]
الثُّلُثْ، وَالثُّلُثُ كَثِيرْ، إِنَّكَ أَنْ تَذَرَ وَرَثَتَكَ أَغْنِيَاءَ خَيْرٌ مِنْ أَنْ تَذَرَهُمْ عَالَةً يَتَكَفَّفُونَ النَّاسْ

[ref]
صحيح البخاري، رقم الحديث [4409] صحيح مسلم، رقم الحديث [1628]

[en]
“A third (is all right), and even a third is a lot. Indeed, leaving your heirs rich is better than leaving them destitute, so that they go about begging from people.”

[en]
Abdullah ibn Abbas, may Allah be pleased with them both, says:

[en]
“I would like people to make wills of less than a third, because the noble Prophet, peace be upon him, said: Even a third is a lot.”

[ref]
صحيح البخاري، رقم الحديث [2743]

[en]
It is narrated from Imam Ibrahim, may Allah have mercy on him, that the noble Companions used to say:

[en]
“The one who makes a will of a quarter is better than the one who makes a will of a third, and the one who makes a will of a fifth is better than the one who makes a will of a quarter.”

[ref]
صحيح. مصنف ابن أبي شيبة [306/7] مصنف عبدالرزاق [66/9]

[en]
It is narrated from Imran ibn Husain, may Allah be pleased with him:

[ar]
إِنَّ رَجُلاً أَعْتَقَ سِتَّةَ مَمْلُوكِينَ لَهُ عِنْدَ مَوْتِهْ، لَمْ يَكُنْ لَهُ مَالٌ غَيْرُهُمْ، فَدَعَا بِهِمْ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ فَجَزَّأَهُمْ أَثْلاَثَا، ثُمَّ أَقْرَعَ بَيْنَهُمْ، فَأَعْتَقَ اثْنَيْنْ، وَأَرَقَّ أَرْبَعَةً وَقَالَ لَهُ قَوْلاً شَدِيدَا

[ref]
صحيح مسلم، رقم الحديث [1668]

[en]
“A man freed six slaves of his at the time of his death, and he had no other wealth besides them. The Prophet, peace be upon him, divided them into three parts (that is, he made them into three pairs of two each), then drew lots between them, set one pair (that is, two slaves) free and returned the other four slaves to slavery, and he said the harshest words about him (the man who had freed them).”

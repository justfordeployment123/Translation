[en]
“Do not sell gold for gold unless it is equal for equal, and do not make either side more or less than the other; and do not sell silver for silver unless it is equal, and do not make either side more or less than the other; and do not sell any of them on credit in exchange for cash.”

[en]
The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, says:

[ar]
وَلاَ تَبِيعُوا مِنْهَا غَائِبًا بِنَاجِزْ

[en]
“Do not sell on credit in exchange for cash.”

[en]
This means: do not sell something that is not present in the sitting in exchange for something that is present in the sitting; rather, the buyer should pay the full amount, and the shopkeeper should hand over the whole of the goods to him in return. This is the correct form of buying and selling.

[en]
The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
الذَّهَبُ بِالذَّهَبْ، وَالْفِضَّةُ بِالْفِضَّةْ، وَالْبُرُّ بِالْبُرّ، وَالشَّعِيرُ بِالشَّعِيرْ، وَالتَّمْرُ بِالتَّمْرْ، وَالْمِلْحُ بِالْمِلْحِ مِثْلاً بِمِثْلٍ سَوَاءً بِسَوَاءْ، يَدًا بِيَدْ، فَإِذَا اخْتَلَفَتْ هَذِهِ الأَصْنَافُ فَبِيعُوا كَيْفَ شِئْتُمْ، إِذَا كَانَ يَدًا بِيَدْ

[ref]
صحيح مسلم، رقم الحديث [1587]

[en]
“Gold for gold, silver for silver, wheat for wheat, barley for barley, dates for dates, salt for salt: the buying and selling must be equal and in cash. When the kinds of these things differ, sell as you wish, as long as it is hand to hand (in cash).”

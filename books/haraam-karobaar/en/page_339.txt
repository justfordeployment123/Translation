[en]
Young man: You mean that dyeing the hair black is forbidden without exception?

[en]
Teacher: No, it is not forbidden without exception; there is one situation in which the hair may be dyed black.

[en]
Young man: What situation is that?

[en]
Teacher: Dyeing the hair black is permissible at the time of war with the enemy, because then everyone will look young and strong, which will unnerve the enemy, and fear of the Muslims will settle in his heart.

[en]
Young man: What is the ruling on any colour other than black?

[en]
Teacher: It is established from the noble Prophet, peace be upon him, that the colour of white hair may be changed with henna and the like, that is, it may be made yellow or red, as is reported about Abu Quhafah, the father of Abu Bakr, may Allah be pleased with him: on the day of the conquest of Makkah he was brought before the noble Prophet, peace be upon him, and his head and beard were as white as the thaghamah (a tree).

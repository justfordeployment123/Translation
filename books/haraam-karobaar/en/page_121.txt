The words of the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him:

[ar]
يَدًا بِيَدْ

[en]
“Hand to hand (in cash).” This is clear proof that taqabud (completing the exchange) is required in the very sitting where the buying and selling takes place. This same principle is also accepted in matters of interest (riba): when a ribawi item is bought or sold for another ribawi item that is not of its own kind, taqabud in the same sitting is a condition.

[en]
In the currency exchange you have just made, too, you must complete the deal in the same sitting.

[en]
Malik ibn Aws, may Allah be pleased with him, says: I turned to the noble Companions and asked: Who will give dirhams in exchange for dinars? Talhah ibn Ubaidullah, may Allah be pleased with him, was with Umar ibn al-Khattab, may Allah be pleased with him, at the time. He said: Show us your gold (dinars); when our servant comes, we will give you silver in exchange for it. Umar, may Allah be pleased with him, said: Certainly not! By Allah, you must give him the silver at once or return his gold (dinars) to him, for the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

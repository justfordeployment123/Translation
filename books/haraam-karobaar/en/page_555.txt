Chapter Ninety-Eight: Working in Banks That Deal in Interest (riba) and in Insurance Companies

[en]
Ammar: Teacher! What is the ruling on working in banks that deal in interest and in insurance companies?

[en]
Teacher: We have already explained, in the light of the Quran and the Sunnah, the ruling on interest: that it is impermissible, forbidden and tantamount to declaring war on Allah the Exalted. Taking part in dealings in interest in any way at all is forbidden, as the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
لَعَنَ اللَّهُ الرِّبَا، وَآكِلَهْ، وَمُوْكِلَهُ وَكَاتِبَهْ، وَشَاهِدَهْ، وَهُم يَعْلَمُوْنْ

[ref]
صحيح. الطبراني، الصحيحة، رقم الحديث [5094]

[en]
“Allah the Exalted has cursed the one who consumes interest, the one who pays interest, the one who writes up dealings in interest and the one who acts as a witness to it, while they know (that it is forbidden).”

[en]
In another hadith he, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
لَعَنَ اللَّهُ الرِّبَا، وَآكِلَ الرِّبَا، وَمُوْكِلَهْ، وَشَاهِدَيْهْ، وَكَاتِبَهْ، هُمْ فِيْهِ سَوَاءْ

[ref]
صحيح مسلم، صحيح الجامع، رقم الحديث [5090]

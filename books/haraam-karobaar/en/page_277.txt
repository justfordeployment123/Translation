Abdul-Khaliq: Teacher! Bahnasi had grown cannabis on his land, and at the same time he had a dispute going on with his neighbours. They seized the opportunity and lodged a complaint with the Anti-Narcotics Ministry, and because of that a case had been brought against him.

[en]
Teacher: I seek Allah's forgiveness; there is no might and no power except with Allah. Bahnasi! Are you in your right mind? Can even a man of prayer like you commit such a serious crime? What a pity!

[en]
Bahnasi: Teacher! Is it forbidden?

[en]
Teacher: In this age some farmers have completely forgotten their Lord; no one has any fear. Shamelessly, everyone is busy earning forbidden provision. Listen! Growing, buying and selling something forbidden, and the like, is impermissible.

[en]

Growing cannabis and opium is a greater crime than buying and selling alcohol. Whoever grows these things is in truth spreading corruption in the land and declaring war on Allah and His Messenger, peace be upon him, and Allah the Exalted has prepared an exemplary punishment for such people.

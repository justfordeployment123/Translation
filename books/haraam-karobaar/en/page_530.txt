[en]
“O Hakim! This wealth is pleasant and sweet. Whoever takes it with generosity of heart, blessing is put into it for him; and whoever takes it with greed and covetousness, no blessing is put into it for him, and he becomes like one who eats but is never full. And the upper hand is better than the lower hand. Hakim says: I said: O Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him! By the One who sent you with the truth, after you I will not seek this from anyone until I leave this world.”

“Do not ask with persistence. By Allah, it never happens that one of you asks me for something and his asking draws something out of me while I dislike it, and then I give it to him and blessing is put into it as well.”

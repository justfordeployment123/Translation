“Have you given each of them a slave as a gift in the same way? My father replied: No. He, peace be upon him, said: Then do not make me a witness to this, for I do not bear witness to injustice.”

[en]
It is often seen that when a father abandons fairness and justice in giving gifts to his children, the children, that is, the brothers and sisters, begin to look at one another with hatred because of it. Envy, rancour and malice against one another arise in their hearts, and because of this, feelings of enmity and hostility rise up among them. In particular, the child who has been treated unjustly no longer holds his father in the respect he once did.

[en]
“At midnight the gates of heaven are opened, and a caller (an angel) calls out: Is there anyone calling, so that his call may be answered? Is there anyone asking, so that he may be given? Is there anyone in distress, so that his distress may be removed? Allah the Exalted answers the call of every Muslim who calls on Him (at that time), except the woman who is immoral and misuses her private parts, and the man who takes (unlawful) taxes (their prayers are not answered).”

[en]
Need, poverty and hunger are no excuse at all for earning money through deeds that trample on the limits set by Allah. The wise have said that a free woman, when she is hungry, does not start tearing at her own breasts; how then could she misuse her private parts?

[en]
In bringing and taking passengers you very often have to deal with women, so you should develop in yourself as much piety and good conduct as you can, so that this God-fearing restraint of yours may prove a means of keeping away from illicit acts. You should build a strong wall between yourself and forbidden deeds, and that wall can be built through piety.

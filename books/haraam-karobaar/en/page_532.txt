“Asking is clawing at oneself; by it a person claws his own face, unless someone asks a ruler, or the one who asks is in desperate need.”

[en]
It is narrated from Abdullah ibn Mas'ud, may Allah be pleased with him, that the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
مَنْ أَصَابَتْهُ فَاقَةٌ فَأَنْزَلَهَا بِالنَّاسِ لَمْ تُسَدَّ فَاقَتُهْ، وَمَنْ أَنْزَلَهَا بِاللَّهِ فَيُوشِكُ اللَّهُ لَهُ بِرِزْقٍ عَاجِلٍ أَوْ آجِلْ

[ref]
سنن أبي داود، رقم الحديث [1645]

[en]
“Whoever is struck by dire need and lays it before people, that need of his will not be met; and whoever lays it (that need) before Allah, Allah the Exalted will surely, before long, give him provision, either immediately or later.”

[en]
It is narrated from Thawban, may Allah be pleased with him, that the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
مَنْ تَكَفَّلَ لِي أَنْ لاَ يَسْأَلَ النَّاسَ شَيْئَا، وَأَتَكَفَّلَ لَهُ بِالْجَنَّةْ، فَقُلْتُ أَنَا، فَكَانَ لاَ يَسْأَلُ أَحَدًا شَيْئَا

[ref]
سنن أبي داود، رقم الحديث [1643]

[en]
“Who will guarantee me that he will not ask people for anything, and I will guarantee him Paradise? (Thawban says:) I said: I will. And so he would never ask anyone for anything.”

[en]
All these texts require that a person should not ask anyone for anything without need.

They neither engage in the remembrance of Allah themselves, nor let the other worshippers do so in peace.

زَجَرَ النَّبِيُّ ﷺ أَنْ تَصِلَ الْمَرْأَةُ بِرَأْسِهَا شَيْئَا

[ref]
صحيح مسلم، رقم الحديث [2126]

[en]
“The Prophet, peace be upon him, sternly forbade a woman to attach anything (artificial) to her head.”

[en]
Today, in our time, all such things found for men and women under the name of hairstyles are forbidden; for example, an actor in dramas, films and the like putting on false hair to disguise himself or to take on the appearance of some other person.

[en]
On this, read a fatwa of the great scholar Ibn Uthaymeen.

[en]
Question: May a woman have false hair attached to her own hair for the sake of her husband?

[en]
Answer: Attaching artificial hair and having it attached are forbidden, and the noble Prophet, peace be upon him, cursed both such women. Using a barukah (wig) is forbidden and impermissible, even if it is with the husband's consent and permission, because no one's consent or permission can make disobedience to Allah permissible.

[ref]
فتاوىٰ المرأة [ص: 179]

[en]
The World Fatwa Council has given a fatwa that a wig, in whatever form it may be, is forbidden, because it involves imitating disbelieving women, and a Muslim woman may not, under the cover of adornment, do anything that involves imitating non-Muslim women. The noble Prophet, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
مَنْ تَشَبَّهَ بِقَوْمٍ فَهُوَ مِنْهُمْ

[ref]
سنن أبي داود، صحيح الجامع، رقم الحديث [6149]

[en]
“Whoever imitates a people is one of them.”

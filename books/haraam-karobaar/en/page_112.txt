Second: By not separating the pearls from the jewellery, you go against the clear command of the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him.

Third: Allah the Exalted says:

[ar]

[en]
And do not deprive the people of their due.

[en]
Depriving anyone of his right is a very great wrong. It is depriving people of their right when you separate a thing when buying it and keep it in when selling it.

[en]
Fourth: You go against the clear command of Allah the Exalted:

[ar]

[ref]

[en]
And weigh with an even balance.

[en]
You go against it because in this way the scales do not stay true; rather, they become crooked.

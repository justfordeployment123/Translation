Bahnasi: Teacher! Is everything forbidden? Things we have seen done since the days of our forefathers, you are declaring forbidden? Until today we have never heard that they are forbidden. What evidence do you have that earnings from a bull are forbidden?

[en]
Teacher: Servant of Allah, I am not declaring it forbidden on my own; rather, the Shariah has made it forbidden.

[en]

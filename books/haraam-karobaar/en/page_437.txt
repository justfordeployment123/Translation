Do you already have so many children that you now want to leave a gap?

[en]
Salim: I have eight children, six sons and two daughters.

[en]
Teacher: Oh, brother! Fear Allah! You have only eight children so far, and from now on you want to stop having any more? I was thinking you must have twenty children, which was why you now wanted to give your wife a little rest. Do not get caught up in ending pregnancies; let there be ten children, and after that you will have a reasonable ground for abortion.

[en]
Salim: Is birth control, that is, not having any more children, impermissible?

[en]
Teacher: Birth control is permissible with certain conditions:

[en]
First: That it is not done by harmful means.

[en]
Second: That it has the husband's permission.

[en]
Third: That reducing the Muslim population is not the aim.

[en]
Fourth: That it is not done out of fear of scarce provision, poverty and hunger.

[en]
Salim: Teacher! How can a small salary meet the expenses of so many children?

[en]
Teacher: Allah the Exalted says:

[ar]

[ref]
[الإسراء: 31]

[en]
And do not kill your children for fear of poverty. We provide for them and for you. Indeed, their killing is ever a great sin.

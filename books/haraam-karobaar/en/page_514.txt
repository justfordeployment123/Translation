“Take this cloth to Abu Jahm and bring me his anbijani cloth (a plain, thick cloth), because it distracted me from my prayer just now.”

[en]

[en]
“I want to shelter the people from the rain; and do not paint the mosques red or yellow, for through that people will fall into temptation.”

[en]
All these narrations are proof that carpets, cloths and the like with designs and floral patterns on them should be neither spread nor hung in the mosque, and that prayer should not be offered on them either.

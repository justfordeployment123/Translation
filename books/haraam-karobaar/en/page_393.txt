[en]
Al-ma'azif means anything with which a melodious, singing sound is produced, such as the drum, the guitar, the daff, the flute, the sarangi and so on; even the bell is counted among these things, because the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
الْجَرَسُ مَزَامِيرُ الشَّيْطَانْ

[ref]
صحيح مسلم، رقم الحديث [2114]

[en]
“The bell is the musical instrument of Satan.”

[en]
The word yastahillun (they will make lawful) makes it plain that in reality these things are forbidden, but they will be made lawful. In our society, gatherings of singing and music are put on at weddings, engagements and other happy occasions, and shamelessness is then found in these gatherings; because of this, desires rise in the young generation, which later lead to fornication and obscenity.

[en]
It is narrated from Anas, may Allah be pleased with him, that the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
لَيَكُوْنَنَّ فِيْ هَذِهِ الأُمَّةِ خَسْفٌ وَقَذْفٌ وَمَسْخْ، وَذَلِكَ إِذَا شَرِبُوْا الْخُمُوْرَ وَاتَّخَذُوْا الْقَيْنَاتِ وَضَرَبُوْا الْمَعَازِفْ

[ref]
سنن الترمذي، رقم الحديث [2212] صححه الألباني فى الصحيحة [2203]

[en]
“A time will come when the people of my Ummah will be swallowed up (into the earth), stones will be rained down on them, and their forms will be disfigured. This will happen when they drink alcohol, keep female singers and put musical instruments to use.”

[en]
These gatherings become even more dangerous when girls dance in them in half-naked and revealing clothes, and the words of the songs are about love, romance and revelry. That is why the scholars have called music the prelude to fornication, meaning that music incites a person to fornication. By buying and selling musical instruments you are equally responsible for the spread of music, because this business of yours amounts to cooperating in acts of sin.

And whoever disobeys Allah and His Messenger and transgresses His limits - He will put him into the Fire to abide eternally therein, and he will have a humiliating punishment.

[en]
And it is obligatory to keep away from forbidden things. The blessed Prophet, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
مَا نَهَيْتُكُمْ عَنْهُ فَاجْتَنِبُوهْ، وَمَا أَمَرْتُكُمْ بِهِ فَافْعَلُوا مِنْهُ مَا اسْتَطَعْتُمْ

صحيح مسلم، رقم الحديث [130]

[en]
“Whatever I forbid you, keep away from it, and whatever I command you, do as much of it as you are able.”

[en]
Usually, when forbidden things are mentioned in front of some people who have little knowledge, who are ignorant and weak in faith, they become very uneasy and troubled, and they say:

[en]
You keep on making everything forbidden! You people have made us despair of life; you have made life hard for us, when the religion is easy, the Shariah is very broad, and Allah the Exalted is Most Forgiving and Most Merciful!

[en]
We will answer the objection of such people in these words: Allah the Exalted commands whatever He wills, and there is none who can question Him. He is the Wise, the All-Aware. He makes lawful whatever He wills and makes forbidden whatever He wills. It is our duty to accept His command gladly and to be content with His decision.

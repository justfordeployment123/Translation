[en]
Can there be any wickedness greater than presenting one's own women before non-mahram men in this way?

[en]
Young man: What if my intention is completely clean, with no corruption in it at all?

[en]
Teacher: Oh brother! When the work itself is forbidden, it makes no difference whether the intention is clean or not. And remember this too: whatever work is forbidden, its wages are also forbidden. A clean intention does not make a forbidden thing lawful. Leave this work at once, repent to Allah and look for a new job. This work is nothing but the adultery of all the limbs, the eyes, hands, nose and ears, which leads a person on to the adultery of the private parts.

[en]
The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

“A person's share of adultery has been written for him, which he will inevitably meet: the adultery of the eyes is looking, the adultery of the ears is listening, the adultery of the tongue is speaking, the adultery of the hands is grasping, the adultery of the feet is walking; the heart desires and longs, and then the private parts confirm that (desire) or deny it.”

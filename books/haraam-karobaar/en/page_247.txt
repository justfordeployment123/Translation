[ar]

[en]
And tell the believing women to reduce (some) of their vision.

[en]
If you and they do not follow these commands, it is as though you are disobeying Allah the Exalted. Is this not setting yourself against Allah and His Messenger, peace be upon him?

[en]
Listen carefully to this hadith and reconsider what you are doing. Jarir ibn Abdullah, may Allah be pleased with him, asked the noble Prophet, peace be upon him, about a sudden glance (falling on a woman), and he, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
اصْرِفْ بَصَرَكْ

[ref]
صحيح مسلم، رقم الحديث [2159]

[en]
“Turn your gaze to the other side at once.”

[en]
That is, if your eyes ever suddenly fall on a non-mahram, turn your gaze away at once, because the first glance is forgiven, but the second glance is not forgiven; rather, it is forbidden. Consider! If there is such a strict ruling about an unintended, sudden glance, then how much stricter will it be about deliberately looking at a non-mahram? Besides this, all the scholars agree that looking at a non-mahram woman without need is impermissible and forbidden.

[en]
Hasan: What greater need could there be than education?

[en]
Teacher: You are taking need in the wrong sense.

[en]
Hasan: How so?

[en]
Teacher: Because in the view of all the scholars, it is not obligatory for women to acquire worldly education; rather, it is permissible and recommended. Especially in today's age, we cannot call it more than a communal obligation (fard kifayah).

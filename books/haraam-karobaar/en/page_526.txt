When the military officer who had brought the copy of the Noble Quran saw this scene, he began to tremble and was overcome by terrible fear. Leaving the function unfinished, he hurried out. Only a few minutes had passed since he came out of there when a powerful earthquake struck, the earth split open, and not a single one of the people at that function survived. All of them perished, and their bodies became heaps of flesh. According to the Turkish newspapers, about three thousand people were taking part in that function. From this incident you can judge how much of the wrath of Allah the Exalted comes down on such places.

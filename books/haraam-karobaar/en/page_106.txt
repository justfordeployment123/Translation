This makes it clear that selling gold for anything else is permissible on condition that the exchange is completed in the same sitting. Many others besides me hold this same view.

[en]
Salim: Who else among the scholars holds this view?

[en]
Teacher: Imam an-Nawawi, may Allah have mercy on him, says:

[en]
“In selling one ribawi item (something in which interest can occur) for another ribawi item, taqabud (completing the exchange in the same sitting) is a condition. According to this narration, Talhah ibn Ubaidullah, may Allah be pleased with him, took the gold from Malik ibn Aws, may Allah be pleased with him, and put off giving the silver until his servant came, and in his view doing so was permissible; but when Umar, may Allah be pleased with him, made him aware, he gave it up.”

[en]
Shaykh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah also said the same in answer to a question.

[en]
Question: A woman has sold her gold bangles on credit for an agreed price. Is this permissible according to the Shariah?

[en]
Answer: The scholars agree that buying and selling gold and silver on credit is not permissible, so the bangles should be returned.

[en]
Salim: Shaykh! So a deal made in this way would be void?

[en]
Teacher: I have already set out all the evidence.

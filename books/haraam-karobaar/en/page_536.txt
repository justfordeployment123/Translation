Chapter Eighty-Nine: Acquiring Wealth Through Theft and Robbery

[en]
Ammar: The questioner asks: Teacher! I would like to know: what is the ruling in Islam on consuming people's wealth through theft and robbery?

[en]
Teacher: We seek refuge in Allah! One should fear Allah's punishment and His seizure. May Allah keep us in His safety and protection from such wicked people. Theft and robbery are counted among the greatest of forbidden acts, and they are a dangerous crime. Allah the Exalted says:

[ar]
وَالسَّارِقُ وَالسَّارِقَةُ فَاقْطَعُوا أَيْدِيَهُمَا جَزَاءً بِمَا كَسَبَا نَكَالًا مِنَ اللَّهِ ۗ وَاللَّهُ عَزِيزٌ حَكِيمْ

[ref]
[المائدة: 38]

[en]
(As for) the thief, the male and the female, amputate their hands in recompense for what they committed as a deterrent (punishment) from Allah. And Allah is Exalted in Might and Wise.

[en]
So severe a punishment is laid down for the thief because he does not attach the slightest importance to the limits set by Allah the Exalted. The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
لَقَدْ جِيءَ بِالنَّارِ حَتَّى رَأَيْتُ فِيهَا صَاحِبَ الْمِحْجَنِ يَجُرُّ قُصْبَهُ أَمْعَاءَهُ فِي النَّارِ كَانَ يَسْرِقُ الْحَاجَّ بِمِحْجَنِهْ، فَإِنْ فُطِنَ لَهُ قَالَ إِنَّمَا تَعَلَّقَ بِمِحْجَنِي، وَإِنْ غُفِلَ عَنْهُ ذَهَبَ بِهْ

Salim: That is how things are done in the gold trade.

Man: Why do you have this double standard? We buy a thing from you and sell the same thing back to you, so why is there such a big difference? The first time, you include these pearls in the weight of the gold and charge for them just as you charge for gold, and the second time, you take them out and weigh it, when Allah the Exalted says:

[en]
Woe to those who give less (than due), Who, when they take a measure from people, take in full. But if they give by measure or by weight to them, they cause loss.

[en]
When you weigh for others, you include the pearls with the gold, and when you weigh for yourselves, you separate them from the gold.

[en]
Salim: As I have said before, this is the way gold is bought and sold.

[en]
Teacher: Even if that way is forbidden?

[en]
Salim: Is it forbidden?

[en]
Teacher: This one proof that this brother has just presented is enough to show that it is forbidden, but to reassure you, countless proofs can also be given from the pure Sunnah. For example, Fadalah ibn Ubaid, may Allah be pleased with him, said: At the time of the Battle of Khaybar I bought a necklace for twelve dinars that had gold and beads in it. I separated them and found more than twelve dinars (of gold) in it.

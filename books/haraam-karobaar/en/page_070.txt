[en]
A person should be certain that Allah the Exalted does not break His promise. A person should be patient, should not act in haste, and should firmly believe that lawful wealth, even if it is little, is better, more blessed and more beneficial than a great deal of the forbidden. Allah the Exalted says:

[ar]
قُلْ لَا يَسْتَوِي الْخَبِيثُ وَالطَّيِّبُ وَلَوْ أَعْجَبَكَ كَثْرَةُ الْخَبِيثْ

[ref]
[المائدة: 100]

[en]
Say, “Not equal are the evil and the good, although the abundance of evil might impress you.”

[en]
Even supposing the profit earned is small, that is no excuse for a Muslim to commit a forbidden act merely for its sake. Rather, he should be patient with the little, be content, and be pleased with what Allah has given. It may be that Allah the Exalted is testing his faith and his trust. It may be that Allah the Exalted will put blessing for him into that little, and open up for him sources of provision that he never even imagined.

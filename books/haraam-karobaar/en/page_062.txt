Teacher: May Allah protect you. The kind of buying and selling you were talking about is called a “deferred sale” in the Shariah. There are many kinds of buying and selling, and this is one of them: that is, the price is paid first and the goods are handed over later, unlike the sale in which the goods are handed over first and the price is paid later. The jurists say about this sale:

[en]
“It is a sale in which a Muslim buys goods whose description is known, and the time at which he will receive the goods from the seller is also known. As soon as the deal is made, he gives the seller the full sum in advance, and when the appointed time comes he receives the goods from him. The jurists have also named it ‘the sale of the needy’, because it meets the needs of both (the seller and the buyer). The man with capital wants to buy some goods with his money, and the owner of the goods seeks to get money so that he can spend it on his needs.”

[en]
This kind of trade is permissible under certain conditions. When those conditions are fully met, the trade is valid; otherwise the trade is void.

[en]
Mahmud: Shaykh! What are those conditions?

[en]
Teacher: The conditions are as follows:

[en]
First: The kind of goods, their quantity and so on must be specified, so that they do not cause a dispute later.

Teacher: Mustafa, I advise you: this is impermissible and forbidden income, and you are bringing up your children on it, whereas the noble Prophet, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
كُلُّ جَسَدٍ نَبَتَ مِنَ السُّحْتِ فَالنَّارُ أَوْلَى بِهْ

[ref]
صحيح الجامع، رقم الحديث [4519]

[en]
“Every body that is nourished on forbidden earnings is more deserving of the Fire.”

[en]
Mustafa, fear Allah and ask Allah to forgive you this crime before the Day of Resurrection comes; otherwise all the people you have deceived will come to claim their rights from you on the Day of Resurrection, and at that time you will be able to do nothing but feel ashamed. Yes, at that time the reckoning will be made with good deeds and bad deeds. Keep this firmly in mind: a life of poverty in this world is better than a life of disgrace and humiliation in the Hereafter.

[en]
Mustafa: Teacher! I call you to witness: I will not do this again.

[en]
Ammar: I think you should turn back to Allah right now and stop it.

[en]

[en]
In the sale of a ribawi item (something in which interest can occur) for another ribawi item, for example the sale of gold for money, it is not right to pay part of the price and leave part of it on credit.

“Selling silver for gold is interest, except when it is in cash (then it is permissible); selling wheat for wheat is interest, except when it is in cash (then it is permissible); selling barley for barley is interest, except when it is in cash (then it is permissible); selling dates for dates is interest, except when it is in cash (then it is permissible).”

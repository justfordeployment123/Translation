Teacher: Brother, it is necessary for every employee to do his full duty and to carry out his responsibilities honestly and faithfully. He should not take days off without reason, or take leave day after day by putting forward all sorts of tricks, pretexts and excuses. When a salary has been agreed with the institution in return for work, then not doing the work fully while taking the full salary comes under the heading of betrayal of trust. Every Muslim should honour the terms agreed with his institution.

[en]
Ubaid: There is a great deal of room in religion, but you are very hard on people!

[en]
Teacher: It is not a matter of strictness; it is a matter of keeping to the commands of Allah the Exalted. The institution's work is a trust in your hands, and you must not be guilty of betraying a trust, because Allah the Exalted says:

[ar]

[ref]
[الأنفال: 27]

[en]
O you who have believed, do not betray Allah and the Messenger or betray your trusts while you know (the consequence).

[en]
Whatever salary the institution has agreed with you in return for this post, you are responsible and answerable for it. If you do not do justice to the work, it is as though you have broken your agreement and violated its conditions, whereas the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
الْمُسْلِمُونَ عَلَى شُرُوطِهِمْ

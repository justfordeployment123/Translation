Buyer: There are four rak'ahs.

[en]
Teacher: And surely you have prayed four from the very beginning?

[en]
Buyer: Yes, everyone prays four.

[en]
Teacher: Yes, I pray four as well. But tell me, in which verse of the Quran is the command to pray four rak'ahs each for Zuhr, Asr and Isha?

[en]
Buyer: What do you mean?

[en]
Teacher: What I mean is that the rulings of the religion are not all in the Quran alone; rather, there are countless rulings that are stated in the hadiths and of which there is no trace at all in the Quran. I said these things because you were speaking so loudly and forcefully against Allah and His Messenger, peace be upon him, when in fact there is also a command in the Quran that makes trading gold in instalments forbidden.

[en]
Buyer: In which verse is that command?

[en]
Teacher: Allah the Exalted says:

[ref]

And whatever the Messenger has given you - take; and what he has forbidden you - refrain from.

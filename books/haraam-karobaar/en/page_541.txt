Chapter Ninety-One: Working in Cafés, Clubs and the Like

[en]
Ammar: Teacher! What is the ruling on working in shisha cafés, clubs and the like?

[en]
Teacher: About shisha cafés I will say just this: according to the noble scholars, merely sitting in such places is considered undesirable and disliked.

[en]
The scholars say:

[en]
“Only a person devoid of sense and wisdom sits in such places, because nowadays the people who gather in such places are criminals, senseless people and people of bad habits. It is very shameful for any respectable and dignified man to go there.”

[ref]
صلاة الأمة [347/5]

[en]
Now judge for yourself: in a place where even sitting is considered disliked and undesirable, what will the ruling be on working there?

[en]
If you work there, it goes without saying that you will serve those who come there cigarettes, paan, cannabis, opium and the like. If you say that you will not do this work there, you will at least see these forbidden things being committed, so in both cases you will be an equal partner in the sin. As for clubs and brothels, remember that if in the café you served cigarettes, here you will serve alcohol; if in the café you see only sinful and wicked men, in clubs and brothels there will be unveiled and indecent women with them as well. That is, the matter of clubs and brothels is even more dangerous than that of cafés.

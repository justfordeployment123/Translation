Ammar: Teacher! Is it disliked to stay up late and talk after the Isha prayer?

[en]
Teacher: Staying up after the Isha prayer is not disliked in every case; rather, in some situations it is permissible. For example, first: for the sake of gaining knowledge; second: for the sake of reforming and training Muslims; third: talking with a guest; fourth: for the sake of teaching and training one's family. That is, staying up and talking after the Isha prayer for purposes of the Shariah is right.

Chapter Forty-One: Hiring Out a Bull for Mating

[en]
Abu Jum'a: Bahnasi! Is your bull in the cattle yard?

[en]
Bahnasi: Yes, it is tied up at such-and-such a spot in the cattle yard.

[en]
Abu Jum'a: We want to have our cow mated.

[en]
Bahnasi: Have her mated whenever you like.

[en]
Abu Jum'a: Bahnasi, my friend! Yesterday I took the cow to such-and-such an old man's cattle yard, and he asked me for ten dirhams.

[en]
Bahnasi: That is unjust. In their dealings people have no pity at all on others. Teacher! What is your view on this?

[en]
Teacher: On what?

[en]
Abu Jum'a: When we have our animals made pregnant, the owners of the bulls charge a heavy price for it.

[en]
Teacher: Is money really charged for it?

[en]
Bahnasi: Yes. Is there anything wrong in that?

[en]
Teacher: Yes, this is a forbidden and invalid sale. Technically it is called bay' 'asb al-fahl (selling a bull's mating).

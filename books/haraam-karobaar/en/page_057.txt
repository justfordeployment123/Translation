[en]
“Whoever buys grain must not sell it on until he has taken it into his possession.”

[en]
Ibn Abbas, may Allah be pleased with them both, said: I think that the ruling for everything is the same as the ruling for grain.

[en]
The third proof: Hakim ibn Hizam, may Allah be pleased with him, said: I said: O Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him! I buy and sell goods; what of this is lawful for me and what is forbidden? He, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
إِذَا اشْتَرَيْتَ شَيْئًا فَلَا تَبِعْهُ حَتَّى تَقْبِضَهْ

[ref]
صحيح الجامع، رقم الحديث [342]

[en]
“When you buy something, do not sell it on until you have taken it into your possession.”

[en]
The majority of scholars say: It is not permissible at all to sell goods on before taking possession of them.

[en]
Ibn Umar, may Allah be pleased with them both, said that the Prophet, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
مَنِ ابْتَاعَ طَعَامًا فَلاَ يَبِعْهُ حَتَّى يَسْتَوْفِيَهْ

[ref]
صحيح البخاري، رقم الحديث [2135] صحيح مسلم، رقم الحديث [1525]

[en]
“Whoever buys grain must not sell it on until he has received it in full.”

[en]
That is, until he has moved it from the place where he bought it to his own warehouse.

[en]
Ayyad: Why, all these hadiths make this kind of dealing forbidden.

[en]
Teacher: Yes, may Allah bless you. Abu Rashid! These texts of the Shariah decide that the way you buy rice and then sell it to another trader before you have even taken it to your warehouse is not permissible. And you farmers! If Abu Rashid starts doing this again in future, do not allow him to leave the rice with you.

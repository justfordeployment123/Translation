[en]
Allah the Exalted has given this warning: that the blessing goes out of wealth gained through interest. Allah says:

[ar]
يَمْحَقُ اللَّهُ الرِّبَا

[ref]
[البقرة: 276]

[en]
Allah destroys interest.

[en]
That is, one by one, interest destroys everything.

[en]
The Prophet, peace be upon him, condemned it even more severely. He, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
اَلرِّبَا اثْنَانِ وَسَبْعُوْنَ بَابًا أَدْنَاهَا مِثْلُ إِتْيَانِ الرَّجُلِ أُمَّهْ

[ref]
الطبراني في الكبير [1/143/1] صحيح الجامع، رقم الحديث [1871]

[en]
Rather, interest even causes the mercy of Allah the Exalted to be held back.

[en]
Abu Ali: I seek refuge with Allah! How can that be?

[en]
Teacher: The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
لَعَنَ اللَّهُ الرِّبَا، وَآكِلَهْ، وَمُوْكِلَهُ وَكَاتِبَهْ، وَشَاهِدَهْ

[ref]
صحيح الجامع، رقم الحديث [5094]

[en]
“Allah the Exalted has cursed interest, the one who consumes interest, the one who pays it, the one who writes up dealings in interest, and the one who acts as a witness to them.”

[en]
He also said:

[en]
“Allah the Exalted has cursed the one who consumes interest, the one who pays it, its scribe and its witnesses.”

[en]
And he, peace be upon him, also said that they are all equal in sin.

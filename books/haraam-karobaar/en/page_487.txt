Chapter Seventy-Nine: A Woman Working as a Singer and Actress

[en]
Husain: Teacher! From your views it seems that, in your opinion, it is forbidden for a woman to sing and play music, to dance, or to take part in the work of music in any way at all, whereas set rules and regulations have been laid down for all these things; no work gets done without a general rule.

[en]
Teacher: Oh, what a pity! Servant of Allah! The matter of women who take part in singing, playing music and dancing is even more serious than that of the women who take part in fashion shows. Women singers are the biggest root of corruption and destruction. This is the worst of deeds, and its earnings are the worst of earnings. There is an Arabic saying that a free woman will endure hunger, but she will not let her breasts be pawed at. So how can a respectable woman put her honour at stake to earn money?

[en]
Ammar: Teacher! May Allah bring benefit to people through you.

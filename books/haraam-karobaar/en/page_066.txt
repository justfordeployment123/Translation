Chapter Five: Packing Goods Short of Their Proper Weight

[en]
The teacher and the student are both eating and talking.

[en]
Ammar: Shaykh! May Allah increase you in knowledge and bless you.

[en]
Teacher: May Allah protect you.

[en]
After eating, the two of them thanked Allah and began their round again. Meanwhile they heard the voice of a man who was standing on his vehicle and calling out: Get fifty kilos for forty-nine pounds! The two of them turned towards him and saw that he was selling rice. The Shaykh said happily to Ammar: This is a better deal. We buy one kilo for one pound and fifty kilos for fifty pounds, but here fifty kilos are going for forty-nine. Come, let us buy some. We will gain a kilo.

[en]
The Shaykh gave the trader forty-nine pounds and bought a sack.

[en]
Ammar: Shaykh! What do you think, if we weigh this sack? This same trader has scales too, because he deals in both wholesale and retail.

[en]
Teacher: Why do you want to weigh it?

[en]
Ammar: So that we can be sure that it really is a full fifty kilos, and not less.

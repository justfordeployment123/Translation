Chapter Ninety-Two: Working on the Construction of Hotels, Restaurants, Tourist Centres and Other Places of Noise and Clamour

[en]
Ammar: Teacher! If being employed in such places is forbidden, then while these places are being built, what is the ruling on working, labouring and so on in their construction?

[en]
Teacher: In our view, the branch follows the root, and places built for forbidden activities are all branches of that root. That is, the root is that the work done in such places is forbidden. We have already explained in detail earlier that all these things fall into the category of evils, indecency, nudity, sin and aggression. So when all these things are forbidden, it goes without saying that working on places being built especially for them is also forbidden. Whoever works on building such places is declaring war on Allah and His Messenger, peace be upon him, and he is helping people who commit evils.

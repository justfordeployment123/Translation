Chapter Eighty-Two: Buying and Selling Clothes with Obscene Pictures and High-Heeled Shoes

In the shop the teacher saw clothes for women, and especially for young girls, with obscene pictures on them, and high-heeled shoes were also lying in one corner of the shop. The teacher turned to the shopkeeper.

[en]
Teacher: May Allah bless you! What are these things?

[en]
Shopkeeper: These are women's clothes. Would you like to buy them?

[en]

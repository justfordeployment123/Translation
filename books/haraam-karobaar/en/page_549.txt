Chapter Ninety-Six: Working in Films, Dramas and the Like

[en]
Ammar: Teacher! What is the ruling on working in the films and dramas shown on television?

[en]
Teacher: To know the ruling on working in the dramas, films, morning shows and other indecent programmes broadcast on television nowadays, it is enough for you to reflect on a few of the expressions and lines that are usually spoken between men and women on the television screen. The following four expressions are generally the most used:

[en]
First: Love and romance, then a quarrel. Second: A quarrel, then love and romance. Third: Marriage, then divorce. Fourth: After divorce, marriage again.

[en]
Besides these lines and expressions, men and women kissing and embracing, touching one another and other indecent acts are also seen openly on the television screen. In this respect, all these programmes that are shown in the name of entertainment and the fine arts become a cause of indecency spreading in society. For example:

[en]
A culture of nudity and vulgarity, the mixing of men and women, the spread of music, the adultery of all the limbs. Children and young people make such sinful and wicked people their ideals; religious and moral values are trampled underfoot; irreligion is promoted; sacred things are mocked; and shirk (associating partners with Allah) spreads, that is, during filming some scenes are made that bring to mind the idea of something other than Allah. So working in this field amounts to declaring war on Allah and His Messenger, peace be upon him.

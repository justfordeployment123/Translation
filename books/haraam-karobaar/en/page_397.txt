Manager: Teacher! Wait a moment, do not pass judgement so quickly. Consider this too: pictures have become a need of ours, and it is worth noting that the pictures that are forbidden are those in the form of statues that cast a shadow; apart from these, pictures that cast no shadow are not forbidden.

[en]
Teacher: If you study carefully all the hadiths that have come about pictures being forbidden, this objection of yours will not remain.

[en]
Manager: How so?

[en]
Teacher: Aishah, may Allah be pleased with her, narrates: The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, came to me, and I had covered a niche of mine with a curtain that had pictures on it. When he, peace be upon him, saw it, he tore it down, and the colour of his face changed. He, peace be upon him, said:

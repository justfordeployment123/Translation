Employment in a bank that deals in interest will certainly fall under one of the categories mentioned above. So if the person who takes such employment does not repent before death, he will die the death of one who is cursed.

The second thing is working for an insurance company. First of all, let us try to find out what insurance itself is.

[en]
Ammar: Yes, please tell me: what is it?

[en]
Teacher: Insurance is of two kinds:

[en]
First: Life insurance: This means that a person insures his life with an insurance company, and an agreement is settled between them that the person concerned will pay the company such-and-such an amount in instalments over a long period. Then, when this person dies, the company will pay his heirs such-and-such an amount and will also fix a monthly allowance in his name.

[en]
Second: Property insurance: This means that a person has his property, for example a car, a plot or a factory and so on, insured, and the agreement he settles with a company is that he will pay such-and-such an instalment every month as insurance for the thing concerned, and during this time, whenever any damage comes to that thing or any accident happens to it, the company will pay all the expenses.

[ar]
وَلاَ تُشِفُّوا بَعْضَهَا عَلَى بَعْضْ

[en]
(Do not make either side more or less than the other): that is, on the grounds that one item is new and the other old, and the new one is more expensive. This is interest (riba) and forbidden, because in such a case one item exceeds the other.

[en]
The noble Prophet, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
لاَ تَبِيعُوا الذَّهَبَ بِالذَّهَبِ إِلاَّ وَزْنًا بِوَزْنٍ مِثْلاً بِمِثْلٍ سَوَاءً بِسَوَاءْ

[ref]
صحيح مسلم، رقم الحديث [1584]

[en]
“Make the sale of gold for gold equal in weight, like for like, and equal.”

[en]
The noble Prophet, peace be upon him, also said:

[ar]
الذَّهَبُ بِالذَّهَبِ وَزْنًا بِوَزْنٍ مِثْلاً بِمِثْلْ، وَالْفِضَّةُ بِالْفِضَّةِ وَزْنًا بِوَزْنٍ مِثْلاً بِمِثْلْ، فَمَنْ زَادَ أَوِ اسْتَزَادَ فَهُوَ رِبَا

[ref]
صحيح مسلم، رقم الحديث [1588]

[en]
“Sell gold for gold and silver for silver, equal for equal. Whoever gives more or takes more, this excess is interest.”

[ar]
زَادَ أَوِ اسْتَزَادْ

[en]
That is, whoever gives more or asks for more.

[en]
Ibn Shaddad, may Allah have mercy on him, says:

[en]
“This hadith is proof that it is impermissible to exchange jewellery for jewellery if they are not equal in weight; likewise it is also impermissible to take extra for the goldsmith's work and craftsmanship.”

[ref]
دلائل الأحكام [289/3]

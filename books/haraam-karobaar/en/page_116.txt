Summary of the Gold Market Tour

[en]
Ammar: Praise be to Allah, today we stopped people from the following five evils:

[en]
First: Buying and selling old gold for new gold with more on one side than the other, or by paying extra money, is forbidden. Gold must be sold for gold equal for equal, whether the gold is old or new.

[en]
Second: Tampering with the weight of gold when weighing it on the scales; its ruling is clear: it is forbidden.

[en]
Third: Polishing old gold and selling it at the rate of new gold without letting the customer know; this too is clearly forbidden.

[en]
Fourth: Buying and selling gold in instalments is forbidden, not permissible, because there are many clear texts and proofs that it is forbidden.

[en]
Fifth: Removing the pearls and beautiful gems from gold jewellery when buying and weighing it.

[en]
Teacher: May Allah bless you.

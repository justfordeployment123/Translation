Chapter Ten: Buying and Selling Gold in Instalments

[en]
Meanwhile a family came to buy jewellery for their bride. They chose some jewellery and bought goods worth about ten thousand pounds, but they had only nine thousand five hundred pounds. The head of the family said to Salim:

[en]
Buyer: Salim! I have nine thousand five hundred pounds with me right now. What do you think: could I come and pay the remaining five hundred pounds tomorrow?

[en]
Salim: You are an honest man. Take this jewellery, and the next time you are passing this way, pay the rest.

[en]
Teacher: Salim! Wait a moment.

[en]
Salim: Yes, Shaykh.

[en]
Teacher: Buying and selling gold in this way is impermissible.

[en]
The man looked at the Shaykh and spoke mockingly.

[en]
Buyer: So now you are issuing fatwas on the gold trade too! What do you mullahs know about this? Is there any verse in the Quran that makes trading gold in instalments forbidden?

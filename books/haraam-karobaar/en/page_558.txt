Chapter Ninety-Nine: Making a Deal over a Muslim's Deal and Buying What He Has Bought

[en]
Ammar: A questioner says: We have heard this hadith of the noble Prophet, peace be upon him, that he, peace be upon him, forbade a Muslim to make a deal over the deal of his Muslim brother. Please explain what this means.

[en]
Teacher: Fix this firmly in your mind: making a deal over a Muslim's deal and buying what he has bought is forbidden, and wealth gained by this means falls into the category of the forbidden. The proof of this is this hadith of the noble Prophet, peace be upon him, in which he, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
لاَ يَبِعْ بَعْضُكُمْ عَلَى بَيْعِ بَعْضْ

[ref]
صحيح البخاري، رقم الحديث [2140] صحيح مسلم، رقم الحديث [1412]

[en]
“None of you should make a deal over another's deal.”

[en]
Ammar: What form does this take? Please explain a little.

[en]
Teacher: It takes two forms:

[en]
First: Making one's own deal over someone else's deal.

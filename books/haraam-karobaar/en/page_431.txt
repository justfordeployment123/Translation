The doctor told the nurses: Leave her alone, lock the room from outside, and let her die. The nurses did just that. They left her alone in the room and locked it from outside. But in reality the woman was not alone; her Lord was with her. The One for whose pleasure she was bearing so much pain, how could He leave her alone?

[en]
After that, the woman suddenly began to have labour pains, and without anyone's help she gave birth to a child, safe and sound. When the nurses standing outside by the door heard the baby crying, they quickly opened the door, and as soon as they saw the baby they were dumbfounded. Then they asked the woman how all this had happened. She told them the whole story.

Sa'd: Ma sha Allah! What is the second story?

[en]
Second: Abu Ammar Wahid Bali relates that in our town there lived a girl who was veiled and of good character. Her father was a so-called enlightened and free-thinking man. One day the girl fell ill. Her father said: Come, let us go to a doctor to get medicine. The girl replied: To a doctor? Am I to uncover my face in front of a non-mahram man? No, this cannot be. I accept death, but I will not go to be treated by a male doctor.

[en]
Her father became furious and said: By Allah, if you will not go to a doctor, then I will not take you to a female doctor either, even if you die. He stubbornly held to this, but the girl stood firm on her word, and her father left her in that state.

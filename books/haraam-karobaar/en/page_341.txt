Chapter Fifty: Joining False Hair (Wigs) to Real Hair

[en]
The barber from next door also came over and began to listen to the conversation. When the teacher had finished answering the young man's question, the barber spoke up: Teacher! Answer a question for me too.

[en]
Teacher: Yes, what is the question?

[en]
Barber: What is the ruling of the Shariah on the “barukah” (wig), that is, joining false hair to one's real hair to add to one's beauty?

[en]
Teacher: There is no doubt that this too is forbidden, because it is narrated from Asma, may Allah be pleased with her, that a woman came to the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, and said: O Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, I have married off one of my daughters, and now she has fallen ill, and because of this her hair has begun to fall out. Shall I attach artificial hair to it? He, peace be upon him, replied:

[ar]

[ref]
صحيح البخاري، رقم الحديث [5935] صحيح مسلم، رقم الحديث [2122]

[en]
“Allah the Exalted has cursed the woman who attaches artificial hair and the woman who has it attached.”

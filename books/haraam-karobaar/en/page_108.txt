Chapter Eleven: Removing the Pearls Set in Jewellery When Buying It, and Counting Them in and Weighing Them When Selling It

[en]
A man came into the shop. He had a gold necklace that was set with beautiful pearls and gems, and he wanted to sell it. He greeted everyone present and turned to Salim.

[en]
Man: Will you buy gold?

[en]
Salim: Yes. What do you want to sell?

[en]
Man: I have a gold necklace.

[en]
Salim: Show it to me.

[en]
Man: Here, have a look.

[en]
Salim took the necklace and began removing the pearls and gems from it, so that he would weigh only the gold and leave the pearls out of the reckoning.

[en]
Man: When you buy jewellery from a customer, you people take the pearls out, but when you sell it, you count them in as well and weigh them along with it. Why is that?

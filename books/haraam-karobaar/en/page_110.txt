“The necklace is not to be sold until (the gold and the pearls) have been separated.”

[en]
Hanash as-San'ani, may Allah have mercy on him, said: We were with Fadalah ibn Ubaid, may Allah be pleased with him, on a military expedition. A necklace fell to the share of my companions and me, with gold, silver and gems in it. I wanted to buy it. I asked Fadalah ibn Ubaid, and he said: Take out its gold and put it in one pan of the scales, and put your gold in the other pan, and on no account take anything but like for like, for I heard the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, say:

[ar]
مَنْ كَانَ يُؤْمِنُ بِاللَّهِ وَالْيَوْمِ الآخِرِ فَلاَ يَأْخُذَنَّ إِلاَّ مِثْلاً بِمِثْلْ

[ref]
صحيح مسلم، رقم الحديث [1591,92]

[en]
“Whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day must take nothing but like for like.”

[en]
Imam an-Nawawi, may Allah have mercy on him, says in his commentary on this hadith:

[en]
“This hadith is evidence that it is impermissible to sell the pearls set in gold jewellery at the rate of gold; rather, they should be separated and sold on their own. The same applies to silver, wheat, salt and all ribawi items; that is, all ribawi items should be separated from the other things attached to them and then sold, whether these are in large quantity or small. The words of the noble Prophet, peace be upon him:

[ar]
لاَ تُبَاعُ حَتَّى تُفَصَّلْ

[en]
(It is not to be sold until it has been separated) command this explicitly.”

[ref]
شرح مسلم [17 , 19/11]

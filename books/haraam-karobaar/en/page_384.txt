Chapter Fifty-Eight: Buying and Selling Obscene Magazines and Periodicals

[en]
In a cupboard in the bookshop the teacher saw magazines and periodicals full of obscenity, vulgarity and shamelessness, containing stories of immoral, sinful and wicked men and women; besides these, he also saw some novels.

[en]
Teacher: May Allah the Exalted keep you safe from every evil! What is this lying here?

[en]
Manager: These are cultural magazines and periodicals. Is there anything wrong with these too?

[en]
Teacher: There is even more obscenity and nakedness in these than in the earlier books. For example:

[en]
First: Such magazines promote obscenity, because they describe means and methods that pave the way to obscenity and nakedness for people.

[en]
Second: These magazines tell of the relationships, mixing, meetings and contacts of immoral, shameless and wicked men and women. Their conversations with each other are printed in them, along with extremely obscene pictures in which men and women are shown in half-naked clothing, chatting with each other and sitting together. These pictures are published with the beauty and good looks of the women brought out to the full, and in their conversations soft and tender words are chosen that stir up a person's feelings, because of which a taste for romance grows in a person.

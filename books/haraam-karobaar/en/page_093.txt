[en]
Woe to those who give less (than due), Who, when they take a measure from people, take in full. But if they give by measure or by weight to them, they cause loss.

[en]
For the trader who gives short measure and weight there is a valley in Hell called “Wayl”, from whose severity Hell itself seeks refuge every day.

[en]
Fear Allah, and be so careful in matters of measure and weight that people have no misunderstanding about you. We are not blaming you, but we do warn you: make your soul so pure that it cannot lead you into this evil. May Allah bless us all with guidance.

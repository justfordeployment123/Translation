Introduction

[en]
A person whose heart has no fear of Allah does not care at all how he is earning his wealth or where he is spending it. To amass wealth he takes up impermissible means, such as theft, bribery, plunder, interest (riba), seizing an orphan's property, or making a forbidden act a source of wages; likewise fortune-telling, singing and playing music, embezzling from the public treasury, unlawfully seizing someone else's property, and begging from people without need. All of the above forms fall under the category of the forbidden. That person then uses the wealth he gained by forbidden means for food and drink, for transport and for building a house, or he does business with it and keeps on increasing it. In reality, this person is putting what is forbidden into his stomach.

[en]
On the Day of Resurrection a servant will be asked: O servant! Where did you earn your wealth from, and where did you spend it? On that day the one who earned forbidden wealth will feel great remorse, and he will be ruined. So a person should quickly free himself of this evil deed.

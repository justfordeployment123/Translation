Chapter Fifteen: Holding Back Milk in Animals' Udders

[en]
Teacher Salih set out with his promising student to make a round of the Livestock Market outside the city. On reaching the market, the two of them went and stood beside a man. The man had come to the market to sell his cow. The cow's udders were greatly swollen, and she was standing with her legs spread wide.

[en]
The teacher addressed the man.

[en]
Teacher: Why are this cow's udders so swollen?

[en]
Man: Because they are full of milk.

[en]
Teacher: It does not look that way to me. Tell me the truth: when did you last milk her?

[en]
Man: There is no might and no power except with Allah... Why do you need to press me so hard? I am telling you that this is milk.

[en]
Teacher: I am surprised, that is why I am asking.

[en]
Man: Will you buy this cow?

[en]
Teacher: No, I am not a buyer, but this sight has astonished me.

[en]
Man: I will tell you: I have not milked her since yesterday. And do not tell anyone this, because I have come to sell her.

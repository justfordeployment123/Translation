Teacher: Abu al-Minhal says: Sharik sold me silver on credit until the Hajj season. After that, Sharik came to me again and said that this deal was not right. I had made the same kind of deal in the market too, and no one had objected, so I went to al-Bara' ibn Azib, may Allah be pleased with him, and asked him about it. He replied: When the noble Prophet, peace be upon him, came to Madinah, we used to buy and sell in this way, and the noble Prophet, peace be upon him, said:

Teacher: The way you make your deals is impermissible and outright interest. When there is inequality in any one kind of goods, whether gold, dates or anything else, it becomes interest. Listen to the fatwa of the great scholar Ibn Baz, may Allah have mercy on him, on this.

[en]
Question: A woman takes old gold to the market and asks the shopkeeper how much it is worth. He tells her the price, and the woman says: Give me new gold in exchange for this price. What is the solution to this issue in the Shariah?

[en]
Answer: This is impermissible, because in buying and selling gold for gold there must be equality and sameness. It is established from the noble Prophet, peace be upon him, that he, peace be upon him, said:

[ar]
الذَّهَبُ بِالذَّهَبِ مِثْلاً بِمِثْلْ، سَوَاءً بِسَوَاءْ، وَزْنًا بِوَزْنْ، يَدًا بِيَدْ، فَمَنْ زَادَ أَوِ اسْتَزَادَ فَقَدْ أَرْبَى

[ref]
صحيح مسلم، رقم الحديث [1584]

[en]
“The sale of gold for gold must be equal for equal and hand to hand. Whoever gives more or takes more has, as it were, taken interest.”

[en]
It is impermissible for you to sell gold for other gold with an excess, because then the condition of sameness is no longer met. In such a case the way laid down by the Shariah is that you sell your gold and take its price, and then after that buy new gold as you need, whether from the same man or from someone else. There is no interest in this.

Chapter Twenty-Seven: Buying and Selling at the Time of the Friday Sermon and the Friday Prayer

[en]
The teacher and his student had left the shop and were coming back to the car when, on the way, they met a friend of the teacher's, Muhammad Hassan, who owned a superstore in the market. The teacher shook hands with Hassan and embraced him. After each had asked after the other's well-being, the teacher asked Hassan:

[en]
Teacher: Hassan! A month ago, on my way back from Cairo, I passed by your superstore when the second adhan of Friday had already been called, and your store was still open at that time?

[en]
Hassan: Shaykh, I had gone to the mosque on time; but yes, our employees were at the store, and they were selling goods and so on. Is there anything wrong with that?

[en]
Teacher: Hassan! Do you not know that buying and selling at the time of the Friday prayer is not merely impermissible but invalid, especially for a man on whom the Friday prayer is obligatory?

[en]
Hassan: Teacher! There is no need for such strictness and severity. Yes, the store should not stay open at the time of the Friday prayer, and in sha Allah we will take care of that in future. But your point that buying and selling after the second adhan is invalid is beyond my understanding.

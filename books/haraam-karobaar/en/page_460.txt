To the man who wants to write a will in favour of one of his heirs, or who wants to deprive the other heirs of their right by some other trick or pretext, we would respectfully point out that this will and these various tricks and pretexts are void and impermissible, because Allah the Exalted has prescribed fixed shares in place of the will. Now, whatever right a person has in the inheritance, he must receive it in every case. However, if you are afraid, in the matter of your daughter, that the other heirs will not treat her well later on, then to avoid such a situation Allah the Exalted has given you an excellent piece of advice. By acting on this advice, you can be free of fear in the matter of your daughter.

[en]
Abdur-Rahim: What is that advice?

[en]
Teacher: Allah the Exalted has advised us to hold fast to fear of Allah, to keep away from acts of disobedience and to speak what is true. Allah the Exalted says:

[en]
And let those (executors and guardians) fear (injustice) as if they (themselves) had left weak offspring behind and feared for them. So let them fear Allah and speak words of appropriate justice.

[en]
So always keep fearing Allah in what concerns you and your children, and aspire to good words and a good intention; Allah the Exalted will protect you.

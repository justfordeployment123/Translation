# English source for book page 316

[en]
Honour and dignity lie only in following and obeying him, peace be upon him.

[en]
Young man: I would like you to give some proofs that the beard is obligatory and that shaving it is forbidden.

[en]
Teacher: Listen to proofs from the Noble Quran:

[en]
First: Allah the Exalted said:

[en]
And I will command them so they will change the creation of Allah.

[en]
Cutting or shaving the beard amounts to changing the creation of Allah the Exalted.

[en]
Second: Allah the Most High says:

[en]
Those are the ones whom Allah has guided, so from their guidance take an example.

[en]
All the prophets and messengers had beards. If you do not believe it, read the story of Musa and Harun:

[en]
Do not seize (me) by my beard or by my head.

[en]

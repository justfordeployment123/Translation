# English source for book page 291

[en]
The noble Companions replied: The bankrupt is the one who has no dirhams and no worldly goods. The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Indeed, the bankrupt of my Ummah is the person who will come on the Day of Resurrection with good deeds such as prayers, fasts and zakah, but along with that he will have insulted one person, slandered another, consumed (wrongfully) the wealth of another, shed the blood of another (without right) and struck another. So this one (the wronged) will be given that one's (the wrongdoer's) good deeds. If the wrongdoer's good deeds run out while the right of those he wronged still remains, their (the wronged ones') bad deeds will be put on him (the wrongdoer), and then he will be thrown into Hell.”

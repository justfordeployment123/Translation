# English source for book page 428

[hen]
Chapter Sixty-Six: Women Being Examined by a Male Doctor When a Female Doctor Is Available

[en]
Teacher: Sa'd! I have seen that, even though a female doctor is available, you have brought your wife to a male doctor to be examined. Why did you do that?

[en]
Sa'd: Is this forbidden too?

[en]
Teacher: Sa'd! It is permissible for women to be examined by male doctors under certain conditions.

[en]
Sa'd: What are these conditions?

[en]
Teacher: First: That there is no female doctor around you and near you. However, if there is a female doctor far away and you can bear the cost of reaching her, you should have the treatment done by her.

[en]
Second: If the illness is truly dangerous and treatment is very necessary, then she may turn to a male doctor. Going to a male doctor merely because of a headache or a slight fever is not right.

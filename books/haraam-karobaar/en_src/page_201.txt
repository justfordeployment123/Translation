# English source for book page 201

[en]
Teacher: Servant of Allah! For a man on whom the Friday prayer is obligatory, buying and selling from the second adhan until the end of the Friday prayer is forbidden and invalid.

[en]
Ammar: Teacher! On whom is the Friday prayer obligatory?

[en]
Teacher: The Friday prayer is obligatory on a Muslim man who is sane, adult and resident; on anyone else, that is, the insane, the traveller, children and women, the Friday prayer is not obligatory.

[en]
Hassan: Is there even one proof that makes trading at the time of the Friday prayer forbidden?

[en]
Teacher: Yes, the proof for it is in the Noble Quran.

[en]
Hassan: Which one?

[en]
Teacher: Allah the Exalted says:

[en]
{{QEN 62:9}}

[en]
The commentators of the Quran say:

[en]
“This blessed verse makes trading impermissible and forbidden during the times of the Friday sermon and the Friday prayer.”

[en]
Hassan: Teacher! What is the solution to this?

[en]
Teacher: Hassan! The solution is very easy: the shop or store and so on should be closed at that time.

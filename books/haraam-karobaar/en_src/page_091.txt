Most of the people who come to you shopkeepers cannot read or write; they cannot read what is written on the scales. And the scales are the kind that have English written on them. Never mind English, most of our people cannot even read Urdu. So they trust the shopkeeper and buy the goods, but you shopkeepers take unfair advantage of their lack of knowledge. Outwardly the shopkeepers seem to show great respect, and they even charge a low price, but in reality they cheat through the scales.

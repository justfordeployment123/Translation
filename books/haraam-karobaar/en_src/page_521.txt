# English source for book page 521

[en]
“Allah the Exalted has taken it upon Himself to give the one who drinks alcohol tinat al-khabal to drink. The noble Companions asked: O Messenger of Allah, what is tinat al-khabal? He, peace be upon him, said: (It means) the pus of the people of Hell.”

[en]
It is narrated from Abdullah ibn Abbas, may Allah be pleased with them both, that the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“If one who is addicted to alcohol dies in that state, he will meet Allah the Exalted in such a state as though he were a worshipper of idols.”

[en]
The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Every intoxicant is forbidden.”

[en]

# English source for book page 311

[hen]
Chapter Forty-Five: Shaving the Beard

[en]
That young man had his hair cut and left, saying As-salamu alaikum. After him, the young man whose turn it was sat down in the chair in front of the barber.

[en]
Barber: Yes, sir, do you want a haircut, or do you want your beard shaved?

[en]
Young man: No, I only want my beard shaved.

[en]
(The barber put a cloth over the young man's chest and began to rub cream on his face. The teacher quickly spoke up.)

[en]
Teacher: Oh brother! Wait a moment and listen to what I have to say.

[en]
Young man: (angrily) Now you are going to say that shaving the beard is forbidden too?!

[en]
Teacher: Please! Just listen to me for five minutes, and after that do whatever you like.

[en]
(The barber stopped, and everyone in the shop turned their attention to the teacher.)

[en]
Teacher: Servants of Allah! The beard is a great blessing. Allah the Exalted has adorned the faces of men with it. It distinguishes men from women, and it shows a man's manliness, strength and dignity. It is not merely hair growing on the face; rather, it is one of the symbols of Islam.

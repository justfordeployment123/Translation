# English source for book page 123

[en]
Just think: the noble Companions forbade making a deal in this way, and along with it they also related the hadith of the noble Prophet, peace be upon him:

[en]
“The noble Prophet, peace be upon him, forbade selling silver for gold on credit.”

[en]
These proofs establish that in a deal like the one you have made, paying the full price is obligatory.

[en]
Imam an-Nawawi, may Allah have mercy on him, says:

[en]
“The scholars agree that it is forbidden to sell gold for gold, or for silver, with payment deferred to a fixed time.”

[en]
Man: You mean it is not permissible to leave any part of the price outstanding?

[en]
Teacher: May Allah bless you, it is not permissible at all.

[en]
Ammar: Teacher! What would the solution be?

[en]
Teacher: The solution is very easy, and it is this: take as much currency for the dollars as they have, and change the remaining dollars somewhere else; or keep the dollars with you, and change them later, when they have the Pakistani currency to exchange.

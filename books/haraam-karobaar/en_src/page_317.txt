# English source for book page 317

[en]
And whatever the Messenger has given you - take; and what he has forbidden you - refrain from.

[en]
The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, has commanded that the beard be grown and the moustache be cut.

[en]
Young man: These are general proofs. I want you to present a proof in which there is a clear command to grow the beard.

[en]
Teacher: If you consider these to be general proofs, then I will give you some more proofs from the hadiths of the Prophet.

[en]
First: The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Act contrary to the polytheists: leave your beards alone and trim your moustaches.”

[en]
Second: The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Act contrary to the polytheists: always have your moustaches trimmed close and let your beards grow.”

[en]
Third: The noble Prophet, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Have your moustaches cut short and let your beards grow.”

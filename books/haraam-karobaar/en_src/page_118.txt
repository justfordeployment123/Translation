# English source for book page 118

[en]
Ammar: Teacher! My brother Muhammad has sent me a hundred dollars. I want to change them into Pakistani currency, because I have to buy some books of hadith and fiqh.

[en]
Teacher: There is a currency exchange market nearby; come, let us go there.

[en]
Ammar: May Allah reward you. Forgive me if I am wasting your time.

[en]
Teacher: It is no trouble.

[en]
The teacher and his student went into the currency shop. The teacher sat down on a chair to wait for Ammar. Ammar went to the counter where the dollars were to be changed; five men were already standing there.

[en]
Ammar: Teacher! Come, let us go.

[en]
Teacher: Where?

[en]
Ammar: Let us go back, because we do not have that much time; there are still five men standing here.

[en]
Teacher: Let us wait a little while. How would we come back again from so far away?

[en]
Ammar: All right.

[en]
Ammar sat down beside the teacher and began to wait for his turn.

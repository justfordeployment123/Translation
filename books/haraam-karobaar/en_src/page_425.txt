# English source for book page 425

[en]
Teacher: I am not joking, nor is this a matter of strictness and extremism.

[en]
Sa'd: How can it not be permissible, when there is nothing in the religion that has come specifically about women's teeth?

[en]
Teacher: Everything is in the religion. Have you not read this blessed verse:

[en]
We have not neglected in the Register a thing.

[en]
That is, whatever you wish to know about, it will certainly be mentioned in the Noble Quran or in the Sunnah of the Messenger, peace be upon him.

[en]
Sa'd: Then is it in the Book of Allah or the Sunnah of the Messenger, peace be upon him, that it is forbidden and impermissible for women to have gaps made in their teeth or to have them made smaller?

[en]
Teacher: Yes, by Allah, it has been called forbidden in the Quran and the Sunnah.

[en]
Sa'd: Where is it written?

[en]
Teacher: Allah the Exalted says:

[en]
And whatever the Messenger has given you - take; and what he has forbidden you - refrain from.

[en]
Sa'd: Where in this verse is the matter of teeth mentioned?

[en]
Teacher: The purpose of quoting this verse is that the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, has declared it forbidden; that is, its prohibition has come from the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him.

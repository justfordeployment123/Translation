# English source for book page 284

[en]
Bahnasi: Teacher! Is everything forbidden? Things we have seen done since the days of our forefathers, you are declaring forbidden? Until today we have never heard that they are forbidden. What evidence do you have that earnings from a bull are forbidden?

[en]
Teacher: Servant of Allah, I am not declaring it forbidden on my own; rather, the Shariah has made it forbidden.

[en]
It is narrated from Abdullah ibn Umar, may Allah be pleased with them both:

[en]
“The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, forbade taking payment for a male animal's mating.”

[en]
It is narrated from Jabir, may Allah be pleased with him:

[en]
“The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, forbade taking payment for putting a male camel to a female.”

[en]
It is narrated from Abdullah ibn Abbas, may Allah be pleased with them both:

[en]
“The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, forbade the sale of what is in the wombs of camels.”

[en]
Imam Ibn al-Mundhir, may Allah have mercy on him, said:

[en]
“The scholars agree that charging a price for the mating of animals is an invalid sale.”

In the end she came down with a very high fever and was confined to her bed. The girl says: When the illness became severe, one day I suddenly fell into a light doze, although before that sleep would not even come near me and I was always awake. In my doze I saw a bird come into my room with some pills in its beak. It came and sat by my head and put the pills down by my pillow. Then, one by one, it put all the pills into my mouth with its beak, and one by one I swallowed them. The bird went away, and after that I sweated a great deal, so much that my clothes and my bed were soaked with sweat. After a little while the sweat dried, and I felt a little strength and health in my body. So I got up by myself, went to the bathroom, bathed, changed my clothes and came back to my room. My family were amazed to see me well, and when they asked the reason, I told them the whole story.

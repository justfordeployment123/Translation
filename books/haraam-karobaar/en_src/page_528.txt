# English source for book page 528

[hen]
Chapter Eighty-Eight: Begging

[en]
Ammar: The questioner says: Teacher, what is the ruling on asking people for things without need, or on begging?

[en]
Teacher: Not only the questioner but all other people as well should know well that asking people for things, begging from them and the like without need is forbidden in the Shariah.

[en]
It is narrated from Sahl ibn Hanzalah, may Allah be pleased with him, that the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Whoever begs while he has as much as suffices him is adding to the embers of Hell for himself. The people said: O Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him! What is the (amount) that suffices a person, so that it is not permissible for him to ask? He, peace be upon him, replied: One who has food for the morning and the evening.”

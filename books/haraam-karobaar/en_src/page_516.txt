# English source for book page 516

[hen]
Summary of Buying and Selling Clothes with Obscene Pictures and High-Heeled Shoes

[en]
The teacher bought the things he needed, and, saying As-salamu alaikum and thanking Zaid, he set off back home. On the way Ammar spoke to the teacher.

[en]
Ammar: Teacher! In this sitting we have told people to keep away from the following forbidden things:

[en]
First: Buying and selling indecent clothes and high-heeled shoes.

[en]
Second: A tailor sewing clothes of indecent design.

[en]
Third: Buying and selling clothes that bear the symbols of the disbelievers and their pictures.

[en]
Fourth: Buying and selling carpets, prayer mats and the like with designs on them for mosques.

[en]
Teacher: Praise be to Allah.

# English source for book page 540

[en]
“Glory be to Allah! How much severity Allah the Exalted has sent down in the matter of debt! By the One in whose hand my soul is, if a man were martyred in the way of Allah, then brought back to life, then martyred, then brought back to life and then martyred again, and he owed a debt, that man would not enter Paradise until his debt was paid off.”

[en]
Even after such severity and such a warning, should people still be negligent?

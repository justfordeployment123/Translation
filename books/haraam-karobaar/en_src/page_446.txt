# English source for book page 446

[hen]
Chapter Seventy: Seeking Treatment Through Magic

[en]
Teacher: How is your cousin Abdul-Badi? Has he got married yet or not? We have been hearing for quite a while that he is getting married.

[en]
Husain: The matter of his marriage has become very complicated.

[en]
Teacher: What has happened?

[en]
Husain: He got married twenty days ago, but he still has not been able to go in to his wife. Perhaps someone has put a spell on them?

[en]
Teacher: What is the situation now?

[en]
Husain: They went to a famous magician to solve this problem.

[en]
Teacher: There is no might and no power except with Allah! Are people now having their affairs put right through magic?

[en]
Husain: Teacher! If a person goes to a magician with the intention that he will remove a binding spell, an evil shadow and the like from him, what harm is there in that? It is an effort for good; what is wrong with it? Of course, if they go to a magician in order to harm someone, that is truly impermissible and forbidden.

[en]
Teacher: Where did you find this pointless excuse?

# English source for book page 23

[en]
{{QEN 6:115}}

[en]
Allah the Exalted has given us a standard by which to tell the lawful from the forbidden. Allah, the Mighty and Majestic, says:

[en]
And makes lawful for them the good things and prohibits for them the evil.

[en]
That is, what is pure is lawful, and what is foul is forbidden. It should also be remembered that declaring anything lawful or forbidden is the right of Allah alone. Whoever claims this right for himself or for anyone else is a disbeliever and is outside the religion of Islam. Allah the Exalted says:

[en]
Or have they other deities who have ordained for them a religion to which Allah has not consented?

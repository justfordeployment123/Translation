# English source for book page 139

[en]
“One who makes a show of something that is not truly his is like a person who is wearing a garment of falsehood.”

[en]
Second: Holding the milk back in the udders for two days makes animals ill, just as a woman becomes ill if she holds milk back in her breast for a single day. If she holds it back for longer, there is a fear that she may die. Yet a human being can speak and, on feeling pain, can get treatment and so on, whereas an animal has no tongue: it can neither describe its condition nor complain to anyone. But on the Day of Resurrection they will certainly complain to Allah.

[en]
Man: What is the solution to this?

[en]
Teacher: The solution is very easy. Choose one of two ways:

[en]
First: Take the cow back home and milk her, and then bring her to the market again some other time in her natural state and sell her.

[en]
Second: If you must sell her today, then make the whole matter clear to the buyer: that milk has deliberately been collected in her udders.

[en]
Man: I ask Allah for forgiveness.

[en]
(Meanwhile a buyer for the cow came along.)

[en]
Buyer: Ma sha Allah, what a beautiful cow, with such big udders! Do you want to sell her?

[en]
Man: For what price will you take her?

[en]
Buyer: What price are you asking? All I care about is a beautiful cow; the money does not matter to me.

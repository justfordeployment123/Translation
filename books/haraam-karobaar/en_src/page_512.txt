# English source for book page 512

[en]
In short, from no point of view, whether of reason, of the transmitted texts or of the Shariah, is it fitting for Muslims to wear such clothes and put them to use, or to buy and sell them, or in any way to have a share in making them common in society.

[en]
Just as these clothes are forbidden, so too the earnings that come from them are forbidden.

[en]
Zaid: In sha Allah, from today on we will not buy and sell them.

[en]
Teacher: May Allah bless you.

Chapter Sixteen: The Deceptive Sale

[en]
As they walked through the Livestock Market, the teacher and his student noticed two men. One had a camel's nose-rein in his hand, and the other was talking with him. The two of them exchanged some secret words; after that, one of them went his own way and was soon out of sight, while the other took the camel to the part of the market where camels were being bought and sold. When this man got there, buyers gathered around him and began bidding for the camel. A man to the Shaykh's left said: I am ready to buy this camel for fifteen hundred dinars. Another, from in front, said: I will give fifteen hundred and fifty dinars. A third man, to the Shaykh's right, said: I will give sixteen hundred dinars. The Shaykh looked closely at this third man on his right, and he was the very one who had come into the market talking with the camel's owner; and now he was pushing forward with the others and raising the price. Drawing the man's attention to himself, the Shaykh said:

[en]
Teacher: Are you not the one who came into the market with this camel's owner a little while ago and said something secret to him, and then the two of you went your separate ways?

[en]
Man: He said in great alarm: What is it to you?

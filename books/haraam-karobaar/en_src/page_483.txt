# English source for book page 483

[en]
Women should not go out of the house wearing clothes that are tight or that show the shape of the body's limbs. Such tight and short clothes are worn by disbelieving and immoral women whose aim is to lead people astray and trap them in their deception. Such clothes increase attraction and desire for women, and the effects they have on everyone who sees them are set out below:

[en]
First: Instead of marriage according to the Shariah, immorality, adultery and indecency are promoted.

[en]
Second: Corruption spreads in the family and in family life, and the divorce rate rises.

[en]
Third: The culture of indecency, carnal desires and shamelessness is promoted.

[en]
Fourth: The mass killing of human beings; that is, when people prefer adultery to marriage according to the Shariah, women will become pregnant illegitimately, and to escape shame they will have abortions.

[en]
Fifth: Disputes and quarrels between men and women, because a peaceful life is found only through marriage.

[en]
Sixth: There is nothing of a woman's honour in it; rather, when a woman goes out of the house made up, thugs and troublemakers harass her and look at her with evil eyes.

[en]
Seventh: The decline of moral values because of these dangerous sins and diseases.

# English source for book page 505

[en]
Like these clothes, buying and selling these high-heeled shoes is also forbidden, because medical experts and doctors say that high-heeled shoes cause the following two kinds of pain:

[en]
First: Pain in the spine.

[en]
Second: Pain in the ankles.

[en]
In this matter the word of physicians and doctors will be relied upon, because the noble Prophet, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Do not cause harm to anyone, and do not cause harm to anyone in return.”

[en]
In another respect too, buying and selling such shoes is forbidden, and it is this: when a woman walks wearing such a shoe, the sound of her footsteps is heard far and wide, so that others can hear the way she walks.

[en]

# English source for book page 493

[en]
However, if a woman keeps to the following conditions, she is permitted to go out of the house:

[en]
First: At a time of extremely important and urgent need.

[en]
Second: Going out of the house, with her guardian's permission, to do permissible work, such as a woman working as a teacher, or doing nursing work only for women.

[en]
Third: Her aim should not be to go to any gathering where men and women mix.

[en]
Fourth: She should not go out of the house made up and adorned, or wearing clothes that draw men's interest towards her.

[en]
Fifth: She should not have put on perfume whose scent reaches others.

[en]
Sixth: She should go out of the house wearing the covering the Shariah requires.

[en]
Here I will also point to a few of the dangers that usually arise from a woman going out of the house to work:

[en]
First: A woman cannot bring up her children properly, because when she has a job she can give her children very little time.

[en]
Second: In today's circumstances, most women who go out of the house to work do so together with men or under them; that is, men and women work together in the same office, factory and so on, and the results of this situation are turning out to be extremely dangerous.

[en]
Third: When a woman goes out of the house, especially when she is heavily made up and wearing attractive clothes, she then becomes a cause of trial for men.

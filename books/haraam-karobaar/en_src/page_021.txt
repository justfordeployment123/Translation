# English source for book page 21

[en]
In another narration, the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“A time will come when a person will not care about the wealth he has acquired: is it from what is lawful or from what is forbidden?”

[en]
Allah the Exalted has made some things obligatory, and it is not permissible to neglect them; He has set limits for some things, and it is not permissible to overstep them; and He has made some things forbidden, and it is not permissible to violate them.

[en]
The noble Prophet, peace be upon him, said: Whatever Allah the Exalted has made lawful in His Book is lawful, and whatever He has made forbidden is forbidden, and in the things about which Allah, the Mighty and Majestic, has remained silent there is ease. So accept this ease from Allah the Exalted, for Allah is not forgetful. Then the noble Prophet, peace be upon him, recited this verse:

[en]
In fact, it is the forbidden things that are the limits of Allah the Exalted:

[en]
These are the limits (set by) Allah, so do not approach them.

[en]

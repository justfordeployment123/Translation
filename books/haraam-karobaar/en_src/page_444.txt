# English source for book page 444

[en]
“In the name of Allah, and upon the religion of the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him.”

[en]
In these hadiths there is no mention of Quran recitation anywhere, nor did the noble Companions do anything of this kind after him, peace be upon him.

[en]
Husain: Teacher! Do not be so strict. Has any of the scholars written anything about this?

[en]
Teacher: Yes, they have. Imam Ibn Kathir, may Allah have mercy on him, says:

[en]
“Quran recitation does not benefit the dead in the slightest, nor does its reward reach him, because it is not his own deed. The noble Prophet, peace be upon him, neither encouraged his Ummah to it nor commanded it, nor is any virtue of it established from the hadiths. Nothing about it has been reported from the noble Companions either.”

[en]
This is not only the view of Imam Ibn Kathir; rather, the four Imams are agreed on it. Imam Ahmad saw a man reciting the Noble Quran at a grave and said:

[en]
“Reciting the Noble Quran at graves is an innovation.”

[en]
This was also the position of the majority of scholars among the early generations, and of the Companions.

[en]
Shaykh Ibn Baz and the Fatwa Council have given the fatwa that holding Quran recitation gatherings by the sick, during the burial, at the grave after the burial, in homes and so on are all innovations.

# English source for book page 358

[en]
“Why did you not put it on top of the heap, so that people could see it too? Whoever deceives is not one of us.”

[en]
Consider this: here the trader did not say to the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him: O Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, the customer only wants grain, whether it is dry or wet. The second point is that after some time this one short-lived part can cause the whole set to break down; that is, by putting in a short-lived part you have in fact spoiled his whole set. In spite of so many proofs, how can you swallow up your brother's wealth by adulteration and deception? Bear this in mind too: you must deal with others in the same way that you like for yourself. Would you like someone to treat you the way you are treating others?

[en]
Muntasir: No.

[en]
Teacher: It is very strange that you dislike a thing for yourself, yet like that same thing for others and keep on doing it. In this way you are betraying the trust of people who leave their things with you with great trust. They think you are trustworthy, but you are deceiving them with great shamelessness.

[en]
Muntasir: In your view, what is its ruling in the Shariah?

[en]
Teacher: I have already set out all the proofs that it is forbidden.

[en]
Muntasir: I repent to Allah, and I promise that from today I will not do this again.

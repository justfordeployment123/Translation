# English source for book page 47

[en]
Teacher: What is the matter between you two?

[en]
Abu Fahd: Shaykh! I sowed my wheat, and a month later, when the wheat was still completely green, I fell into a severe financial crisis. My son fell ill, and at that time I had no money at all for his treatment. I came to Abu Ali and said to him: Buy this year's entire crop of my wheat for one thousand pounds. Our deal was settled, and he gave me one thousand pounds. At that time the wheat had not even put out shoots, and its grain had not appeared at all.

[en]
Now that the wheat has ripened and I have harvested it, the crop is very large, and it is worth two thousand pounds. I have told him to take wheat from me to the value of his money and leave the rest of the crop, but he insists that he will take the whole crop.

[en]
Abu Ali: He made the deal with me of his own free will. What is he making this demand for now? And when we made the deal, I gave him the option of changing it before we parted, but he said nothing. Our deal was settled four months ago now.

[en]
Teacher: May Allah keep you both safe. Before you disagree about the price and so on, consider whether this deal of yours is even valid.

[en]
At this question Abu Fahd and Abu Ali looked at each other in astonishment, and both said together: Valid or invalid? Shaykh, what does that mean?

[en]
Teacher: This deal of yours is wrong and impermissible. Neither does the seller have the right to take the price, nor does the buyer have the right to take the goods, because this transaction is void.

# English source for book page 381

[hen]
Chapter Fifty-Seven: Buying and Selling Books Based on Indecency and Filthy Material

[en]
In the bookshop the teacher's eye fell on the following books:

[en]
First: Fusus al-Hikam.

[en]
Second: Al-Futuhat al-Makkiyyah.

[en]
Third: Shams al-Ma'arif.

[en]
Fourth: Bada'i' az-Zuhur fi Waqa'i' ad-Duhur.

[en]
Fifth: Two Hundred Questions about Love and Sexual Matters.

[en]
Teacher: (Turning to the manager) Why do you keep these indecent books of innovation in the shop?

[en]
Manager: Teacher! These are very useful books.

[en]
Teacher: No, brother. How can books that breed misguidance, corruption and moral decline be useful? They are extremely dangerous for a Muslim.

[en]
Al-Fusus and al-Futuhat al-Makkiyyah are books by an author who believed in divine indwelling (hulul), and this belief is plainly against the heavenly Shariah. Shams al-Ma'arif teaches sorcery, because of which strife is breaking out among the Muslims. Bada'i' az-Zuhur is full of false, made-up, fabricated and baseless tales that meet the standard of neither the Shariah nor reason.

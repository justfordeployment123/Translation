# English source for book page 216

[en]
Allah the Exalted says:

[en]
And cooperate in righteousness and piety, but do not cooperate in sin and aggression.

[en]
Lutfi, rent out the premises for some lawful business instead of forbidden work. Is there any shortage of lawful businesses, such as computers, restaurants, fruit, bookshops and so on?

[en]
Lutfi: So renting out premises for these things (a bank, tobacco) is forbidden?

[en]
Teacher: I have given you a proof from the Noble Quran; now hear a proof from the hadith as well. The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Allah the Exalted has cursed alcohol, the one who drinks it, the one who serves it, the one who sells it, the one who buys it, the one who presses it, the one for whom it is pressed, the one who carries it, the one to whom it is carried, and the one who consumes its price.”

[en]
Now just think: in a place where forbidden things will be bought and sold and impermissible things will be kept, will the owner of that place also share in spreading what is forbidden, or not?

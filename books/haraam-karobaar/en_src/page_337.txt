# English source for book page 337

[en]
The woman said: I have seen some of these things in your own wife. Abdullah, may Allah be pleased with him, said: Go and look. She went to his wife and found nothing. Then she came back and said: I did not see any of these things. Abdullah, may Allah be pleased with him, said: If she had done so, we would not have loved her.

[en]
It is narrated from Abu Hurairah, may Allah be pleased with him, that a woman who did tattooing was brought to Umar, may Allah be pleased with him. Umar, may Allah be pleased with him, stood up and said (to the Companions): I ask you by Allah, has anyone heard anything from the Prophet, peace be upon him, about the woman who tattoos? Abu Hurairah, may Allah be pleased with him, says: I stood up and said: Commander of the Believers! I have heard something. Umar, may Allah be pleased with him, asked: What did you hear? Abu Hurairah, may Allah be pleased with him, said: I heard the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, say:

[en]
“Do not tattoo, and do not have yourselves tattooed.”

[en]
In such things there is falsification and deception, which is why the hadiths above condemn them and give a severe warning about them. Besides this, it amounts to changing the creation of Allah the Exalted, so neither is this work permissible nor the wage for it.

[en]
Young man: But this is my only means of earning a living. What am I to do now?

[en]
Teacher: Once you have heard that this thing is forbidden, there is no longer any room to ask, “What am I to do?” My brother, give it up at once and look for a living by some lawful means.

[en]
Young man: May Allah reward you! In sha Allah, I will certainly act on it.

# English source for book page 209

[en]
The Shariah has given us a rule about alcohol that puts an end to every trick and excuse. The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Every intoxicant is alcohol, and every intoxicant is forbidden.”

[en]
That is, anything that clouds the mind and robs a person of his senses is forbidden, whether a little or a lot. The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“An intoxicant is forbidden, whether a little of it or a lot.”

[en]

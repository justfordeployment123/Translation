# English source for book page 467

[hen]
Chapter Seventy-Five: If the Husband Is Miserly, May the Wife Take as Much Money as She Needs from his Pocket Without his Permission?

[en]
Abdur-Rahim: Teacher! If a wife takes more money than is needed from her husband's pocket without his permission, for household expenses and the like, while the husband takes no interest in the household expenses or is miserly, is this right?

[en]
Teacher: In normal circumstances no woman may take her husband's wealth without his permission, because Allah the Exalted has made it forbidden to seize one another's wealth in this way. The noble Prophet, peace be upon him, announced this on the occasion of the Farewell Pilgrimage:

[en]
“Indeed, your blood, your wealth and your honour are as sacred to you as this day of yours is sacred, in this month of yours and in this city. Take heed! Have I conveyed (the religion)?”

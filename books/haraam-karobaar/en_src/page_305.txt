# English source for book page 305

[hen]
Chapter Forty-Four: Removing Hair from the Face

[en]
The teacher went to the salon. There, on the door, this blessed verse was written:

[en]
{{QEN 88:8}}

[en]
Saying As-salamu alaikum, the teacher went into the barbershop. Five young men were already sitting there, waiting for their turn. The teacher addressed the owner of the salon.

[en]
Teacher: If you do not mind, may I ask you something?

[en]
Barber: Yes, ask.

[en]
Teacher: Why have you written this blessed verse on the door of your shop?

[en]
Barber: The purpose of writing this verse is clear: whoever comes here goes back with a face that shines like silver.

[en]
Teacher: So, in your view, Allah the Exalted revealed this blessed verse to describe the self-made good looks of a man who has his beard shaved?

[en]
Barber: That is how I see it.

[en]
Teacher: Oh brother! This blessed verse does not mean what you take it to mean. Rather, in it Allah the Exalted describes the blessings of the people of Paradise; that is, on the Day of Resurrection their faces will be shining.

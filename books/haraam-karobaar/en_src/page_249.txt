# English source for book page 249

[en]
Today our hearts and minds are crammed with desires. Besides this, if, setting aside the Shariah side of it, we look at the harmful effects that teaching girls has on a teacher's personality, our heads hang in shame.

[en]
Hasan: What do you mean?

[en]
Teacher: I mean that in such a situation it often happens that some girls begin to appeal to the teacher; he starts to like them, and he begins to look at them in the wrong way. In the same way, some teachers begin to appeal to the girls, and the girls start to see them as something more than a teacher. In the end, things reach the point of love and romance, and this is nothing strange or new. Countless people who claim to be chaste get badly caught up in it.

[en]
Hasan: May Allah protect us from teachers and girl students with such base and dirty thinking! Is this true?

[en]
Teacher: Yes, there are countless such examples. An incident came before one of our muftis in the form of a question. A girl wrote in her question: A few of us girl students decided together to take tuition in mathematics. For this we chose an academy and began regular lessons there with a male teacher. After fifteen or twenty days, one by one all the girl students left, and I was left alone.

# English source for book page 524

[hen]
Chapter Eighty-Seven: Working in Hotels, Restaurants and Tourist Centres

[en]
When the teacher had explained the ruling on working in a factory for alcohol, cigarettes and the like and fell silent, a young man stood up from the middle of the gathering and spoke to the teacher.

[en]
Young man: Teacher! Hearing what you said, a question has come to my mind as well. May I ask for its answer?

[en]
Teacher: Yes, ask.

[en]
Young man: What is the ruling on working in hotels, restaurants and tourist centres?

[en]
Teacher: Its ruling is the same as the ruling on working in a factory for alcohol and cigarettes; that is, working in hotels, restaurants and the like is forbidden.

[en]
Young man: How is it forbidden?

[en]
Teacher: Because countless evils are found in it.

[en]
Young man: Tell us any three of them, for example.

[en]
Teacher: First: The greatest evil is committed in this way: there, men and women mix; men and women wander about like animals without any shame or modesty, and going unveiled is common. In such places a person silently watches all this and, like a mute man, is unable to speak the truth.

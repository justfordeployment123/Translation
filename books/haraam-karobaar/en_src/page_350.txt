# English source for book page 350

[en]
They ask you about wine and gambling. Say, “In them is great sin and (yet, some) benefit for people. But their sin is greater than their benefit.”

[en]
From this blessed verse comes the principle that whatever has more sin in it than benefit is forbidden. And in television too, the sinful things found far outnumber the beneficial things, so it is forbidden.

[en]
(Meanwhile Salah, one of the shop's employees, came and stood by the teacher and Faisal, and addressed the teacher.)

[en]
Salah: Teacher, I keep a television at home only to listen to the news. Is there anything wrong with that?

[en]
Teacher: For two reasons, a television may not be kept at home even for listening to the news:

[en]
First: Those who read the news are usually both men and women, and it is not permissible for you to look at a non-mahram woman.

[en]
Second: In between the news, pictures of women often appear, unveiled and half-naked, advertising for various companies, and it is not permissible for you to look at them either.

[en]
Salah: Besides that, we watch sports and the like. Are these forbidden too?

[en]
Teacher: Abu Ammar Wahid Bali has written something very fine about watching sports and the like on television.

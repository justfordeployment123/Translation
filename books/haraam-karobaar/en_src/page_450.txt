# English source for book page 450

[hen]
Chapter Seventy-One: Not Being Fair When Giving Gifts to One's Children

[en]
There was a knock at the door.

[en]
Teacher: Who is it?

[en]
Visitor: It is me.

[en]
Teacher: Who is “me”? Can that be anyone's name?

[en]
(Then the teacher quickly got up and opened the door. Outside stood the teacher's neighbour, Abdur-Rahim. After welcoming him, the teacher advised him that when the householder asks one's name, one does not say, “It is me”; rather, one should give one's name in clear words.)

[en]
Jabir, may Allah be pleased with him, says: I came to the noble Prophet, peace be upon him, about a matter of my father's debt. I knocked at the noble Prophet's door, peace be upon him, and he, peace be upon him, asked:

[en]
“Who is it?” I replied: It is me. He, peace be upon him, said: Me, me! It was as if he, peace be upon him, disliked it.

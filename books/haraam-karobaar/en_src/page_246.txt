# English source for book page 246

[en]
Teacher: Servant of Allah! Get yourself transferred from there as soon as you can.

[en]
Hasan: Get myself transferred!... Why? Why should I get myself transferred?

[en]
Teacher: Because a man teaching grown-up girls involves several kinds of evil.

[en]
Hasan: I am surprised at you! What evils are there in it? You can debate this subject with me. Maulana! Teaching is a most excellent profession. Shawqi has put it so well:

[en]
“Honour the teacher and stand up out of respect for him, for this excellent work is the work of the prophets.”

[en]
I will never accept, until the Day of Resurrection, that men teaching girls is a crime or a blameworthy act. Tell me the evils that you think there are in it.

[en]
Teacher: A male teacher teaching in a girls' school seems very improper, because it goes against modesty, shame, chastity and purity. What must the state of a servant's faith be at the moment when he sees girls sitting in front of him in tight clothes and decked out in finery? Then, while teaching, as you explain the lesson to the girls, how can you say: Look at me, pay attention to me, listen to me? How can you address a girl student and say: Have you learned the lesson? How can you raise your eyes and look at them, when Allah the Exalted has commanded men:

[en]
Tell the believing men to reduce (some) of their vision.

# English source for book page 458

[en]
{{QEN 24:52}}

[en]
Abdur-Rahim! Fear Allah, and never again say anything that goes against your faith and your creed. Your second statement, that “I have written a will for my only daughter”, is even more dangerous and improper than the first; in fact, it is an open declaration of war on Allah and His Messenger, peace be upon him.

[en]
Abdur-Rahim: How?

[en]
Teacher: Because the One who has fixed the shares of inheritance is Allah the Exalted. He has fixed the shares by His power and His wisdom. No one has the right to challenge His decision; even the prophets and messengers are bound by His decision. Allah the Exalted has given everyone who has a right his right.

[en]
The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Indeed, Allah the Exalted has given everyone who has a right his right, so there is no bequest for an heir.”

[en]
Whoever, by impermissible tricks and pretexts, deprives a person of what is rightfully his and gives someone more than his share is in reality opposing the decision of Allah and His Messenger, peace be upon him, and this is the same as inviting the punishment of Allah the Exalted.

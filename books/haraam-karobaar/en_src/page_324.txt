# English source for book page 324

[en]
And cooperate in righteousness and piety, but do not cooperate in sin and aggression.

[en]
Wealth earned by forbidden means is deprived of goodness and blessing. The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Every body that is nourished by forbidden earnings is more deserving of the Fire.”

[en]
My dear brothers! Make your faith firm and put your complete trust in Allah. Do not worry at all that giving up shaving beards will reduce your provision, because Allah the Exalted says:

[en]
And whoever fears Allah - He will make for him a way out and will provide for him from where he does not expect.

[en]
That is, Allah the Exalted becomes sufficient for such a person in all his religious and worldly affairs. Whoever gives something up for the sake of Allah the Exalted, Allah the Exalted will give him something better than it. Be certain that Allah the Exalted never breaks His promise. A person should avoid haste and seek what is lawful with patience. A little lawful provision is better and more blessed than a lot of forbidden provision.

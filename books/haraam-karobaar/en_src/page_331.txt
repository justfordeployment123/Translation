# English source for book page 331

[hen]
Chapter Forty-Seven: A Male Barber Cutting Women's Hair

[en]
One of the young men in the barbershop spoke up:

[en]
Young man: Teacher! May I ask you a question?

[en]
Teacher: Yes, go ahead and ask.

[en]
Young man: I have a beauty parlour. I get brides ready there. Is this all right?

[en]
Teacher: It is not all right at all. It is not permissible for any man to cut a woman's hair. Think it over yourself and tell me: if looking at a woman is forbidden, how can touching her, holding her hair and so on be permissible?

[en]
Allah the Exalted says:

[en]
Tell the believing men to reduce (some) of their vision.

[en]
And tell the believing women to reduce (some) of their vision.

[en]
The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

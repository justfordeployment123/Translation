# English source for book page 266

[en]
“Whoever unjustly seizes a hand-span of land, Allah the Exalted will charge him with digging it down through all seven earths, and then on the Day of Resurrection a collar of that land will be put around his neck until judgement is passed among the people.”

[en]
Likewise, changing the markers and boundaries of land falls into the same category: that is, changing the original boundary markers of a neighbour's land, or of a road and the like, and wrongfully adding part of it to one's own land.

[en]
The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“May Allah curse whoever changes the boundary markers of the land.”

[en]
Abu Jum'a: Teacher! I told you that my land is small; that is why I did this.

[en]
Teacher: Abu Jum'a! If land is small, does one make it bigger by forbidden means? You also know that such a man will be made to carry seven earths on the Day of Resurrection. How will you bear such a heavy load? Besides being forbidden, such provision also brings curses and reproach: those who suffer from the narrow road will surely speak ill of such a man. So fear Allah, and before death comes, return your land to its original state. Save yourself from disgrace on the Day of Resurrection, and save your children from forbidden provision too.

# English source for book page 153

[en]
“A Muslim is the brother of a Muslim. It is not permissible for a Muslim to sell his brother something that has a defect unless he makes that defect clear.”

[en]
Some traders think that they are free of responsibility once they have told the buyer to look it over well himself and satisfy himself, whereas because of this the blessing goes out of the sale. The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“The two parties to a sale have the option (to cancel or change it) as long as they have not parted. If they both tell the truth and make (the defects and so on) clear, blessing is put into their sale; and if they hide (the defects and so on) and lie, the blessing is taken out of their sale.”

[en]
Seller: Shaykh! I did not deceive him; rather, he looked at the cow again and again and bought her of his own free will.

[en]
Teacher: Brother! If the goods have a defect, it is not enough for the buyer merely to look at them. Besides, this defect of the teats was such that it could not possibly have been discovered at that time; and in fact, at the time of the sale you knew about the cow's defect, but you deliberately deceived the buyer.

[en]
In the same way, when a car or other vehicle is being bought and sold, the owner usually says: Brother, have this vehicle checked by a mechanic and satisfy yourself, and do not raise any objection later. But the vehicle has a defect that the mechanic does not know about at the time, for example the engine making a noise at high speed, smoke coming out, or the vehicle having been repainted somewhere. These are all defects that the mechanic may not be able to discover, but the owner knows about all of them. So in such a case it is the owner who is responsible, and he must make the vehicle's defects clear; otherwise it will be deception, and deception is forbidden.

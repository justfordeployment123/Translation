Fourth: If you ban your wife and children at home from watching dramas and the like, the inevitable result will be quarrels and fights in your home, which will bring unrest and disorder into the house. So rather than that, it is better not to keep a thing that stirs up such disorder in the house at all.

It is clear that although television has some useful things, because of its other evils it becomes a doubtful thing, and our religion has commanded us to keep away from doubtful things. The waywardness society has fallen into because of television, and the results being produced by the films and dramas shown on it, are hidden from no one either.

[en]

# English source for book page 478

[en]
Abdur-Rahim: You mean that, in your view, it is forbidden for a woman to work as an air hostess?

[en]
Teacher: Yes, there is no doubt at all that it is forbidden. The following four things are found in it, because of which this work is forbidden:

[en]
First: The mixing of men and women, which is entirely forbidden on the basis of the definitive proofs of the Quran and the Sunnah; there is no room for it at all.

[en]
Second: In this job your daughter will make long journeys alone, without a mahram, and it is forbidden for a woman to travel alone without a mahram. The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“It is not lawful for a woman who believes in Allah and the Day of Resurrection to make a journey of a day and a night alone, unless a mahram is with her.”

[en]
Abdullah ibn Abbas, may Allah be pleased with them both, heard the Prophet, peace be upon him, saying:

[en]
“No man should be alone with a (non-mahram) woman, and no woman should travel unless a mahram of hers is with her. A man stood up and said: O Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him! My wife is setting out for Hajj, and I have put my name down for such-and-such an expedition? He, peace be upon him, said: Go, and perform Hajj with your wife.”

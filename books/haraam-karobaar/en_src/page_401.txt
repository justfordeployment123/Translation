# English source for book page 401

[hen]
Summary of the Book Market Tour

[en]
Ammar: Teacher! By the grace and favour of Allah, today you have stopped people from the following matters:

[en]
First: Buying and selling books that contain innovations.

[en]
Second: Buying and selling obscene magazines and periodicals.

[en]
Third: Buying and selling school bags with pictures.

[en]
Fourth: Buying and selling musical instruments.

[en]
Fifth: Buying and selling pictures.

[en]
Teacher: Praise be to Allah, Lord of the worlds.

[en]
(The teacher and Ammar arrived back home.)

[en]
Ammar: Teacher! May I take my leave now?

[en]
Teacher: First the two of us will eat together; after that you may go.

[en]
(The two of them went into the house and ate, and an appointment was made for the time of the Asr prayer the next day.)

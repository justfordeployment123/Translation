[en]
Husain: Soon I am going to put my first house in Yasir's name alone.

[en]

[en]
Teacher: Some people, when giving gifts to their children or making something over to them, do not act with fairness and justice, and without any justification in the Shariah they prefer one son over another, although this is forbidden.

[en]
Husain: What is a justification in the Shariah?

[en]
Teacher: A justification in the Shariah is that if a son is ill, in debt, or not working because he is seeking knowledge, or his expenses are greater because he has more children while his income is small, then the father may favour him with a greater gift than his other children. But remember that when another child faces a similar situation, the father should favour him too with a greater gift, just like the first son. It must not be that when it is his more beloved son's turn, the father makes full use of the Shariah's justification, and when it is the other's turn, he ignores it.

# English source for book page 96

[en]
“Whoever practises adulteration is not following our way, and the one who deceives and tricks will be in the Fire.”

[en]
Shopkeeper: Wait, sir! Listen to what I have to say.

[en]
Teacher: Yes, go on.

[en]
Shopkeeper: I am very skilled at this work. I polish old gold and make it so new that sometimes, if I do not pay attention, even I find it hard to tell new from old. I have been doing this work for thirty years. All kinds of customers come to me.

[en]
Teacher: Leave aside your idle talk for a moment and listen to me.

[en]
Shopkeeper: Yes, go on.

[en]
Teacher: Tell me: the customers who come to you, do they come to buy new gold, or polished old gold?

[en]
Shopkeeper: Obviously, they come to buy new gold.

[en]
Teacher: Then do you point out the difference and tell them that this is new and this is polished old gold?

[en]
Shopkeeper: No, sir! Why do I need to tell them that, when there is no difference left at all between new and old?

[en]
Teacher: Come now, brother, this is deception, adulteration and an outright wrong against the Muslims.

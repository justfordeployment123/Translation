# English source for book page 206

[en]
Ahmad: I seek refuge in Allah! How am I one of them?

[en]
Teacher: The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Allah the Exalted has cursed alcohol, the one who drinks it, the one who serves it, the one who sells it, the one who buys it, the one who presses it, the one for whom it is pressed, the one who carries it, the one to whom it is carried, and the one who consumes its price.”

[en]
You have heard the hadith: the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, cursed all these people, and among them is the trader who sells alcohol.

[en]
And Allah the Exalted also says:

[en]
Indeed, intoxicants, gambling, (sacrificing on) stone alters (to other than Allah), and divining arrows are but defilement from the work of Satan, so avoid it that you may be successful.

[en]
In this blessed verse Allah the Exalted commanded us to keep away from alcohol, and He mentioned it together with the worst of deeds, the worship of idols. Both these points are proof that it is forbidden, so after this blessed verse there is no option but to call alcohol forbidden. Indeed, the Prophet, peace be upon him, announced an even sterner warning for the one who drinks alcohol.

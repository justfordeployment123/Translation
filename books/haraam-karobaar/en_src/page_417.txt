# English source for book page 417

[en]
You drive this man's car of your own will and choice, so you should keep your agreement and your promise, and not change it by even a single rupee, up or down.

[en]
Driver: What is the proof of this?

[en]
Teacher: The noble Prophet, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Muslims fulfil their conditions.”

[en]
The owner of the car has kept you as a driver on the condition that out of every hundred pounds he will give you ten pounds. So if you break this condition and help yourself to money without letting the owner know, it will be impermissible and forbidden for you.

[en]
Driver: Teacher! Answer one more question for me.

[en]
Some people travel with me and, when they get out of the car, give me two pounds in fare, more than is due, although the fare for that journey comes to one pound. Is that extra pound lawful for me?

[en]
Teacher: That extra pound is not lawful for you, because you are entitled to as much pay as the work you have done. But if the passenger knows that his fare is one pound, and he gives two pounds of his own free will, there is no harm in that.

[en]
Driver: Teacher! Thank you for making me aware of the correct ruling. From now on I will not take extra money without the passenger's consent.

[en]
Teacher: May Allah reward you with good.

# English source for book page 518

[en]
When they got back home, Ammar spoke to his teacher.

[en]
Ammar: When will we meet next?

[en]
Teacher: Can you pray the Maghrib prayer with us today? For a lesson has been arranged in our mosque today.

[en]
Ammar: All right, I will be there.

[en]
Ammar went back to his own home, and at the time of Maghrib he came again, early, to the mosque, so that he could pray in congregation in the first row, sit close by after the prayer to listen to the teacher's lesson, and after that listen carefully to the answers to the questions that would be put to the teacher.

[en]

[en]

[en]
Buyer: If I wanted to sell this same AC to you for cash, how much would you take it for?

[en]
Faisal: For three thousand pounds.

[en]
Buyer: All right, I agree.

[en]
Teacher: What are the two of you doing?

[en]
Faisal: Teacher! This man is suffering financial hardship. He buys various things on instalments from various shops, and then sells those things for cash to the same shop and takes the money. For example, if he needs ten thousand, he goes to some shop and buys something worth twelve thousand on instalments, and after the deal is made he sells that same thing to the same shop for ten thousand. He meets his need with the ten thousand and pays the shopkeeper the twelve thousand in instalments.

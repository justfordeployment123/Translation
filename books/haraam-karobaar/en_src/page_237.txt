# English source for book page 237

[hen]
Chapter Thirty-Three: Taking Leave Without Need

[en]
Abdul-Wahhab: (taking a long breath) Teacher! The situation is that the whole system of our government institutions has fallen apart. There is no notion of order and discipline at all. A crime even more serious than false testimony has become common among our employees.

[en]
Teacher: May Allah bless you! What crime is that?

[en]
Abdul-Wahhab: Most government employees come to work at ten o'clock, when they are due at eight o'clock. A further dishonesty is that even after coming at ten, they do not work properly: they sit for an hour or two, then, without any excuse or business, write an application for short leave and go back. They busy themselves with their own homes, shops or other affairs. What is the ruling of the Shariah on this?

[en]
Teacher: Like the previous matter, this too is a lie and false testimony, and, as we have mentioned, Allah the Exalted said:

[en]
And avoid false statement.

# English source for book page 513

[hen]
Chapter Eighty-Five: Buying and Selling Decorated Carpets for Mosques

[en]
(After this the teacher began choosing a carpet for prayer, but there was no plain carpet there; all of them had designs and floral patterns on them.)

[en]
Teacher: Do you not have any carpet without these floral patterns on it?

[en]
Zaid: The only carpets we have are these that you see.

[en]
Teacher: Which carpets do you sell for mosques?

[en]
Zaid: We sell these very ones. Is there anything wrong with them?

[en]
Teacher: The Shariah has forbidden spreading carpets and cloths that have designs on them in mosques, or hanging them on the walls, and there are countless proofs which show that praying on a cloth, carpet or the like that has designs on it is impermissible. It is narrated from Aishah, may Allah be pleased with her, that the noble Prophet, peace be upon him, prayed on a cloth with designs on it; then he, peace be upon him, looked at it once and said:

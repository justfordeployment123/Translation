# English source for book page 75

[en]
The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Whoever deceives us is not following our way.”

[en]
This is outright deception. Fawzi trusted you, but you deceived him and took advantage of his lack of knowledge.

[en]
The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“On the Day of Resurrection, every deceiver will have a banner, and it will be said: This is the deceit of so-and-so.”

[en]
You have proved to be a deceiver in this matter. What greater deceit could there be than that a man thought you trustworthy and you dealt with him treacherously?

[en]
The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“The two parties to a sale have the option (to change or cancel it) as long as they have not separated. If they both tell the truth and make (any defects and so on) clear, blessing is placed in that deal of theirs; and if they both hide (the defects and so on) and lie, the blessing is taken away from that deal of theirs.”

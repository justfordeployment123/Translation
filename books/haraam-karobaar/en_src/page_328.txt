# English source for book page 328

[en]
“You will surely follow the ways of the nations before you, span by span and cubit by cubit (that is, step by step), so much so that if they had entered a lizard's hole, you would enter it too. The noble Companions, may Allah be pleased with them, asked: O Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, (by the nations before us, do you mean) the Jews and the Christians? He, peace be upon him, said: Who else could it be?”

[en]
In another hadith, the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, gave a severe warning to Muslims who imitate the disbelievers. He, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Whoever imitates a people is one of them.”

[en]
How very sad it is that if the people of Europe began putting their shoes on their heads and walking barefoot, our young men would imitate them with their eyes closed and very happily adopt that way too.

[en]

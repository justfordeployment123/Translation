# English source for book page 92

[en]
The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Whoever deceives is not following our way.”

[en]
He, peace be upon him, also said:

[en]
“Whoever practises adulteration is not following our way, and the one who deceives and tricks will be in the Fire.”

[en]
Allah the Exalted gives such a dishonest trader respite for a while, but after that comes such a seizing from Allah the Exalted as he never even imagined. He earns fifty thousand by this forbidden means, but Allah the Exalted afflicts him with some illness on which far more than those fifty thousand is spent, and the punishment of the Hereafter is still to come. On the Day of Resurrection, Allah the Exalted will disgrace and humiliate him in front of all the people.

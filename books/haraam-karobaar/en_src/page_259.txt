# English source for book page 259

[hen]
Summary of the Tour of Forbidden Things Found among Office Employees

[en]
The teacher looked towards Atif and said:

[en]
Teacher: We have taken up a good deal of your time today. May Allah reward you for being of help to us.

[en]
Atif: Teacher! Meeting you is a blessing for us. With such excellent admonition and advice as you have given, we wish we could always have meetings like this with you.

[en]
Teacher: You have honoured us greatly; all we can do is pray for you. We have been sitting in this gathering for quite a while, and we have talked about a great many things. I think we should go over once again the things we have talked about.

[en]
Ammar: Teacher! Today you have explained the ruling on the following seven things:

[en]
First: Taking a bribe in return for work is forbidden.

[en]
Second: Not fulfilling one's duty is also forbidden.

[en]
Third: Giving false testimony, that is, wrongfully marking someone as present.

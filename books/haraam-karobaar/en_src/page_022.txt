# English source for book page 22

[en]
{{QEN 4:14}}

[en]
And it is obligatory to keep away from forbidden things. The blessed Prophet, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Whatever I forbid you, keep away from it, and whatever I command you, do as much of it as you are able.”

[en]
Usually, when forbidden things are mentioned in front of some people who have little knowledge, who are ignorant and weak in faith, they become very uneasy and troubled, and they say:

[en]
You keep on making everything forbidden! You people have made us despair of life; you have made life hard for us, when the religion is easy, the Shariah is very broad, and Allah the Exalted is Most Forgiving and Most Merciful!

[en]
We will answer the objection of such people in these words: Allah the Exalted commands whatever He wills, and there is none who can question Him. He is the Wise, the All-Aware. He makes lawful whatever He wills and makes forbidden whatever He wills. It is our duty to accept His command gladly and to be content with His decision. Nothing in His knowledge and wisdom is pointless, as Allah the Exalted says:

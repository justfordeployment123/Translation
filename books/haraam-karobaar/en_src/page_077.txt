# English source for book page 77

[hen]
Summary of the Grain Market Tour

[en]
Ammar: Shaykh! May Allah benefit you and bless you.

[en]
Teacher: How many wrongs did we stop today?

[en]
Ammar: Today we stopped people from the following wrongs:

[en]
First: Giving short measure and weight, that is, taking more when receiving and giving less when giving; this is forbidden.

[en]
Second: Buying and selling fruit before it is ripe is impermissible.

[en]
Third: Reselling grain before taking it into one's possession; its ruling too is the same as that of the first.

[en]
Fourth: Buying and selling on deferment without fixing the price and without taking the price in the same sitting; this is prohibited.

[en]
Fifth: Selling short of the proper weight; this too is forbidden, because it is deception.

[en]
Sixth: Using deception in the goods one sells.

[en]
Teacher: Well done! We ask Allah for sincerity in our words and deeds. Come, let us go home.

[en]
Ammar: I think that a round of the grain market alone is not enough.

[en]
Teacher: What do you mean?

[en]
Ammar: What if we take a look at the Gold Market too?!

[en]
Teacher: That is a very good idea. I have just remembered that I have to buy a gold ring as a gift for my daughter, because she has completed memorising the Quran.

[en]
Ammar: Come! Let us go.

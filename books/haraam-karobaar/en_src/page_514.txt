# English source for book page 514

[en]
“Take this cloth to Abu Jahm and bring me his anbijani cloth (a plain, thick cloth), because it distracted me from my prayer just now.”

[en]
Zaid: Teacher! This has nothing to do with me.

[en]
Teacher: Why has it nothing to do with you? Have you not heard this hadith of the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him?

[en]
“The Hour will not be established until people begin to boast about their mosques.”

[en]
Abdullah ibn Abbas, may Allah be pleased with them both, said:

[en]
“You will certainly decorate them (the mosques), just as the Jews and the Christians decorated (their places of worship).”

[en]
When Umar, may Allah be pleased with him, ordered a mosque to be built, he said:

[en]
“I want to shelter the people from the rain; and do not paint the mosques red or yellow, for through that people will fall into temptation.”

[en]
All these narrations are proof that carpets, cloths and the like with designs and floral patterns on them should be neither spread nor hung in the mosque, and that prayer should not be offered on them either.

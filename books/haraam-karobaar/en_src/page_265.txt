# English source for book page 265

[en]
“Whoever takes even a small part of any land without right will be made to sink down through seven earths on the Day of Resurrection.”

[en]
Aishah, may Allah be pleased with her, said that the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“If anyone unjustly takes even a hand-span of another's land, a collar of seven earths will be put around his neck (on the Day of Resurrection).”

[en]
These hadiths prove that whoever seizes someone's land wrongfully and without right is a usurper.

[en]
Ammar: Shaykh! What is the position of our senior scholars on this?

[en]
Teacher: The scholars say:

[en]
When a person no longer fears Allah, a kind of strength and power comes into him that is in truth a disaster for him and drives him to injustice, so that he devours people's wealth and seizes their land.

[en]

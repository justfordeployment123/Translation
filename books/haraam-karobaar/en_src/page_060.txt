# English source for book page 60

[hen]
Chapter Four: Selling Goods on Deferment Without Fixing the Price or Receiving It in the Sitting of the Sale

[en]
Shaykh Salih and Ammar went to a restaurant. They took two chairs and sat down to one side to rest. Ammar went ahead, offered the Shaykh cold water and ordered food.

[en]
Teacher: Laughing, he turned to Ammar: What great work have we done so far today that we have sat down to eat so early?

[en]
Ammar: Shaykh! May Allah protect you and bless you.

[en]
The two of them drank the cold water, and meanwhile the food was brought to them. Close by, two men were sitting and talking. One said to the other: Mahmud! Soon, in about forty days, we will be harvesting the maize, but!

[en]
Mahmud: Kamal! What do you mean by “but”? Is something troubling you?

[en]
Kamal: I am in financial difficulty at the moment. The children's schools are about to open, and their fees and so on have to be paid.

[en]
Mahmud: I am glad to help you. Tell me, how much money do you need?

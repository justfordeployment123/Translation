# English source for book page 564

Second: There will be one muhallil, not more.

[en]
Third: That his (the muhallil's) mount will be equal to the mounts of the two (the two participants other than the muhallil), and he himself will be equal to the two of them.

[en]
Fourth: His (the muhallil's) archery will be equal to the archery of the two (the two men other than the muhallil). So it would not be right for the other participants in the competition to be grown men and the muhallil a small child.

[en]
Ammar: What is the reason for adopting the muhallil arrangement?

[en]
Teacher: This arrangement has been adopted so that through it the doubt and suspicion of gambling can be avoided. For if every one of those taking part in the competition puts in money, each will expect either to win or to lose, and this is exactly the situation of those who gamble. But if they include a third man who puts no money into it, this arrangement will become distant and separate from the form of gambling. And Allah knows best.

[en]
Ammar: Teacher! But people at many mosques regularly hold competitions of this kind.

[en]
Teacher: Whoever you see doing this, you should make him aware that this kind of competition is wrong, so that he may keep away from it.

[en]
Ammar: O our teacher! May Allah the Exalted bless you, may Allah the Exalted increase you in knowledge, and may He bring the Muslims as much benefit as possible through you.

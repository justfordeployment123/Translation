As usual, Ammar went home and noted down in his notebook everything that had happened that day. The next day at ten o'clock he set off for the teacher's house, and when he reached the door he knocked.

[en]
Teacher: Who is it?

[en]

[en]
Teacher: Welcome. The teacher embraced Ammar warmly, took him into the house and showed him hospitality.

[en]
Ammar: Teacher! Which market do you intend to go round today?

[en]

[en]

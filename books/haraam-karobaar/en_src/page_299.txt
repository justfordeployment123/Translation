# English source for book page 299

[en]
(Everyone ate together and offered Atiyyah the gift of their duas.)

[en]
Atiyyah: Teacher! Today is a day of great happiness for me. We have learned a great deal from you, and on top of that we have also had the honour of sitting and eating with you.

[en]
Atiyyah told his son to take the car out of the garage. The teacher thanked Atiyyah, and then Atiyyah set off in the car to drop the teacher and Ammar home. The others left from there for their own homes.

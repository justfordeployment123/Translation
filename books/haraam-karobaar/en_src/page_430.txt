# English source for book page 430

[en]
And when you ask (his wives) for something, ask them from behind a partition.

[en]
These are clear texts showing that a woman may ask a man for something from behind a partition, and that otherwise it is absolutely impermissible. So one should not turn away from this text of the Noble Quran.

Sa'd: Teacher! A female doctor is not so skilled, whereas male doctors are more knowledgeable and more skilled than they are.

[en]
Teacher: Before I answer this objection of yours, I want to tell you two stories that strengthen faith.

[en]
Sa'd: Yes, tell them.

[en]
Teacher: First: This is the story of a chaste woman who said that she would accept death, but would not have an operation at the hands of any male doctor. This woman was pregnant, and when the time of delivery drew near, her family took her to the hospital. The nurses took the woman into the operating theatre and laid her on the bed, and then they began to wait for the female doctor to come. After a while, a male doctor suddenly came into the operating room. As soon as the woman saw him, she began to scream and cry out; she quickly covered her head and face with her scarf and said: Get him out of here, get him out of here! When that ignorant doctor saw what the woman did, he became furious and began to insist that he would do her operation himself, but the woman would not agree in any way to have the operation done by him. Meanwhile, the time of delivery had drawn near.

# English source for book page 248

[en]
If it were obligatory, or an individual obligation (fard ayn), we would certainly accept it as a need. Allah the Exalted has commanded so much caution that He said:

[en]
And when you ask (his wives) for something, ask them from behind a partition.

[en]
Hasan: Maulana! Your way of thinking and ours are as far apart as the earth and the sky.

[en]
Teacher: How so?

[en]
Hasan: You are talking about a man whose intentions are corrupt, whose heart is diseased and whose mind is filthy; but thanks be to Allah, my heart is free of every kind of crookedness and my intentions are good. There is no treachery in my eyes. These girls are as dear to me as my own daughters.

[en]
Teacher: That is simply a delusion of yours.

[en]
Hasan: How?

[en]
Teacher: Because we are not equal even to the laces of the shoes of the people to whom this verse was revealed. I mean people like Abu Bakr as-Siddiq, Umar al-Faruq, Uthman al-Ghani, before whom even the angels feel shy, Ali, Aishah, Fatimah, Asma and Umm Salamah, may Allah be pleased with them, and others, about whose goodness, piety, purity and chastity Allah the Exalted Himself said:

[en]
That is purer for your hearts and their hearts.

[en]
Are my heart and yours purer than the hearts of those people? If the noble Companions, despite their fine qualities, their character and the purity of their hearts, acted on these commands, then what standing do I and you have left, when neither are our hearts like theirs, nor our intentions like theirs, nor the sincerity of our intentions like theirs?

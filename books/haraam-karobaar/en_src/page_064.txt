# English source for book page 64

[en]
Teacher: Because these two conditions are missing, this deal is not valid; rather, it is void.

[en]
Mahmud: Shaykh! Why is this deal void?

[en]
Teacher: Because it is established in the hadith that the people of Madinah used to make advance sales (salam) of fruit for one, two and three years. When the Prophet, peace be upon him, learned of this, he, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Whoever makes an advance sale, let him make it for a known measure, a known weight and a fixed term.”

[en]
Because of this hadith, it is necessary to specify the measure, the weight, the term and the price.

[en]
Kamal: What should we do now?

[en]
Teacher: The two of you should agree on the price, and the buyer should pay the sum at once.

[en]
Mahmud: Shaykh! May Allah grant you a good reward and bless you.

[en]
Teacher: May Allah protect you. We congratulate you on following the proof so quickly.

# English source for book page 211

[en]
Teacher: The solution is this: remove it from here at once and pour it away somewhere. Taking it back to the factory or the like, or selling it, is also impermissible and forbidden.

[en]
Ahmad: Teacher! In sha Allah, after I have sold the present stock, I will never buy or sell it again.

[en]
Teacher: What you say now is even more absurd than what you said before.

[en]
Ahmad: How so?

[en]
Teacher: Do you know that you will live until this stock runs out, and that you will be granted repentance before you die?

[en]
Ahmad: Yes.

[en]
Teacher:

[en]
“Alas! I have seen you content with life, yet, O you whose heart is set on this world, the grave alone is your dwelling. Today you take great pride in worldly finery and adornment, yet tomorrow you are to be buried in the graves. A person will walk about among people for only a few moments; in the end you will be buried. Your family will weep, beat their breasts and wail, but after a few days they too will forget their grief for you. How deeply people sleep in heedlessness, while the mill of death keeps grinding! Turn back to your Lord now, while this way is open.”

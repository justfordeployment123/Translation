# English source for book page 545

[hen]
Chapter Ninety-Four: Working with Singers, Musicians and Bands at Weddings

[en]
Ammar: Teacher! Does the same ruling also apply to someone who works with bands at weddings?

[en]
Teacher: (speaking with a smile) When music itself is forbidden, what do you think of those who work in music?

[en]
May Allah keep you safe! Dancing at weddings, playing instruments and holding other musical gatherings are all impermissible and forbidden acts, and whoever takes part in these acts in any way at all is, as it were, committing a forbidden act. In the same way, taking wages for this work is also forbidden, because music paves the way to fornication, it breeds hypocrisy and corruption in the heart, and it is one of the weapons of Satan, with which he leads people astray.

[en]

# English source for book page 220

[en]
Ammar: Teacher! What is our schedule from here on?

[en]
Teacher: Tomorrow I have some important business at the education office. You come too, and we will go together.

[en]
Ammar: In sha Allah, I will certainly come.

[en]
Teacher: May Allah bless you.

[en]
Ammar: My most respected teacher! If there is anything else I can do for you today, please tell me.

[en]
Teacher: May Allah reward you. Now go home.

[en]
Ammar: As-salamu alaikum wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh.

[en]
Teacher: Wa alaikum as-salam wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh. Come on time tomorrow.

[en]
Ammar: In sha Allah.

[en]
Ammar returned home and, as usual, noted down the whole day's conversation in his notebook.

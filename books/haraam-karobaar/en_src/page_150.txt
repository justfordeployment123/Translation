# English source for book page 150

[en]
“Only that meaning of your oath will be accepted which your companion would confirm.”

[en]
Amr (the narrator of the hadith) asked about the words:

[en]
What do they mean? So he, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
That is, the oath will be taken to mean what accords with the intention of the one who asked for the oath.

[en]
Imam an-Nawawi says:

[en]
“Because of tricks, deception and tawriyah (speaking in a way that hides the truth), that man will be a sinner.”

[en]
The sum of all the proofs is that swearing many oaths and using tricks is absolutely impermissible and forbidden.

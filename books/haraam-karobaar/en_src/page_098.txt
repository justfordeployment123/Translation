# English source for book page 98

[en]
Shopkeeper: You mean it is forbidden?

[en]
Teacher: Yes, it is forbidden.

[en]
Shopkeeper: Shaykh, with great apologies, forgive me: this ring that you have chosen is not new; it has been polished.

[en]
Teacher: What is the price of each, the old one and the new one?

[en]
Shopkeeper: The price of the new one is thirty-five pounds and the price of the old one is thirty pounds. Shaykh, I have been doing this detestable thing for a long time, but now I repent of it.

[en]
Teacher: You have asked Allah for forgiveness and made the defect of this ring clear, so now I will pay the price of a new ring. And remember one thing: Allah the Exalted says:

[en]
And whoever fears Allah - He will make for him a way out.

[en]
Shopkeeper: He said, weeping: Shaykh, what am I to do now about what I did in the past?

[en]
Teacher: If you know the people you deceived, give them back what is rightfully theirs, and if you do not know them, give charity on their behalf.

[en]
Shopkeeper: I do not even remember how many there were.

[en]
Teacher: Act on your best estimate.

# English source for book page 389

[hen]
Chapter Fifty-Nine: Buying and Selling School Bags with Pictures

[en]
Manager: Teacher! What is your view of school bags or satchels for schoolboys and schoolgirls that have obscene and extremely shameful pictures on them which arouse the passions, such as obscene pictures of women and the like?

[en]
Teacher: Anything that leads to obscenity among boys and girls, as when we see obscene pictures on cars, walls, books, school bags and so on: all of this is impermissible, because the following evils are found in it:

[en]
First: Looking at something forbidden.

[en]
Second: Such things arouse the passions of young boys and girls.

[en]
Third: Base morals spread among the believers.

[en]
Fourth: Such things give rise to trials and temptations.

[en]
Fifth: Lusts are born.

[en]

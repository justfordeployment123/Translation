# English source for book page 372

[hen]
Chapter Fifty-Six: On Bay' al-'Inah

[en]
The teacher was sitting there talking when a man came into the shop; he wanted to buy an AC.

[en]
Buyer: As-salamu alaikum wa rahmatullah.

[en]
Everyone: Wa alaikum as-salam wa rahmatullah.

[en]
Buyer: I want to buy such-and-such a kind of AC. What is its price?

[en]
Salah: If you want to buy it in cash, three thousand pounds, and if you want to buy it on instalments, four thousand.

[en]
Buyer: I want to buy it on instalments, but at the moment I have only four hundred pounds.

[en]
Salah: Come, let us ask the shop's manager, Faisal, and see what he says.

[en]
Faisal: Yes, what is the matter?

[en]
Salah: This gentleman wants to buy an AC on instalments, but he has only four hundred pounds to pay in advance.

[en]
Faisal: No, brother; according to the rules you will have to pay one thousand pounds in advance.

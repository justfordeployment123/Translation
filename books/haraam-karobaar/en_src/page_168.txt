# English source for book page 168

[en]
Teacher: Never take up the old way again. We are the owners of this land and all its produce, and we will not let anything be done in it that displeases Allah the Exalted. As for loss and damage, do not worry about that. If there is a fear of loss, we will help you, and you will get your full due.

[en]
Entrusting the matter to Allah, the worker, following the owner's instructions, packed the tomatoes in the way the Shariah requires, filled a truck, and took it to the market in the morning. See the wonder of Providence: this truck sold out before all the other trucks in the market, and far from making a loss, they made a good profit as well.

[en]
Mustafa! When I heard this story, this saying of Allah the Exalted came to my mind at once:

[en]
And whoever fears Allah - He will make for him a way out. And will provide for him from where he does not expect.

[en]
Ammar: May Allah the Exalted include us among the righteous.

[en]
Mustafa: May Allah reward you for the advice and admonition you have given us.

[en]
Workers! Turn these baskets out and pack them again in the way the Shaykh has just described.

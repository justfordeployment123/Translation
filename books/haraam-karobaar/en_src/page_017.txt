# English source for book page 17

[hen]
Publisher's Note

[en]
It is absolutely necessary for every Muslim to hold to the rulings of the Shariah and the teachings of the religion in all his affairs of this world and of the Hereafter, because Allah the Exalted says:

[en]
{{QEN 2:208}}

[en]
It is not permissible for any Muslim to act on the Book and the Sunnah in his acts of worship, yet do as he pleases in business dealings and social matters and consider himself free of the limits of the Shariah. It is part of the breadth and completeness of our religion that it gives a full account of every kind of act of worship and of every kind of business dealing and its rulings, and every Muslim can easily understand them and put them into practice.

[en]
In the book before you, modern questions about trade and buying and selling are set out in a most excellent way. The distinguishing feature of this book is that all these rulings of the Shariah and teachings of the religion are written in the form of a story and a dialogue, so that the reader cannot help being moved by it, and once he begins the book he does not want to put it down until he has finished it.

# English source for book page 527

[en]
How will you be able to keep your eyes lowered? How will you be able to avoid cooperating with them in sin and aggression? In such places you will inevitably be their partner in every evil deed. The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“A person's share of adultery has been written for him, which he will inevitably meet. The adultery of the eyes is looking, the adultery of the ears is listening, the adultery of the tongue is speaking, the adultery of the hands is grasping, the adultery of the feet is walking; the heart desires and longs, and then the private parts confirm that (desire) or deny it.”

[en]
That is, by going to such a place you will commit the adultery of your eyes, ears, hands, feet, nose and so on, and in the end the adultery of these limbs will pave the way for the adultery of the private parts. In such places it becomes very hard to stay within the limits and bounds of the Shariah, and very easy to fall into forbidden acts.

[en]
Young man: What is the solution to this?

[en]
Teacher: Leave this work at once and seek your provision by lawful means, because this work and its wages are forbidden.

# English source for book page 202

[en]
Hassan: That is very difficult, because at that time there is a queue of customers.

[en]
Teacher: Brother! Talk like this is a sign of weak faith, and it is also a test from Allah of how far you follow Him. Remember this saying of Allah the Exalted:

[en]
Satan threatens you with poverty and orders you to immorality, while Allah promises you forgiveness from Him and bounty.

[en]
So do not imagine that the earnings from the times of the Friday prayer will make you rich; rather, these earnings bring poverty. Fear Allah and purify your trade of the work of Satan.

[en]
Hassan: How is this poverty and hardship, when we sell a great deal at those times?

[en]
Teacher: By poverty is meant trade that is empty of blessing.

[en]
Hassan: By Allah, Teacher, you have spoken the truth. A person earns so much money, yet he cannot understand where it goes; it flies off into the air like birds.

[en]
Teacher: That is exactly what I said.

[en]
Hassan: Teacher! From now on, in sha Allah, we will close the store at the time of the Friday sermon, the Friday prayer and every prayer. May Allah reward you for advising us.

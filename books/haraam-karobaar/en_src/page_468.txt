# English source for book page 468

[en]
However, when the husband is miserly and does not spend as much as is needed on his children and the household expenses, the wife may take his wealth, money and so on, as much as she needs, without letting him know, because it is reported that Hind bint Utbah, the wife of Abu Sufyan, may Allah be pleased with him, came to the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, and said: O Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him! Abu Sufyan is a miserly man. He does not give me as much for my children as I need, so may I take (money for expenses) from his pocket without his knowing? The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Take as much as is enough for you and your children, in the customary and reasonable way.”

[en]
In the light of this hadith we can say that a wife may take her husband's wealth without his permission in the following two cases:

[en]
First: The husband is miserly and does not spend as much as is needed on his children.

[en]
Second: The wife does not take out more money than is needed.

[en]
But in this matter there are some other rules and principles too, which must be observed.

[en]
Husain: What are these rules and principles?

[en]
Teacher: First: The woman should take out money to spend on the expenses in the customary and reasonable way, without extravagance or luxury as her aim.

[en]
Second: She should not do this in front of the children, or else the thought will come into the children's minds that their mother is stealing.

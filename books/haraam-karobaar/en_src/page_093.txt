# English source for book page 93

[en]
“On the Day of Resurrection, every deceiver will have a banner, and it will be said: This is the deceit of so-and-so.”

[en]
If, even after such a severe warning, a man does not give up this vile crime, it is a sign of his weak faith. A person should worry: having deceived and cheated today, with what face will I appear in Allah's court tomorrow?

[en]
Allah the Exalted says:

[en]
{{QEN 83:1 83:2 83:3}}

[en]
For the trader who gives short measure and weight there is a valley in Hell called “Wayl”, from whose severity Hell itself seeks refuge every day.

[en]
Fear Allah, and be so careful in matters of measure and weight that people have no misunderstanding about you. We are not blaming you, but we do warn you: make your soul so pure that it cannot lead you into this evil. May Allah bless us all with guidance.

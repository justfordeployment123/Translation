Advice to Husbands:

[en]
Here I also want to advise husbands that as long as you keep using good sense in household matters and keep watching over the affairs of the home, you will remain safe from every kind of trouble. A man should not be heedless of household matters even for a moment. Bringing up the family and the children in the best way, and providing for their expenses, should be the husband's first priority. He should not be miserly in spending on the home, the children and his wife; rather, staying within his means, he should do as much for them as he can. This increases love, affection and sincerity, and the household keeps running in the best way.

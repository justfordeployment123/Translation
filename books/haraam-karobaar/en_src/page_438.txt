# English source for book page 438

[en]
Allah the Exalted is the One who is responsible for provision.

[en]
Salim: Yes, it is true that provision is the responsibility of Allah the Exalted, but reason does not accept it. So how will the expenses of ten children be met from such a small salary?

[en]
Teacher: Allah the Exalted puts blessing into it, and this same small salary becomes enough. Provision is of two kinds:

[en]
First: Positive. Second: Negative.

[en]
First: Positive: what Allah the Exalted gives His servants in the form of wealth and riches.

[en]
Second: Negative: that is, Allah the Exalted removes trials, misfortunes and hardships from His servant. This is a provision that is more beneficial than the positive kind.

[en]
Salim: Teacher! Thank you for explaining all these things to us.

[en]
(After the conversation, Sa'd and Salim got up and went back without having the check-up done.)

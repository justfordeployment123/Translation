And do not display yourselves as (was) the display of the former times of ignorance.

Third: In the hadiths the mixing of men and women has been forbidden.

[en]
Fourth: There is a hadith of the Prophet:

[en]
(A woman is something to be covered), meaning that a woman must keep herself covered.

[en]
Fifth: Among women's responsibilities are bringing up the children, protecting the honour of the home and the family, and so on.

[en]
Sixth: When a woman goes out of the house, she becomes a trial for men.

[en]
Especially when a woman's husband, father or guardian is there and is working to provide her daily bread, no excuse remains for the woman to go out of the house to take a job and work.

[en]
The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Each of you is responsible, and each of you will be asked about his responsibility... A man is responsible for his family, and he will be asked about his responsibility...”

# English source for book page 263

[hen]
Chapter Thirty-Seven: Taking Part of the Road into One's Own Land

[en]
The teacher, his student and Atiyyah were drinking tea when Hajji Farid Abu Jum'a passed close by, walking about his land. When Abu Jum'a caught sight of the teacher, he ran over to him. The teacher stood up and embraced Abu Jum'a, and they asked after each other. Standing there, the teacher noticed something very strange on Abu Jum'a's land.

[en]
A road ran past Abu Jum'a's land. He had planted trees along the edge of the road, so that part of the road was being taken into his land and the way was becoming narrow for those passing along it.

[en]
The teacher asked Abu Jum'a about it.

[en]
Teacher: Abu Jum'a, what is this? You have dug up the edges of the road, planted trees there and made them part of your property, as if this were your own private land.

[en]
Abu Jum'a: Teacher, we have little land and our household expenses are very high. In this way we have widened our land a little. Still, we have left room for people to pass.

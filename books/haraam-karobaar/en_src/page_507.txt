# English source for book page 507

[hen]
Chapter Eighty-Three: Sewing Indecent Clothes

[en]
While the teacher was speaking to Zaid, a buyer came into the shop and began listening to what the teacher was saying. Now he turned to the teacher.

[en]
Buyer: Teacher! If in your view fashion shows are forbidden, then what do you think of those who sew these indecent clothes?

[en]
Teacher: What is your name?

[en]
Buyer: My name is Ali.

[en]
Teacher: Ali! My name is Salih. The answer to the question you have asked is that sewing indecent clothes is forbidden for the very same causes and reasons that we have already explained; however, some even more dangerous things are found in it as well.

[en]
Ali: Such as what?

[en]
Teacher: First: In this work there is always occasion to talk with women and to have other dealings with them.

[en]

# English source for book page 130

[hen]
Summary of the Currency Market Tour

[en]
First: Buying and selling one currency for another must be equal. It is not permissible to pay part of the amount and leave part of it on credit.

[en]
Second: Selling torn and worn banknotes for less is impermissible.

[en]
Third: Buying and selling a cheque for less is impermissible.

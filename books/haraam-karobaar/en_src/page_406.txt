# English source for book page 406

[en]
The next day, at the time of the Asr prayer, Ammar arrived at Teacher Salih's house. The teacher seated Ammar and showed him hospitality.

[en]
Ammar: Teacher! Where are we going today?

[en]
Teacher: I have an appointment for today with the dentist at the dental hospital, so let us head for the hospital.

[en]
Ammar: May Allah heal you! All right.

[en]
Teacher: I am not in any pain; I only want to have my teeth cleaned.

[en]
(The teacher and Ammar set off towards the hospital.)

[en]
Teacher: Ammar! Stop a taxi to take us to the hospital.

[en]
(Ammar stopped a taxi, the two of them got in, and the taxi set off towards the hospital.)

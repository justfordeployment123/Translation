# English source for book page 126

[en]
It is narrated from Abdullah ibn Umar, may Allah be pleased with them both, that Bilal, may Allah be pleased with him, brought some fine dates to the noble Prophet, peace be upon him. The noble Prophet, peace be upon him, asked: Where are these from? Bilal, may Allah be pleased with him, said: I had some poor dates; I sold two sa' of them and bought this one sa' of fine dates in exchange, so that I could feed the noble Prophet, peace be upon him. He, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Oh, oh! This is interest itself.”

[en]
The way the noble Prophet, peace be upon him, objected to what Bilal, may Allah be pleased with him, did is clear proof that buying and selling of this kind is not right, in which a ribawi item is exchanged for another ribawi item without being equal and in cash.

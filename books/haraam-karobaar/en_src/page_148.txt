# English source for book page 148

[en]
The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Beware of swearing too many oaths, for it makes goods sell, but no blessing remains.”

[en]
Imam an-Nawawi, may Allah have mercy on him, says:

[en]
“In this hadith, swearing many oaths in trade is forbidden.”

[en]
The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Whoever wrongfully takes possession of a Muslim's property by means of a false oath, Allah the Exalted will make the Fire obligatory for him and Paradise forbidden to him.”

[en]
A man said: O Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him! Even if it is something trivial? He, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Even if it is just a twig from a tree.”

[en]
This is forbidden in countless hadiths.

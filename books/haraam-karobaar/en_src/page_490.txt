# English source for book page 490

[hen]
Chapter Eighty-One: Women and Men Working Together

[en]
Abdur-Rahim: Teacher! In today's gathering we would also like to know whether women and men can work together. Is it forbidden or lawful?

[en]
Teacher: For women to go out of the house to work and take jobs is sheer injustice and wrong done to them, because a woman, by her physical make-up and her natural disposition, does not have the strength to work shoulder to shoulder with men. In terms of strength and power, a woman does not have the abilities that Allah, Lord of the worlds, has given to men.

[en]
A woman is weaker than a man for the following reasons:

[en]
First: Menstruation: This remains part of a woman's life for a long span of time, and during the days of her period a woman does not have the capacity for work that she has in normal circumstances. At times a woman also has to bear hardship because of it.

[en]
Second: Pregnancy: The state of pregnancy causes a woman even more discomfort than menstruation, and during those days a woman finds her work quite difficult.

[en]
Third: Childbirth and the postnatal period: In this period a woman goes through severe pain and suffering, and because of heavy bleeding she also becomes weak.

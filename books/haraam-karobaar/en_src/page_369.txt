# English source for book page 369

[hen]
Chapter Fifty-Five: Charging a Fine When an Instalment Is Late

[en]
Muhammad: Faisal! There is one condition for buying on instalments that I did not understand.

[en]
Faisal: Which condition?

[en]
Muhammad: Condition number five.

[en]
Faisal: Yes, it means that if the customer does not pay an instalment by the fixed date, he may be fined twenty pounds a day.

[en]
Muhammad: This is plain injustice to the customer. A person's instalment can be late because of hardship. What need is there for such strictness and for taking extra money?

[en]
Faisal: This is not injustice or strictness; it is a way of protecting our rights.

[en]
Muhammad: Teacher! What do you think?

[en]
Teacher: This is forbidden without any disagreement; in fact, it is outright interest (riba) of the kind practised in the Age of Ignorance, about which Allah the Exalted says:

[en]
{{QEN 3:130}}

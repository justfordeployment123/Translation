Abbas: Maulana, do not be so strict. There is a great deal of room in religion.

[en]
Teacher: My dear brother! Fix this firmly in your mind: honour, position, rank and standing are a blessing from Allah the Exalted, so a servant should thank Allah for them, and the best way of giving thanks is for a servant to benefit people through his position. This is the meaning of this saying of the noble Prophet, peace be upon him. He, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Whoever among you is able to benefit his believing brother should certainly do so.”

[en]
Whoever benefits his Muslim brother without committing anything forbidden, keeps some injustice away from him or helps him get his right, and helps him with a sincere heart, Allah grants him reward and recompense.

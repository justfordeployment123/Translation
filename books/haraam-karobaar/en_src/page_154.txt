# English source for book page 154

[en]
Buyer: Shaykh! What is the solution to this problem?

[en]
Teacher: You have a choice between two things; that is, there are two solutions to this problem:

[en]
Buyer: Which are they?

[en]
Teacher: First: Return the cow and take your money back; and if you want this same cow, then make the deal again from the beginning, since now you know about the defect.

[en]
Buyer: What is the second solution?

[en]
Teacher: Second: Take back part of the price in proportion to the defect.

[en]
Buyer: What does that mean?

[en]
Teacher: That is, estimate how much difference in price there is between a defective cow and a sound one; then take back your money according to that estimate and keep the cow yourself.

[en]
Buyer: Addressing the seller, he said: What do you want?

[en]
Seller: I will give back part of the price in proportion to the defect, and I ask Allah to forgive me for this mistake.

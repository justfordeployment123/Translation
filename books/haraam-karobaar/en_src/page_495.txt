# English source for book page 495

[en]
In today's circumstances it is not a praiseworthy act for a woman to go out of the house to take a job; it is better for her to stay at home. Besides this, all men and women should reflect on this saying of Allah the Exalted:

[en]
And abide in your houses.

[en]
This command is for all women. The woman who is closest to her Lord is the one who stays at home, especially when, staying at home, she worships Allah and obeys her husband.

[en]
Ali, may Allah be pleased with him, asked his wife Fatimah, may Allah be pleased with her: O Fatimah! What is best for a woman? Fatimah, may Allah be pleased with her, replied: That men do not see her and she does not see men.

[en]
Ali, may Allah be pleased with him, used to say: Why do you feel no shame, why do you feel no sense of honour, that one of you leaves his wife among men, so that she looks at them and they look at her?

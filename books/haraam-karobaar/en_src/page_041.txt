Teacher: Now let us come to your case. In the Shariah this act is given the following name: giving short measure and weight. That is, when a person measures or weighs for himself he takes the full amount, and when he measures or weighs for others he gives less and cheats people of their due. This is a most blameworthy act. Allah the Exalted has made it forbidden and has revealed verses about it that will be recited until the Day of Resurrection:

[en]
{{QEN 83:1 83:2 83:3 83:4 83:5 83:6}}

[en]
Allah the Exalted has given a severe warning that for those people who cheat others of their due through measuring and weighing there is a valley in Hell in which they will be given blood and pus to drink.

# English source for book page 367

[en]
Second: Whether this deal takes place or not depends on the future, so ownership does not stay settled.

[en]
Faisal: What is the proof for this?

[en]
Teacher: The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Whoever makes two deals in one deal, then for him there is either the lower of the two prices or interest (riba).”

[en]
That is, dealing in this way cannot escape one of two cases:

[en]
First: Either he will take the lower price and suffer a loss.

[en]
Second: He will take the higher price and so receive interest.

[en]
Any deal in which either of the two cases is found will be invalid.

[en]
Muhammad: What is the solution, and the way the Shariah lays down?

[en]
Teacher: The solution is very easy: the two of you settle in this same sitting whether its price will be paid in cash or in instalments.

[en]
Muhammad: Praise be to Allah! Today we have benefited a great deal from the rulings of the Shariah, and we have learned that these forms of buying and selling are forbidden. Faisal! I want to buy this AC in instalments. What do you say?

[en]
Faisal: All right, I agree.

Teacher: It is narrated from Abdullah ibn Mas'ud, may Allah be pleased with him, that the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“On the Day of Resurrection, those who make pictures will have the severest punishment with Allah.”

[en]
It is narrated from Abu Hurairah, may Allah be pleased with him, that the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Allah the Exalted says: Who could be a greater wrongdoer than the one who sets out to create like My creation? Let him create a single grain, or let him create a single ant.”

[en]
It is narrated from Abdullah ibn Abbas, may Allah be pleased with them both, that the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Every maker of pictures will go to Hell, and for every single picture a living being will be made, by which he will be punished in Hell.”

[en]
All these hadiths show that pictures are forbidden, whether the pictures are of people, of animals or of other living things; whether they cast a shadow or not; whether they are printed on paper, taken with a camera, or carved into stone and wood. In the hadiths all these kinds of pictures have been declared forbidden.

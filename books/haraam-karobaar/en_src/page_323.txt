# English source for book page 323

[en]
Oh brother! Act on all the commands of Allah and His Messenger, peace be upon him, whether they are small or great, and you will find happiness in this world and in the Hereafter.

[hen]
Advice to the Shopkeeper:

[en]
O my shopkeeper brother! You have heard the whole conversation. Now understand this well too: just as having the beard shaved is forbidden, in the same way taking payment for shaving beards is also forbidden. When Allah the Exalted declares something forbidden, its price also becomes forbidden. So try to make your shop free of this forbidden business, so that the Sunnah of the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, is not insulted and degraded in it.

[en]
The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, has declared himself free of a man who turns away from his Sunnah, peace be upon him. He, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Whoever turns away from my Sunnah is not of me.”

# English source for book page 332

[en]
“For an iron comb to be run through the head of one of you is better for him than touching a woman who is not lawful for him.”

[en]
With these clear texts, how can any barber cut a woman's hair? Moreover, there the matter does not stay limited to cutting hair; many other things against the Shariah also take place, such as women going unveiled, men and women mixing, and so on. How do people tolerate their mother, sister or daughter sitting in front of a non-mahram man and having her hair cut by him? Have such people no shame or modesty at all, who themselves hand their women over to the barber so that he can commit the adultery of the hands, eyes, nose, ears and so on to the full? I seek Allah's forgiveness... And the height of it is that outside beauty parlours it is written, “Men are not allowed to enter.”

[en]
Today the situation is such that creams have been invented that are put on the visible parts of the body so that the colour of those parts matches the colour of the clothes as well. And the clothes are such that the arms, legs, chest and so on are seen bare, and with great shamelessness the barber rubs those creams onto the woman's body with his own hands.

[en]
The men responsible for such women are dayyuths. The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“A dayyuth will not enter Paradise. The noble Companions asked: O Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him! Who is a dayyuth? He, peace be upon him, replied: The man who sees wickedness in his family and keeps silent.”

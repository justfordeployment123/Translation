# English source for book page 107

[en]
Salim: May Allah reward you. Now what is the solution?

[en]
Teacher: The solution is very easy: buy as much gold as the money you have will pay for, and buy the rest later, when you have the money.

[en]
Buyer: Shaykh, I apologise for my rude behaviour. I did not know this ruling. Brother Salim! Weigh out nine thousand five hundred pounds' worth of gold for me, and I will pay for the rest and take it another time.

[en]
When the deal was done, the man took his goods and left. As the Shaykh and Ammar were about to leave the shop, another man came in to sell a gold necklace.

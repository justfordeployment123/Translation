# English source for book page 472

[en]
Ammar: Teacher! Is it disliked to stay up late and talk after the Isha prayer?

[en]
Teacher: Staying up after the Isha prayer is not disliked in every case; rather, in some situations it is permissible. For example, first: for the sake of gaining knowledge; second: for the sake of reforming and training Muslims; third: talking with a guest; fourth: for the sake of teaching and training one's family. That is, staying up and talking after the Isha prayer for purposes of the Shariah is right.

[en]
It is narrated from Anas, may Allah be pleased with him:

[en]
“The iqamah was called for the (Isha) prayer while a man kept on talking privately with the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, until the noble Companions began to doze.”

[en]
Another report says:

[en]
“Abu Bakr, may Allah be pleased with him, came to the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, in the evening, and stayed until as much of the night had passed as Allah willed.”

[en]
Fifth: a person who wants to pray; sixth: a traveller.

[en]
These six people may stay up after the Isha prayer.

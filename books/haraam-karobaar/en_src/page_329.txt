# English source for book page 329

[en]
“The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, forbade qaza'.”

[en]
Young man: What does qaza' mean?

[en]
Teacher: Imam an-Nawawi, may Allah have mercy on him, says:

[en]
“Qaza' means shaving the hair of part of the head and leaving part unshaved.”

[en]
It is narrated from Abdullah ibn Umar, may Allah be pleased with them both, that the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, saw a child, part of whose head had been shaved and part left. He, peace be upon him, forbade doing this and said:

[en]
“Shave all the hair of the head, or leave all of it.”

[en]
Some scholars hold that such a style and haircut is satanic, or is the way of the Jews.

[en]
Think about yourself: you are imitating your enemies, and you do not even realise it.

[en]
Young man: Teacher! Do not be so harsh; this is the age of enlightenment.

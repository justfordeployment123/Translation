# English source for book page 535

[en]
That is, asking is permissible in these three situations:

[en]
First: In the case of taking on a financial responsibility, that is, taking on responsibility for the financial affairs of two groups while bringing about peace between them and the like, or standing surety for someone and so on.

[en]
Second: When a calamity strikes by which all of one's wealth is lost.

[en]
Third: Severe poverty and destitution, that is, when a person has nothing at all left.

[en]
There is also a fourth situation: that a man asks the ruler for zakat, khums (the one-fifth share of the spoils of war) or the like.

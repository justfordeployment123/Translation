Chapter Twenty-Eight: Buying and Selling Alcohol

[en]
While going round the supermarket, the teacher saw some strange kinds of drinks set out in the fridges and freezers of most of the shops. Going to one shop, the teacher opened the fridge of juices and drinks so that he could take a good look at this suspicious drink and learn something about it. The teacher asked an employee about these drinks, and he replied that they were various kinds of alcoholic drinks. On hearing this, the teacher felt that there was a pressing need there for the work of enjoining good and forbidding evil, so he went into one of the stores.

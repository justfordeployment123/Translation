# English source for book page 245

[hen]
Chapter Thirty-Five: Male Teachers Teaching Grown-Up Girls in Girls' Schools

[en]
Muhammad Hasan, a teacher at a higher secondary school, came into the room.

[en]
Hasan: As-salamu alaikum wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh.

[en]
Everyone: Wa alaikum as-salam wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh.

[en]
Atif: Teacher! Brother Hasan here teaches Urdu to girls at a higher secondary school.

[en]
Teacher: Welcome. Hasan, how are you?

[en]
Hasan: Praise be to Allah, all is well. I have learned that teachers have been promoted and that my name is among them, because of which I will soon be transferred to another school. Is there any such news?

[en]
Ubaid: Is it necessary that when someone is promoted, his school is changed?

[en]
Hasan: I do not know whether it is necessary or not, but I am very settled at the school where I have been teaching for five years, and I do not want to be moved anywhere else.

[en]
Teacher: Is any man really at ease and happy teaching grown-up girls?

[en]
Hasan: I certainly am at ease.

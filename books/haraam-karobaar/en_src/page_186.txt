# English source for book page 186

[en]
Ammar: What way is there to make shopkeepers give up buying and selling these things that spread disease?

[en]
Teacher: I think shopkeepers should be encouraged, and made to realise how harmful it is.

[en]
Ammar: Teacher! That seems rather a hard task. Isn't there some interesting way by which we can get shopkeepers to understand?

[en]
Teacher: Yes, there is a way. Some of our friends tried it. They say: We made a plan that we would go one at a time to large shops that sell cigarettes, tobacco and the like. First one man would go to a shop and quietly look to see whether there were cigarettes and so on there. If the shopkeeper kept cigarettes, he would buy goods worth about ten thousand rupees from him. Naturally the shopkeeper would be pleased that such a big customer had come. Then he would have the goods packed, and after that, taking the money out of his pocket, he would settle the bill with the shopkeeper. And at the very moment the shopkeeper put out his hand to take the money, he would look at the cigarettes, cry out and say angrily: I seek Allah's forgiveness! There is no might and no power except with Allah! We seek refuge in Allah! Do you sell cigarettes too? The shopkeeper would say: Yes, is there anything wrong with that?

[en]
Buyer: We do not buy from shopkeepers who also sell cigarettes. After that he should put the money back in his pocket, leave the goods and come away. The shopkeeper will surely be sorry that such a big customer went away without buying anything. Then, a little later, a second man should go to the same shop, do the same thing, and in the end leave the goods and come away. In this way the thought will come into the shopkeeper's mind: My goods are not selling because of these cigarettes.

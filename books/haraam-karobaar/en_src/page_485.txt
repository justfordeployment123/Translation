[en]
Without any embarrassment or shame, those people would take me to take part in various fashion fairs. My purpose was to strip off my modesty and give pleasure to the onlookers. Sometimes I would feel how worthless a thing I had become for those people. There was only one condition for working there: that I should strip off my modesty and keep saying yes to their every word, and the secret of my success lay in bidding farewell to all my feelings, my self-respect, my reason and my sense of honour. Before I could lose what was left of my reason, my dignity and my sense of honour, I gave up that work and repented.

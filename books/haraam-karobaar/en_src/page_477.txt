# English source for book page 477

[hen]
Chapter Seventy-Seven: A Woman Working as an Air Hostess

[en]
Abdur-Rahim: Teacher! Allow me to leave; I want to sleep now, because tomorrow morning I have to go to Cairo.

[en]
Teacher: Is all well there?

[en]
Abdur-Rahim: Yes, all is well. My daughter works for an airline company, and she has been posted to Cairo as an air hostess. I have to go and drop her off there.

[en]
Teacher: What will you do there?

[en]
Abdur-Rahim: I will drop her off and come back.

[en]
Teacher: There is no might and no power except with Allah! How fearlessly you will go and leave your own flesh and blood there alone, when the times are so full of trials, and you know the atmosphere of Cairo too.

[en]
Abdur-Rahim: Should I keep her sitting at home for fear of the times? People struggle hard to get a job like this; how can we let this opportunity go?

[en]
Teacher: Before Allah it is your responsibility to keep your daughter safe from every kind of trial. This is a matter of your family's honour.

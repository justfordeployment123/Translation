# English source for book page 391

[hen]
Chapter Sixty: Buying and Selling Musical Instruments

[en]
When the teacher saw some musical instruments in a corner of the shop, he was filled with deep grief and sorrow, and he turned to the manager.

[en]
Teacher: What is all this?

[en]
Manager: (Laughing loudly, he said:) It seems that, like the earlier things, these will now be forbidden too. Teacher! Today you have declared my whole business forbidden.

[en]
Teacher: Do not wander off the subject; rather, quickly try to learn what guidance we get from the Quran and hadith about music. Is there any room in Islam for singing and playing music, or is it forbidden?

[en]
Manager: Yes, tell me! What do the Quran and hadith say about music?

[en]
Teacher: Allah the Exalted says:

[en]
And of the people is he who buys the amusement of speech to mislead (others) from the way of Allah without knowledge.

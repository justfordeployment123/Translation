# English source for book page 533

[en]
They seat their families outside the mosque so that people will give them charity as they go home. Then they get up from there and go and stand in another mosque. As for the truth about such people, Allah the Exalted alone knows best whether they are really deserving or not. However, there are also some other people who are truly in need, but they do not let a request pass their lips and do not beg as beggars do. It is about these very people that Allah the Exalted said:

[en]
An ignorant (person) would think them self-sufficient because of their restraint, but you will know them by their (characteristic) sign. They do not ask people persistently (or at all).

[en]
So special care should be taken of this second kind of people.

# English source for book page 125

[hen]
Chapter Thirteen: Selling Torn and Worn Banknotes for Less

[en]
When the teacher had finished his conversation, another man, who had been sitting nearby listening to what he said, asked a question.

[en]
Man: Sir! What is the ruling of the Shariah on selling torn and worn banknotes for less, for example selling ten old pounds for nine new pounds?

[en]
Teacher: Because of the earlier hadith, this practice is not right, for the noble Prophet, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Gold for gold, silver for silver, wheat for wheat, barley for barley, dates for dates, salt for salt: the buying and selling must be equal for equal and in cash, and whoever gives more or asks for more has dealt in interest (riba).”

[en]
The one who gives more and the one who takes more are both sinners. By the rule, a thousand rupees should be sold for a thousand rupees.

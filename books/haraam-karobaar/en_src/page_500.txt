# English source for book page 500

[en]
At the appointed time Ammar arrived at the teacher's house and knocked on the door.

[en]
Teacher: Who is it?

[en]
Ammar: It is Ammar.

[en]
Teacher: Welcome, welcome.

[en]
Ammar came into the house, and the teacher entertained him as his guest.

[en]
Ammar: Teacher! Where do you intend to go today?

[en]
Teacher: We have to buy some clothes for the children, and we also have to buy a plain prayer mat to pray on.

[en]
Ammar: Come, let us go.

[en]
The two of them set off towards the market for clothes, carpets and the like. In the market, clothes, prayer mats and so on could be seen in one shop, so the two of them went into that shop.

# English source for book page 534

[en]
“Stay until the charity comes to us, and then we will give an order about it in your favour. He says: He, peace be upon him, said: O Qabisah! Asking is permitted only to one of three: a man who takes on a financial responsibility (an indemnity); asking is permissible for him until he obtains it, and then he should refrain (from asking). A man who is struck by a calamity that destroys his wealth; asking is permissible for him until he finds support for his living, or the gap in his living is filled. And a man who faces destitution, until three people of understanding from his people testify that so-and-so has been overtaken by destitution; asking is permissible for him until he finds support for his living, or the gap in his living is filled. O Qabisah! Asking apart from these is forbidden, and the one who does it consumes what is forbidden.”

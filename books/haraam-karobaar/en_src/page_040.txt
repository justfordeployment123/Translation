# English source for book page 40

[en]
Abu Shawqi: I told you, it is business shrewdness and cleverness.

[en]
Ammar: If you will permit me, may I disagree with you on this very matter? I want to tell you that what you are doing is impermissible; in fact, it is forbidden.

[en]
Abu Shawqi: (He began to speak angrily and heatedly, his veins swelling) You keep on making everything forbidden for us! But Ammar is listening to him with a smile.

[en]
Ammar: May Allah reward you! Instead of disagreeing with each other, we should ask our Shaykh about this.

[en]
Abu Shawqi: This question seems rather complicated. Where is the Shaykh? Let us ask him.

[en]
Ammar: May Allah keep you safe. The Shaykh is standing over there.

[en]
The two of them went to the Shaykh and said: As-salamu alaikum wa rahmatullah.

[en]
Teacher: Wa alaikum as-salam wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh.

[en]
Ammar: Shaykh! May Allah bless you. You saw just now the way Abu Shawqi was measuring and weighing. What is its ruling in the Shariah?

[en]
Teacher: Abu Shawqi, may Allah keep you safe! Do not think that we declare anything forbidden or lawful on our own account; rather, it is Allah who makes a thing lawful or forbidden. Whatever our religion makes lawful for us is lawful, and whatever it makes forbidden is forbidden. It is not permissible for us in any way to say anything of our own that goes against Allah and His Messenger.

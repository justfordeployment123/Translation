# English source for book page 271

[en]
Allah is the Greatest! What ignorance it is that people, without any fear, trample on the rights of their weak brothers, sisters and children. This is not only injustice and wrongdoing; it is like feeding oneself and one's children the fire of Hell.

[en]
Allah the Exalted says:

[en]
{{QEN 4:10}}

[en]
This blessed verse holds a severe warning for every person who wrongfully and unlawfully devours the property of orphans.

[en]
Some people set aside their sisters' share of their parents' inheritance in such a way that the sisters have to face great difficulty in taking possession of it.

[en]
For example, the sisters' share of the land is marked out where it is far from the road, of low value, unfit for farming, where no one will rent or lease it, or where there is no buyer for it, and so on. In other words, worthless, poor land is left for the sisters, or little bits of land are marked out for them in different places. Then the sister is forced either to buy all the land, his share and her own, or to sell her share to him, when in fact she is not able to buy all that land.

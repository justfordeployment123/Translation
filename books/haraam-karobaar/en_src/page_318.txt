Fourth: It is narrated from Abdullah ibn Umar, may Allah be pleased with them both, that the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“The noble Prophet, peace be upon him, commanded that the moustaches be trimmed and the beard be grown.”

[en]
Fifth: It is narrated from Abu Hurairah, may Allah be pleased with him, that the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Unlike the Magians, have your moustaches cut and let your beards grow.”

[en]
Sixth: Some of the noble Ansari Companions, may Allah be pleased with them, said to the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, that the People of the Book cut the hair of their chins (the beard) and leave the hair above their lips (the moustache). He, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Cut the hair above your lips (your moustaches), let the hair of your chins (your beards) grow, and act contrary to the People of the Book.”

[en]

Second: The heart must not be greedy for it.

[en]
Abdullah ibn Umar, may Allah be pleased with them both, said: We would not ask for anything of this world, and we would not turn back provision that came from Allah.

[en]
Bahnasi: In sha Allah, from today on we will not take payment for the bull.

[en]
Abu Jum'a: Now what should I do to have my cow mated?

[en]
Bahnasi: Go to our cattle yard whenever you like and have your cow mated, and just pray for us.

[en]
(Abu Jum'a took the cow to Bahnasi's cattle yard and had her mated. When he came back, he told his son to take a large bundle of fodder from their own cattle yard and leave it in Bahnasi's cattle yard.)

[en]
Bahnasi: Abu Jum'a! I told you just to pray for us; that is enough.

[en]
Abu Jum'a: I am sending this of my own free will.

[en]
Teacher: May Allah the Exalted bless you. This way is fine; there is no harm in it.

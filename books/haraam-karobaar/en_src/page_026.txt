# English source for book page 26

[en]
Allah the Exalted has described those who are forbidden in marriage in this way:

[en]
Prohibited to you (for marriage) are your mothers, your daughters, your sisters, your father's sisters, your mother's sisters, your brother's daughters, your sister's daughters, your (milk) mothers who nursed you, your sisters through nursing, your wives' mothers.

[en]
With regard to earning wealth, Allah the Exalted has described the forbidden things in this way:

[en]
But Allah has permitted trade and has forbidden interest.

[en]
Concerning lawful things, Allah the Exalted has laid down a principle: all pure things are lawful, and because there are so many of them, Allah the Exalted has not listed them.

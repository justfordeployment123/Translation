# English source for book page 42

[en]
That is, when they buy something from people, they take it by full measure and full weight.

[en]
That is, when they weigh out for others:

[en]
They give short measure and short weight. Then Allah the Exalted rebukes such people, saying:

[en]
That is, those who give short measure and weight have forgotten that after death they will be brought back to life and presented before Allah the Exalted on the Day of Reckoning.

[en]
That is, people will be waiting in fear, with heads bowed, for the judgement of Allah the Exalted, and they will remain standing for a long time. Standing in that intense heat, some will be floundering in their own sweat, and some will be drowned in sweat up to their earlobes.

[en]
The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“On the Day when mankind will stand before the Lord of the worlds to give their account, they will be drowned in sweat up to their earlobes.”

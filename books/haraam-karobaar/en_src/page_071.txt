# English source for book page 71

[en]
Teacher: If you know those buyers, give them back what they are owed; and if you do not know them, give the money for their share in charity.

[en]
Trader: I do not even know how many people there were.

[en]
Teacher: Give in charity according to your best estimate.

[en]
Trader: Shaykh! We will start with you first of all. Here is your one pound.

[en]
Teacher: You have followed Allah's command, so I happily give this one pound back to you, and we will buy another sack from you.

[en]
Trader: May Allah bless you. When one hears Allah's command, one should obey it.

[en]
A sherbet seller passed by, and Ammar hurried over to him.

[en]
Ammar: Two glasses of sherbet, please.

[en]
The Shaykh sat down to one side and began to drink the sherbet. Meanwhile the trader also finished selling his rice, and Allah the Exalted gave him a great deal of profit. People came flocking to him to buy rice.

[en]
The Shaykh came back to him, saying:

[en]
Teacher: Did I not tell you:

[en]
And whoever fears Allah - He will make for him a way out. And will provide for him from where he does not expect.

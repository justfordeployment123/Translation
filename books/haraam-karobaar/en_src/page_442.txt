# English source for book page 442

[hen]
Chapter Sixty-Nine: Holding Quran Recitation Gatherings in Homes and Reciting the Quran at Graves

[en]
Teacher: (turning to Husain) How is everyone in the village? I hope all are well.

[en]
Husain: Praise be to Allah, all are well. However, last week Uncle Abdul-Hamid Abu Ali passed away.

[en]
Teacher: To Allah we belong and to Him we return! May Allah the Exalted send down His mercy upon him. Ma sha Allah, he was a very righteous man who was regular in prayer. How did he die? Was he ill?

[en]
Husain: He was ill for three days and passed away on the fourth day.

[en]
Teacher: What did his children do after he died?

[en]
Husain: His children appointed a reciter for a fee of a thousand pounds, who used to sit in their house reciting the Noble Quran from the Asr prayer until the Isha prayer. Three days after the death they hired five more reciters for a fee, and every day all of them arrange a Quran recitation in the house. May Allah the Exalted reward them; ma sha Allah, they are carrying out this duty very well.

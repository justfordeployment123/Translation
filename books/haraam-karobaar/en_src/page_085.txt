# English source for book page 85

[en]
What you did amounts to two deals in one sale, and the Prophet, peace be upon him, forbade doing this.

[en]
That is, if a sale of gold for gold is not equal, it is not permissible. This man gave you a hundred grams of old gold; you should have given him a hundred grams of new gold in exchange for it.

[en]
Abu Faris: May Allah protect you. Is this not a losing deal for me? Who will pay for the craftsmanship and the goldsmith's work...? This way I will soon be bankrupt.

[en]
Ammar: Shaykh! Is there no way of doing this according to the Shariah?

[en]
Teacher: The way according to the Shariah would have been for this shopkeeper to buy the old gold from him and pay him its price, and for him to put the money in his pocket. That deal would then be over. After that he would begin a new deal: the shopkeeper would weigh the new gold and give it to him, and take its price from him. In this way these would be two separate deals.

[en]
Abu Faris: He said jokingly: Shaykh, it comes to the same thing. What difference does it make whether I keep the money with me or give it to him? In the end it is going to come back to me anyway.

[en]
Teacher: There is a great deal of difference.

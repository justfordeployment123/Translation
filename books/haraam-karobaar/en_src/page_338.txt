# English source for book page 338

[hen]
Chapter Forty-Nine: Making the Hair Black (Applying Dye)

[en]
Young man: Teacher! There is another question.

[en]
Teacher: Yes, go ahead.

[en]
Young man: I used to dye my hair black. What is the ruling on that?

[en]
Teacher: Dyeing the hair black is forbidden because of the following hadith. The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“At the end of time there will be people who dye their hair with black, like the breasts of pigeons. These people will not find the fragrance of Paradise.”

[en]
Young man: I seek Allah's forgiveness! Such a severe punishment that they will not even be granted the fragrance of Paradise!

[en]
Teacher: Countless old people among us dye their hair black, and this has the following harms:

[en]
First: It falsifies the creation of Allah the Exalted.

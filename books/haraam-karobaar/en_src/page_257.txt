# English source for book page 257

[en]
Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal, may Allah have mercy on him, said:

[en]
“All musical instruments, such as the drum, the bell, the guitar, the tambourine, the sarangi, the tabla and the like, are forbidden because of this hadith of the Prophet, peace be upon him, since they carry the sound of instruments and music. The new musical instruments made in our time have more melody and a stronger effect than the old ones. Some people even say that the intoxication of music is more dangerous than the intoxication of alcohol.”

[en]
Imam Ibn al-Qayyim, may Allah have mercy on him, said:

[en]
“There is no doubt that the more intense the music's melody, its sound, the beat of the drum, the love words of the song, the enchanting ghazal and the martial verses become, the more its forbiddenness and its sin increase.”

[en]

# English source for book page 158

[en]
Teacher: Ammar! A very old friend of mine lives close by here. I have not met him for a long time. He has a beautiful garden, and Allah the Exalted has blessed him with a great deal of wealth. It is very hot today, so let us go there. We will rest for a while in the shade of the trees in the garden. We will eat fruit there and drink cold water, and there is a congregational mosque close by, where we will pray Zuhr.

[en]
Ammar: Shaykh! As you wish. Come, let us spend some free time there, and sit and settle our plans for what comes next as well.

[en]
Teacher: Ma sha Allah, what a good suggestion!

[en]
(The teacher and his student reached the gate of the friend's garden. After giving the greeting of peace, Shaykh Salih spoke to the watchman.)

[en]
Teacher: Is Mustafa here?

[en]
Watchman: Yes, he is right here. Your name?

[en]
Teacher: Tell Mustafa: Your old friend is standing at the gate.

[en]
(The watchman told Mustafa.)

[en]
Mustafa: Let him come in.

[en]
Watchman: Teacher! Please come in.

[en]
When the teacher and his student entered the garden, Mustafa was left speechless with joy at seeing his guest, Shaykh Salih, and Mustafa greeted Shaykh Salih warmly.

[en]
Mustafa: Welcome, welcome.

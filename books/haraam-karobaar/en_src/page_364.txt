# English source for book page 364

[en]
Teacher: I seek Allah's forgiveness! Fear Allah. How is it possible that the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, who was the embodiment of justice and fairness, would keep someone else's wealth for himself? If Urwah had had any right to it, he would certainly have given it to him. You are making a very grave false accusation against Urwah. A believer should not persist in his mistake; rather, once the truth has become clear, he should act on it at once. The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, has made clear everything permissible and impermissible; now it is not permissible for anyone to make something impermissible permissible for himself because of his own need and wishes.

[en]
Muhammad: You mean the remaining money is not lawful for me in any way?

[en]
Teacher: Yes, though in one case it is permissible: when you get permission for it from the owner of the freezer. The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“It is not lawful (to take) the wealth of a Muslim without his consent.”

[en]
That is, if you take these two hundred pounds with Sufyan's consent, there is no harm in it.

[en]
Muhammad: In sha Allah, I will give it back.

[en]
Teacher: May Allah bless you.

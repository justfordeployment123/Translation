# English source for book page 147

[en]
Judge for yourself: if true oaths have been forbidden, how severe the warning must be for false oaths!

[en]
Man: We need to do this. Do not be so harsh.

[en]
Teacher: There is nothing harsh about it; rather, the Shariah has made it forbidden.

[en]
Man: Brother! If we do not swear oaths, you people do not believe us.

[en]
Teacher: Even if swearing oaths is forbidden?

[en]
Man: Oh, servant of Allah! I have said it before: do not be so harsh. Do you have even a single proof?

[en]
Teacher: If you are given sound proofs, will you accept them then?

[en]
Man: Yes, what is wrong with that!

[en]
Teacher: Then listen carefully.

[en]
Man: I am listening carefully.

[en]
Teacher: The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Swearing oaths makes goods sell quickly, but no blessing remains in those earnings.”

[en]
That is, more money can be earned through a false oath, but that wealth is empty of blessing, and wealth empty of blessing brings loss in both this world and the Hereafter.

# English source for book page 190

[hen]
Chapter Twenty-Four: Buying and Selling Playing Cards

[en]
Shopkeeper: Laughing loudly, he said: Teacher, may Allah protect you! Buying and selling games of chance is forbidden, but what about selling playing cards?

[en]
Teacher: Buying and selling them is forbidden too, because children mostly buy playing cards to gamble and bet. After winning and losing, the children fight among themselves and fall out. And even if that does not happen, the children move up step by step from this low-level game and in the end start gambling on big things. So, in view of these harms, buying and selling playing cards and the like is forbidden. In the same way chess, ludo and the like are forbidden too, because these things are also used in gambling.

[en]
Allah the Exalted says in the Noble Quran:

[en]
Satan only wants to cause between you animosity and hatred through intoxicants and gambling and to avert you from the remembrance of Allah and from prayer.

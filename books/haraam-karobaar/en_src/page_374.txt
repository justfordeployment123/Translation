Teacher: It means buying goods on credit and then selling them for cash in the same place and in the same condition. Its form is this: I sell Zaid a car on credit for twenty thousand, to be paid in a year, and then I buy that same car back for eighteen thousand in cash. This is forbidden and impermissible, because in it nothing is really given and nothing taken; there are only words spoken.

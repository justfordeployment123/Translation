# English source for book page 290

[en]
“Pay the worker his wage before his sweat dries.”

[en]
(Meanwhile Abdul-Jawwad arrived at the gathering, glaring angrily at Abdul-Khaliq.)

[en]
Abdul-Jawwad: As-salamu alaikum wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh.

[en]
Teacher: Wa alaikum as-salam. Abdul-Jawwad! Sit down. I am talking to Abdul-Khaliq about your wages. In sha Allah, he will soon pay all of you your wages.

[en]
(Atiyyah poured tea into a cup for Abdul-Jawwad. He sat down and began to listen closely to the admonition and advice the teacher was giving Abdul-Khaliq.)

[en]
Teacher: Abdul-Khaliq! In our society the following unjust practices are found concerning the rights of workers and employees:

[en]
First: Some people flatly refuse to pay workers their wages, and because the workers are weak, they cannot get their right. But with Allah the Exalted their right is not lost. On the Day of Resurrection, Allah the Exalted will have the wronged given their right from the wrongdoer. On the Day of Resurrection the wrongdoers will have no wealth or riches; rather, judgement there will be by good deeds and bad deeds. Allah the Exalted will give the wrongdoer's good deeds to the one he wronged, and if the wrongdoer's good deeds run out, the bad deeds of the wronged will be put in the wrongdoer's scale, and after that the wrongdoer will be thrown into Hell. The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, asked the noble Companions: Do you know who the bankrupt is?

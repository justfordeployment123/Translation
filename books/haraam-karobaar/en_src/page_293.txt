# English source for book page 293

[en]
The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Allah the Exalted says: There are three kinds of people against whom I Myself will bring the case on the Day of Resurrection: a man who made a covenant in My name and then broke it; (the second,) a man who sold a free person and consumed his price; (the third,) a man who hired a worker, took full work from him, but did not pay him his wage.”

[en]
Think about it! Can any man succeed when he has declared war on Allah the Exalted? When Allah the Exalted Himself brings the case against someone, who will decide his fate? Such a man will never, ever succeed.

[en]

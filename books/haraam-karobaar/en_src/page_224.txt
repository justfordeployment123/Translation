# English source for book page 224

[en]
Teacher: All right, find out.

[en]
Atif: (went to find out about Abbas) and came back after a little while.

[en]
Teacher: What is this on the other side of your room?

[en]
Atif: Come! Let us go over there. Abbas has arrived; let us get your work done by him. Everyone got up and went into Abbas's room. Atif took a cigarette out of his pocket and, tossing it towards Abbas, said: Good morning, sir!

[en]
Abbas: Your Honour, the morning has arrived.

[en]
Teacher: What an ignorant way you have of addressing each other!

[en]
Atif: (whispering to the teacher) Teacher! This too is a way of flattering him.

[en]
Teacher: What does that mean?

[en]
Atif: Teacher! May Allah guide this friend of ours (Abbas). He does not do any work without a bribe. Until he is given something, he keeps the work hanging and puts forward countless excuses. Since we are his colleagues and know his habit, we get our work out of him by giving him cigarettes and flattering him falsely.

[en]
Ammar: Teacher! What is the ruling of the Shariah on such a habit?

[en]

# English source for book page 207

[en]
It is narrated from Jabir, may Allah be pleased with him, that the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Allah the Exalted has taken it upon Himself to give the one who drinks alcohol tinat al-khabal to drink. The noble Companions asked: O Messenger of Allah, what is tinat al-khabal? He, peace be upon him, said: It is the sweat, blood and pus of the people of Hell.”

[en]
It is narrated from Abdullah ibn Abbas, may Allah be pleased with them both, that the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“If one who is addicted to alcohol dies in that state, he will meet Allah the Exalted as though he were a worshipper of idols.”

[en]
All these texts of the Shariah give the ruling that alcohol is forbidden.

Summary of the Taxi Stand Tour

[en]
The teacher and Ammar got out of the car and went into the hospital. They collected a slip for the check-up and sat down in the waiting room to wait for their turn.

[en]

[en]

[en]

[en]
Third: Misusing a government or company car.

[en]

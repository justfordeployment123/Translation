# English source for book page 61

[en]
Kamal: I do not want to take a loan.

[en]
Mahmud: Then what do you want?

[en]
Kamal: I want to sell a tonne of maize, and the buyer can collect it from me at harvest time.

[en]
Mahmud: I am willing to buy it, but what will the price of a tonne be?

[en]
Kamal: At harvest time the price of a tonne will be about five hundred pounds.

[en]
Mahmud: Kamal! That is a very high price. That is today's rate. When the harvest comes, the rate will be much lower than that.

[en]
Kamal: How much will you pay?

[en]
Mahmud: I think we should not fix the price. We will set the price according to whatever the rate is at harvest time. Tomorrow, in sha Allah, I will send you five hundred pounds.

[en]
Kamal: May Allah reward you. Now drink your tea quickly, so that we can leave the market before the crowds come.

[en]
Mahmud: Yes, that is quite right.

[en]
At that moment the Shaykh turned to them with a smile and said very courteously: As-salamu alaikum wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh.

[en]
I heard the conversation between the two of you. If you allow me, may I discuss it with you a little? It will take only five minutes.

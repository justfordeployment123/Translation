# English source for book page 113

[en]
{{QEN 4:9}}

[en]
The things that affect a person's children after his death are these:

[en]
First: Keep fearing Allah the Exalted in your dealings, acts of worship, beliefs, trade and business dealings, pledges and agreements, and so on.

[en]
Second: Speak true and honest words, and never speak without evidence.

[en]
Salim: Teacher, I will repent to Allah the Exalted, but the state of the market is that all the traders are doing this, so what difference will it make if I alone stop doing it?

[en]
Teacher: If other people are doing wrong, that does not mean you should start doing wrong too.

[en]
Salim: What should I do now?

[en]
Teacher: The choice is yours; choose one of two things:

[en]
First: Sell to the factory in the same way you buy, that is, buy with the pearls separated and sell to the factory in the same way.

[en]
Second: Do not do the kind of business that brings you a loss.

[en]
Salim: In sha Allah, I will go to the factory next Tuesday and speak to them about this. If they agree with me, well and good; otherwise I will not make deals of this kind.

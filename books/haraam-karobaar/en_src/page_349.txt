# English source for book page 349

[en]
Sixteenth: Television causes neglect of acts of worship, such as the prayer and so on.

[en]
Seventeenth: It harms Islamic unity and worldwide brotherhood, and encourages nationalism.

[en]
These are some of the harms generally found in television. More detail on this can be found in Abu Ammar Wahid Bali's book “al-Muttaham al-Awwal” (The Prime Suspect).

[en]
Faisal: What is its ruling in the Shariah?

[en]
Teacher: Abu Ammar Wahid Bali has also made its ruling in the Shariah clear.

[en]
Faisal: What ruling has he given?

[en]
Teacher: He has written that, without doubt, both kinds of things are found on television:

[en]
First: Films, dramas, singing and music, and other indecent acts and gestures.

[en]
Second: Religious, educational and beneficial programmes. But what must be seen is which of the two prevails, and it is settled that music, drama and the like prevail. Most people watch these very things on television, and most of what television broadcasts is these very things too, while other beneficial things are broadcast only in token amounts and are watched just as little.

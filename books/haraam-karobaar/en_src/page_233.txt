# English source for book page 233

[en]
If you want to raise your children on lawful earnings, then take any one of the three ways above.

[en]
Ubaid: May Allah the Exalted grant me the ability to take the right way.

[en]
Teacher: This will be no favour of yours to the government; rather, it is your responsibility.

[en]
Ubaid: Teacher! I promise you that from now on I will take care of my responsibility and arrive for my duty on time.

[en]
Teacher: And when will you finish for the day?

[en]
Ubaid: Since there is also the shop's work, I take leave two hours before the set time. Besides, there is no particular work in the last two hours.

[en]
Teacher: Taking leave before the set time will be permissible only when the conditions we have stated above are being met.

[en]
Ubaid: Well, glory be to Allah! Is there not even that much leeway?

[en]
Teacher: I have already said that if you want to feed your children what is lawful, you will have to fear Allah and adopt one of the three ways above.

[en]
Ubaid: Teacher! In sha Allah, I will certainly put myself right.

[en]
Teacher: Praise be to Allah! May Allah put blessing in your family and children, your wealth and your business. May Allah the Exalted grant you the ability to follow the truth.

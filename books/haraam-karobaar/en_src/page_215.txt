# English source for book page 215

[en]
Teacher: So has any trader been in touch?

[en]
Lutfi: Yes, a representative of a bank came. They have been in touch about renting this building to open a new branch.

[en]
Teacher: Anyone else?

[en]
Lutfi: Likewise, a trader has been in touch about opening a cigarette and tobacco factory.

[en]
Teacher: And what have you told him?

[en]
Lutfi: So far I have not settled anything with them.

[en]
Teacher: You have done very well. Never rent your building to them.

[en]
Lutfi: Why?

[en]
Teacher: Because renting out a building for impermissible work is forbidden.

[en]
Lutfi: If it is forbidden, it is forbidden for them. What part do I have in it? I only take rent for the premises; the rest is their own business.

[en]
Teacher: If you rent out premises for impermissible work, you too will be their equal partner and helper, because in that case it will amount to cooperating in sin and transgression.

[en]
Lutfi: Shaykh! That is harsh.

[en]
Teacher: It is not harsh, nor am I saying it on my own authority. Rather, the people of knowledge say that renting out premises for tobacco, cigarettes, songs, film CDs, banks that deal in interest (riba) and the like is forbidden, because it is a form of helping in sin.

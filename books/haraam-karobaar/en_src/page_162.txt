# English source for book page 162

[en]
Teacher: Turning to Mustafa again: Is he right?

[en]
Mustafa: He said in embarrassment: Teacher! The truth is that the harvest has been very small this year, so we are putting in more grass so that we can make a little more profit.

[en]
Teacher: Mustafa! Really?

[en]
Mustafa: Teacher! I am not lying.

[en]
Teacher: I do not want you to lie either, but nor do I like you to deceive like this, because the noble Prophet, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Whoever deceives us is not one of us.”

[en]
He, peace be upon him, said this about a man who did the same wrong that you are doing. That is, he, peace be upon him, passed by a heap of grain set out for sale; he, peace be upon him, put his hand into it and his hand felt wetness. He, peace be upon him, asked its owner: What is this? He answered: O Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him! It had rained. He, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Why did you not put it on top of the heap so that people could see it too? Whoever deceives is not one of us.”

[en]
This is deception, fraud and adulteration, and it is forbidden. You are committing a very dangerous sin, because on the Day of Resurrection a banner of treachery will be set up for you because of it.

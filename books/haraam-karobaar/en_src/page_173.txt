# English source for book page 173

[en]
Teacher: My brother! I have said it before: this is interest, and an extremely serious crime. In the Noble Quran, nothing other than interest has been described as amounting to a declaration of war with Allah the Exalted. Allah says:

[en]
{{QEN 2:278}} And if you do not, then be informed of a war (against you) from Allah and His Messenger.

[en]
This one blessed verse of the Noble Quran is enough on its own to show how vile, evil and hateful this crime is.

[en]
The Prophet, peace be upon him, has called accursed anyone who takes part in dealings in interest, in whatever capacity. Jabir, may Allah be pleased with him, says that the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, cursed the one who consumes interest, the one who pays it, the one who writes it down and those who act as witnesses. The words of another narration are that they are all equal in sin.

[en]
From this we learn that merely writing up dealings in interest, acting as a witness to them, considering a business built on interest to be right, or supervising dealings in interest and so on are all impermissible and forbidden.

[en]

# English source for book page 225

[en]
“The curse of Allah is on both the one who takes a bribe and the one who gives a bribe.”

[en]
The one who takes a bribe and the one who gives it are both deprived of Allah's vast mercy. Such people have no worth in Allah's sight. So a person should not betray the trust of his position and his employment. To misuse one's position and take bribes merely in order to favour some friend or relative is a very great crime, and it is forbidden. The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“Whoever we appoint to some work and also provide with a salary for it, whatever he takes beyond that will be a betrayal of trust.”

[en]
That is, if this man swallows up for himself wealth that is meant to be spent on the poor, orphans and widows, that will be a betrayal of trust. Now hear another incident of this kind as well, which took place in the time of the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him.

[en]
Ammar: Tell us, what incident is it?

[en]
Teacher: The Prophet, peace be upon him, sent a man of the tribe of Azd, Ibn al-Lutbiyyah, to collect charity. When he came back, he said to the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, that this wealth was for the public treasury and this had been given to him as a gift.

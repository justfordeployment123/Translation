# English source for book page 392

[en]
Manager: What is meant by lahw al-hadith (the amusement of speech)?

[en]
Teacher: Abdullah ibn Mas'ud, may Allah be pleased with him, says:

[en]
“By lahw al-hadith is meant singing and music.”

[en]
Hasan al-Basri, may Allah have mercy on him, says that according to most of the commentators it means singing and music.

[en]
In another place Allah the Exalted says:

[en]
And incite (to senselessness) whoever you can among them with your voice and assault them with your horses and foot soldiers.

[en]
The scholars say that this means singing and music and musical instruments.

[en]
The Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“A time will come when some people of my Ummah will make fornication, silk, alcohol and music lawful.”

[en]
Manager: What does this hadith mean?

[en]
It means that soon there will be people among the Muslims who think that fornication, wearing silk clothing, drinking alcohol and music are lawful, whereas in reality these things are forbidden.

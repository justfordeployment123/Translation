# English source for book page 170

[hen]
Chapter Twenty-One: Taking Agricultural Loans on Interest (riba)

[en]
The teacher, his student and Mustafa were busy talking when suddenly Mustafa's neighbour Abu Ali arrived.

[en]
Abu Ali: As-salamu alaikum wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh.

[en]
Mustafa: Wa alaikum as-salam wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh. May Allah always keep you happy and well.

[en]
Abu Ali: And may Allah keep you too on the path of obedience to Him.

[en]
Mustafa: It looks as though you are coming back from a journey?

[en]
Abu Ali: Yes, I had gone over to the bank.

[en]
Mustafa: Was all well there?

[en]
Abu Ali: I had gone to the bank to take an agricultural loan in easy instalments. The bank was offering twenty thousand rupees at a small rate of interest (about one per cent). I thought I would take the loan and meet my needs.

[en]
Mustafa: The idea is a good one, but is it also permissible?

[en]
Abu Ali: Yes, it is permissible.

[en]
Mustafa: Is it really permissible?

[en]
Abu Ali: Brother! This is a great convenience that the government provides for farmers. Besides, it is a need of today, so one should not be strict about it.

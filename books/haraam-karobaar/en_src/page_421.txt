# English source for book page 421

[en]
“Muslims fulfil their conditions.”

[en]
If the conditions on which you are working include the condition that you may use the car for yourself and may also give your relatives and kin free rides, then it is fine. One thing you said is that reason does not accept this. Remember, then, that reason and custom count only so long as they do not lead to going against the Shariah; and where reason is pushing towards going against the Shariah, reason will be given no weight.

[en]
(Ten days earlier, Husam had left his tape recorder at this same shop to be repaired.)

[en]

[en]
Everyone: Wa alaikum as-salam wa rahmatullah.

[en]
Husam: How are you? I hope you are all well.

[en]
Faisal: Praise be to Allah, we are all well, thanks be to Allah.

[en]
Husam: Has my tape recorder been repaired?

[en]
Salah: No, it has not been repaired yet. Some of its parts have gone bad and stopped working.

[en]
Husam: Then you should have put in new parts from your own stock.

[en]
Salah: We do have new parts, but they are for trade.

[en]
Husam: What do you mean? I did not understand you.

[en]
Salah: I mean that these parts are not genuine, and we keep them only for selling, because they do not last more than three months.

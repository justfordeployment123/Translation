# English source for book page 424

[hen]
Chapter Sixty-Five: Having Gaps Made Between the Teeth for Beauty

[en]
At the clinic, the teacher saw that one of his classmates, Sa'd, was sitting on his right.

[en]
Teacher: Welcome, Sa'd! How are you? How is everyone at home?

[en]
Sa'd: Praise be to Allah! All is well. How is your health?

[en]
Teacher: Praise be to Allah! I am well too.

[en]
Sa'd: May Allah heal you and keep you safe from every kind of trouble. I hope it is nothing serious that has brought you to the clinic.

[en]
Teacher: I have only come to have my teeth cleaned; I have not come to get medicine for any illness. And yes! What has brought you here?

[en]
Sa'd: My wife's upper and lower front teeth have become quite long and large. She wanted to make them beautiful and have them made smaller, so I have brought her here for that purpose.

[en]
Teacher: Is it right to have the teeth made smaller, or to have gaps made between them for beauty?

[en]
Sa'd: Teacher! Do not be so strict. You still have not given up your extremist views. How can this be impermissible?

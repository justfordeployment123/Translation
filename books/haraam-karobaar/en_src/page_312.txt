# English source for book page 312

By respecting it and adorning our faces with it, we can draw near to Allah the Exalted. Allah the Exalted says:

[en]
{{QEN 22:32}}

[en]
More important than this is that it is the Sunnah of the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, which he, peace be upon him, commanded to be grown in full. But sadly, today most Muslims have fallen short with regard to this great blessing. Honour, esteem and respect for it have gone from their hearts, and shaving it and cutting it into all sorts of designs has become the habit of Muslim young men. This beloved Sunnah of the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, has been reduced to a joke.

[en]
Young man: Teacher! What is the importance of the beard in Islam?

[en]
Teacher: Keeping a beard was the most beloved Sunnah of the prophets, the noble Companions and the righteous early generations. All the best of this Ummah honoured it; not one of them had his beard shaved.

[en]
Listen to a few incidents to refresh your faith:

[en]
First: Qais ibn Sa'd, may Allah be pleased with him, naturally had no beard on his face. The people of his tribe used to say: Our chief Qais ibn Sa'd is a very brave and wise man, but sadly he has no beard on his face. If only a beard could be bought for dirhams, we would buy him one, so that he would look like a complete man.

# English source for book page 76

[en]
What greater loss could there be than that Allah the Exalted takes the blessing away from your trade and your wealth brings you no benefit?

[en]
Take up piety and fear Allah. Deception will be of no benefit at all with Allah the Exalted.

[en]
Shopkeeper: He said with regret and shame: Shaykh, I really did do it on purpose. I apologise for it.

[en]
Teacher: You must give the wronged man back his right, so that you may be saved from sin.

[en]
Shopkeeper: What should I do now?

[en]
Teacher: Take back the earlier rice from Fawzi and give him the rice he asked for.

[en]
Shopkeeper: Fawzi! Tomorrow, with Allah's help, I will deliver that rice to your house.

[en]
Fawzi: I will wait for you tomorrow. My house is next to the mosque.

[en]
Teacher: May Allah reward you and bless your business, because you have accepted the truth. Was-salamu alaikum wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh.

[en]
Shopkeeper: Wa alaikum as-salam wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh.

[en]
The Shaykh bade Fawzi farewell and, as he was going back from the market, turned to his student Ammar.

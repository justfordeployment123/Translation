# English source for book page 340

[en]
“Change the colour of this whiteness with something, but keep him away from black.”

[en]
Men and women are equal in this ruling; that is, for both of them dyeing the hair black is prohibited, and making it red or yellow is permissible. Taking a wage for dyeing hair black is also forbidden, because the Prophet, peace be upon him, said:

[en]
“When Allah the Exalted forbids something, He also forbids its price.”

[en]
Young man: May Allah reward you with good.

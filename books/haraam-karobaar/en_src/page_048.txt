# English source for book page 48

[en]
Abu Ali: Void! Shaykh, is the form of the deal we have put before you not valid?

[en]
Teacher: Should we not first of all know what this form of sale is called?

[en]
Abu Fahd: Shaykh! Tell us, what is it called?

[en]
Teacher: This kind is called bay' ath-thimar qabla buduwwi salahiha (dealing in fruit before it ripens). Whether it is fruit or grain, buying and selling it before it ripens is impermissible.

[en]
Abu Ali: Shaykh! But all the farmers do it this way, don't they?

[en]
Abu Fahd: Shaykh! How can one tell that the fruit has ripened?

[en]
Teacher: When the fruit changes colour and reaches the state in which it can normally be used, that is, when it can be eaten; and if it is grain or the like, when it has become firm. Buying and selling fruit and the like in any state other than this is void and impermissible.

[en]
Abu Ali: He spoke angrily and in a loud voice: Void, not valid! Why is this forbidden? Who has forbidden it? Give us the evidence for it! Tell us its ruling from the Shariah, not from people's opinions.

[en]
Abu Fahd: Brother, be patient. We did not know about this before; now let us understand it properly. Yes, Shaykh, please explain it.

[en]
Teacher: Buying and selling fruit before it ripens is forbidden, because in that case it is the sale of something unknown, which counts as deception, and it is a way of consuming people's wealth wrongfully. The evidence for this is the following hadith of the Prophet.

# English source for book page 252

[en]
Hasan! It is a result of co-education itself that court marriages are increasing, the trend of fornication and the things that lead to it is growing, and going without the veil and wearing un-Islamic dress are becoming common. In your view, should this filthy and dirty game go on, or should it stop?

[en]
Hasan: Teacher! But these harms come from the co-education system, not from the male teacher?

[en]
Teacher: Hasan! However good a teacher's character may be, however virtuous his conduct, it is still a fact that he will raise his eyes and look at the girls, he will speak to them, and so on. A poet has said:

[en]
“Men who look at women are like dogs that circle around meat.”

[en]
My only prayer is that Allah the Exalted may save you from burning in this fire and keep you in His protection and safety.

[en]
Hasan: A prayer to be saved from fire? What does that mean? Am I burning in a fire?

[en]
Teacher: I mean burning in the fire of love and passion in this world and, if one does not repent, burning in the fire of Hell in the Hereafter. The summary of what I have said is that male teachers teaching girls is forbidden for the following two reasons:

[en]
First: The mixing of the male teacher and the girl students.

# English source for book page 262

[en]
Teacher: Ammar! I think we should pick up some vegetables on the way as we go home. A farmer friend of mine has his farmstead close by here, and he is surely working in the fields there at this time.

[en]
Ammar: As you wish.

[en]
(The teacher and his student set off for their farmer friend's farmstead. There, Atiyyah was busy working in the fields.)

[en]
Teacher: As-salamu alaikum wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh, Atiyyah!

[en]
Atiyyah: Wa alaikum as-salam wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh. Atiyyah was delighted to see Teacher Salih arrive. Welcome!

[en]
Teacher: We have come to ask how you are and to get some fresh vegetables from your fields.

[en]
Atiyyah: Welcome, come and sit down.

[en]
Teacher: We are in a bit of a hurry. If you have time, pick us some vegetables.

[en]
Atiyyah: Now you will not be able to leave without having lunch.

[en]
(Atiyyah's house was right there. He wanted to hurry and open the door of the sitting room, but the teacher stopped him, so he told the household to make tea.)

[en]
Teacher: Sitting in the open air is better for one's health than sitting in a room.

[en]
(Atiyyah spread a mat right there under the trees in the fields, and everyone sat there and began to drink tea.)

# English source for book page 59

[en]
Teacher: Wa alaikum as-salam wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh.

[en]
Everyone hurried off to their homes so as to keep away from sin.

[en]
How true it is:

[en]
“The one who guides to good is like the one who does it.”

[en]
The Shaykh said to Ammar with a smile: Ammar! Did you see how much blessing there is in meeting people? And one also has to be patient with the harm that comes from them.

[en]
Ammar: Shaykh! May Allah benefit you.

[en]
The Shaykh and Ammar now began to walk about the market to look into some other matter. Suddenly the Shaykh said:

[en]
Ammar! What do you think? What if we go to the restaurant at the start of the market, rest for a little while, drink some water and so on, and then start our round again?

[en]
Ammar: Shaykh! As you wish.

[en]
Teacher: Come, let us go! May Allah the Exalted grant us blessing.

# English source for book page 52

[hen]
Chapter Three: Selling Goods On Before Taking Possession of Them

[en]
Shaykh Salih and his student Ammar passed by two men in the market. One was saying to the other: Abu Rashid! What you are doing is forbidden for you. There is rice lying everywhere in my house. My house is small. I have tied my horse up in the children's bedroom.

[en]
Abu Rashid: Abu Khalid! I have spoken to a wholesale trader, and our deal has been settled too. He will soon come to your village and take the rice from everyone I bought it from. Wait until next Saturday.

[en]
Abu Khalid: Saturday! Am I to wait another week? Two weeks have already gone by. This is not right. Then Abu Khalid saw a group of the people from whom Abu Rashid had bought rice coming from a distance, and Abu Rashid saw that they too were angry.

[en]
Abu Khalid: Come, sir! Sa'd, Hamuda, Mukhaimir, Gharib and old Ayyad: these are all the people you bought rice from.

[en]
As soon as they arrived, they all flared up at Abu Rashid, and their voices began to rise.

[en]
Sa'd: This is not good at all.
